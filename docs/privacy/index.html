Privacy notice

Last updated 25 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data Northing handles and your rights. Northing is run by William Mikollovci, who is the data controller. Contact: opening an issue on GitHub (public; do not include personal details there).

Visitors to the site

The site sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads nothing from other companies' servers: fonts and scripts are served with the site. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses in its server logs for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

People in company records

The analysis uses Companies House records, which include the names of directors and of people with significant control (owners). This is personal data even though it is public. Northing uses it only to understand who owns a company and whether it could be sold, and to notice changes such as an owner leaving. Names of individuals are not published on the site or in the data files; pages describe ownership in general terms (for example "founder-owned"). Some descriptions draw on company websites and news reports.

Lawful basis and retention

The lawful basis is legitimate interests: analysing companies for investment research, using information the law already makes public, with names kept out of what is published. Working data is kept while a company is covered and deleted within twelve months after it is removed.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of personal data held about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, or object to its use, by contacting opening an issue on GitHub (public; do not include personal details there). You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
