Ten UK digital-infrastructure companies worth a closer look

Every week this page reads the public accounts of UK fibre, wireless, satellite, data-centre and network-building companies, and ranks the best fits for a private investor buying a £10–250m business.

    The type of deal says what kind of investor a company suits. It is a label and does not change the score.

    Steady compounder
    Mostly repeat revenue and steady margins. Suits infrastructure funds that want reliable income.
    High-growth
    Sales up 25% or more in a year, with less predictable profit. Suits growth and mid-market private equity.
    Fixable dip
    Sales falling or margins thin, with a possible fix still to be confirmed. Suits funds that buy and turn around.
    Between types
    Fits none cleanly, often because sales come project by project. The reason is shown for each company.
