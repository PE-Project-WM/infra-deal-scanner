What a ranking means

A ranking measures how closely a company fits one written investment case for a mid-market buyer. It is not a rating of the company's quality, management, solvency or prospects, and a low position is not a criticism. Companies have not been contacted and have not endorsed their inclusion.

Accuracy

Figures are read automatically from filed accounts, some by text recognition, and some inputs are estimates; each is labelled. Accounts can be more than a year old. Errors are possible.
