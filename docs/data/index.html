Data & API

The rankings, every company profile, the sectors and the method, as JSON and CSV. Free to read, no key needed.

Open data, free to read

Everything on this site is also published as plain files that people and programs can read: JSON for programs and AI agents, CSV for spreadsheets. There is no key, sign-up or rate limit; the files are static and rebuilt every Monday with the site. The base address is:

https://pe-project-wm.github.io/infra-deal-scanner/api/v1/

AI agents can start from llms.txt, a short guide to the site and its data in plain text.

Example

One entry from rankings.json:

{
  "rank": 1,
  "company_number": "03921568",
  "name": "Wifinity",
  "sector": "wireless",
  "score": 93.4,
  "confidence": "Low",
  "deal_type": "Steady compounder",
  "likely_deal_size": "Could be above £250m",
  "page": "companies/03921568/",
  "json": "api/v1/companies/03921568.json"
}

Fetch it from the command line:

curl https://pe-project-wm.github.io/infra-deal-scanner/api/v1/rankings.json

Fields to know

  • score: out of 100, the sum of score_parts. Fit with the investment case, not a price.
  • confidence: High, Medium or Low, with confidence_reasons. Never added to the score.
  • likely_deal_size: an estimate from EBITDA and a multiple range; see likely_deal_size_basis.
  • Money is in pounds (GBP) as plain numbers, converted where the accounts are in another currency. Margins and growth are fractions (0.25 means 25%).
  • Dates are ISO 8601 (YYYY-MM-DD). company_number is the Companies House number and links to the official record.

Use and attribution

You may read, quote and reuse the data, including in other tools and AI systems, if you credit "Northing" with a link to https://pe-project-wm.github.io/infra-deal-scanner/. Companies House material is under the Open Government Licence v3.0. The data is information, not advice; see the terms of use.

Updates

Last update 25 November 2026. Files are replaced each Monday; meta.json carries the date and method version so you can tell when something changed.

Not included

Names of directors and owners are left out, because they are people. The official company record has them.
