About Northing

Northing ranks private UK infrastructure companies for investors. It starts with UK digital infrastructure: every company with £10–250m of sales that a mid-market private equity or infrastructure investor could buy. Energy transition, water and waste, transport and social infrastructure are planned.

Why it exists

Mid-market investors spend weeks finding companies before they come to market. Most company databases list who exists; Northing tries to say which companies deserve attention, why, and roughly what they might cost, with every step written down so it can be checked.

Who runs it

Northing is run by William Mikollovci, independently. No company pays to be included or to change its position, and no company has endorsed the ranking.

How it is built

The data comes from Companies House: the public register and each company's filed accounts. A pipeline written in Python reads the accounts, applies the filters and scores each company; it runs every Monday on GitHub Actions and rebuilds this site. It was built with help from Claude, an AI assistant. The method and code are public on GitHub.

Accessibility

The site aims to meet WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, keyboard navigation, visible focus, and pages that work without JavaScript. If something is hard to use, please report it through the corrections page.
