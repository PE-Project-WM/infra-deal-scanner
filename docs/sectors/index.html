What each sector covers

Fibre networks

Companies that own or operate fixed fibre networks: full-fibre altnets, wholesale and regional network operators.

Not included: Fibre build contractors (Network build and maintenance) and resellers of other firms' lines (Business connectivity and communications).

Wireless and mobile networks

Companies that own or operate wireless networks: managed wifi, fixed wireless access, private mobile networks and IoT connectivity.

Not included: Towers and small cells (own sector) and mast build contractors (Network build and maintenance).

Towers and small cells

Owners of passive mobile infrastructure (masts, rooftops, small cells) leased to mobile operators.

Not included: Companies that only design or build masts.

Data centres and hosting

Operators of data centres and providers of hosting, colocation and cloud infrastructure.

Not included: Contractors that build or fit out data centres (Network build and maintenance).

Satellite

Satellite operators and providers of satellite connectivity.

Critical communications

Radio, paging and secure communications for emergency services, defence, rail and other users who cannot lose service.

Network build and maintenance

Contractors that design, build, install and maintain fibre, mobile, utility and data-centre networks for the owners.

Business connectivity and communications

Providers of connectivity, telephony, mobile and managed network services to businesses and the public sector, mostly over other firms' networks.

Subsea cables

Owners, operators and maintainers of subsea telecoms cables landing in the UK.
