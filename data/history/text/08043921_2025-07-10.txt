Business review

Aerial Direct operates within the telecommunications sector, delivering mobile and fixed line services, system

installations, handset financing, and maintenance to over 160,000 customers across the UK.

For FY2024, the company reported:

° Revenue: £39.6 million

* Adjusted EBITDA: £8.8 million

Despite the modest growth in revenue, EBITDA declined largely due to a reduction in annual network price
: increases, driven by a fall in inflation from 13.8% to 4.5%. This change alone impacted EBITDA by £2 million
i year-on-year.

Strategic Investments and Innovation

Aerial Direct has maintained its commitment to becoming the UK’s leading data-driven mobile and unified

communications provider through continued investment in technology and talent.
Key Highlights:

¢ Acquisition: In November 2023, Aerial acquired a 51% stake in Layered Technology Limited, trading as

Vantage Cloud. Known for its fully automated telecoms framework, Vantage Cloud delivers instant pricing

from over 190 APIs, digital contracts, and industry-first coverage checkers—enabling hundreds of quotes in

under 60 seconds. This positions Aerial as an innovation leader in telecoms.
e Aland Data Science: Through advanced analytics and Al tools, Aerial has enhanced performance across

KPls, leading to smarter operational efficiencies and stronger targeting across six segmented customer

groups.

¢ Product Expansion:

° Mobile: Building on its strong heritage, Aerial expanded strategic partnerships to enhance its mobile
offerings.

° Unified Communications: A major platform rewrite of IP Cortex (acquired in 2019) is underway, with a
launch slated for September 2025. It incorporates containerisation for scalable upgrades and is
designed to rival the best global IP telephony platforms.

° IT Solutions, Private Cloud & Cybersecurity: A full IT managed services suite was launched in
October 2023. Early successes include IT audits that uncovered and resolved significant security risks
for customers.

° Connectivity: In addition to the Vantage Cloud acquisition, Aerial launched Beebu. forming new
partnerships to offer highly competitive national connectivity solutions.

2.4 Revenue

Revenue is recognised to the extent that it is probable that the economic benefits will flow to the

company and the revenue can be reliably measured. Revenue is measured as the fair value of the

consideration received or receivable, excluding discounts, rebates, value added tax and other sales
taxes. The following criteria must also be met before revenue is recognised:

* network commissions revenue is recognised with reference to the stage of completion of the
service under the individual contract with the Mobile Network Operator (‘MNO’), as outlined in
network commissions section;

* revenue from the sale of of goods is recognised at the point of sale or, where later, upon delivery
to the customer and is stated net of returns;

* revenue earned from ongoing performance obligation to provide customer support is recognised
over the term of the contracts when the company delivers the performance of its contractual
obligations. At the end deferred income predominantly related to deferred customer support
revenues; and

¢ revenue generated from the provision of fixed asset and mobile network services is recognised
as it is earned over the lives of the relevant customer contracts.

Network commissions

The company operates under contracts with a MNO. Over the life of these contracts the service

provided by the company to the MNO is the procurement of connections to the MNO's network.

The individual consumer enters into a contract with the MNO for the MNO to supply the ongoing

airtime over that contract period.

The company earns a commission for the service provided to each MNO (‘network commission’).

Revenue is recognised with reference to the stage of completion of the service under the individual

contract with the MNO.

A key judgement associated with this recognition is the unit of account used in recognition. The

sSompany has determined that the number and value of consumers provided to the MNO in any given

Month represents the best output measure of stage of completion of each contract. The level of

network commission earned is based on a share of the monthly payments made by the consumer to

the MNO. The total consideration receivable is determined by both fixed (monthly line rental) and
variable elements (being out of bundle and out of contract revenue share). The company recognises
ali of the fixed revenue share expected over a consumers contract when a consumer is connected to
the MNO. This gives rise to accrued income being recognised, which is collected over the consumer

Contract terms.

Estimating in advance variable elements of revenue is subject to significant judgements and is

dependent on consumer behaviour after the point of recognition. The company does not consider

that the amount of out of bundle and out of contract revenue can be measured reliably in advance,
and therefore these revenues are recognised in the month they are received.

The method of measuring the fair value of the revenue and associated receivables in the month of

connection is to estimate all future cash flows that will be received from the network and discount

these based on their timing of receipt.

discounting these based on their timing of receipt. Subsequently, network commission receivables are

obligation, revenue and profit recognition is based on the costs incurred to deliver the on going service.
Measuring the time value of money for asset and liability recognition

be at its present value required to settle the liability or asset receipt. The discount rate used for this
calculation is a pre-tax rate which reflects management's estimate of the current market assessment of
the time value of money and risks specific to the asset or liability in question. A discount rate of 4% (2023 -
4%) is used.
