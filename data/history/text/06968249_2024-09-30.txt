NATTERBOX LIMITED
Strategic report
For the Year Ended 31 December 2023
Principal activities and review of the business
The principal activity of Natterbox Limited in the period was the provision of hosted telecommunication services software and operating as part of the N2JB group, whose parent undertaking is N2JB Limited. Natterbox Limited licenses its software products developed by other group companies and makes and receives management recharges within the group. The company has a wholly owned US subsidiary, Natterbox, Inc. 
In 2023, Natterbox Limited achieved revenue growth of 13.8% across the UK, Europe, and the Rest of the World.
The group’s technology roadmap was a key strategic priority for 2023, and new products and enhancements were released to the company’s customers throughout the year on a regular basis. The group continues to develop its roadmap into 2024, including AI and platform infrastructure improvements.
Key performance indicators
Turnover for the year increased by 13.8% to £17.5m in total and across all geographic regions in which the company operates. UK revenues grew by 29.7% and in the US and the Rest of the World by 4.9%. Cost of sales (including license costs from other group companies) increased by 49.8%, giving an increase in gross profit of 3.6% over the previous year to £12.4m.
Administrative expenses were reduced by over 5%, leading to profit before tax of £1.77m after license fees and management charges (2022: £0.89m)
At the end of 2023, Natterbox Limited had cash resources of £1.2M and has continued to effectively manage its cash and financing resources within the group’s overall resources throughout the period and into the future.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The board of directors continues to review its business models and forecasts and identify key areas of risk to their outcomes.
Natterbox Limited is committed to prioritizing the health and well-being of its workforce. It continues to support flexible hybrid working arrangements for its workforce and to provide a safe working environment.
The company continues to explore scenarios for revenues, costs and cashflows in the short and longer terms.
The business continues to manage its liquidity by effective credit control and debt management to balance its growth objectives with credit extended within appropriate limits. The business model is based on securing longer-term contracts with its customers payable in advance to mitigate credit risk.
The board monitors and seeks to mitigate the impact of inflationary pressure on input costs and the profitability of contracts, for example by including contractual pricing variation clauses. The company manages its cash position to offset increases in bank base rates and currency fluctuations where possible and is exploring hedging strategies to manage its interest rate risks within its group context.
The company promotes an inclusive working environment and seeks to engage with its employees’ voice to develop options to alleviate the impact of cost-of-living increases on its workforce and to reduce pressures on employment costs.
The board recognizes its responsibilities towards its data security and privacy and has policies and training programs in place to maintain a resilient, secure infrastructure and foster security awareness and compliance. 
The board continues to be committed to play an active role in overseeing its tax and regulatory compliance obligations across all aspects of its business and in all jurisdictions in which it operates to ensure that its strategic approach remains aligned with the group’s corporate values.
As a result, the board considers its business model to be sustainable and is focusing attention on developing its business systems and operations to ensure that it is scalable for the future.
The board considers its business’ telephony products, network and infrastructure will continue to provide opportunities for increased productivity for its customers’ changing working environments. The group closely manages its product release programs to ensure system availability and to work with its key strategic partners to bring to market new and diverse ranges of products and technologies.
Exposure to currency exchange fluctuation risks is kept under review. Using natural hedging and ensuring that its foreign currency holdings are appropriate within the group context, the board anticipates that it will continue to fulfil its strategic objectives.
As the company expands into new markets and territories, there will be continued exposure to other business and technological risks that the group will meet and seek to prevent.

Other key performance indicators
Natterbox Limited monitors its sales pipeline, order intake, win/loss ratios, cost of acquiring its customers and recurring revenues so that the board of directors can ensure that its sales and marketing functions are effective, and that the company can fulfil its growth objectives.
Customer retention rates remain high but are anticipated to come under pressure due to global economic pressures on its customer base, so the group’s executive management team continues to focus on minimizing customer churn.
=== page 5 ===
A core strategic objective is to develop and maintain the ability of the group to attract and retain its staff. The company continues to direct resources into the recruitment and retention of its highly skilled workforce. Both in the UK and internationally, the company is successfully developing a highly experienced management team that will continue to fulfil the group’s objectives in 2024 and beyond.
Future developments
In 2024, Natterbox Limited’s parent undertaking is seeking to secure expanded borrowing facilities with its lenders that will provide further market opportunities for the company’s products and services globally. 
The company has continued to deliver important updates of its core Service Cloud Voice, SMS, Freedom, Insight, PCI Payments and AI products to its customers throughout 2023 and into 2024. The group continues to expand its sales and marketing presence internationally and to align itself further with its key business partners.

The directors consider this appropriate as N2JB Limited, its ultimate parent company, has confirmed that it will provide the company with whatever financial support may be necessary in order to enable Natterbox Limited to meet its liabilities and commitments as they fall due within the foreseeable future.
