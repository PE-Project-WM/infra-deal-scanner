STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 MAY 2025
Introduction
Nano Fibre UK Limited (“the Company”) presents its Strategic Report for the year ended 31 May 2025. The report provides a fair review of the business, an analysis of performance using financial and other key performance indicators, and outlines the principal risks and uncertainties facing the Company.
Business review
The Company continued to operate in the wireless telecommunications and engineering consultancy sector.
Turnover increased to £21.9 million (2024: £9.9 million), driven by new client contracts and the expansion of technical services. Gross profit rose to £5.59 million (2024: £3.32 million).
Administrative expenses increased to £5.25 million (2024: £3.38 million) due to higher recruitment, relocation and training costs linked to operational expansion. Despite the increased cost base, the Company achieved a profit before tax of £144,758 (2024: loss £171,422) and a profit after tax of £457,830 following recognition of a deferred tax credit.
Net cash used in operating activities amounted to £0.15 million (2024: inflow £0.57 million), primarily reflecting increased working capital requirements arising from growth. Capital expenditure during the year totalled £0.43 million (2024: £0.45 million), principally in operational vehicles and equipment.
The Company had an average of 315 employees during the year (2024: 169), reflecting significant scaling of operations.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The directors identify the following key risks:
• Market risk – fluctuations in demand from the telecoms and technology sectors;
• Operational risk – maintaining service quality while scaling capacity;
=== page 7 ===
• Credit risk – increased exposure as trade debtors rose to £1.7 million;
• Liquidity risk – management of working capital, finance lease obligations and shareholder funding arrangements in light of the Company’s net liability position. The directors closely monitor cash flow forecasts and funding facilities to ensure the Company is able to meet its obligations as they fall due;
• Regulatory and compliance risk – ensuring full adherence to telecom, data-protection, and safety legislation.
The Company actively monitors these risks through financial forecasting, customer credit assessments, and continual internal-control review.
Financial key performance indicators
To assess performance and business health, the directors monitor several key performance indicators:
• Revenue growth – +121% (£21.9 m vs £9.9 m)
• Gross margin – 25.5% (2024: 33.5 %)
• Operating margin – 1.8% (2024: -0.6%)
• Current ratio – 0.70 (2024: 0.87)
• Employee growth – +86% (315 employees vs 169)
These metrics confirm strong top-line growth, margin compression during scale-up, and ongoing liquidity management.

Going Concern
The financial statements have been prepared on the basis of accounting policies applicable to a going concern. The directors believe that the company has adequate financial resources to continue in operation for the foreseeable future and accordingly the financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.
