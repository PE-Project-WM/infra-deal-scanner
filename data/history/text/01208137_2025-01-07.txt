Strategic Report
This strategic report has been prepared for Target Group Limited and its subsidiary undertakings as a whole (the
: “Group” or “Target Group”), so includes matters which are significant to these entities.
Review of the business
Principal activities
The principal activities of Target Group are the provision of IT Services, Business Process Services (BPS), Managed
Services and software development alongside Business Process Outsourcing (BPO) to clients across the public and
private sector. We aim to transform customer experiences by delivering market leading services and technology with
a clear focus on regulatory compliance. Our services are delivered in highly regulated mission critical environments,
and our platform supports billions of pounds of business, We have over 40 years’ experience and are trusted by over
25 clients.
Through Elderbridge Limited which is part of the Target Group, we act as lender of record on a number of lending
portfolios, providing management services for portfolio owners in the FCA regulated environment.
Financial review
The Company has reported a consolidated loss before taxation of £14,677k (2023: £12,834k). Turnover for the year
ended 31 March 20240f £54,919k(31 March 2023 of £66,268k) represents a decrease of 17%
(2023: decrease of 14%). The gross margin for the year was 20% (2023: 16%).
Operating loss increased from £12,644k for the year ended 31 March 2023 to an operating loss of £14,718k for the
year ended 31 March 2024, this increase includes the impact of the above-mentioned provision. The macro-economic
climate has continued to be challenging with regards to our financial performance: transaction volumes have continued
to reduce, as has growth in the originating portfolios we service and there have been inflationary uplifts in our cost
base. In implementing our new strategy, we have focussed on the simplification of the running of the company in
order to reduce the impact of the macro-economic environment on the operating costs of the business.
Under a new leadership team, Target Group has continued to transform the way the business operates through a clear
focus on simplifying the business. This year we have focused on streamlining the way we work; enhancing how we
deliver services to clients; right sizing the organisation; and building stronger ties with our parent organisation Tech
Mahindra (“Tech M”). Target Group is integrating with Tech M’s Business Process Services arm. Through the
integration and the partnership approach this entails, Target Group will be able to scale its business into new
geographies and present further opportunities to expand and grow.
We continued the development of our award-winning Mortgage Hub product, which aims to transform the home
buying journey for lenders and customers. The product development will continue in the coming year to launch and
then expand on the various customer journeys it can support. We are in the exploratory stages of several opportunities
which could leverage this solution.
Target Group Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of Tech Mahindra Fintech Holdings Limited, with the ultimate
parent company being Tech Mahindra Limited. In the year to 31 March 2024, Tech Mahindra Fintech Holdings
Limited completed two equity injections into the Target Group: in June 2023 (£13.2m) and March 2024 (£7.5m). This
demonstrates Tech Mahindra’s investment in Target Group’s future, confidence in the strategic direction of the
business under the new management team’s leadership and a commitment from Tech Mahindra to ensure the
customers of the Target Group are treated fairly. These capital injections returned the Target Group to a shareholder
funds surplus, provided a positive cash balance and gave the Directors of the Target Group confidence in the Group’s
ability to continue as a going concern. The appropriate level of funds was flowed down into the regulated entities in
the Group to ensure they remained solvent.
This year Target Group continued a project to review various historical issues related to the servicing of portfolios,
for which a contingent liability was recorded in the last financial year. As detailed in note 16 to the financial
statements, the project is now sufficiently developed that we expect remediation will be required and, as a result, a
provision of £7.2m has been recognised in the year.

Financial risk management objectives and policies
Our activities expose Target Group to numerous financial risks including credit risk, cash flow risk and liquidity risks.
Cash flow risk
All treasury matters are coordinated via the relevant group functions of the ultimate parent entity Tech Mahindra
Limited.
There is minimal interest rate risk to Target Group as no debt is held with parties external to the Tech Mahindra group,
except finance leases and an overdraft facility provided by HSBC.
Credit risk .
Target Group’s principal financial assets are cash at bank and trade and other receivables.
Target Group’s credit risk is primarily attributable to its trade receivables. The amounts presented in the balance sheet
are net of allowances for doubtful receivables. An allowance for impairment is made where there is an identified loss
event which, based on previous experience, is evidence of a reduction in the recoverability of cash flows.
Target Group has no significant concentration of credit risk, with exposure spread over a large number of clients.
Most of our clients are blue chip investment, retail banking, finance and insurance companies, and government bodies
which represent a low credit risk.
Liquidity risk
, In order to maintain liquidity to ensure that sufficient funds are available for ongoing operations and future
developments, we use short-term debt finance when required. Finance leases are used by thc company to facilitate the
purchase of long-term assets (note 1).
Regulatory risk
Target Servicing Ltd is classified as an Article 3 MiFLD exempt firm and also holds the status as ISA Plan Manager
with HMRC. Target Group’s compliance function reviews regulatory requirements on an ongoing basis to ensure
compliance with all relevant permissions held.
Elderbridge Limited was established in 2014 as a loan servicing company authorised and regulated by the Financial
Conduct Authority (FCA). The entity holds various lending permissions for consumer credit and mortgages.
Target Group’s compliance function reviews the group’s regulatory requirements on an ongoing basis to ensure
compliance with all relevant permissions held.
