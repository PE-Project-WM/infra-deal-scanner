GROUND CONTROL TECHNOLOGIES UK LTD
STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 30 NOVEMBER 2023
The directors present their Strategic Report for Ground Control Technologies UK Ltd (the ‘Company’) for the
year ended 30 November 2023.
Review of business
The Company is a leading Internet of Things (‘loT’) and Machine-to-Machine (‘M2M’) end to end platform _
provider. The Company provides SCADA, telemetry, monitoring and tracking platforms for loT and M2M
assets, generally in remote or challenging environments, and operates globally. The Company provides a
one-stop solution combining secure infrastructure, resilient software platforms and multiple network
connectivity. Working in partnership with multiple major satellite and cellular networks and key hardware
manufacturers, the Company provides managed service solutions tailored to the loT and M2M connectivity
needs of a range of customers from global blue chip organisations, government agencies and small
enterprises.
In August 2017 the Company was acquired by Horizon Capital LLP who planned to back the management
team to execute a buy and build growth strategy to significantly accelerate the growth of the business as part
of a group. This transaction provided the group with access to significant funding in order to deliver these
plans. In 2018 the group acquired Rock Seven Mobile Services Ltd and YB Tracking Ltd (together “Rock
Seven’) in the UK and in 2019 the group acquired Ground Control Systems (“Ground Control”) in the United
States of America. In August 2021 the group rebranded the business with a new look and feel under the
Ground Control name and relaunched the groundcontrol.com website.
During the year the Company's installed base of satellite and cellular !oT and M2M, and consequently revenue
from connectivity and support contracts, continued to grow. Connectivity and services revenue grew by 71%
(compared to 86% in 2022), representing 71% of total revenue. The key enabler of recurring revenue growth
is the sale of hardware solutions, hardware revenue grew by 81% (compared to a decline of 15% in 2022). In
November 2022, the operations of Rock Seven Mobile Services Ltd. (‘Rock Seven’), a company under
common control, were hived across to the Company. Accordingly, 2023 reflects twelve months of revenue
from the Rock Seven customer base as compared to one month for 2022.
Emerging from a period of chaltenging supply chain conditions, the Company continued to invest substantially
in securing a high-growth future. Significant progress was made developing and launching new products and
new product variants, substantially strengthening the sales‘and marketing operation, and establishing its own
IP networking capability. This will allow the Company to maximise the opportunity presented by new products
and achieve top tier partner status with key suppliers in the sector, further diversifying supply base risk. The
Company finished the year in a strong position and well placed to continue and accelerate growth.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks facing the Company and planned mitigation are:

+ Supplier relationships and supply chain - relationships with the leading satellite network operators are
important to the Company's on-going success. The Company is an important channel and route to
market for them and these co-dependent relationships are managed and protected via frequent and
open dialogue and contact. The global supply chain of communication technology products has been
disrupted by several events including the pandemic. The Company has recruited a supply chain
manager and invested in inventory planning systems to manage this risk;

« Technology - loT and M2M technology continues to develop at a fast pace. However, most of the
Company's assets are field based in remote locations, meaning the cost of technological refresh or
supplier change is often prohibitive as once installed, these applications tend to benefit from classic
loT/M2M stickiness. The Company continues to invest in platforms and infrastructure allowing
customers to manage the disparate pace of change in these technologies; and

* Dependency on key senior managers - as is typical for a Company of this size, the leadership of the
business relies on key senior managers. To mitigate this the Company has appropriate incentive
schemes and is developing processes to support learning and education and career development in
order to promote internal succession.

Strategy

The Board undertook major strategic review in 2021 which reflected the increasingly global nature of the
Company. The strategy was refined in 2023, increasing the focus on five growth pillars which will be delivered
over time through both organic growth and mergers and acquisitions (‘M&A) activity:

* Platform: expand and continuously develop the Company's leading Cloudloop® platform, supporting
an expanding range of core services, enabling value-added services layers, and creating sticky high-
value connectivity with partners across our complete range of products, solutions and market;

* New brand positioning: supporting the strategy of the integrated Company and its broad geographic
footprint and service offerings;

* Sales acceleration: growing sales capabilities, verticalising and focusing on key markets, and
partnering with systems integrators and other channel partners;

* Broader connectivity: repositioning the Company as connectivity agnostic, adding new connectivity
partner relationships and new technologies and increasing prominence with key satellite and cellular
network partners;

* Products: continuing to leverage the Company's flexible development capabilities, creating exclusive
hardware to drive connectivity and services, focusing on recurring revenue enhancers, looking
towards higher bandwidth loT/M2M applications, data hosting, management and analytics.

In the short term the focus of the business is to continue to deliver organic growth, with greatest emphasis on
solutions driving high recurring revenue and maximised customer lifetime value, whilst evaluating mergers
and M&A opportunities and completing the remaining operational consolidation. The longer-term strategy of
the Company remains to become a global category leader in loT and M2M solutions:

* Positioned as a connectivity-agnostic service delivery platform;

* Providing customers with market-leading solutions for high-availability, mission-critical applications in
remote or challenging environments; and :

* Challenging the service provider/solution landscape as a disrupter.

Objectives and key performance indicators (“KPIs”)
Management has adopted a number of indicators to measure and monitor the overall performance of the
Company. These include:

+ Active device connectivity subscriptions: Device count increased by 5% in the year ending 30
November 2023 (compared to 13% in 2022).

+ Recurring revenue: This grew by 71% overall in 2023 (compared to 86% in 2022). Recurring revenue
represented 71% of total revenue in 2023 (compared to 72% in 2022). In November 2022, the
operations of Rock Seven Mobile Services Ltd. (‘Rock Seven’), a company under common control,
were hived across to the Company. Accordingly, 2023 reflects twelve months of revenue from the
Rock Seven customer base as compared to one month for 2022.

* Gross margin: Gross margins increased to 37% in 2023 as compared to 36% in 2022;

A budget is set each year and monthly performance, including the KPI's above, are monitored against
historical trends and budgetary targets. The Company’s employee remuneration strategy is designed to align
employee remuneration with successful delivery against these and other non-financial KPIs.
As well as striving to outperform objectives and KPI targets, the Company continues to build on its reputation
as a leader in the satellite and cellular loT and M2M connectivity industry.
Business model
The Company's business model is focused on innovating to provide bespoke solutions to our customers and
partners, strong ongoing customer support and delivering profitable growth.
The Company benefits from high levels of recurring turnover providing confidence in future performance and
the ability to continue to invest in platform and solution development to further enhance customer service
levels.
Future outlook :
The future outlook for the industry and the Company remains positive as the Company holds an excellent
position in its respective markets and is expected to grow. Analysis of the loT and M2M markets by external
experts forecasts continued rapid market expansion. This is driven by growth in the number of end users, as
the use of communications technology and automation in consumer, industrial and governmental applications
continues to expand. This increases the need for both resilient private infrastructure and the platforms the
Company offers to enable customers to manage their growing estate of devices.
The Board has performed a rigorous assessment of the business’s forecasts and projections at a group level
and taken into account a range of possible trading performance outcomes. It has concluded that the group will
be able to operate within the level of its facilities and remain compliant with the associated covenants for a
period of at least 12 months from the date these financial statements were approved. In addition to the cash
on hand at 30 November 2023, the group has a £2.0m revolving credit facility committed until November 2024
and none of the group's senior debt or loan notes are repayable until November 2025. Furthermore, the
ultimate parent has expressed their willingness to provide the necessary financial support in an appropriate
form should the need arise. Accordingly, the Company continues to adopt the going concern basis in
preparing its financial statements.
