GROUP STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2024
Group strategic report
The directors present the group strategic report for Spektrix Limited and its subsidiary companies for the year
ended 31 December 2024.
Principal activities

The principal activities of the group comprise the provision of a comprehensive ticketing and CRM solution to
entertainment organisations, accompanied by specialist consultative support. Our software is delivered through a
multi-tenant, cloud-based platform, enabling frequent upgrades and feature releases across our entire client
base.
Business review
Key performance indicators
The directors were pleased with the performance of the business in 2024, as shown by the key metrics below:

2024 2023 Change
Recognised turnover (£’m) 20.6 17.7 16.4%
Number of clients live on Spektrix 713-601 18.6%
Spektrix average headcount 248 8221 12.2%
Growth and business developments
2024 was another year of strong turnover growth (+16.4% globally), reflecting both new client wins and increased
ticket sales from our existing customer base. The sector continues to show renewed appetite for system
investment as sales volumes recover and, in many cases, exceed pre-pandemic levels.
Three major developments contributed to this growth:
1. Payments revenue — In partnership with Adyen, we launched payment processing services that provide a new
and scalable income stream while delivering seamless value to clients.
2. Theatre Manager acquisition - The acquisition of Theatre Manager goodwill and client base accelerated our
North American growth. This expansion also led to the incorporation of our Canadian entity, strengthening our
presence and competitiveness in that market.
3. Product delivery — Our Engineering teams are now delivering a steady cadence of new features and fixes,
while also making significant long-term improvements to the platform. Clients are benefitting from both tactical
enhancements and the continued investment in system resilience and scalability.
We also maintained our ISO 27001 certification, underlining our ongoing commitment to information security and
best practice in data management. ,

Principal risks and uncertainties
Cybersecurity risk
We remain alert to the growing risks of cyberattacks and ransomware across the sector. An incident affecting
our systems or those of a key provider could significantly impact our clients and our business. We continue to
strengthen our security posture by investing in processes, awareness, and vulnerability management, and by
maintaining our ISO 27001 accreditation.
Economic environment
Persistent economic uncertainty, inflationary pressures, and high interest rates across the UK and North America
continue to affect our sector, employees, partners, and clients. While Spektrix is in a strong financial and
competitive position, we continue to monitor these conditions closely and assess their potential impact on long-
term client sustainability and our growth.
Future developments
Spektrix enters 2025 from a position of financial strength, with cash reserves supporting continued investment in
growth and product innovation. We expect to see:
* Further expansion in North America, driven by organic growth, our Canadian incorporation and Theatre
Manager client base.
* Strong uptake of our new payment processing services, adding resilience to our revenue mix.
+ Continued delivery of system improvements and new features, further embedding Spektrix as a strategic
partner to our clients.
The directors remain confident that Spektrix is well positioned to deliver sustainable growth while managing risks
responsibly.

2.10 Current and deferred taxation
The tax expense for the year comprises current and deferred tax. Tax is recognised in the

the Group operate and generate income.

. The recognition of deferred tax assets is limited to the extent that it is probable that they will be

. Any deferred tax balances are reversed if and when all conditions for retaining associated tax
allowances have been met; and
. Where they relate to timing differences in respect of interests in subsidiaries, associates,
branches and joint ventures and the Group can control the reversal of the timing differences
and such reversal is not considered probable in the foreseeable future.
Deferred tax balances are not recognised in respect of permanent differences except in respect of
business combinations, when deferred tax is recognised on the differences between the fair values of
assets acquired and the future tax deductions available for them and the differences between the fair

using tax rates and laws that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.
