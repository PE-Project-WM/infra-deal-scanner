Introduction :
Genius Sports Technologies Limited (the Company’) is a leading provider of key sports technology and
solutions to leagues and federations worldwide, enabling improved fan engagement and the digitisation of sport.
Business review
The Company's revenues grew to £35.7m for the year representing an increase of 22% against the previous
year (2021: £29.2m). The Company increased its revenue as it was able to recharge the higher data and
“streaming costs incurred during the year; and it achieved greater revenue from Value in Kind transactions.
During the year Genius Sports Technologies Limited invested in Genius Sports Canada Corporation as part of
the companies global growth plans.
During the year Genius Sports Technologies Limited recognised impairment of it's investments in wholly owned
subsidiaries, Oppia Performance BBVA amounting to £680k and Genius Sports ANZ amounting to £1.4m.
The Company's earnings before interest, taxation, depreciation, amortisation, onerous contract expense and
Impairment of investment (“Adjusted EBITDA") for the year was an Adjusted EBITDA of negative £1.2m (2021:

Principal risks and uncertainties
Accurate and timely risk identification and implementation of effective mitigation procedures is a vital component
of the Company’s overall business model. The Company has implemented a risk management framework which
assesses risk across function areas to effectively mitigate risks and monitor the progress of mitigation
procedures. Focus is placed on the key risks which could have a material impact on the realisation and delivery
of the business strategy. The Company considers utilising both financial and non-financial key performance
indicators to monitor and manage risks.
This section highlights the principal risks which are considered to potentially have an impact on the Company's
performance and ability to deliver its business strategy. It is not intended to be an extensive list of all risks which
may affect the Company, as additional risks and uncertainties presently unknown or deemed not material may
also adversely affect the business in future periods.
COVID-19 . .
COVID-19 has adversely affected the Company’s business, financial condition, results of operations and :
prospects, including as a result of the reduction in the quantity of global sporting events, closures or restrictions
on business operations of the Company's customers and sports organizations and a decrease in consumer
spending, and it may continue to do so in the future.

Interest rate and liquidity risk . ,
The risk that the Company has insufficient funds to finance normal operations or to respond to the crystallisation
of any risks is managed through regular financial forecasting and monitoring of financial performance alongside
operational KPi's on Company and Group level.
Credit risk
: The Company's policy is to provide for any trade or other debtor balance whose collection is deemed doubtful.
The Company is exposed to credit risk on its trade and other debtors to the extent they are not specifically
provided for as doubtful debts.
Foreign currency risk ,
Due to the international nature of the Company's business operations, the Company is exposed to the impact of
foreign currency and cash flow fluctuations.
Adverse foreign currency fluctuations could impact Company revenues due to a number of customer contracts
being denominated in Euros, US Dollars and Australian Dollars and the Company's significant cash assets which
are held in USD and GBP. The Company's exposure to foreign currency is partially hedged by the Company's
cost and payment structure and is further managed by its parent company at the Group level. The Company
therefore considers foreign currency risk to be well managed and that there is limited material exposure to
currency fluctuations.
: The Company monitors exposure to foreign exchange risk and has implemented appropriate measures to
mitigate associated risk where it is viewed that these are not naturally hedged within the Company.
Regulatory risk
There is a risk that the business model is impacted by future changes in regulations in the online sports betting
industry. The Company regularly reviews activity in this area through proactive monitoring of the regulatory
landscape and through discussions with key industry officials, professional advisors and trade associations, and
directly submits information to government consultations where requested to do so as part of the regulatory
review process in a country. The Group is a member of established trade associations operating in a number of
the Group’s operating regions.
A large number of the Group's current customers are online sportsbook operators who market and sell to their
customers on a worldwide basis. Jurisdictions continue to regulate the online gambling market through the
continuation or introduction of licensing regimes. The Company liaises regularly with its customers regarding
their active management of this risk.
Systems and infrastructure
: The Company relies on information technology and other services, systems, and platforms, including data
centers, Amazon Web Services and certain other third-party platforms. Failures, errors, defects, or disruptions
therein could diminish disrupt business, and adversely affect operating results and growth prospects. The
Companies product offerings and other software applications and systems, and certain third-party platforms
could contain undetected errors or errors that are not identified as material.
Risks of system outages, loss of connectivity and software errors are regularly reviewed, and continuity of
access to data and integrity of data are supplemented with a variety of other capabilities including, incident
. response, built in redundancy, back-up and restore procedures. The Company adopts a cloud first approach to
infrastructure and systems, and so are protected with built-in availability and resilience which is tested by third
party service providers.

Going concern

The Company is part of the Genius Sports Group (the ‘Group’) and given the function of the Company, going

concern has to be considered in the context of the Group as a whole. The directors of the ultimate parent

company (Genius Sports Limited) have assessed current market conditions and have prepared budgets and

cash flow forecasts for the Group for a period of at least 12 months from the date of signing these financial

statements. Based on this review the directors have concluded that the Group is able to meet its obligations as
they fall due. . ,

The Directors have received written confirmation that Genius Sports Limited will support the Company for at

least 12 months after these financial statements are approved. This letter also acts as support for the

recoverability of the Company's intercompany debtors. As a result the directors have, having made appropriate
enquiries, prepared the financial statements on the going concern basis.
