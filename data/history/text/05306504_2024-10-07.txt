, Principal risks and uncertainties
Senior management are aware of their responsibility for managing risks within their business units. Each
business unit head reports to the board on the status of these risks through management reports. Risk is
regularly reviewed at board level to ensure that risk management is being implemented and monitored
effectively. This includes monitoring the commercial and general risk, credit risk and liquidity risk of those
subsidiaries, which included consideration of the impact of the Russia-Ukraine war. We have identified that
liquidity is a risk, and this is mitigated by a group letter of support. We have considered and concluded there is
no material impact resulting from the Russia-Ukraine war on the principal risks and uncertainties. We have also
considered the impact of the general macro-economic conditions, such as rise in interest rates, inflation of
. ‘energy costs. Regarding interest rates, we have not had an impact as the Company hag no external financing
