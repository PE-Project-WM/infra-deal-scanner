Introduction
The directors present their Strategic Report for the year ended 31 December 2024.
Business review
STACK Infrastructure is a trusted digital infrastructure delivery specialist, providing powered land and data
centre facilities worldwide. STACK Infrastructure EMEA Limited is a management company, with the sole
purpose of supporting the growth and operations of the STACK EMEA region.

, During the year the Company performed favorably, reporting a profit of £1,534,044 before tax. The
Company continues to grow to support the significant opportunities within the EMEA region and anticipates
significant increases in headcount and, therefore, revenue in 2025.

Principal risks and uncertainties

The Company generates revenue by billing the companies who manage the day to day EMEA operations.

Consequently, the Company has a secure source of revenue and, as such, is not sensitive to market

fluctuations. The Company’s principal risk therefore relates to loss of key management. in the event of the

loss of key personnel, the Company would leverage the strength of the international STACK brand to

attract the best possible talent. ,

2.4 Turnover ,
Turnover is recognised to the extent that it is probable that the economic benefits will flow to

2.6 Research and development
- In the research phase of an internal project it is not possible to demonstrate that the project will
generate future economic benefits and hence all expenditure on research is recognised as an .
expense when it is incurred. Intangible assets are recognised from the development phase of a
project if and only if certain specific criteria are met in order to demonstrate the asset will
generate probable future economic benefits and that its cost can be reliably measured. The
capitalised development costs are subsequently amortised on a straight-line basis over their
useful economic lives, which is 5 years.
If it is not possible to distinguish between the research phase and the development phase of an
internal project, the expenditure is treated as if it were all incurred in the research phase only.
