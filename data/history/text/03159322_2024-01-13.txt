Strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2022
The directors present their strategic report on the company for the year ended 31 December 2022.
Principal activity and review of the business
The principal activity of the company continues to be the provision of litigation support services, which includes
computer forensics, hosting, electronic discovery, paper discovery and document review services.
The legal technologies business in the UK is coming under increased market pressure not only from traditional :
technologies companies but also from companies which in the past were involved in other consultancy activities
such as accounting and compliance and legal firms. The growth in technology which permits companies to be
able to collect and manage data internally is also affecting our UK business. We also continue to expand the
business by adding new products into our portfolio.
Turnover for the 12 months to 31 December 2022 is £18, 108,973 (2021: £20,848,238) this includes revenue from
our core business of document processing and hosting and also computer forensics. Gross margin for the year is
48% (2021:43%).

‘ The company’s strategy continues to be expansion into other international markets while maintaining our
position as one of the market leaders in the UK. We continue to develop new technologies, which enable us to
provide our service to our clients in a way that permits them to access the information wherever they are and
have the information available to them 24 hours a day. Building on the many strengths of our existing business
and strategy, we are focused on continuing to enhance our proprietary solutions, expand our ecosystem, and
extend our reach to capitalize on our large and growing market opportunity.

Key performance indicators (“KPIs”)
Turnover for the year to 31 December 2022, which is also considered as a KPI of the company, decreased 13.1%
compared to the same period to 31 December 2021.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Directors consider the following to be the key risks and uncertainties of the Company:
Financial instrument risk (Credit risk)
The company does not use derivatives to manage its financial risks. The most important components of financial
risk are interest rate risk, currency risk, credit risk, cash flow risk and price risk. Due to the nature of the
company’s business and the assets and liabilities contained within the company’s balance sheet, the only financial
risk the directors consider relevant to this company is credit risk. Credit risk is the risk that one party to a financial
instrument will cause a financial loss for that other party by failing to discharge an obligation. Most of our clients
are legal firms who depend on payment from their clients in order to be able to settle our invoices. Company
policies, such as turnover recognition and credit control policy, are aimed at minimising such losses.
The company recognises that the successful management of risk as part of our everyday activities is essential to
support the achievement of its strategic objectives.
Macroeconomic challenges risk
Management believes that global macroeconomic challenges, including inflation and the war in Ukraine, had a
significant adverse impact on the Company and the overall market in 2022. The company’s success in part
depends on the ability to anticipate and effectively manage the risks associated with the current macroeconomic
challenges, and there can be.no assurance that company will be able to do so without experiencing a reduction :
in revenue or incurring unexpected costs. In the long-term, we plan to increase our net revenue retention rate
by increasing the number of solutions that we sell on a subscription-basis, as well as broadening the scope of
our proprietary platform offerings, to promote strong product adoption. As the company expands our products
beyond eDiscovery to other information governance solutions such as big data hosting and processing, we
expect clients to leverage our technology earlier in the data lifecycle, providing further opportunity for us to
increase our product and service penetration and client retention. Furthermore, we plan to establish and broaden
our channel partnerships over time and leverage these strong relationships to further our awareness of our
products and overall usage within the industry.

Directors’ report for the year ended 31 December 2022
The directors present their report and the audited financial statements of the company for year ended 31 December

Insurance of Directors
Throughout the period, Directors' and Officers’ liability insurance has been maintained by the company.
Future developments
Our principal business is the provision of legal technologies to large corporates and legal firms. Our commitment
to providing exceptional and efficient client service through innovation and continual improvement remains an
important driver of organic growth. We are working on ways of providing our clients with our service which will
give them complete control of their data while enabling us to be able to quickly respond to their requests.
Results and dividends
The profit for the year after taxation amounted to £1 951,155 (2021: £1,550,277). The directors do not recommend
the payment of a dividend (2021: £nil).
Political Contributions
The Company made no disclosable political donations and did not incur any disclosable political expenditure
during the year.
Going concern
The directors of the company have reviewed the activities of the company and taken into consideration the current
trading conditions in the main markets of the company. Due to the evolving situation and current macroeconomic
uncertainties our business may be impacted in ways that we cannot predict. In the long-term, we plan to increase
our net revenue retention rate by increasing the number of solutions that we sell on a subscription-basis, as well
as broadening the scope of our proprietary platform offerings, to promote strong product adoption. As the
company expands our products beyond eDiscovery to other information governance solutions such as big data
hosting and processing, we expect clients to leverage our technology earlier in the data lifecycle, providing further
opportunity for us to increase our product and service penetration and client retention. Furthermore, we plan
to establish and broaden our channel partnerships over time and leverage these strong relationships to further our
awareness of our products and overall usage within the industry. The company is consolidating its position in
new markets and the directors believe that the company is well-placed to manage its business risks successfully
despite the current uncertain economic outlook.
The company has positive net current assets and positive shareholders’ funds at 31 December 2022. The directors
have considered the applicability of the going concern basis in the preparation of these financial statements. This
included a review of current financial results, as well as a preparation and review of Income Statement and
Balance Sheet forecasts with the projections of relevant cashflows for the period to 31 December 2024. The
ultimate parent company owns all the technology, software, and tradenames crucial for the Company's operations
and therefore the Company is reliant on parental support to continue in operational existence.

"The company has received a letter of support from its ultimate parent company, KLDiscovery, Inc. Taking into ~
account the support from KLDiscovery Inc., the directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has
adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the going concern period to 31 December 2024.
Notwithstanding the above, the directors are aware that KLDiscovery Inc., the company’s ultimate parent
company, has historically incurred losses and in certain years cash flows have been negative. As of September
30, 2023, the Parent Company’s cash balance was $25.4 million and the Parent Company’s debt balance was
$544.8 million, including a balance of $252.3 million under the Convertible Debentures and a balance of $292.5
million in Initial Term Loans under the Amended 2021 Credit Agreement (no amounts were outstanding under
the Delayed Draw Term Loans or Revolving Credit Loans). As of September 30, 2023, the Parent Company does
not anticipate repaying the Convertible Debentures by June 19, 2024 and as such, the Initial Term Loans debt of
$292.5 million is included in the current portion of long-term debt in the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet
at September 30, 2023. As of September 30, 2023, the Parent Company does not have sufficient cash on hand,
and does not expect to generate sufficient liquidity from forecasted future cash flows to repay its current
obligations including the Initial Term Loans, at the accelerated maturity date.
