- 1 -
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2026.
Review of the business
The directors are pleased to report that the group has had a record year achieving pre-tax profits of £
7,678,696
The group’s success is a direct result of our employees' hard work, the loyalty of our customers, and management’s overarching strategic vision. Our team is our most important asset, distinguished by an average tenure of 18 years for management and 5 years across all other departments. We pride ourselves on a culture that actively recognises and rewards employee contributions. 
We operate two well-established, highly respected brands in the industry. Our directors understand the value of this reputation and strive to deliver a superior experience across our breadth of services, which is reflected in a customer retention rate exceeding 95% for our B2B offerings.
Group Managing Director, Craig Smith is an esteemed industry veteran. Supported by years of executive experience, our leadership team stays ahead of the curve by adapting quickly to shifting market conditions.
At our core, our operations are driven by three key pillars, customer trust, compliance, and sustainability. Underpinning all these efforts is the group’s relentless pursuit of excellence.
Data and analytics are at the heart of our daily operations, allowing us to quickly identify performance trends and focus resources where they are needed most. By equipping our entire team with key metrics, we empower timely, informed decision-making. We have also heavily invested in advanced IT systems to deliver complete transparency, giving customers seamless access to their own information. Backed by strong shareholder support, we are well-positioned to accelerate our growth across the UK.
Mazuma Mobile
As a multi-award-winning leader in the UK device trade-in sector, Mazuma remains committed to industry excellence. This year marks our 20th anniversary, a major milestone that highlights two decades of innovation and trusted consumer service.
=== page 2 ===
Strong shareholder backing and reinvestment has enabled Mazuma to enhance its UK offerings and scale, paving the way for exciting growth in the new financial year.
A key contributing factor to Mazuma’s continued success and growth in a challenging market, is its continued investment in technology and innovation. Over the past year, these strategic insights have streamlined our customer journey and operational efficiency, while significantly boosting customer acquisition and retention. 
These insights have made a considerable impact, however, the changes made have only been possible with the effort and commitment of our staff. Culture and ethos are at the heart of our values, and the team have built an environment that promotes inclusion, communication, care, and growth. Staff retention is incredibly strong, which has enabled us to share knowledge and build consistency throughout. Staff are rewarded for their commitment with remuneration schemes for performance, progression, and service. Mazuma makes a commitment to continuous improvement on delivering our core values to its staff and customers. 
Further investment in our value-added services department has led to an increase in the number of refurbished devices available for resale. For those devices beyond repair, Mazuma has a strategic partnership that allows us to leverage technology that maximises the potential for parts harvesting on scale, creating a truly circular ecosystem, another of our core values.

ICT Reverse Asset Management 
ICT Reverse continues to operate successfully within the expanding IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) sector, with our primary objective remaining the delivery of a fully accredited, comprehensive service that provides customers with confidence and assurance. Data security remains at the core of our operations, and we are committed to maintaining robust, rigorous processes that align with industry regulations and best practice standards.
Our commitment to a zero-landfill tolerance policy remains a fundamental pillar of our business strategy. We firmly believe that responsible environmental stewardship is essential within the ITAD industry and complements our commitment to providing secure and compliant data destruction services. By combining data security with environmental responsibility, we continue to deliver sustainable outcomes for our customers and stakeholders.
During this financial year, ICT Reverse achieved two significant accreditations: CAS-S and NPSA. These credentials have already contributed to the acquisition of substantial new business opportunities, strengthening our market position and reinforcing customer trust. Alongside these achievements, we have invested in several customer-focused developments, including the launch of a new ICT Reverse website featuring refreshed branding and an enhanced customer portal. The new portal provides increased functionality and strengthens security for users, improving the overall customer experience. Building on these developments, we are now focused on delivering a new and streamlined ERP system to further enhance operational efficiency and scalability.
Our Corporate Sales team has also undergone significant growth, increasing by over 30%. This expansion has enabled us to dedicate a team exclusively to new business acquisition, while allowing our experienced Account Managers to focus on deepening relationships within their existing customer portfolios. By exploring new geographical territories and introducing additional services to established clients, we have successfully generated increased demand for both onsite shredding and onsite decommissioning services.
In line with our ongoing commitment to social value and community impact, ICT Reverse successfully achieved the Social Value Quality Mark Bronze Award and is now preparing for assessment at silver level. We continue to strengthen our partnership with The Big Goal, and during the year launched the Big Green Goal initiative. This collaborative programme has been developed to generate funding while helping organisations meet their Environmental, Social and Governance (ESG) objectives. Importantly, the initiative also supports efforts to address youth homelessness, enabling businesses to contribute to meaningful social change whilst delivering positive environmental outcomes.
Overall, this financial year has been characterised by strategic investment, operational development, commercial growth, and continued progress against our environmental and social commitments. These achievements position ICT Reverse strongly for future growth while ensuring we continue to deliver secure, sustainable, and customer-focused ITAD solutions.
Principal risks and uncertainties
Consumer trade-in continues to be a changeable and increasingly competitive landscape. The refurbished technology market is growing, as awareness and trust grows in refurbished devices. This is excellent news for the circular economy and our industry, the challenge comes in leveraging our knowledge, technology, and value-added services capabilities to keep up with the growing demand and avoid this demand being fulfilled by product from other territories. 
Technology continues to be a key focus to better understand our customers and the market conditions we’re navigating, as consumer behaviour is changing in both the buying and selling markets. 
The group works closely with our partners and compliance team to keep abreast of changes in legislation and identifying opportunities, to mitigate the ever-increasing costs as a result of the international conflicts.

Liquidity Risk

Foreign currency risk
The company’s principal foreign currency exposures arise from trading with overseas companies. Company seeks to invoice and be invoiced in its principal trading currency wherever possible so as to minimise its exposure to foreign currency movements. 
Credit risk

1.11
Impairment of fixed assets
At each reporting 
period 
end date, the 
group

Recognised impairment losses are reversed if, and only if, the reasons for the impairment loss have ceased to apply. Where an impairment loss subsequently reverses, the carrying amount of the asset (or cash-generating unit) is increased to the revised estimate of its recoverable amount, but so that the increased carrying amount does not exceed the carrying amount that would have been determined had no impairment loss been recognised for the asset (or cash-generating unit)
