Strategic Report for the Year Ended 31 December 2022
The directors present their strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2022.
Principal activities
EagleCrest Telecoms Limited (the “Company”) is incorporated and domiciled in England. The address of its registered office is
3rd Floor Queensherry House, 3 Old Burlington Street, London, United Kingdom, W1S 3AE.
The principal activity of the Company is as a holding company. It wholly owns IslaLink Holding S.L.U (“IslaLink”) and
fagleCrest Iris Limited (together “the Group”). IslaLink builds and operates neutral, independent infrastructure for the
development of the Digital Economy. [slaLink is a private company limited by shares and is incorporated and domiciled in
Spain. The address of its registered office is: Street Goya 59, Madrid.
On 2 February 2022 the company formed a 100% owned subsidiary, a private company limited by shares and incorporated and
domiciled in the UK. The address of its registered office is 3rd Floor Queensberry House, 3 Old Burlington Street, London,
United Kingdom, W1S 3AE. EagleCrest Iris Limited is a holding company and during the year acquired a total of 23.7% of the
share capital of Medianet Invest SA (“Medianet”), a private company limited by shares and incorporated and domiciled in
Greece. The address of it registered office is Pefkon 56, 14451 — Metamorfosi, Greece. Medianet provides full fibre internet
services in Greece.
Business review :
During the year the company issued a further 4 ordinary £1 shares at €15,773,737, and together with the receipt of additional
capital contributions of €4,463,500 from shareholders, used these funds for the investment in Medianet, The company made an
additional capital contribution in [slalink to help fund their investments.

, The directors consider revenue and operating profit to be the key performance indicators of the Group. Revenue for the Group
rose to €10,853,472 (2021: €10,090,930) and with an operating loss of €976,257 (2021: €1.919,616 profit) primarily as a result
of the transaction costs associated with the acquisition of Medianet. The Group’s balance sheet was in a net current assets position
at the year end — €5,215,313 (202/: €38,808 net current liabilities) from an increase in cash and trade receivables in excess of
the increase in trade payables.

The group has continucd its investment in new technical installations primarily in relation to the [onian project, a submarine and
terrestrial fibre optic network that will connect Athens and Thessaloniki with Milan and Rome increasing to €13,745,798
(2021:€4,336,845). Since the year end these assets have become operational.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal activity of the Group is communications services, the technical availability of the equipment that is used to deliver
services to clients is the key risk. If the business was to suffer an extended outage for a given period of time, this has the potential
to damage the performance of the Group. The submarine infrastructure is buried in certain areas to reduce this risk. Within the
provision of fibre to home internet service in Athens and Thessaloniki the key risk is slower than expected customer takc-up,
and this has been mitigated via an increase in marketing activities as well as introducing new tariffs and offerings.
The performance of the Group is linked to economic activity in the telecommunications sector and fluctuations in the economy
in general may affect the Company performance, as do inflationary and other pressures from the wider economy
During 2022 and at the date of preparation of these financial statements, no negative economic impacts on the Group’s activities
have been identified as a result of the Russian invasion of Ukraine. In addition, the Group has not committed any breach of its
contractual obligations nor is any such breach expected in the future, and therefore no consequences arising from contractual or
any other kind of non-compliance are foreseen.
