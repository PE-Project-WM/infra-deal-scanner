STRATEGIC REPORT 
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 30 NOVEMBER 2025
- 1 -
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 30 November 2025.
Review of the business
The Group’s core activity continues to be the provision of outsourced contact centre services, primarily handling voice communications on behalf of clients. During the period, the Group further advanced its strategic vision by integrating artificial intelligence (AI) voice solutions and Live Chat capabilities to complement its human-agent services, enhancing overall customer experience and operational efficiency.
During the year, the Group made significant investment in its AI and Live Chat technologies as part of its long-term strategy to broaden its service offering and position the business for future growth. This investment, combined with lower revenues currently associated with these emerging product lines as they scale, has had an impact on both revenue and margin for the year. The directors view this as a necessary and deliberate phase of investment, and remain confident that these capabilities will deliver meaningful returns as adoption increases and the products mature.
In addition, the Group has launched a Customer Experience (CX) consulting practice during the period. This new practice has been established to support our customers in realising improved performance across their contact centre and customer engagement operations. By combining the Group’s operational expertise with insight-led advisory services, the CX consulting practice is expected to deepen client relationships, diversify revenue streams, and reinforce the Group’s position as a strategic partner rather than solely a service provider.
Principal risks and uncertainties
Technology Risk
The Group is exposed to risks including power outages, internet disruptions, and reliance on key technology vendors. To mitigate these, the Group operates a hybrid workforce model with built-in redundancies, including independent power sources at critical sites, multiple internet providers, and failover systems. All systems are cloud-based, eliminating risks associated with on-premise infrastructure. A comprehensive disaster recovery and business continuity plan supports operational resilience.
Artificial Intelligence (AI) Risk
The growing capabilities of AI technologies present a threat to low-complexity human voice interactions, which may be replaced by automation. In response, the Group has developed and launched proprietary AI voice and Live Chat solutions that complement rather than replace human agents. Continued investment in this area, alongside the Group’s new CX consulting practice, will support consistent and reliable service delivery and enable the Group to guide clients through their own AI and customer experience transformations.
=== page 2 ===
Credit Risk
There is an inherent risk of delayed or non-payment from customers. The Group actively monitors credit exposure through regular review processes and maintains open lines of communication with clients to address potential issues early.
Liquidity Risk
The Group funds its operations through a mix of retained earnings and bank facilities. Regular cash flow forecasting and scenario analysis are conducted to ensure sufficient liquidity is maintained to support both current operations and future expansion, including the ongoing development of the Group’s AI, Live Chat and CX consulting capabilities.

Disabled persons
