The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Fair review of the business
The Group is an integrated provider of telecommunications, cloud communications and

execute the Group's long-term strategy of evolving the business into an AI-led unified

increasingly important to long-term competitiveness.
The defining strategic event of the year was the acquisition of Splicecom Limited in April 2025. The
acquisition strengthened the Group's control over its core communications technology platform,reducing reliance on third-party software and providing greater control over future product
development. The Directors believe this strategic investment establishes the technological foundation
necessary to support the Group's long-term vision of delivering a fully integrated, AI-enabled
communications platform.
Management's primary focus throughout the year was the successful integration of Splicecom into the
wider Group while maintaining continuity of service for customers. Integration activities progressed
throughout the year as the Group worked to align operations, technology and product strategy behind
a common long-term vision.
Trading conditions became increasingly challenging during 2025, particularly during the second half of
the year. The telecommunications sector continued to experience elevated customer churn, reflecting a
combination of business closures, increased competition and rapidly evolving customer expectations.
Customers increasingly expect modern communications platforms offering deeper integration,
automation and artificial intelligence capabilities, reinforcing the importance of continued investment in
product innovation.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The Directors regularly review the principal risks facing the Group and implement appropriate
mitigation strategies.
Market Conditions
The telecommunications sector continues to experience significant competitive pressure alongside
increased levels of customer churn and cautious business investment. These conditions continue to
influence customer purchasing decisions and place pressure on both customer acquisition and
retention.
The Group seeks to mitigate these risks through maintaining a diversified customer base, investing in
differentiated technology and continuing to strengthen long-term customer relationships.
Technology and Innovation
The unified communications market continues to evolve rapidly, with artificial intelligence, cloud
technologies and software integration becoming increasingly important competitive differentiators.
The Directors believe continued investment in proprietary technology and product innovation is
essential to maintaining the Group's long-term market position and responding effectively to changing
customer requirements.
Product Delivery
The Group's strategic objectives depend upon the successful delivery of its product roadmap.
Management continues to review development priorities to ensure investment remains closely aligned
with customer requirements and market demand.
=== page 3 ===
Integration
The successful integration of acquired businesses remains an important strategic objective. The
Directors continue to monitor integration activities to ensure the Group is well positioned to realise the
long-term strategic benefits of its acquisitions.
Liquidity
The Directors continue to monitor liquidity and working capital closely, maintaining disciplined financial
management whilst ensuring sufficient investment is made to support the Group's long-term strategic
objectives.
Key performance indicators
The Directors monitor a range of financial and operational measures including:
Revenue and Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)
Gross profit and gross profit margin
EBITDA
Operating cash generation
Customer retention and churn
Customer satisfaction
Employee engagement and retention
Delivery of strategic product development objectives
Research and Development
Research and development remains central to the Group's long-term strategy.
Following the acquisition of Splicecom Limited, investment has continued in the development of the
Group's proprietary communications platform with particular focus on cloud technologies, artificial
intelligence and enhanced integration capabilities.
The Directors believe ownership of the Group's core technology platform provides greater flexibility to
respond to changing customer requirements, accelerate innovation and deliver a differentiated unified
communications offering over the longer term.

- 3 -
Future developments
The Directors expect trading conditions within the telecommunications sector to remain challenging
during 2026 as businesses continue to face economic uncertainty and customer expectations continue
to evolve.
The Board's immediate priority is to continue the Group's strategic transformation whilst maintaining
disciplined financial management. Investment will remain focused on the continued development of
the Group's proprietary communications platform, particularly the delivery of AI-enabled functionality
and the next generation of unified communications capabilities.
The Directors believe that successful execution of this strategy will strengthen the Group's competitive
position and provide the technological foundation required to support sustainable long-term growth.
Financial instruments
The Group's principal financial instruments comprise cash balances, trade receivables, trade payables
and bank borrowings.
The Directors manage liquidity centrally through regular cash flow forecasting and ongoing monitoring
of working capital requirements. Credit risk is managed through established credit control procedures
and regular review of customer balances, whilst financing arrangements are monitored to ensure the
Group remains appropriately funded in support of its strategic objectives.
Going concern
The Directors' assessment of the Group's ability to continue as a going concern is set out within the
accounting policies to the financial statements.
Disabled employees
Applications for employment by disabled persons are always fully considered, bearing in mind the aptitudes of the applicant concerned. In the event of members of staff becoming disabled, every effort is made to ensure that their employment within the group continues and that the appropriate training is arranged. It is the policy of the group that the training, career development and promotion of disabled persons should, as far as possible, be identical to that of other employees.
Employee involvement
The Group recognises that its employees are fundamental to the successful delivery of its long-term
strategy. The Directors remain committed to supporting employee development, maintaining an
inclusive working environment and ensuring the business continues to attract and retain the skills
required to support future growth and innovation.

Carrying value of goodwill;
