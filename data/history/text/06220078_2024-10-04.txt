GROUP STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2023
Group Strategic report
The directors present the group strategic report for Spektrix Limited and its subsidiary companies for the year
ended 31 December 2023
Principal activities
The principal activities of the group comprise the provision of a comprehensive ticketing and CRM solution to
entertainment organisations accompanied by specialist consultative support. Our software is delivered through a
multi-tenant, cloud-based platform allowing for frequent upgrades and feature releases across our entire client
base.
Business review
Key performance indicators
The directors were pleased with the performance of the business in 2023, as shown by the key metrics below:
2023 2022 Change
Recognised revenue (£’m) 17.7 14.4 23%
Number of clients live on Spektrix 601 548 10%
Spektrix average headcount 221 173 28%
Significant product and delivery improvements to support growth
2023 was a year of significant revenue growth for Spektrix (+23% globally), with new business generating 15%
YoY growth, and existing clients’ increase in ticket sales responsible for the rest. We've seen the sector renew
its appetite for system changes as a result of ticket sales recovering or even exceeding pre pandemic levels.
Following the expansion of our Engineering team in 2022, we released some important new features to Spektrix
clients in 2023, for example with Spektrix Link, and accelerated the development of long term improvements to
our system. We also implemented an improved delivery model for our support and consultancy services, which
we believe makes us a better partner for our clients to help them achieve their goals. Finally, we were certified
ISO 27001 in 2023, which demonstrates to stakeholders and clients that Spektrix is committed to manage
information securely and to continually improve processes and policies in place to protect data.
Principal risks and uncertainties
Cybersecurity risk
We remain highly aware of the impact a cyber incident or ransomware attack on our system or one of our key
providers could have on our clients and our business. The growing sophistication of those attacks and our
increased dependency to electronic systems in a remote environment require us to continually review our risk
management strategy. As a result, we continue to invest in improving our processes and policies, in growing our
knowledge and awareness, and identifying and fixing vulnerabilities.
Economic uncertainty and inflation
Record high interest rates and inflation, alongside economic volatility, continued through 2023 and put significant
pressure on the UK and North American economies, and therefore on our sector, our employees, partners and
other stakeholders. We feel in a strong financial and competitive position to weather this uncertainty, but we are
keeping close attention to the impact this environment will have on our clients and our business in the long term.

2.2 Basis of consolidation
The consolidated financial statements present the results of the Company and its own subsidiaries

companies are therefore eliminated in full.
The consolidated financial statements incorporate the results of business combinations using the
purchase method. In the balance sheet, the acquiree's identifiable assets, liabilities and contingent
liabilities are initially recognised at their fair values at the acquisition date. The results of acquired
operations are included in the consolidated statement of comprehensive income from the date on
which control is obtained. They are deconsolidated from the date control ceases.

The financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis which the directors consider
