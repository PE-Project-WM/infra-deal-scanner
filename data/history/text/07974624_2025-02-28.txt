Introduction
Tela Technology Limited ("Tela") is one of the UK’s leading converged unified communications providers and is amongst a select few businesses that is and continues to successfully diversify into the managed technology services arena from the mobile centric space. It is well placed to capitalise on the wealth of up sell and cross sell IT, Security, Connectivity, Hosted Telephony, 5G, and IOT (Internet-of-Things) opportunities in future years.
Established in April 2012 (with a rich pedigree of over 35 years), Tela is one of 3 ‘Vodafone Total Communications Partners’ and one of its Top 3 ‘Strategic Partners’, having won the Communications Business Digital Transformational Solution award in 2017. Tela has also won the Unified Communications Partner of the Year 2013 award and has been twice in the Sunday Times Technology Track Fastest Growing UK 100 Businesses (61 in 2013 and 82 in 2012) having completed 14 acquisitions in the last nine years.
Tela has excellent partnerships with Ringcentral, Xelion, Samsung, Google, Gamma, ITS, Microsoft, and a whole host of other stakeholders. Furthermore, it has BSI accreditations in ISO 9001 and 27001 (Security) and supports businesses in their Cybersecurity accreditations. Tela is also regulated by the FCA (Financial Conduct Authority) and has the Safe Contractor accreditation vital in Health and Safety. 
Tela received the 2022 and 2023 Vodafone’s independent “Customer Value Management” award in recognition of the proactive work carried out with new and long-standing customers. This is following Tela’s customer obsessive and customer first approach recognised by Vodafone and achievement of an industry leading Customer NPS (Net Promoter Score) of 89. Tela also received a Vodafone award in 2022 and 2023 for its commitment to “Sustainability”, with its green, corporate responsibility, and ethical credentials in conjunction with transparent processes leading the way in the telecoms and IT industry. In 2024 Tela achieved the prestigious Vodafone & Ringcentral award of being the best partner in 2024 with a 250% growth from a standstill start. Tela also won Samsung Partner of the Year Award 2022 and in August 2023 won the Microsoft Infinigate Modern Workplace partner of the year against stiff competition from significantly larger Managed Service Partners (MSP’s).  This truly demonstrates the values by which Tela has successfully risen amongst its far larger peers to be a business respected by its competitors and revered by its customers.
Tela supports over 40 charities across the UK and continues to invest 5% of its EBITDA into organisations which provide essential services to its communities across the UK.
Tela transforms business performance by helping customers embrace current and emerging technologies. Through our ‘Communication Made Simple’ solution, Tela aims to change the way people live, by changing the way they work. Tela’s solutions and services empowers customers to be flexible by leveraging their investment in IT and infrastructure, giving them the freedom to work effectively and efficiently, both in and out of the office. We provide Mobility Consultation, IT Managed Software Solutions (e.g. Microsoft Office 365), Desktop Support, Cybersecurity, IOT and Telecom’s expertise across the UK on a 24 x 7 x 365-days basis. The business has offices in London, Birmingham, Worcester, Bristol, Warrington, Glasgow and Aberdeen.

Business review
The business continues to perform very strongly in a demanding and challenging environment. Annual turnover was at £21.7m (2023 - £21.1m) with annual gross profit of £9.0m (2023 - £9.1m) reflecting a gross profit margin of 42% (2023 - 43%), and EBITDA remained positive at £2.2m (2023 - £2.1m) with 100% EBITDA-to-Cash Conversion. Tela is committed to continual internal investment and organic growth and the director continues since incorporation to take no dividends and invest any-and-all cashflow into its people, infrastructure and geographic / capability-based acquisitions.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks for the Company going forward are perceived to be:
1) The Economic Climate
The Company has continued to demonstrate a strong and robust cash position year on year, with only Shareholder debt and long-term customer provisions recorded as liabilities in the Balance Sheet. The Company continues to write down Goodwill (non-cash item) from its historic acquisitions aggressively at circa £1m per annum which has contributed to the continued Shareholders deficit and loss before tax. However, the Company has strong processes and controls which will help to identify and mitigate any impact if there was to be any further economic downturn.
2) Lack of Diversification
The Company is continuing to invest in its Connectivity, Hosted Solutions VOIP business and IT division which showed strong growth, and this is expected to continue accelerating. From its onset in 2012 the business has transitioned from a 95% mobile mix to a 50% mobile and 50% other services mix. These new services have offset the competitive margins in mobile. IT in 2025 will represent £6m of Tela’s run-rate turnover of circa £25m.
3) Regulatory Changes
The Company actively monitors regulatory development on an on-going basis and where applicable will implement strategies and policies to address any commercial pressures or compliance requirements.
4) Embracing New Technologies
The speed of various advancements and customer expectations are regularly monitored by the Company. Tela is able to change and adapt to these influences and offer new products to its diverse customer set across the UK. We have pushed ahead with Microsoft Teams integration / collaboration, Co-Pilot and an Enterprise grade Hosted Telephony system powered by Ringcentral and Vodafone known as Vodafone Business Unified Solution (VBUC).
5) Commoditisation of Mobiles
The commoditisation of mobiles and the competing pressures of the three major mobile networks means diversification into IT and United Communications and increasing the business’ recurring revenue generation at a sustainable gross margin %. Long-term customer relationships with 3- year contracts is critical for 2025 and the next five years of its business plan. 
Financial Risk Management 
Interest Rate Risk
The Company’s exposure to market risk for changes in interest rates has been removed as debt obligations have been repaid.
Credit Risk
The Company trades with only recognised, creditworthy third parties. It is the Company’s policy that customers who wish to trade on credit terms are subject to credit vetting procedures.
Liquidity Risk
The Company’s objective is to maintain a balance between continuity of funding and flexibility through the use of a non-utilised bank overdraft and finance facility arrangements.
