- 1 -
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 30 September 2024.
Review of the business
Having delivered strong growth in both turnover and profit in 2023, the main objective for 2024 was to maintain growth in turnover and generate higher margins through purchasing efficiencies. This objective has been achieved with an increase in turnover by £1.74m to £23.01m (2023 £21
.
26m) and net profit by £1.3
3
m to £2.
16
m (2023 £
0.83m
).
Radiocoms continued to achieve material benefits from the long-term framework agreements it has in place which have delivered a material number of new short and medium-term orders. Radiocoms forecasts that these short and medium term contract opportunities will continue for the foreseeable future and deliver continued business growth. Radiocoms’ operating procedures remain robust to manage growth without a step increase in overheads; and human resource requirements have been anticipated and managed to provide strength in depth of appropriately skilled staff to support the actual and anticipated growth of the company..  
Principal risks and uncertainties
The global component shortage has eased, however there remain challenges with some core products taking 13-26 weeks to deliver. Radiocoms continues to manage product delivery delays and long lead times by placing large stock orders well in advance, which enables the company to supply customer demands in a shorter time than most competitors. It is anticipated that this stock policy will continue for the foreseeable future.
=== page 2 ===
The UK economy is forecast to achieve only moderate growth in the next 12-18 months and there are clear indications of government spending restrictions to meet published fiscal restraints. Such restrictions may result in government departments extending the life of its equipment in use and delaying implementing projects involving capital expenditure when not considered critical.
The 2024 budget under the new Labour administration increased taxes on business through the increase in employers’ National Insurance contributions which adds to the cost of employment; and there is a medium-term risk of further tax increases.
The directors have identified the existence of the above risks and are confident that they have strategies in place to manage and mitigate their effects. . 
Analysis of Key Performance
Radiocoms’ performance improved at all levels during 2024: a £1.74m (8.2%) increase in turnover, a 1.89m (143.0%) increase in operating profit and a £1.93m (188.0%) increase in profit before tax.  
Likewise, the balance sheet position has improved with net assets increasing to £5.27 million from £3.81 million.
Position at the year end
The directors are forecasting continued growth of both turnover and profits in 2025 and with the level of orders already placed are certain of achieving the budget for the first half of the financial year and have a high degree of confidence that the budget for the whole year will be exceeded.  
Radiocoms has made consistent progress with de-gearing the business and as at September 2024 is debt free, enabling material levels of free cash to be up-streamed to its holding company where it is retained.

1.11
Equity instruments
Equity instruments issued by the company are recorded at the proceeds received, net of transaction costs. Dividends payable on equity instruments are recognised as liabilities once they are no longer at the discretion of the company.
1.12
Taxation
The tax expense represents the sum of the tax currently payable and deferred tax.
Current tax
The tax currently payable is based on taxable profit for the year. Taxable profit differs from net profit as reported in the 
profit and loss account
 because it excludes items of income or expense that are taxable or deductible in other years and it further excludes items that are never taxable or deductible. The 
company’s 
liability for current tax is calculated using tax rates that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the reporting end date.
Deferred tax
Deferred tax liabilities are generally recognised for all timing differences and deferred tax assets are recognised to the extent that it is probable that they will be recovered against the reversal of deferred tax liabilities or other future taxable profits. Such assets and liabilities are not recognised if the timing difference arises from goodwill or from the initial recognition of other assets and liabilities in a transaction that affects neither the tax profit nor the accounting profit.
The carrying amount of deferred tax assets is reviewed at each reporting end date and reduced to the extent that it is no longer probable that sufficient taxable profits will be available to allow all or part of the asset to be recovered. Deferred tax is calculated at the tax rates that are expected to apply in the period when the liability is settled or the asset is realised. Deferred tax is charged or credited in the 
profit and loss account
, except when it relates to items charged or credited directly to equity, in which case the deferred tax is also dealt with in equity. Deferred tax assets and liabilities are offset when the 
company 
has a legally enforceable right to offset current tax assets and liabilities and the deferred tax assets and liabilities relate to taxes levied by the same tax authority.
