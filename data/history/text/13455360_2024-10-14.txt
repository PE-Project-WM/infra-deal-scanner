The Directors present their strategic report on the Group for the 18-month period ended 31 December 2022.
Principal activities
During the financial period the principal activities of the Group were the provision of advanced solutions and services for
electricity, water and gas utilities. .
Review of operations
CrescoNet Group is an advanced utility solutions and services company, enabling utilities to deliver on the promise of a more
sustainable future through a unique offering of next-generation smart metering, Distributed Energy Resources (‘DER’)
registration and management, and many other integrated digital technologies.
The Group operates in competitive markets that are continuing to look for sustainable smart energy and water solutions. Our
business strategy relies upon the following key elements: .
e Innovating and investing in optimizing and modernizing the core smart metering processes and applications in liberalised
and unbundled utility markets through our mdeX product portfolio;
e Innovating and investing in DER registration and flexibility management services through our deX product portfolio; and
e Innovating and investing in design of intelligent measurement, communication and control end points.
During the period, the Group continued to grow in number of services and meters under management in Australia and New ,
Zealand and has expanded its operations with three acquisitions.
In June 2021, the Group acquired Smart Earth Technologies, LLC ('SET'), a US-based provider of environmentally sustainable
utility management solutions for water utilities. .
In November 2021, the Group acquired Aetheros Inc., a US-based provider of loT technology powering the next wave of smart
meter deployments with distributed intelligence. :
In January 2022, the Group acquired GreenSync Pty Ltd ('GreenSync’), an Australian-based technology company that
’ specialises in DER registration and management solutions for the energy market in Net Zero transition.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The following is a summary of material business risks that could adversely affect our financial performance and growth
potential in future years and how we propose to mitigate such risks.
Competitive risks ,
The Group operates in a competitive market. Innovation is constant and superior products that may be released to the market
places pricing pressures on our product and causes our product to be repositioned by the market. We manage this risk through
maintaining product development teams that are highly experienced and remain abreast of latest technological advances and
implications for our current and future products.
Economic and political climate
The current economic and political climate the Group operates within has a number of uncertainties and external market forces
in play. The Group's expansion, however, and investments align with one of the most important global energy transitions, a
transition to Net Zero. Government regulation and decisions relating to Net Zero transition may have an impact on the Group.
The management monitors changes through regular. engagement with regulators and other relevant governmental bodies in
the markets the Group operates in. Our team has detailed knowledge of the market requirements and participates in working
groups and committees to help shape the future.
Strategies and expansion plans : .
The Group's strategies and expansion plans may result in unforeseen costs or require significant management attention. With
extensive experience and partnerships worldwide, the Group is well-positioned to continue driving innovation and integrated
solutions for utilities, enabling economic growth and fostering a smarter, safer and more sustainable future.
Compliance risks
The Group's operations are covered by best practices as well as government or industry regulations. The Group has committed
resources, which support maintaining compliance currently and in the future. Policies and procedures are in place, mandatory
employee training is carried out and formal management meetings are held to discuss risk and compliance.

Environmental risks .
Despite its asset light strategy, the Group is subject to environmental regulation in respect of its technological activities. The
relevant authorities are kept updated and, to the best of the Directors' knowledge and belief, all responsibilities under the
regulations have been discharged and there have been no breaches of any environmental regulation.
Data security and data privacy
The Group is subject to data and privacy regulations, particularly General Data Protection Regulation ('GDPR') and Privacy
Acts. Failure to comply with legal or regulatory requirements relating to data security or data privacy in the course of
the Group's business activities can result in reputational damage, fines or other adverse consequences, including criminal
penalties and consequential litigation, adverse impact on the Group's financial results or unfavourable effects on the Group's
ability to do business.
Exposure fo price, credit, liquidity and cash flow risk
With long term agreements in place for the majority of the Group’s revenues, the Group is not exposed to significant price risk.
With the majority of Group revenues coming from one major customer, Intellihub, the Group is exposed to concentration and
credit risk on the existing Intellinub business, if this counterparty would default on its contractual obligations, it would impact the
Group. This is a long-term contract with high dependency of the counterparty on the Group delivering its services hence the
risk is low. .
People ,
The Group's ultimate success would not be possible without its high calibre of people. The Directors and staff continue to be
focused, loyal and dedicated to providing high levels of customer service.
Equal opportunity :
The Group is committed to an active equal opportunities policy. It is the Group's policy to promote an environment free from
. discrimination, harassment and victimisation, where everyone receives equal treatment regardless of gender, colour, ethnic
or national origin, disability, age, marital status, sexual orientation or religion. Employment practices are applied which are fair,
equitable and consistent with the skills and abilities of the employees and the needs of the Group.
Employee consultation
The Group places considerable vaiue on the involvement of its employees and has a practice of keeping them informed on
matters affecting them as employees and on the various factors affecting the performance of the Group, which is achieved
through formal and informal meetings.
Likely future developments :
During 2022 it became evident that the quality of SET manufacturing and design of products was not on the level demonstrated
to the Group in the due diligence. Despite all of the best efforts to manage the warranty cases, the warranty exposure and
liabilities inherited through the acquisition led to SET entering into a financial restructuring that will culminate in an orderly
liquidation and wind-down of the entity.
Despite this, the Group continues with innovation in technological solutions, organically and/or through further acquisitions,
growing its customer base and global expansion. :

to the carrying amounts of assets and liabilities (refer to the respective notes) within the next financial year are discussed
below.
Provision for impairment of inventories ,
The provision for impairment of inventories assessment requires a degree of estimation and judgement. The level of the
provision is assessed by taking into account the recent sales experience, the ageing of inventories and other factors that affect
inventory obsolescence.
Goodwill and other indefinite life intangible assets
The Group tests annually, or more frequently if events or changes in circumstances indicate impairment, whether goodwill and
other indefinite life intangible assets have suffered any impairment, in accordance with the accounting policy stated in note 2.
The recoverable amounts of cash-generating units have been determined based on value-in-use calculations. These
calculations require the use of assumptions, including estimated discount rates based on the current cost of capital and growth
rates of the estimated future cash flows.
Income tax
The Group is subject to income taxes in the jurisdictions in which it operates. Significant judgement is required in
determining the provision for income tax. There are many transactions and calculations undertaken during the
ordinary course of business for which the ultimate tax determination is uncertain. The Group recognises liabilities for
anticipated tax audit issues based on the Group's current understanding of the tax law. Where the final tax outcome
of these matters is different from the carrying amounts, such differences will impact the current and deferred tax
provisions in the period in which such determination is made.
Business combinations
As discussed in note 2, business combinations are initially accounted for on a provisional basis. The fair value of assets
acquired, liabilities and contingent liabilities assumed are initially estimated by the Group taking into consideration all available
information at the reporting date. Fair value adjustments on the finalisation of the business combination accounting is
retrospective, where applicable, to the period the combination occurred and may have an impact on the assets and liabilities,
depreciation and amortisation reported.
