BUSINESS REVIEW
The Company is wholly owned by Airspan Networks Inc. (“Parent”), registered in Delaware, USA, and
ultimately owned by Airspan Networks Holdings Inc. (“Airspan “TopCo””), Airspan TopCo and all
subsidiaries are referred to as the “Airspan Group”. The Company is a designer and producer of wireless
network equipment for 4G and 5G networks for both mainstream public telecommunications service
providers and private network implementations. The Company provides Radio Access Network (“RAN”)
products based on Open Virtualized Cloud Native Architectures that support technologies including 5G new
radio (“SG NR”), Long-Term Evolution (“LTE”), and Fixed Wireless standards, operating in licensed,
lightly-licensed and unlicensed frequencies.
The market for Airspan Group’s wireless systems includes mobile carriers, other public network operators
and private and government network operators for command and control in industrial and public safety
applications such as smart utilities, defence, transportation, mining and oil and gas. Its strategy applies the
same network technology across all addressable sectors.
Airspan Group’s main operations are in Slough, United Kingdom; Mumbai and Bangalore, India; Airport
City, Israel; Tokyo, Japan; and the Company’s corporate headquarters are in the United States (“U.S.”) in
Boca Raton, Florida.
The Company has historically incurred losses from operations that have been financed by Airspan Networks
Inc., the parent company, by intercompany loans or equity share purchases.
FINANCIAL OVERVIEW
The Company reports its financial statements and the notes to the accounts in United States dollars (“S$”).
The Company’s operating loss after tax for the year ended 31 December 2023 was $20.6 million, (2022:
$18.6 million loss). Turnover at $4.1 million in 2023 decreased 80% from $21.2 million in 2022, with gross
margins at -23% and 67% for 2023 and 2022, respectively.
GOING CONCERN
The Company incurred a loss for the year of $20.6 million, and had total liabilities at 31 December 2023 of
$308.6 million, of which $304.0 million is due to the parent company. The Company relies on its parent
company to provide financing for its operations.
In calendar year 2023, the Airspan Group continued to invest in 4G and 5G development activities and
customer engagements. These significant investments, combined with the impact of the continuing global
economic conditions in 2023, had a significant impact on available resources and credit facilities. The
Airspan Group was not in compliance with certain covenants under its credit facility agreements dated 29
November 2022, which is an event of default under those agreements for which a waiver was obtained.
The Airspan Group amended its credit facility agreements in November 2023 which brought the Airspan
Group into compliance with certain covenants.
The ultimate parent company Airspan Networks Holdings Inc. and three United States subsidiaries filed for
bankruptcy protection under Chapter 11 in Delaware, on 31 March 2024 under a prepackaged plan of
reorganization. Airspan Networks Holdings Inc. emerged from Chapter 11 as Airspan Networks Holdings
LLC on 11 October 2024.
Airspan Networks Holdings LLC is majority owned by affiliates of Fortress Investment Group and all
previously incurred third party indebtedness has been eliminated. Fortress Investment Group provided
additional equity to the Airspan Group and the Airspan Group has access to a line of working capital which
the directors of the Company expect will allow the parent company to continue to provide the necessary
financial support to the Company to meet its obligations as they fall due for the foreseeable future.
Management has reduced costs, primarily through headcount reductions, has eliminated burdensome
interest expense and other non-strategic costs. Management have prepared forecasts, based on reasonably
achievable assumptions, that indicate that available resources will be sufficient for the ongoing needs of the
Airspan Group, including the Company.

PRINCIPAL RISKS AND UNCERTAINTIES FACING THE COMPANY
Technology Risk
The Company relies on technology for day-to-day operations that has potential system vulnerabilities,
failure due to hacking, phishing and ransomware and potential data loss. All modern companies operate
with this risk and the Company addresses this risk with a robust attention to system vulnerabilities and
education of users. The Company has strict controls in place and regular communications to staff members
to remain cautious and vigilant to the threats.
Supply Chain Risk
The Company is reliant on key suppliers for components that are from a single source. An interruption of
supply from these key suppliers could disrupt or halt production for products that are designed using these
key components.
Key Staff Risk
The Company is reliant on the wealth of expertise of its staff, and in some cases particular staff members,
to research, develop and improve the product line. Losing these key employees could risk the development
of the products and cutting-edge technologies. .
Funding Risk
The Company is reliant and funded through its parent company, which has provided the financial support
for the Company to grow to where it is now. The directors believe there is a reasonable expectation that the
Company will continue to be supported and meet its strategic targets.

Basis for opinion
We conducted our audit in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (UK) (ISAs (UK)’)
and applicable law. Our responsibilities under those standards are further described in the
‘Responsibilities of the auditor for the audit of the financial statements’ section of our report. We are

Material uncertainty related to going concern
In forming our opinion, which is not modified, we draw attention to disclosures made in the Strategic
report and Note 1 in the financial statements concerning the Company’s ability to continue as a going
concern. The Company is in a net liability of $83.0 million as at 31 December 2023 and incurred a
total comprehensive loss of $20.6 million for the year then ended. These events and conditions, along
with other matters as set forth in Note 1, indicate that a material uncertainty exists that may cast
significant doubt on the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern.
The financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis which assumes that the
Company will continue in operational existence for the foreseeable future. The validity of this
assumption as stated in Note 1 depends on the outcome of the actions to be taken by management
to mitigate the on-going losses by reducing non-strategic costs, effective intra-group loan
restructuring strategies as well as obtaining additional working capital. The financial statements do
not include any adjustments relating to the recoverability and classification of recorded asset amounts
and classifications of liabilities that might be necessary should the Company be unable to continue in
existence.
In auditing the financial statements, we have concluded that the directors’ use of going concern basis
of accounting in the preparation of the financial statements is appropriate. Our responsibilities and
the responsibilities of the directors with respect to going concern are described in the relevant sections

Foreign currency transactions are translated into the functional currency using the average rates in effect at
