Strategic report
Introduction
The Company was incorporated on 17 November 2023.
The directors present their Strategic Report for Toob Topco Limited (“the Company”) and its subsidiaries (together,
“the group”) for the period ended 31 December 2024.
On 17 January 2024 the Company acquired the full share capital of Toob Limited and has merged this subsidiary
undertaking and treated as if they had always been a member of the group.
The Group has successfully invested in rolling out a full fibre broadband to c. 280,000 premises. in the south of
England.
2024 has been a year that has seen the Group complete the main phase of its build in its chosen geographies and
transition its build activities to a consolidated in-house capability allowing the Group to address opportunities to infill
network gaps as they arise and maintain its existing network. The Group is expanding its full fibre broadband services
under its ISP agreement with CityFibre to additional towns in new geographies served by the growing CityFibre
network.
The Group has continued to execute strongly the commercialisation of the network investment it has made and has
delivered significant growth in customer connections both on its own network and that of its wholesale partner
CityFibre.
The network and customer expansion is funded by the capital that the Group secured from funds managed by Ares
Management’s Infrastructure Debt Strategy and further equity from Funds managed by the Amber Infrastructure
Group.
During 2025 the Group will leverage the network investment made to date to opportunistically build out areas of infill
to complement its current network, taking the build to over 300,000 premises. Commercialisation of the Group’s
network remains a priority as the Group drives towards being EBITDA positive, alongside which it will market its
services to customers able to connect to the CityFibre network in the areas that the Group chooses to address.
Principal Activities :
The Company’s principal activity is that of a holding Company. The Group’s principal activity designs, builds, owns,
sells and operates its own full fibre network and resells a third-party full fibre network for the benefit of consumers
and as appropriate, small businesses.
The Company was incorporated on 17 November 2023. It acquired Toob Limited on 17 January 2024 through a share
for share exchange. The incorporation of the Company represented a group reconstruction and has been accounted for
using merger accounting.

Business Review
The Group has a clear strategy and business plan to scale the Group as a full fibre network provider.
It has a very experienced and seasoned management team who have diligently managed the deployment of the Group’s
network in its chosen geographies and who continue to successfully commercialise the network investment by
acquiring and retaining connected customers.
The Group is supported by a mixture of equity and debt of up to £395M to deliver their plan to create a sustainable
business focusing on the south of England and to take advantage of market consolidation if opportunities arise.
2024 has seen the Group densify and extend its fibre deployment in towns in the south of England and extend the
successful customer acquisition metrics from its earliest cohorts across its wider footprint. The Group has leveraged
the success of its brand in the region into its ISP agreement with CityFibre, adding incremental customers to its base
to deliver additional business synergies and incremental margin and is also seeking to extend its reach to areas outside
of the immediate region.
The financial statements reflect the final phase of deployment of significant capital into the network, as the major
build phase comes to an end, together with the continued investment in sales, marketing, and customer service
capability to support the growing customer base. These cash outflows are offset by revenues from the growth in
connected customers.
The Group is focused on achieving cash breakeven under its existing committed investment and will, seek to release
further investment, as required, to deliver its strategy. ,

The directors identify, assess and manage principal risks and uncertainties that might directly affect the Group’s
strategy and operations. Risks generally arise from either external factors driven by the market in which the Group
operates or from intemal factors driven by the operational aspects of the business.

Changes in government policy can adversely affect the Group. The Group works with industry groups such as ISPA

Digital Disruption and Emerging Technology
Generative AI is a specific subfield of AI that focuses on creating new content and therefore represents both an
opportunity and a risk for the Group. AI processes large amounts of data which gives rise to both privacy and cyber
security issues where AI has the potential to drive network attacks that exploit possible vulnerabilities.
Deepfake content and algorithms have the potential to create bias that influences opinion and decision making.
Generative AI tools have the potential to pose ethical, legal, and existential conflicts. The application of generative
Al tools in the workplace is therefore being evaluated by the government and the NCSC to assess its longer-term
impact.
The Group has a Use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) policy advising employees on acceptable and prohibited uses of
AL
Data and Cyber Security
Toob Topco Limited as an infrastructure provider, is exposed to the ever-increasing risk of cyber-attacks. The Group
is continually enhancing its physical defences through application of latest patch updates, regular reviews and testing
of its perimeter defences through penetration testing supported by continuous network monitoring.
The Group maintains its Cyber Essentials and Cyber Essentials Plus qualifications together with renewal of its
18022301 and ISO27001 accreditations.
The Group’s physical defences are supported by logical defences vested in robust business processes and training to
ensure employees understand and can respond to business continuity, data protection and security breaches and
phishing attacks.
Competition
The Group competes for customers with other ISPs who either own or resell the wholesale networks of BT Openreach,
Virgin Media and CityFibre or compete as a standalone AltNet. The Group acquires customers from competitors who
either do not have access to a full fibre product or are unable to provide a suitably differentiated full fibre proposition
that is able to compete with the Group proposition. The Toob network has very limited overbuild with other AltNets
which significantly reduces any competitive tensions in those geographies. The Group continually reviews its products
and services to ensure its offer meets the needs of its customer and remains attractive to customers considering an
alternative to their current provider.

Operations
Toob Limited maintains a practice of dual sourcing for its key materials and equipment and ensures that minimum
inventory levels can mitigate potential supply chain risks. Service interruptions are a risk to brand and reputation so
the Company architects its network to be resilient at appropriate levels of consolidation in its core network and its
Points of Presence and responds promptly to minimise the effect of outages on its customers.
Outsourced Construction
Toob Limited awards NEC (New Engineering Contract) contracts to principal construction partners to build its full
fibre network and relies on their industry expertise and capability to deliver in line with agreed contractual terms and
within the UK legal and regulatory framework. The Company has a robust supplier selection process and awards long-
term contracts to multiple principal contractor partners to reduce the exposure to any one contractor. Contracts are
designed to ensure the Company only pays for what has been built to the required network standards and has a process
of ongoing review and monitoring that takes place throughout the contract cycle. Contracts have a built-in mechanism
for finance retentions to ensure completion to the Company standard and delivery timeline.
Climate
Toob Limited recognises and actively manages the risks that climate change presents to the Company, including the
increased likelihood of extreme weather events such as floods, storms, and heatwaves.
The Company conducts environmental risk assessments during the construction planning phase for its infrastructure
placements to ensure resilience against possible future weather-related disruptions.
Future Developments
The directors see no material change in the nature of the Company’s activities but expect to enter wholesale
agreements that will allow third party channels to resell the Toob Limited network under various retail brands.
Financial Risk Management
The company is exposed to a variety of financial risks; credit risk, liquidity risk, interest rate risk. The company adopts
a risk management approach to manage these risks.
Liquidity
Cashflow is managed by ensuring there are sufficient funds available to support continuing operations and plans for
future growth. Toob Limited maintains a detailed rolling cashflow forecast to ensure there are adequate funds available
to meet commitments as they fall due. The Company has put in place appropriate equity and debt facilities that
underpin its funding requirements.
interest
Recent funding rounds have increased the leverage of Toob Limited which exposes the Company to greater cost risk
from any increases in interest rates. The Company has put in place an interest rate cap to mitigate the exposure.
Credit risk
Exposure to credit risk is monitored on an ongoing basis. As most customers pay in advance on a monthly basis the
Company has the ability to withdraw services should a customer become overdue.

Going Concern
Notwithstanding the Group net liabilities of £150,883 as at 31 December 2024, a loss for the year then ended of
£52,825k and operating cash outflows for the year of £7,362k, the financial statements have been prepared on a going
concern basis which the directors consider to be appropriate for the following reasons.
As at 3) December 2024 the business had £4,996k cash available and as at 31 March 2025 £1,387k.
The directors have prepared cash flow forecasts for a period of 12 months from the date of approval of these financial
statements (‘base case model’) which indicate that, taking account of reasonably possible downsides, the Group and
the Company will have sufficient funds, through the availability of debt funding from funds managed by Ares
Management’s Infrastructure Debt Strategy entered into on 16 May 2023 as disclosed in note 14 that sets out the
repayment terms on the loan and further equity from Funds managed by the Amber Infrastructure Group to supplement
its initial investment.
The debt facility is subject to five covenant requirements; total net debt to equity ratio, net debt per premise passed,
net debt per premise connected, total premises passed and average revenue per customer. Cashflow forecasts under
the base case model produce debt and revenues to satisfy the covenants for at least the next 12 months.
Furthermore, the directors have carefully reviewed the prospects of the Group and its future cash flows for potential
adverse economic impacts. The directors have prepared a severe but plausible downside model (‘downside model’)
using the following assumptions: reduced customer take up versus base case, and network infrastructure build
discontinued leaving maintenance of services for existing customer base only.
This downside model forecasts that the cash available to the business from its debt facility will also satisfy the covenant
requirements for at least the next 12 months.
In modelling the above, the Group has not included additional cost saving measures that are in the control of the
directors which could be taken to further reduce costs or cash outflows such as reduction in capital expenditure and
payroll costs.
Consequently, the directors are confident that the Group and the Company will have sufficient funds to continue to
meet its liabilities as they fall due for at least 12 months from the date of approval of the financial statements and
therefore have prepared the financial statements on a going concern basis. Accordingly, these financial statements do
not include any adjustments to the carrying amount or classification of assets and liabilities that would result if the
Group and the Company was unable to continue as a going concern.

1.12 Taxation :
The tax expense represents the sum of the tax currently payable and any deferred tax.
The current tax charge is based on the taxable profit for the year. Taxable profit differs from net profit as reported in
the Statement of Financial Position because it excludes items of income or expense that are taxable or deductible in
other years and it further excludes items that are never taxable or deductible. The Group’s liability for current tax is
calculated using tax rates that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the year end date.
Deferred tax is the tax expected to be payable or recoverable on differences between the carrying amounts of assets
and liabilities in the financial statements and the corresponding tax bases used in the computation of taxable profit
and is accounted for using the liability method. Deferred tax liabilities are generally recognised for all taxable
temporary differences and deferred tax assets are recognised to the extent that it is probable that taxable profits will
be available against which deductible temporary differences can be utilised. Such assets and liabilities are not
recognised if the temporary difference arises from goodwill or from the initial recognition (other than in a business
combination) of other assets and liabilities in a transaction that affects neither the taxable profit nor the accounting
profit.
Deferred tax liabilities are recognised for taxable temporary differences arising on investments in subsidiaries, except
where the Group is able to control the reversal of the temporary difference and it is probable that the temporary
difference will not reverse in the foreseeable future.
Deferred tax is calculated at the tax rates that are expected to apply in the period when the liability is settled, or the
asset realised. Deferred tax is charged or credited to the Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income,
except when it relates to items charged or credited directly to other comprehensive income, in which case the deferred
tax is also dealt with in other comprehensive income.
Deferred tax assets and liabilities are offset when there is a legally enforceable right to set off current tax assets against
current tax liabilities and when they relate to income taxes levied by the same taxation authority and the Group intends
to settle on a net basis.

immediately in the income statement. The carrying value of the hedged item is adjusted by the change in fair value
that is attributable to the risk being hedged (even if it is normally carried at cost or amortised cost) and any gains or
losses on remeasurement are recognised immediately in the income statement (even if those gains would normally be
