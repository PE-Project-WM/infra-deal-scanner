Interest rate risk
Interest rate risk arises due to the fluctuations in interest rates, which the company is exposed to through its
borrowing and lending. This is mitigated through using fixed interest rates.
Liquidity risk . ;
Liquidity risk represents the risk that the Company will not be able to meet its financial obligations as they fall
due. The Company's approach to managing this risk is to ensure, as far as possible, that it will always have
sufficient liquidity to meet its liabilities when due, under both normal and stressed conditions, without incurring
unacceptable losses or risking damage to the Company's reputation. Should the Company ever have difficulty
maintaining liquidity, it witli be supported by its ultimate parent entity.
Credit risk
The Company's policy is to provide for any trade or other debtor balance whose collection is deemed doubtful.
The Company is exposed to credit risk on its trade and other debtors to the extent they are not specifically
provided for as doubtful debts.
Foreign currency risk °
Due to the international nature of the Company's business operations, the Company is exposed to the impact of
foreign currency and cash flow fluctuations.
Adverse foreign currency fluctuations could impact Company revenues due to a number of customer contracts
being denominated in Euros, US Dollars and Australian Dollars and the Company's significant cash assets which
are held in USD and GBP. The Company's exposure to foreign currency is partially hedged by the Company's
cost and payment structure and is further managed by its parent company at the Group level.. The Company
therefore considers foreign currency risk to be well managed and that there is limited material exposure to
currency fluctuations. :
The Company monitors exposure to foreign exchange risk and has implemented appropriate measures to
mitigate associated risk where it is viewed that these are not naturally hedged within the Company.
Non-financial risks
Regulatory risk
There is a risk that the Group's business model is impacted by future changes in regulations in the online sports
betting industry. The Group regularly reviews activity in this area through proactive monitoring of the regulatory
‘landscape and through discussions with key industry officials, professional advisors and trade associations, and
directly submits information to government consultations where requested to do so as part of the regulatory
review process in a country. The Group is a member of established trade associations operating in a number of
: the Group's operating regions. _
A large number of the Group's current customers are online sportsbook operators who market and sell to their
customers on a worldwide basis. Jurisdictions continue to regulate the online gambling market through the
continuation or introduction of licensing regimes. The Company liaises regularly with its customers regarding
: their active management of this risk.

Systems and infrastructure
The Group relies on information technology and other services, systems, and platforms, including data centres,
Amazon Web Services and certain other third-party platforms. Failures, errors, defects, or disruptions therein
could diminish disrupt business, and adversely affect operating results and growth prospects. The Companies
product offerings and other software applications and systems, and certain third-party platforms could contain
undetected errors or errors that are not identified as material. ;
Risks of system outages, loss of connectivity and software errors are regularly reviewed, and continuity of
access to data and integrity of data are supplemented with a variety of other capabilities including, incident :
response, built in redundancy, back-up and restore procedures. The Company adopts a cloud first approach to
infrastructure and systems, and so are protected with built-in availability and resilience which is tested by third
party service providers. .
Technology risk . . ,
Our future business and financial success will depend on our ability to continue to anticipate the needs of
customers and potential customers, to achieve and maintain broad market acceptance for our existing and future
products and services, to successfully introduce new and upgraded products and services and to successfully
implement our current and future geographic expansion plans. To be successful, we must be able to quickly
adapt to changes in technology, industry standards and regulatory requirements by continually enhancing our
technology, services and solutions. Developing new services and upgrades to products and services, as well as
integrating and coordinating current products and services, imposes burdens on our product development team,
management and researchers. These processes are costly, and our efforts to develop, integrate and enhance
our services may not be successful. In addition, successfully launching and selling a new or upgraded product or
service puts additional strain on our sales and marketing resources. Expanding into new markets and investing
resources towards increasing the depth of our coverage within existing markets impose additional burdens on
our research, systems development, sales, marketing and general managerial resources. If we are unable to
manage our expansion efforts effectively, in obtaining greater market share or in obtaining widespread adoption
of new or upgraded products and services, we may not be able to offset the expenses associated with the launch

: and marketing of the new or upgraded product or service, which could have a material adverse effect on our

. financial results.

Rights risk
If we lose any official accreditation from one of our league or federation partners, we could lose our exclusive
tights to collect certain data, streams or other properties and our services would be less attractive to customers.
Our revenue may decrease as a result, which could have a material adverse effect on the results of our
operations.
We must use all efforts to retain and acquire key sports data, streaming and other properties and to protect and
enforce our rights. If we are unable to do so or otherwise unable to provide products and services that customers
want, then customers may become dissatisfied and use our competitors’ services. If we are unable to continue
offering innovative services, we may be unable to attract additional customers or retain our existing customers,
which could harm our business, results of operations and financial condition.
