STRATEGIC REPORT ,

FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2023

The Directors present their Strategic Report on the Company for the year ended 31 December 2023.

Principal activity :

SymphonyAl NetReveal UK Limited (hereafter “the Company’) is a private limited company organised under the

laws of the United Kingdom and incorporated in the United Kingdom. :

The principal activity of the Company is provision of enterprise investigation and intelligence software solutions

that enable financial institutions to combat financial crime. The Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of

SymphonyAl NetReveal BidCo Limited.

Business review

There is a rapid growth in the Al SaaS industry. SymphonyAl NetReveal is a leading provider of enterprise

investigation and intelligence software solutions that enable financial institutions to combat financial crime. The

Company added new customers and revenues rose to £67m in 2023 from £27m in 2022. The Company

generated a total net profit of £15,149,730 for the year ended 31 December 2023 (2022 - £155,956 as restated).

The total shareholder's surplus totalled £15,305,686 (2022 - £155,956 as restated) at the end of the year.

Principal risks and uncertainties

Given the rapid growth of the Al SaaS industry, competitive pressure both domestically in the UK and

internationally is a significant risk for the Company. To manage this risk, the Company focuses on staying ahead

of the fast-paced SaaS environment by continually upscaling its operations, innovating, and regularly updating its
product offerings to meet the evolving needs of customers. This proactive approach ensures that the Company
remains competitive and can effectively respond to market dynamics.

The Company actively manages other key business risks, including data privacy and security and compliance

risks. Robust controls and risk management frameworks are in place to mitigate these risks, ensuring that they

remain within manageable limits for the Company. These include, but are not limited to, the implementation of
data encryption, the use of access controls and multi-factor authentication and regular review of the laws and
regulations. As a result, the impact of these risks on the Company is effectively minimised.

Financial key performance indicators ,

e Operational Efficiency: The Company aims to ensure cost-effectiveness of delivering the product relative to. ~
the revenue generated. Key factors include, but are not limited to, gross margin (revenue minus the cost of
goods sold), earnings before income tax, depreciation and amortisation (EBITDA) and the cost of supporting
Al infrastructure. High operational efficiency indicates the Company is managing costs effectively while
maximising revenue potential. :

e Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) / Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR): Ensuring to promote the Company's
competitive products in order to maintain or increase the MRR and ARR generated from subscriptions on a
monthly or annual basis is one of the Company's performance indicators.

e Market Share and Competitive Position: Market share measures the Company's revenue as a percentage of
the overall market, helping to assess its growth relative to industry trends. Competitive positioning tracks the
Company's standing against competitors in areas such as product offerings, pricing, customer base and
innovation.

These KPls provide a comprehensive view of the Company's performance, covering key areas such as

customer acquisition and retention, product innovation and financial health. The Company is committed to.

regularly tracking and analysing these KPis to make data-driven decisions, optimise operational efficiency, and
align its strategy with growth objectives. This approach will help ensure the Company's long-term success in an
increasingly dynamic and competitive market.

The Directors present their report and the financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2023. The
comparatives represent the 9 month period from incorporation 23 March 2022 to 31 December 2022. The
Company started operating activities in October 2022.
Business overview
SymphonyAl NetReveal is a leading provider of enterprise investigation and intelligence software solutions that
enable financial institutions combat financial crime. The Company's solutions help financial institutions detect,
analyse, investigate and action, on an enterprise basis, areas of financial crime such as money laundering,
debit/credit card fraud, identity theft, market abuse, terrorist financing, and the illegal transfer of funds to named
individuals and organisations. The Company's suite is deployed across four continents in the retail banking,
insurance, and investment banking sectors and monitors millions of transactions on a daily basis. The
technology platform of the Company is at the core of its financial crime prevention solution offerings. The
platform is the catalyst that turns data into knowledge and knowledge into action. It does this by combining
advanced data mining techniques with behavioural and transactional analysis to enable the detection and
reporting of financial crime. It enables immediate action to be taken, to prevent and combat crime, through
sophisticated case and response management methodologies embedded into the platform. The combined
approach of analysis and action enables financial services organisations to not just meet their regulatory
reporting requirements but also build up in-depth knowledge on the risk profile of their business and take action
to prevent crime and reduce their operational losses.
Results and dividends
The profit for the year, after taxation, amounted to £15,149,730 (2022 - £155,956 as restated).
The Directors do not recommend payment of a dividend.

selling prices exist for the other services promised in the contract. When the only performance
- obligations sold are license and software support, the Company allocates the transaction price first to
the software support using the determined SSP based on observable standalone sales and then
