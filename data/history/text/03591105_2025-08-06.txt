Introduction
JSM Group Services Limited is a leading provider of integrated utility infrastructure solutions. Our combination
of end-to-end technical expertise, decades of industry experience, and unlimited fresh thinking make us a
trusted partner for the implementation of next-generation connectivity in energy and communications. Our focus
is on building for tomorrow by delivering every day for customers right across the UK, Europe and beyond.
In July 2024 the JSM Group, including the Company, was acquired by TowerBrook Capital Partners L.P., a
private equity firm. The acquisition and related refinancing of the Group puts the JSM Group business into a
well-funded structure with a strong balance sheet position and access to the funds needed to support the next
phase of growth in the business.
Key markets
JSM Group serves market sectors which are seen as critical infrastructure areas for the future and are
experiencing strong long-term growth: in particular, energy transition and decarbonisation, data demand and
connectivity. Ambitious net zero targets are driving demand for sustainable, distributed renewable energy and
the electrification of heating and transport, placing increasing strain on an under-invested electricity distribution

: network. Technology advancements, Al and big data are exponentially increasing demand for data, processing
capacity and improved connectivity which is driving significant investment in infrastructure.
The business is operating at the top-end of a fast-moving area of the economy and is currently experiencing
high-growth, particularly from the Power division which is supporting blue-chip customers in the installation of
large-scale data centres, predominantly in the UK at the moment. Growth from renewables sector, including
battery production facilities has also been a feature of the last twelve months.
Business review
UK growth in renewables facilities and data centres resulted in a very successful 2024 performance for the
company. Revenue in the year increased by 23% to £182.2m (2023: £148.1m). The Power division continued its
strong growth, increasing revenue by 51% to £136m as a result of several large contract wins. The
Communications division saw a temporary drop in revenue to £40m (2023: 51.9m), while Gas services grew
from £4.9m to £6.2m.
Operating profit increased to £22.8m (2023: 20.2m), with gross margins increasing from 24.6% to 25.5% driven
by the strong growth of the Power division, operational efficiencies and pass-through of the cost inflation
experienced in 2023. Administrative expenses increased from £14.3m to £16.9m to support the growth of the
business and there were £6.9m of exceptional costs, relating to the acquisition and related restructuring costs.
The improved profit before tax of £23m (2023: £20.3m) reflects the improved year on year operating profit
explained above.
The Company balance sheet position is strong with net assets of £36m (2023: £62.4m) and a positive cash
balance. The Group balance sheet, consolidated at JSM Group Services Holdings International Limited level,
shows net assets of £209m.
JSM Group has continued its focus on Environmental, Social and Governance issues, as detailed later in this
report and its award-winning health and safety record.
JSM Group was recently honoured with the Product Innovation: Services & Software award in recognition of its
innovative cable extraction technology.
This award reflects the Company's continued commitment to delivering advanced engineering solutions that
enhance operational efficiency and promote safety and environmental sustainability.

Principal risks and uncertainties
In 2024, JSM Group Services Limited has continued to show its resilience to the impact of the continuing
economic and political uncertainties in the UK, Europe and beyond. These issues remain the principal risks and
uncertainties for the year ahead, although the buoyancy of the Group's main markets, growth in data centre
facilities in particular, and the high level of secured contracts is a strong mitigating factor against these risks.
The company has been successful in absorbing significant inflationary cost increases, and this will continue to
be a key focus area for the year ahead.
Future growth will have an impact on cashflows and balances, as working capital requirements increase. This
will be mitigated by ensuring contracts are sustainable, including separate cash flow analysis at tender stage
and closely monitoring cash forecasts. We have strong relationships with both our clients and banks. These
relationships ensure both good payment profiles and strong short and long term funding for working capital.
Financial Risk management
The Company's operations expose it to a variety of financial business risks including foreign currency exchange
rates, credit risk and liquidity risk:
Foreign currency risk .
The Company purchases some materials from Europe in UK sterling. Although there is an implicit underlying
exchange rate risk, exposure to currency fluctuations is not felt to be a major risk. Any major price change
arising from currency fluctuation would be covered by a variation in the price to our customers.
Credit risk
The Company's credit risk is primarily attributable to its trade debtors and amounts recoverable on contracts.
Credit risk is managed by monitoring the aggregate amount and duration of exposure to any one customer and
any credit limit in place. Historically over 82% of debt is current and not overdue.
Liquidity risk
The Company's risk is managed by financial management on its major contracts by negotiating appropriate
payment terms with respective customers and suppliers. The Company's objective is to ensure an overall
positive or neutral cash flaw on all projects.
Interest rate risk
The Company, via the wider group, is exposed to interest rate risk on its borrowings. While no hedging
instruments are currently in place, the Company manages this risk through regular monitoring of market interest
tates and incorporating interest rate sensitivity analysis into financial planning and cash flow forecasting. The
Company also ensures sufficient headroom in its available facilities to absorb potential cost increases resulting
from rate fluctuations.

Outlook/Prospects
As a market leader in infrastructure and utility services across several sectors JSM Group is seeing rapid and _
sustained growth in revenues and profits. The reputation of the business for delivering quality projects on
schedule and overcoming technical challenges, has resulted in very strong customer relationships that should
lead to continuation of the strong growth seen in recent years. We have a high level of secured orders for the
next year and a strong pipeline beyond that.

" JSM Group continues to invest in capital expenditure, new employees and retention of current employees to
continue to operate in what is a fast-moving market to ensure that we can continually look for ways to become
more efficient. Investment in project controls, contract risk management and IT are also a big part of JSM
Group's philosophy as we continue to grow over the coming years and at a significant rate.

Growth to date has been mainly in the UK markets but the group has attracted a lot of interest from customers
about carrying out similar activities overseas. Opportunities have been assessed, and current plans include
significant expansion in Europe and the Middle East markets, driven by our strong relationships with current
: customers. Bases have already been established in the Republic of Ireland and Germany, with Middle East in
progress. JSM Group is well positioned to drive scale increases as an established business with strong
relationships with suppliers and skilled labour markets, retaining focus on a few key areas.

Directors' statement of compliance with duty to promote the success of the Company
The directors consider that in the interests of the shareholders and stakeholders they promote a sustainable,
profitable and cash generative business for the benefit of the business. The directors consider that the current
business strategy supports this purpose and is taken into account when reviewing the annual business plan.
Since its incorporation in 1998, the Company's long-term success has been supported by strong relationships
with employees, customers, supply chain partners, and other stakeholders. Many of these relationships have
been developed and maintained over the long term.
The directors recognise the importance of creating a positive and engaging working environment where
employees feel motivated to work and remain a part of the Company's overall strategy. To support this goal, the
directors have established objectives focused on employee retention, the recruitment of apprentices, and the
continuous development of staff through training programs. These initiatives aim to enhance employees’ skills
and career progression, ensuring they contribute effectively to the Company's success. These objectives are
reviewed annually to monitor progress in retaining existing talent and developing new talent.

success of the Company for the benefit of its shareholders as a whole, and in doing so have regard (amongst
other matters) to:
e the likely consequences of any decisions in the long-term;
e the interest of the Company's employees;
e the need to foster the Company's business relationships with suppliers, customers and others;
« the impact of the Company's operations on the community and environment;
e the desirability of the Company maintaining a reputation for high standards of business conduct; and
e the need to act fairly as between shareholders of the Company.
