VCG Technology Services Limited
Strategic Report
For the Year Ended 31 March 2024
Introduction
The Directors present the Strategic Report for the year ended 31 March 2024.
The Company’s principal activity was the provision of business-critical connectivity, managed networks, ICT solutions and related services. 
Business review
Following the emergence from the global COVID-19 pandemic, lead times for hardware were reduced, which enabled the orders placed previously to be delivered, further enabling the transformation projects of our customers to be completed.
The Board identified the importance of ESG (Environmental, Social and Governance) to its stakeholders. As succy is being created with specific targets being set that will be incorporated into key future decisions and business strategy.
Following changes being made in the Senior Leadership Team, with the appointment of a new CEO and Sales Director, the Company went through a transitional period to ensure that it was repurposed and had clear targets and objectives to achieve current objectives and deliver future growth.
The Board reinforced the importance of diversification in the customer base and partner offerings.  The new business acquisition team started to perform under the leadership of the new Sales Director.  New go to market strategies and materials were created, to drive margin rich, contracted re-occurring revenue streams for the Company.  The creation of this new team, along with partner diversification, will enable the existing Enterprise team to focus on increasing our interactions with the existing customer base.  The changes being made are starting to flow into the business results, seen by the increased recurring revenue in both value, and mix of turnover, with an increase of £4.91m in this area against the prior year.  The Board are focused on continuing the growth of recurring revenue, delivering a predictable cash flow to the business, and supporting a wider number of customers with an improved managed service offering.
Towards the end of the year, a number of large customer contracts were re-signed, enabling the business to continue its partnerships for the coming years.
For the year ended 31 March 2024 turnover was £29.4m (
2023: £24.4m
) which represents an increase of 20% during the year. After interest, depreciation, amortisation and exceptional items, the Company made a pre-tax profit of £722k (
2023: £165k
) with EBITDAE of £1,060k (
2023: £409k
).
Principal risks and uncertainties
Macro-economic factors
With most of the activity within the Company UK based, the Company is dependent on the country's economic performance however the Company is constantly developing new business opportunities in different sectors to better hedge the Company's performance in the future. 
Changing industry
The Company continues to assess new and innovative solutions to partner with customers in providing solutions to support the changing ways they work and do business. 
Supply chain
The Board continues to work closely with our distributors and customers to manage the supply chain.  A Vendor Relationship Manager was appointed and there was a review and recruitment of Account Managers during the year to strengthen these relationships.

Financial instruments
Cash flow management and associated liqudity risk is a key factor for the business. 
The company's ultimate parent entity holds a loan with an investor which attracts a fixed rate of interest. Management ensure that sufficient cash is generated by the company (as the active trading entity) to service this loan within its terms. 
The company also has finance agreements in respect of some purchases, these are displayed as other loans and amounts due under hire purchase and finance agreements.
Management closely monitor cash flows and ensure that sufficient working capital is available to allow the business to operate effectively. Customers are required to adhere to credit terms and in normal conditions there are limited bad debts.
