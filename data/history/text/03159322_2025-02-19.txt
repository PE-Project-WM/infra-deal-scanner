Strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2023
The directors present their strategic report on the company for the year ended 31 December 2023.
Principal activity and review of the business
The principal activity of the company continues to be the provision of litigation support services, which includes
computer forensics, hosting, electronic discovery, paper discovery and document review services.
The legal technologies business in the UK is coming under increased market pressure not only from traditional
technologies companies but also from companies which in the past were involved in other consultancy activities
such as accounting and compliance and legal firms. The growth in technology which permits companies to be
able to collect and manage data internally is also affecting our UK business. We also continue to expand the
business by adding new products into our portfolio.
Turnover for the 12 months to 31 December 2023 is £19,466,463 (2022: £18, 108,973) this includes revenue from
our core business of document processing and hosting and computer forensics. Gross margin for the year is 46%
(2022:48%).
The company’s strategy continues to be expansion into other international markets while maintaining our
position as one of the market leaders in the UK. We continue to develop new technologies, which enable us to
provide our service to our clients in a way that permits them to access the information wherever they are and
have the information available to them 24 hours a day. Building on the many strengths of our existing business
and strategy, we are focused on continuing to enhance our proprietary solutions, expand our ecosystem, and
extend our reach to capitalize on our large and growing market opportunity.
As part of the company’s efforts to optimize its real estate footprint, the company partially abandoned London
office location in 2023 and as a result recognised a provision in respect of the onerous lease contract in the
financial statements.
Key performance indicators (“KPIs”)
Turnover for the year to 31 December 2023, which is also considered as a KPI of the company, increased 7.5%
compared to the same period to 31 December 2022.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Directors consider the following to be the key risks and uncertainties of the Company:
Financial instrument risk (Credit risk)
The company does not use derivatives to manage its financial risks. The most important components of financial
risk are interest rate risk, currency risk, credit risk, cash flow risk and price risk. Due to the nature of the
company’s business and the assets and liabilities contained within the company’s balance sheet, the only financial
risk the directors consider relevant to this company is credit risk. Credit risk is the risk that one party to a financial
instrument will cause a financial loss for that other party by failing to discharge an obligation. Most of our clients
. are legal firms who depend on payment from their clients in order to be able to settle our invoices. Company .

policies, such as turnover recognition and credit control policy, are aimed at minimising such losses.
The company recognises that the successful management of risk as part of our everyday activities is essential to
support the achievement of its strategic objectives.
Macroeconomic challenges risk
Management believes that global macroeconomic challenges, including inflation, rising interest rates and the wars
in Ukraine and the Middle East, had a significant adverse impact on the Company and the overall market in 2023.
The company’s success in part depends on the ability to anticipate and effectively manage the risks associated
with the current macroeconomic challenges, and there can be no assurance that company will be able to do so
without experiencing a reduction in revenue or incurring unexpected costs. In the long-term, we plan to increase
our net revenue retention rate by increasing the number of solutions that we sell on a subscription-basis, as well
as broadening the scope of our proprietary platform offerings, to promote strong product adoption. As the
company expands our products beyond eDiscovery to other information governance solutions such as big data

The directors present their report and the audited financial statements of the company for year ended 31 December

Insurance of Directors
Throughout the period, Directors' and Officers’ liability insurance has been maintained by the company.
Future developments
Our principal business is the provision of legal technologies to large corporates and legal firms. Our commitment
to providing exceptional and efficient client service through innovation and continual improvement remains an
important driver of organic growth. We are working on ways of providing our clients with our service which will
give them complete control of their data while enabling us to be able to quickly respond to their requests.
Results and dividends
The profit for the year after taxation amounted to £282,206 (2022: £1,951,155). The directors do not recommend
the payment of a dividend (2022: £nil).
Political Contributions
The Company made no disclosable political donations and did not incur any disclosable political expenditure
during the year.
Going concern
The directors of the company have reviewed the activities of the company and taken into consideration the current
trading conditions in the main markets of the company. Due to the evolving situation and current macroeconomic
uncertainties our business may be impacted in ways that we cannot predict. In the long-term, we plan to increase
our net revenue retention rate by increasing the number of solutions that we sell on a subscription-basis, as well
as broadening the scope of our proprietary platform offerings, to promote strong product adoption. As the
company expands our products beyond eDiscovery to other information governance solutions such as big data
hosting and processing, we expect clients to leverage our technology earlier in the data lifecycle, providing further
opportunity for us to increase our product and service penetration and client retention. Furthermore, we plan to
establish and broaden our channel partnerships over time and leverage these strong relationships to further our
awareness of our products and overall usage within the industry. The company is consolidating its position in
new markets and the directors believe that the company is well-placed to manage its business risks successfully
despite the current uncertain economic outlook.
The company has positive net current assets and positive shareholders’ funds at 31 December 2023. The directors
have considered the applicability of the going concern basis in the preparation of these financial statements. This
included a review of current financial results, as well as a preparation and review of Income Statement and
Balance Sheet forecasts with the projections of relevant cashflows for the period to 28 February 2026. The

. ultimate parent company owns all the technology, software, and tradenames crucial for the Company's operations
and therefore the Company is reliant on parental support to continue in operational existence.
The company has received a letter of support from its ultimate parent company, KLDiscovery, Inc. Taking into
account the support from KLDiscovery Inc., the directors have a reasonable expectation that the company has
adequate resources to continue in operational existence for the going concern period to 28 February 2026.
The ultimate parent company KLDiscovery Inc. completed a financial restructuring in August 2024 which
included conversion of debt to equity, extension of its credit agreement to August 2027, and a new term loan.
This restructuring removed the substantial doubt regarding the parent company’s ability to continue as a going
concern noted in the prior year financial statements.
Environment
The company recognises the importance of its environmental responsibilities and designs and implements policies
to reduce any damage which might be caused by the company’s activities. In December 2022, KLDiscovery
Ontrack Limited, the parent company, signed Carbon Reduction Plan where the company commits to achieving
Net Zero by 2030. During 2023, the company continued to monitor and measure its progress against the identified
base line emission footprint.

6) Provisions
Provisions are recognised when the company has a present legal or constructive obligation as a result of past
events; it is probable that an outflow of resources will be required to settle the obligation; and the amount of
the obligation can be estimated reliably.
Where there are a number of similar obligations, the likelihood that an outflow will be required in settlement is
determined by considering thc class of obligations as a whole. A provision is recognised even if the likelihood
of an outflow with respect to any one item included in the same class of obligations might be small.
Provisions are measured at the present value of the expenditures expected to be required to settle the obligation
using a pre-tax rate that reflects current market assessments of the time value of money and the risks specific
to the obligation. The increase in the provision due to passage of time is recognised as a finance cost.

event (as defined in the award agreements governing the RSUs). The amount and timing of the vesting of the
RSUs depends on the type and timing of the liquidity event as it relates to the Closing Date. Generally, a portion
of the RSUs will first vest upon the occurrence of the liquidity event and the remainder will vest in up to three
annual installments thereafter, provided that if the liquidity event occurs after the third anniversary of the Closing
Date, all RSUs will vest immediately upon the liquidity event. The vesting of the RSUs held by a grantee is
generally subject to his or her continued employment with the Company.

. 20. Onerous contract provision
During the year, the company has recognized a provision in respect of onerous lease contract. In March 2023,
the Company decided to abandon 37% of the premises and determined that it would not sublease the
abandoned space. The Company determined that the partially abandoned space of 37% of premises, constitutes
a separate unit of account because it was physically and functionally distinct from the remainder of the leased :
space. Therefore, under Section 21 of FRS 102, a provision was recognized for the unavoidable costs
associated with the abandoned portion of the lease. The provision of £616,131 is included in the financial
statements and the associated cash outflows are primarily expected to take places withing one year.
