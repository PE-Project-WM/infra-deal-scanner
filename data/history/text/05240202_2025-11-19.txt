The directors present their Strategic Report for Ground Control Technologies UK Ltd (the ‘Company’') for the
. year ended 30 November 2024. :
Review of business .
The. Company is a leading Internet of Things (‘oT’) and Machine-to-Machine (‘M2M’) end to end platform
: provider. The Company provides SCADA, telemetry, monitoring and tracking platforms for loT and M2M
_ assets, generally in remote or challenging environments, and operates globally. The Company provides a one-
stop solution combining secure infrastructure, resilient software platforms and multiple network connectivity.
‘Working in partnership with multiple major satellite and cellular networks and key hardware manufacturers, the
Company provides managed service solutions tailored to the loT and M2M connectivity needs of a range of
: customers from global blue chip organisations, government agencies and small enterprises.
In August 2017 the Company was acquired by Horizon Capital LLP who planned to back the management
team to execute a buy and build growth strategy to significantly accelerate the growth of the business as part
of a group. This transaction provided the Group with access to significant funding in order to deliver these
plans. In 2018 the Group acquired Rock Seven Mobile Services Ltd and YB Tracking Ltd (together “Rock
Seven’) in the UK and in 2019 the Group acquired Ground Control Systems (“Ground Control”) in the United
States of America. In August 2021 the Group rebranded the business with a new look and feel under the
Ground Control name and relaunched the groundcontrol.com website. i
During the year the Company's installed.base of satellite and cellular loT and M2M continued to grow as
active device connectivity subscriptions increased. However, connectivity and services revenue declined by
. 3% (compared to a growth of 71% in 2023) due to unfavourable foreign. exchange movements experienced
during the year. Connectivity and services revenue represents 65% of total revenue. The key enabler of
recurring revenue growth is the sale of hardware solutions, hardware revenue grew by 29% (compared to
_ 81% in 2023). .
Emerging from a period of challenging supply chain conditions, the Company continued to invest substantially
. ‘in securing a high-growth future. Significant progress was made developing and launching new products and
. new product variants, substantially strengthening the sales and marketing operation, and establishing its own
, IP networking capability. This will allow the Company to maximise the opportunity presented by new products
‘and achieve top tier partner status with key suppliers in the sector, further diversifying supply base risk. The
Company finished the year in a strong position and well placed to continue and accelerate growth.

open dialogue and contact. The global supply chain of communication technology products has been
disrupted by several events including the pandemic. The Company has recruited a supply chain __

loT/M2M stickiness. The Company continues to invest in platforms and infrastructure allowing :
customers to manage the disparate pace of change in these technologies; and

Strategy

The Board undertook major strategic review in 2021 which reflected the increasingly global nature of the

Company. The strategy was refined in 2023, increasing the focus on five growth pillars which will be delivered

over time through both organic growth and mergers and acquisitions (‘M&A’) activity:

, * Platform: expand and continuously develop the Company's leading Cloudloop® platform, supporting
an expanding range of core services, enabling value-added services layers, and creating sticky high-
value connectivity with partners across our complete range of products, solutions and market;

* New brand positioning: supporting the strategy of the integrated Company and its broad geographic
footprint and service offerings;

* Sales acceleration: growing sales capabilities, verticalising and focusing on key markets, and
partnering with systems integrators and other channel partners;

* Broader connectivity: repositioning the Company as connectivity agnostic, adding new connectivity
partner relationships and new technologies and increasing prominence with key satellite and cellular
network partners;

* Products: continuing to leverage the Company's flexible development capabilities, creating exclusive
hardware to drive connectivity and services, focusing on recurring revenue enhancers, looking
towards higher bandwidth loT/M2M applications, data hosting, management and analytics.

In the short term the focus of.the business is to continue to deliver organic growth, with greatest emphasis on
solutions driving high recurring revenue and maximised customer lifetime value, whilst evaluating mergers
and M&A opportunities and completing the remaining operational consolidation. The longer-term strategy of
the Company remains to become a global category leader in loT and M2M solutions:

+ Positioned as a connectivity-agnostic service delivery platform;

* Providing customers with market-leading solutions for high-availability, mission-critical applications in
remote or challenging environments; and

* Challenging the service provider/solution landscape as a disrupter.

Objectives and key performance indicators (“KPIs”)
Management has adopted a number of indicators to measure and monitor the overall performance of the
Company. These include:
: + Active device connectivity subscriptions: Device count increased by 52% in the year ending
30 November 2024 (compared to 5% in 2023).
« Recurring revenue: This declined by 3% overall in 2024 (compared to 71% in 2023) due to
. unfavourable foreign exchange movements experienced during the year. Recurring revenue
represented 65% of total revenue in 2024 (compared to 71% growth in 2023).

« Gross margin: Gross margins increased to 39% in 2024 as compared to 37% in 2023; . .

A budget is set each year and monthly performance, including the KPI's above, are monitored against
historical trends and budgetary targets. The Company’s employee remuneration strategy is designed to align
employee remuneration with successful delivery against these and other non-financial KPIs.

higher of an asset's (or CGU's) fair value less costs to sell and value in use. For the purposes of

identifiable cash flows (CGUs). Non-financial assets that have been previously impaired are reviewed at

recognised in prior periods may no longer exist or may have decreased.
