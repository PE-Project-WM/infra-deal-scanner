- 1 -
The directors present their strategic report and financial statements in respect of Nesscoinvsat Limited ("The company") for the year end 31 March 2025. 
Fair reveiw of the business
Turnover was £20.8 million (2024: £19.1 million), and gross profit was £2.0 million (2024: £1.8 million). Profit before tax was £11.4 million (2024: loss of £1.7 million). This result included a £13.2 million waiver of balances owed to the former group and transfer pricing charges of £1.9 million (2024: £2.7 million). Excluding these items, profit before tax was approximately £0.1 million.
Net assets at 31 March 2025 were £16.6 million (2024: £4.6 million).
Change in Ownership
Until 18 December 2024, the company was part of the Viasat Inc. group. On that date, Viasat completed the sale of its Energy Services System Integration business, and Nessco Holdings LLC became the company’s controlling party. The majority of the financial year therefore fell under the former ownership.
Following the change of ownership, the company was required to establish standalone reporting arrangements and controls.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks are a reduction in customer activity if oil and gas investment is deferred, contract-delivery and credit risk on project work, liquidity pending milestone receipts, and foreign-currency exposure on contracts denominated other than in sterling.
Financial Risk Management 
The company's activities expose it to certain financial risks including liquidity risk, market risk and credit risk. The company does not use derivatives for either financial risk management or speculative purposes. 
Liquidity risk 
In order to maintain liquidity and to ensure sufficient funds are available for ongoing operations and future developments, management monitor the timing of cash flows and align these with its strategic planning. Milestone receipts on projects are negotiated with customers in order to help with cash flow requirements. Further cash resources are available from the wider group if required, however the company's primary source of finance is the operational cash generated from trading activities. 
Market Risk 
The company's activities expose it primarily to the financial risk of changes in foreign currency exchange risk, with some customer contracts being denominated in currencies other than GBP (primarily USD). Bank accounts are maintained in foreign currencies, which assists the company to monitor this exposure, through being able to receive and make payments in these currencies and thus operating natural hedging wherever possible.

Credit Risk 
The company;s principal financial assets are bank balances, trade receivables and amounts due from fellow group undertakings. Credit risk is primarily attributable to trade receivables and is managed through maintaining good customer relationships and the monitoring of credit levels and settlement periods. The amounts presented within the balance sheet are net of allowances for doubtful receivables. The credit risk on liquid funds is considered limited because the counterparty is a bank with a recognised credit rating assignment by international credit rating agencies.

Future developments
The company will continue to focus on delivering its existing contracts, securing extensions and additional scope from current customers, and bidding for new work in its established and developing markets.
A key priority is supporting the group’s expansion into the Middle East through the establishment of new entities. This regional presence is intended to strengthen customer relationships, facilitate local procurement and project support, and create further opportunities for the company’s engineering, manufacturing and systems integration services.
The company has taken full possession of its Westhill facility, providing the space required to increase manufacturing capacity to approximately three times its previous level. This expansion will support existing contractual commitments and anticipated growth in demand. The company’s growth plans are supported by engagement with ETZ, UK Export Finance (UKEF) and Scottish Enterprise to assist with the development of its facilities, export activities and associated financing requirements.
The directors will continue to manage growth with a focus on operational capability, contract profitability and working capital, ensuring that new opportunities are assessed against the resources and funding required to deliver them.
Key performance indicators
The directors consider turnover, gross profit, profit before tax and net assets to be the company’s key performance indicators.
