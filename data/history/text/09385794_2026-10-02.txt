Introduction
The directors of Zevantel Europe Limited ("the Company") present their strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2024.
Principal activity
The Company is acting as a Mobile Virtual Network Operator ("MVNO") in the Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark, and Austria (until March 2023), and as a full MVNO in Germany (until September 2024). The Company is a full MVNO where it holds an MVNO agreement with Mobile Network Operators directly and acts as an MVNO where MVNO agreements are currently held by related party entities. The company provides mobile telecommunication services to predominantly ethnic migrant populations.
Business review
The Company reported turnover of €123.8m for the year ended 31 December 2024 (2023: €147.7m) and achieved an increased gross profit margin of 48.9% (2023: 47.0%). The decrease in turnover during the year was principally driven by changes to the operating model in certain territories, whereby local market activities are now undertaken through local entities. In addition, from October 2024 the Company ceased offering customer bonuses, reducing the associated impact on reported turnover. The Company continues to focus on expanding its national and international airtime offerings to enhance its competitive position and is investing in the retention and customer value management ("CVM") team to support customer acquisition and reduce churn. 
The Company is focusing on providing cutting edge customer services to capture additional market share, supported by promotional and advertising activity in the European market.
Operating loss for the year is €18.7m (2023: loss of €3.6m). The decrease in turnover and the increased margins (noted above), were offset by the heavy increase in administrative expenses mainly due to the increase in the bad debt position and the increase in management fees with related parties.  
The Company adopts a usage policy for recognising turnover. The amount of turnover which is deferred until future use is held within the deferred income account in the balance sheet. The Company defers the recognition of turnover until such time as the customers use their balances or when the balances expire after a period of non utilisation as set out in the terms and conditions of the customer's contract. All amounts held in deferred income relating to future usage are identifiable to individual customers.
Key performance indicators
The Company continued to focus on improving its gross margin which is around 1.9% (2023: 10.1%) higher than the previous year. Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties facing the Company have been reviewed in detail by the directors and no material additional risk or uncertainty has been identified other than those detailed below. These risks are broadly encompassed within competitive, operational and financial risk. The directors' risk management objectives consist of identifying and monitoring those risks which could have an adverse impact on the company's assets, profitability or cash flows.
Competitive risk
The Company is acting as a Mobile Virtual Network Operator ("MVNO") in Germany (pre September 2024), Netherlands, Sweden, Denmark and Austria (pre March 2023), providing mobile telecommunication services to the predominantly ethnic migrant populations. The Company's various MVNO markets remain competitive with new entrants able to join relatively easily, resulting in pricing risk. It has proven difficult for any new entrant to achieve any scale, however, and the combination of any new entrant's inability to match Zevantel's tariff rates for any length of time as well as a lack of national distribution mitigates this risk. This competitive risk is further mitigated by regular reviews of competitive offerings and market providers. Actions are taken immediately where possible.
Operational risk
The main operational risk relating to the company is the operation of the telecommunication services agreement with the Mobile Network Operators and related party entities. The Company's ability to provide services to its customers depends on maintaining agreements with its related parties and Mobile Network Operator or acquiring new agreements with other Mobile Network Operators for network services. The Company does not have any dependency on voice or non-voice services from the Mobile Network Operator which it provides to its customers itself. The Company relies on its related party for voice and non-voice services. This overall operational risk is mitigated by ensuring that the term of the network service agreement is extended to a duration exceeding 12 months wherever possible.
Financial risk
The area of most significant financial risk is the ongoing reduction of MTR rates. MTR overall is on a downward glide path and is phasing out completely worldwide in the mobile telecoms industry as a result of regulatory action. This has an impact on the Company and implies a loss of MTR turnover in the future. This overall decline in MTR rates is not specific to Zevantel Europe Limited, it affects telecom companies across the world. However, to mitigate this potential loss of future turnover, termination costs of outgoing calls will be reduced, which will partly offset the turnover decline. The overall effect of this MTR decline is not regarded as significant to the company which, as with other telecom companies, will be seeking to replace the loss of turnover from alternative sources, for example, data services.
Foreign exchange risks
The Company's significant sales continue to be made in Euros (€). The Company also has certain balances due to or from related parties in other currencies and the company is therefore exposed to currency movements. As a matter of policy, the company chooses not to currently use financial derivatives or currency hedging to manage its exposure.
Credit risk
The Company's principal financial assets are bank balances, trade and other receivables. The Company's credit risk is primarily attributable to its trade receivables and balances from the related parties. The Company gives significant attention to credit risk as significant balances are due to and from related parties. Each related party is analysed individually for creditworthiness.
Liquidity risk
The Company's policy on liquidity risk is to ensure that sufficient cash is available to fund ongoing operations. In this respect, financing may be received from related party entities when required.
Interest rate risk
The Company has no interest-bearing liabilities and no interest-bearing assets other than its loans with related party entities and cash.

Going concern
The Company has reported:
- a loss after tax for the year of €21.5m (2023: €10.4m);
- net current liabilities of €31.6m (2023: €10.1m); and
- net cash outflows from operating activities for the year of €1.2m (2023: €0.5m) as evidenced on page 16 in the Statement of Cashflows.
The majority of the cash flows associated with investing and financing activities are ultimately either discretionary and/or with related parties under the control of Mr A Subaskaran.
The directors have reviewed the Company's business activities, together with the factors likely to affect its future development, performance and position. This going concern assessment has given consideration to the Company's available cash flow, business model, strategy, regulatory environment, principal risks and uncertainties, recent financial performance and outlook. The going concern of the Company has been assessed taking into account the potential impact of certain scenarios arising from those uncertainties which have the greatest potential impact on the Company's ability to continue in operational existence throughout the assessment period.
Specific consideration has been given to ongoing tax matters in certain European jurisdictions, including matters that remain subject to correspondence, appeal processes and ongoing discussions with relevant tax authorities. The directors have considered a range of potential outcomes, resulting in potential additional liabilities becoming payable during the going concern assessment period.
The directors have also considered the wider economic environment and its potential impact on the Company's performance and that of its related parties. Scenario planning considered, amongst other matters:
• a general market slowdown in new customer acquisitions and changes in customer behaviour;
• changes in competitive market tariffs and market share;
• reductions in operating costs, primarily in relation to subscriber acquisition costs, marketing spend and third-party customer service costs;
• potential adverse outcomes in relation to ongoing tax matters; and
• the timing and recoverability of material intercompany balances.
The directors have reviewed trading and operational cash flow forecasts for the period to 30 September 2027 and have no reason to believe that cash generated from operating activities will be materially lower than that historically generated. This includes those factors which they consider could materially affect forecast cash flow during the period, both positively and negatively. This review has included sensitivity analysis over key assumptions and demonstrates that there are no material variations to the forecast cash flows generated from operations under reasonably foreseeable scenarios.
The directors have also reviewed the asset and liability bases of the Company as at the date of approval of these financial statements and separately considered those balances which are due to or from third parties and those arising from transactions with other companies under common control.
The Company and its related parties form part of an operating model that ensures turnover and profits are economically allocated to the entities within the Group which have earned them. As such, the Company has substantial trading transactions with related party companies and there may be significant amounts due to or from those parties that are repayable on demand. The Company may also be called upon to provide funding to related parties, however there is no obligation to do so.
Third-party liabilities are expected to be settled from forecast operating cash flows as they fall due in the normal course of business.
The directors have further mitigated potential related party liquidity risk by obtaining undertakings from the owners of certain material related party creditor companies that outstanding balances will not be demanded for repayment for at least twelve months from the date of approval of these financial statements, unless sufficient surplus funds are available or repayment would not jeopardise, in the opinion of the directors, the Company's ability to meet its liabilities as they fall due.

The directors remain optimistic for the future. Uncertain tax positions and ongoing enquiries relating to VAT
There had been ongoing discussions and enquiries between Zevantel Europe Limited and HMRC regarding potential imputed interest on related party transactions between Zevantel Europe Limited and certain related party companies. Post year end Zevantel Europe Limited received communication from HMRC that this matter will not be pursued.

A provision has been recorded of €116,965,657 to reflect Zevantel Europe Limited's current best estimate of the potential exposure at 31 December 2024, including penalties and interest. The directors have recalculated the required amount as at 31 December 2024 in line with the positions taken by Zevantel Europe Limited. In some cases tax authorities have raised assessments and Zevantel Europe Limited has sought advice and taken necessary steps to appeal or challenge the positions and postponed collections of most of the amounts. Zevantel Europe Limited expects a number of these disputes to continue for a number of months prior to any resolution.

Mobile service turnover
This includes national and international airtime, data and roaming services provided to the end user. Mobile termination turnover is recognised when the calls are terminated to Lycamobile Europe Limited through an interconnect partner.
