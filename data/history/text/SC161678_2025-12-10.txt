Strategic report

- 1 -
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2025.
Review of the business
Turnover for the year to 31 March 2025 (“FY25”) was 12% higher than the prior year, at £11.4m. The company recognised revenue growth across its newer revenue streams such as: Brightsolid Cloud, Licence Reselling, Cyber Resilience, Backup as a Service and Public Cloud services. Colocation continued to perform strongly whilst there was some churn across legacy Private Cloud services.
The increase of third-party costs into the revenue mix, contributed to gross margin % decreasing from 48% to 46% in FY25.
Overheads increased by 9% in the year to 31 March 2025 with a contributing factor being costs relating to M&A activities. This ultimately led to a post year-end acquisition of Synergi Software Limited; a company based in Northeast England that will enhance Brightsolid’s customer offering through AI, Business Applications, Process Automation and Managed Service capabilities.
This resulted in an operating loss for the year of £1.4m in FY25, an increase of 14% on the prior year.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Brightsolid Board has developed, and regularly reviews, a risk appetite statement and treatment strategies for all risks outside of appetite. Risk appetite is lowest for cyber security risks, broadly the risk that a failure to address IT vulnerabilities in a timely manner leads to compromised company and customer systems and data. 
Loss of key employees is also a risk focus for management. Digital skills continue to be in high demand and recruitment and retention of talented colleagues remains critical to the success of the business.
An inability to deliver leading products and services in rapidly changing markets could materially constrain growth and profitability, jeopardising longer term investment and innovation.
Key performance indicators
The Brightsolid Board monitors a range of KPIs measuring financial performance, customer satisfaction, 
investment in 
employee

, technical capabilities and marketing execution.
