Introduction
The directors present their strategic report for lvy Network Technology U.K. Limited (“the Company”) for the
year ended 31 December 2023.

Business review

The principal activity of the Company is the provision of installation, maintenance and repair services of robotics
equipment and aftermarket repair and logistics services to customers in the information technology sector.

The statement of comprehensive income is set out on page 10.

The Company's turnover for the year is USD$8,840,814 (2022: USD$22,139,976), generating operating loss of
USD$647,855 (2022: USD$4,011,478 profit). Turnover declined this year due to reduced demand for fulfilment
centre robotics fitouts from customers arising from the impact of the easing of COVID restrictions causing a
slowdown in expected demand in online commerce. The decline in operating profit is mainly a result of reduced
turnover, as gross margin percentage improved year on year. The macroeconomic environment has also
brought about some inflationary pressures which the company has had to carefully manage.

Strategy and business model

The Company's business objective is to provide high quality robotics installation and maintenance services and
end to end repair and logistics which will generate a reasonable return for the Company's shareholders. This is
achieved through carefully selecting commercial! opportunities, diversifying the customer base and configuring
the company's internal resources and facilities to realise those opportunities in a value accretive manner.

. . The Company devoted substantial resources to the diversification of its customer base in financial year ended
31 December 23 and expects to see return on this investment in financial year ended 31 December 24.
Business environment
The economic impact of the current Russian / Ukrainian conflict, particularly in respect of the significant
increase in the cost of energy, utilities and consumables, along with the increased pressure on supply chains
and availability of certain raw material inputs, is presenting a challenging environment for the Company,
particularly in respect of recovering such increases. Management continues to monitor the situation closely,
implementing changes as required.

Notwithstanding this, the Directors consider that the Company is well placed to take advantage of the industry's
increased focus on sustainability, recycling, and repair.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The Directors of the Company and the execution of its strategy are subject to a number of risks. These risks are
controlled on a group wide basis. Where relevant, the impact of these risks on the Company is set out below.
Business risk: Management have established formal processes for identifying, evaluating, and managing
business risk exposures with ongoing reviews of progress against strategic objectives. The business risks
reviewed include:
+ External business risks, including regulator and compliance obligations;
+ Operational risks arising from supplier dependency, fire damage, material damage, etc.;
+ Legal risks including risks arising from factory leases and supplier contracts; and

. + Information risks including the integrity of IT systems and the security of information.

Competition risk: The Company's continued ability to trade is predicated on the Company being able to deliver a
superior offering compared to competitors in terms of service price, quality, reliability, and supply among other
factors. An increase in the competitive intensity in the Company's key markets could adversely impact the
Company's trading performance. In mitigation, the Company's commercial management team collate and
review market intelligence to obtain insight into competitor activity so that the Company can respond in a timely
and well considered manner.
Service quality risk: Service quality is of paramount importance. The Company's service processes are subject
to rigorous quality control checks leveraging both human and technical resources to streamline the service
management process. Bespoke information technology processes have been developed, refined, and
implemented to reduce this risk.

- Liquidity and cash flow risk: The Company manages its liquidity and cash flow within appropriate limits.
Significant emphasis is placed on frequently updated cashflow forecast to provide early indication of any
possible liquidity issues.
