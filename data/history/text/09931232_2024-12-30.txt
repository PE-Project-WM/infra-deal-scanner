STRATEGIC REPORT
for the financial year ended 31 December 2023
Introduction
The directors present their strategic report on the Group and Parent Company for the financial year ended 31 December
2023.
The principal activity of Flo Live Limited (“the Company or Parent Company”) is developing through its subsidiaries
("the Group"), a cloud-based connectivity management, billing, and core network platform, as well as global cellular
connectivity services. The Company provides support to customers mainly throughout Europe, Middle East, and Africa
(EMEA), Americas and Asia-Pacific (APJ). _ :
Business Review and Key Performance Indicators .
The Company has subsidiaries located in United Kingdom, Israel, United States of America, Cyprus, Bulgaria,
Singapore, Guernsey and Turkey as of 31 December 2023.
The revenues of the Group increased by 78.3% to $8,511,319 (2022: $4,773,496) mainly as a result of the increase in
the number of customers. The operating loss decreased by 0.95% to $19,101,444 (2022: $19,283,832)
As of 31 December 2023, the net assets of the Group were $43,239,372 (2022: $13,853,816), the increase is mainly
due to issue of preference shares resulting in a cash inflow of $46,661,937.
The directors consider key performance indicators to be revenue as detailed above. The directors believe that preparing
the financial statements on the going concern basis is appropriate, this is discussed in the Directors’ Report on
page 5.
Supplier Groups’ Payment Policy
The Groups' standard supplier payment policy is to pay valid invoices within agreed terms.
Principal Risks and Uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties affecting the Group are primarily the economic climate, with higher inflation and
rising interest rates.
Credit risk is the risk of loss in value of financial assets due to the counter parties failing to meet all or part of their
obligations. Credit risk is estimated by the Group to be limited.
Liquidity risk is the risk that the Group does not have sufficient liquid assets to meet its obligations as they fall due.
Liquid funds are only placed with reputable institutions with high credit ratings.
