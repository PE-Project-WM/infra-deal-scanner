For the year ended 31 March 2026
The directors present their annual report and the audited financial statements of National Grid Telecoms Limited
(the "Company") (registered number 02386327) for the year ended 31 March 2026.
Ownership :
The Company is a wholly-owned subsidiary within the National Grid Electricity Distribution plc group (the "Group"
or "NGED Group" or "“NGED"). The primary business of National Grid Electricity Distribution pic and its subsidiaries
is the distribution of electricity in the South West and Midlands regions in England and in South Wales. The NGED
Group is owned by National Grid plc. National Grid plc is one of the world's largest investor-owned energy utilities,
and operates in the UK and the US.
National Grid Telecoms Limited is a wholly-owned subsidiary of National Grid Electricity Distribution Network
Holdings Limited which is owned by National Grid Ple.
Principal activity and business review : ;
The principal activity of the Company during the period under review continued to be the provision of:

+ — dark optical fibre capacity:

+ — carrier wholesale bandwidth;

. + leasing of telecommunications sites and facilities; and

* — regional non-switched lease lines.
These services provide links between cable landing stations, data centres and major towns and cities in South
Wales, the South West and Midlands areas of England to the rest of the United Kingdom, interconnecting with
other carrier networks in Bristol, Cardiff and London. The network includes over eighty points of presence including
Bristol, Bath, Exeter, Plymouth, Lands End, Bude, Cardiff, Newport, Swansea, Birmingham, Nottingham, Leicester,
Coventry, Worcester and Gloucester.
The Company's products are aimed at national and international telecommunications operators including large
broadband media providers. Customers include the corporate, business, educational, public sector and utility
sectors of the market. The Company has also seen an increase in demand for its fibre, carrier bandwidth and site
sharing facilities from the emerging alternative network provider sector. Interest from this sector remains strongest
in the South West and South Wales but the trend for increased interest from Midlands based alternative network
providers, particularly in the East Midlands, continues. :
The Company also operates and maintains the telecommunications network for NGED and as such a proportion of
turnover is attributable to work for other companies within the Group. This wil! continue to be the case for the
foreseeable future.

* The Company operates as a Public Electronic Communications Network provider under the authorisation scheme
for ‘electronic communications networks and services'. Regulation is implemented and administered by the Office
of Communications ("OFCOM") who have set out requirements for network providers. This includes provision of a
fund for liabilities and for interconnection registration.

Through the implementation and continuous review of quality and security management systems, the Company is
accredited and certified to BS/EN 1S09001:2015, 1S027001:2022. The Company is actively working towards being
compliant with the Telecoms Security Requirements ("TSR"), introduced by the UK Government late in 2023 and
more detailed guidelines issued throughout 2024. National Grid Telecoms Limited is focused on complying with the
guidelines set out for an organisation to achieve Tier 3 TSR compliance status.

Principal risks and uncertainties
Competitive Risks
For its external revenue, the Company faces competitive risks from other operators providing similar services. In
order to minimise risk, the Company operates only in the wholesale telecommunications market, has a reliable
network covering key sites in the South West and Midlands areas of England and South Wales, and has a strong
regional presence. The Company offers a high quality service at competitive prices.
The Company faces the risk of non-compliance with regulations and best practices within the industry resulting in
* loss of business and reputation. To mitigate this, the senior management is actively involved in all compliance and
best practice related matters and the Board maintains direct oversight to ensure that the Company is compliant
with all relevant industry standards. The Company is committed to the ISO 9001 standard (quality management).
The Company is also compliant with the OHSAS 18001 standard (health and safety), and the ISO 27001 standard
, {information security management).
Reliance on Suppliers
There is a risk of reliance on suppliers for operating and maintaining its activities. The Company operates mainly
over networks and sites that are its own or belong to fellow subsidiaries, and relies to a small extent on the BT
Group and other connectivity providers, who themselves are regulated. Where third party fibres are used, there is
a supply arrangement with that party, mitigating material risk.

Customers
There is a risk of the loss of key external customers resulting in loss of revenue. The Company's focus remains
exclusively in the wholesale market where there is less exposure to customer ‘churn’. The Company continues to
grow market share with new and existing ‘alt-net' providers within the South West of England. It has also witnessed
noticeable increased interest from these businesses for new services in South Wales and the East coast of
England as they expand their networks to try and reach communities and locations not well served by the main
providers. We continue to support mobile network operators ("MNOs") in the expansion and roll out of their 5G
networks.
Whilst business in this sector remains strong, the impact of Code rights granted to MNOs by the Electronic
Communications Code are forecast to have a negative impact on some segments of the rental returns achievable
for the services we provide to this sector. National Grid Telecoms Limited has provided critical connectivity services
to the organisation providing key communication channels for the blue light services for a number of years. They
had expected this requirement to cease with the anticipated launch of the ESN ("Emergency Services Network”)
but now expect to retain this business for the foreseeable future due to the delayed roll out of ESN.
The Company aims to mitigate the risk of customer churn by providing quality services.

Following Appropriate Strategies

The Company's main focus remains on developing and extending its strong regional presence in the wholesale
market, providing fibre, managed services and radio connectivity. The Company will continue to expand the reach
of its fibre network within its own geographical footprint in the coming years. Significant fibre expansion is planned
and funded within NGED's area of operations throughout the current Distribution Review period (ED2) running until
March 2028.

Works are continuing to deliver a ‘North of the Severn' diverse fibre route utilising existing underground duct and
overhead pylon to connect the existing South Wales fibre network to the Midlands and South West network
avoiding the Severn Bridge crossing. This new route will offer resilient connectivity in and out of South Wales. We
are also exploring fibre expansion into the the northern reaches of South Wales as well as continued expansion of
additional routes across all regions.

The programme to fibre enable existing hill top radio sites commenced in 2021 to support NGED's transition from a
Distribution Network Operator ("DNO") to a Distribution Systems Operator ("DSO"). This transition remains a key
focus as increasingly smart and active communication solutions are required to meet the demands of a low carbon
and digital future. The Company's wholesale market customers will also benefit from access to this fibre enabled
tadio site portfolio. The Company benefits from access to the electricity network of its fellow NGED companies
across the South West and Midlands areas of England, and South Wales. Compliance with current security
standards, combined with a high quality of service, enables the Company to attract customers operating in both
the public and corporate sectors.

Liquidity and interest rate risk

The Company can face liquidity risk and interest rate risk if external borrowing is needed. This risk is mitigated as :
National Grid Electricity Distribution (South West) plc ("NGED South West"), a fellow subsidiary, provides day to
day banking for NGED, including the Company. NGED South West is a regulated electricity distribution company
which must maintain an investment grade credit rating. NGED affiliates pay or receive interest to/from the
Company based on the Bank of England base rate.

Capital investment and future developments

The Company's fibre optic network is predominantly attached to the high voltage electricity overhead line network
of NGED plc's assets and extends some 3,150km across the South West peninsula of England, the East and West
Midlands and the Southern area of Wales.

The strategy of extending and densifying the network through a rolling programme to add new and upgrade
additional points of presence throughout the four DNO regions using both third party fibre and fibre attached to
NGED's network continues. The Company is also utilising BT Openreach's Physical Infrastructure Access ("PIA")
selectively as a means of extending our fibre reach to meet network expansion objectives.

The Company will continue to improve its current range of services, expand its existing operations and invest in its
network in order to meet the demands of the telecommunications market through existing and new channel
partners, both within and outside its geographical area. The Company is focused on increasing the reach and
density of the existing fibre network within region, offering greater opportunity to provide current and potentially
new services to new and existing customers over a greater geographical area.

The Company's upgrade of it's South West Dense Wavelength-division multiplexing ("DWDM") network allowed it
to offer multiple 100Gb services. The upgrade went live early in 2023 and enables the Company to meet the
continued demand for greater bandwidth capacity at competitive pricing. This has been well received with interest
from both new and longstanding customers.

Section 172 Statement
The directors of all UK companies must act in accordance with their duties under the Companies Act 2006. This
includes a fundamental duty to promote the success of the Company for the benefit of its members as a whole.
This duty has been central to the decision-making process of the directors of all the Companies within National
Grid. The Board has well-established policies defining the Board's duties and responsibilities including those under
section 172.
The Board ensures that the directors have acted both individually and collectively in a way that they consider, in
good faith, would be most likely to promote the success of the Company for the benefit of its members as a whole
with regard to all its stakeholders and to the matters set out in paragraphs (a) to (f) of section 172.
(a) The likely consequence of any decision in the long term
’ The Board is focused on promoting the success of the Company by supporting the DNOs within the NGED Group, ,
as well as by delivering customer focused performance to external customers in a manner that is environmentally
sustainable, provides long term stability and meets the needs of its key stakeholders.
Flexible and effective networking solutions is the key focus for the Board. We provide data technology in a variety
of ways to deliver truly connected bespoke communications solutions that meet the needs of our customers. The
Board places great emphasis on ensuring that the Company is working with the most advanced technology and
infrastructure and is adapting and innovating to ensure that the needs of all its customers are met effectively. One
key area that the directors consider critical to the strategic growth of the Company is the need to meet the growing
demand for greater capacity, and therefore expansion of our network has been a key focus for the directors. The
extensive reach of our network will allow businesses and our communities to access low cost, high performance
internet with extremely high resilience. :
The directors devolve day-to-day management and decision-making to the senior management team. The senior
management has regular contact and dialogue with the directors of the Company and regular financial and
tegulatory meetings are conducted to provide updates on any key accounting, business and legal issues.
Processes are in place to ensure that the Company Board receives all relevant information to enable it to make
well-judged decisions in support of the Company’s long-term stability and to ensure adequate monitoring of the
Company's strategic priorities.
Policies, applicable to all the companies operating within the NGED Group, are in place defining the powers of
delegation by the Board, the matters reserved for the Board and the areas of responsibilities and accountability of
the directors. Policies have been established that define the framework within which the NGED Group expects
managers and employees to operate. These policies represent one of the means through which decisions on
stakeholder interests within the NGED Group are enacted.

Goodwill acquired through business combinations has been allocated to one cash-generating unit (CGU). This
represents the lower level within the Company at which goodwill is monitored for internal management purposes.
In assessing whether goodwill has been impaired, the carrying amount of the CGU (including goodwill) is
compared with the recoverable amount of the CGU. The recoverable amount is the higher of fair value less costs
to sell and value in use. In the absence of any information about the fair value of a CGU, the recoverable amount
is deemed to be the value in use.
The recoverable amount of the Company's CGU has been determined based on a value in use calculation using
cash flow projections based on financial budgets reviewed by management covering a five-year period.
The key assumptions for the value in use calculation are those regarding the discount rate, expected cash flows
arising from revenues, direct costs, and capital expenditure during the period, and the growth rate used to
extrapolate cash flows beyond the budget period.
The discount rate applied to cash flow projections is 12.5% (2025: 12.0%).
At 31 March 2026, the Company's recoverable amount exceeded its carrying amount by £15.71m (2025: £5.28m).

16. Pension commitments
Defined benefit scheme
The Company participates in one defined benefit pension scheme, a section of the ESPS, to which most
employees in the Group in the South West of England and South Wales belong, and which has approximately
8,224 members. The ESPS is a group defined benefit pension plan that shares risks between entities under
common control. The ESPS is closed to new members.
There is no contractual agreement or stated policy for charging the net defined benefit cost and, therefore, NGED
(South West) and NGED (South Wales), fellow subsidiaries of the Company that are jointly responsible for the
scheme, recognise the whole of the scheme surplus or deficit in their financial statements, allocated in accordance
with pensionable salaries. In accordance with IAS 19 (Revised 2011), the Company recognises a cost equal to its
contribution payable for the period, which in the year ended 31 March 2026 was £950,000 (2025: £1,080,000).
Further details of the Group defined benefit scheme are disclosed in the consolidated financial statements of
National Grid Electricity pic which are available as disclosed in note 20 below.
Defined contribution plan
The Company also participates in a defined contribution scheme, the National Grid UK Retirement Plan
("NGUKRP"). The assets of the scheme are held separately from those of the Company in funds under the control
of trustees. The total cost charged to income of £601,000 (2025: £450,000) represents contributions payable to
this scheme by the Company at rates specified in the rules of the plan.
