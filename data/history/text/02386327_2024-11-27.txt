Strategic report
For the year ended 31 March 2024
The directors present their strategic report on National Grid Telecoms Limited (the "Company") (registered number
02386327) for the year ended 31 March 2024.
Ownership
The Company is a wholly-owned subsidiary within the National Grid Electricity Distribution ple group (the "Group"
or "NGED Group" or "NGED"). The primary business of National Grid Electricity Distribution ple and its
subsidiaries is the distribution of electricity in the South West and Midlands regions in England and in South Wales.
The NGED Group was acquired by National Grid in June 2021. National Grid ple is one of the world's largest
investor-owned energy utilities, and operates in the UK and the US. As part of the integration of the Company and
other NGED Group subsidiaries into the National Grid Group, on 20 September 2022 the name of the Company was
changed from WPD Telecoms Limited to National Grid Telecoms Limited.
Review of the business
The principal activities of the Company during the period under review continued to be the provision of:

+ dark optical fibre capacity;

* carrier wholesale bandwidth;

* leasing of telecommunication sites and facilities; and

* regional non-switched leased lines.
These services provide links between cable landing stations, data centres and major towns and cities in South Wales, ‘
the South West and Midlands areas of England to the rest of the United Kingdom, interconnecting with other carrier
networks in Bristol, Cardiff and London. The network includes over eighty points of presence including Bristol, Bath,
Exeter, Plymouth, Lands End, Bude, Cardiff, Newport, Swansea, Birmingham, Nottingham, Leicester, Coventry,
Worcester and Gloucester. .
The Company's products are aimed at national and international telecommunications operators including large
broadband media providers. Customers include the corporate, business, educational, public sector and utility sectors
of the market. The Company has also seen an increase in demand for its fibre, carrier bandwidth and site sharing
facilities from the emerging alternative network provider sector. Interest from this sector remains strongest in the
South West and South Wales but the trend for increased interest from Midlands based alternative network providers,
particularly in the East Midlands, continues.
The Company also operates and maintains the telecommunications network for NGED and as such a proportion of
turnover is attributable to work for other companies within the Group. This will continue to be the case for the
foreseeable future.
The Company operates as a Public Electronic Communications Network provider under the authorisation scheme for
‘electronic communications networks and services’. Regulation is implemented and administered by the Office of
Communications ("OFCOM") who have set out requirements for network providers. This includes provision of a fund
for liabilities and for interconnection registration.
Through the implementation and continuous review of quality and security management systems, the Company is
accredited and certified to BS/EN ISO9001:2015, IS027001:2022. The Company is actively working towards being ‘
compliant with the Telecoms Security Requirements ("TSR"), introduced by the UK Government late in 2023 and
more detailed guidelines issued throughout 2024. National Grid Telecoms Limited is focused on complying with the
guidelines set out for an organisation to achieve Tier 3 TSR compliance status.
During the year During the year, the Company allotted additional shares of 1,317,000 of £1 each. The amount paid by
shareholders for the additional share capital was £1.58 each, resulting in a share premium of £763,860 as at 31 March
2024. The purpose of issuance of the additional share capital was to ensure availability of funds for further
development activities of the Company. .

1 Principal risks and uncertainties
Competitive Risks
For its external revenue, the Company faces competitive risks from other operators providing similar services. In
order to minimise risk, the Company operates only in the wholesale telecommunications market, has a reliable
network covering key sites in the South West and Midlands areas of England and South Wales, and has a strong
regional presence. The Company offers a high quality service at competitive prices.
The Company faces the risk of non-compliance with regulations and best practices within the industry resulting in
loss of business and reputation. To mitigate this, the senior management is actively involved in all compliance and
best practice related matters and the Board maintains direct oversight to ensure that the Company is compliant with
all relevant industry standards. The Company is committed to the ISO 9001 standard (quality management), The
Company is also compliant with the OHSAS 18001 standard (health and safety), and the ISO 27001 standard
(information security management).
Reliance on Suppliers
There is a risk of reliance on suppliers for operating and maintaining its activities. The Company operates mainly
over networks and sites that are its own or belong to fellow subsidiaries, and relies to a small extent on BT Group and
other connectivity providers, who themselves are regulated. Where third party fibres are used, there is a supply
arrangement with that party, mitigating material risk.

Customers
There is a risk of the loss of key external customers resulting in loss of revenue. The Company's focus remains
exclusively in the wholesale market where there is less exposure to customer ‘churn’. The Company continues to grow
market share with new and existing ‘alt-net' providers within the South West of England. It has also witnessed
noticeable increased interest from these businesses for new services in South Wales and the East coast of England as
they expand their networks to try and reach communities and locations not well served by the main providers. We
continue to support mobile network operators ("MNOs") in the expansion and roll out of their SG networks, Whilst
business in this sector remains strong the impact of Code rights granted to MNOs by the Electronic Communications
Code are forecast to have a negative impact on some segments of the rental returns achievable for the services we
provide to this sector. National Grid Telecoms Limited has provided critical connectivity services to the organisation
providing key communication channels for the blue light services for a number of years. They had expected this
requirement to cease with the anticipated launch of the ESN ("Emergency Services Network") but now expect to
retain this business for the foreseeable future due to the delayed roll out of ESN.
The Company aims to mitigate the risk of customer churn by providing quality of services.

Following Appropriate Strategies
The Company's main focus remains 6n developing and extending its strong regional presence in the wholesale
market, providing fibre, managed services and radio connectivity. The Company will continue to expand the reach of
its fibre network within its own geographical footprint in the coming years. Significant fibre expansion is planned and
funded within NGED's area of operations throughout the current Distribution Review period (ED2) running until
March 2028.
Works are progressing on a new and significant ‘North of the Severn’ diverse fibre route utilising existing
underground duct and overhead pylon to connect the existing South Wales fibre network to the Midlands and South
West network avoiding the Severn Bridge crossing. This new route will offer resilient connectivity in and out of
South Wales. This was expected to be operational and available to new customers from late 2024. COVID pandemic
related delays mean that the 'go live’ date is now estimated as QI/Q2 2025. We are also exploring fibre expansion
into the northern reaches of South Wales as well as continued expansion of additional routes into South Devon and
Cornwall. ;
The programme to fibre enable existing hill top radio sites in 2021 was delayed as a result of the COVID pandemic
but is now progressing. This is required to support NGED's transition from a Distribution Network Operator ("DNO")
to a Distribution Systems Operator ("DSO"). This transition remains a key focus as increasingly smart and active
communication solutions are required to meet the demands of a low carbon and digital future. The Company's
wholesale market customers will also benefit from access to this fibre enabled radio site portfolio. The Company has
the benefit of being able to use the electricity network of its fellow NGED companies in the South West and
Midlands areas of England, and South Wales. Compliance to current security standards and a high quality of service
enables the Company to attract customers who operate in the public and corporate sectors.

Principal risks and uncertainties (continued)
Liquidity and interest rate risk :
The Company poses a liquidity risk and interest rate risk if external borrowing is needed. This risk is mitigated as
National Grid Electricity Distribution (South West) plc ("NGED South West"), a fellow subsidiary, provides day to
day banking for NGED, including the Company. NGED South West is a regulated electricity distribution company
which must maintain an investment grade credit rating. NGED affiliates pay or receive interest to/from the Company
based on the Bank of England base rate.
Capital investment and future developments
The Company's fibre optic network is predominantly attached to the high voltage electricity overhead line network of
NGED plc's assets and extends some 2,700km across the South West peninsula of England, the East and West
Midlands and the Southern area of Wales.
The strategy of extending and densifying the network through a rolling programme to add new and upgrade additional
points of presence throughout the four DNO regions using both third party fibre and fibre attached to NGED's
network continues. The company is also utilising BT Openreach's Physical Infrastructure Access ("PIA") selectively
as a means of extending our fibre reach to meet network expansion objectives. .

: The Company will continue to improve its current range of services, expand its existing operations and invest in its
network in order to meet the demands of the telecommunications market through existing and new channel partners,
both within and outside its geographical area. The Company is focused on increasing the reach and density of the
existing fibre network within region, offering greater opportunity to provide current and potentially new services to
new and existing customers over a greater geographical area.

The Company's upgrade of it's South West Dense Wavelength-division multiplexing ("DWDM") network allowed it
to offer multiple 100Gb services later than expected, due to the impact of the COVID pandemic and lengthy delays on
equipment delivery from a key supplier. The upgrade went live early in 2023 and enables the Company to meet the
continued demand for greater bandwidth capacity at competitive pricing. This has been well received with interest
from both new and longstanding customers. .

discount rate applied is the rate implicit in the lease or if that is not available, then the incremental rate of borrowing
for a similar term and similar security. The lease term takes account of exercising any extension options that are at the
Company's option if the Company is reasonably certain to exercise the option and any lease termination options
unless the Company is reasonably certain not to exercise the option. Each lease payment is allocated between the
liability and finance cost. The finance cost is charged to the profit and loss account over the lease period using the
effective interest rate method.
