Introduction
The Directors present their report for the year 31 December 2024.
Purpose & Principal activity
Our aim is to remove complexity from business communication—making it simple, secure, and empowering for every organisation.
Our mission is to deliver powerful communication platforms that blend digital and physical messaging, enabling businesses to connect efficiently and securely
Our principal activity is providing Customer Communications Management (CCM) SAAS technology that digitally transforms customers’ communication workflows by providing document composition, secure multi-channel delivery (including digital mail, email, SMS, portal), storage, and retrieval through an easy-to-use web interface. 
Business review
Our vision is to lead the way in digital messaging transforming business communication. 
2024 was a pivotal year
 in this journey as we evolved from a Hybrid Mail provider to a comprehensive SaaS-based Customer Communications Management (CCM) solution.
Key Achievements of 2024:
• Processed record volumes of communications.
• Achieved 
Great Place to Work accreditation
 in the Technology category.
• Fully deployed 
SaaS-based communication technology
 within Microsoft Azure Cloud.
Turnover this year increased by 13.6% to £32.3m, mostly from Hybrid Mail revenues with a good swing of customers beginning to use our Digital Mail platform. We, therefore, expect Digital/SAAS revenues to grow through 2025 as clients continue to migrate.
While interest in our Digital Mail solution has been strong, we recognise that some customers require additional onboarding support. We have launched enhanced migration assistance to address this, including dedicated migration technicians and client and employee training programs.
This year, capex spend related to completing our SAAS communication technology totalled £834k with most customers now migrated to this platform, allowing clients to migrate to our full range of encrypted Digital communication solutions.
The rollout of new technologies assisted in developing new revenues across our client base, particularly with channel partners where our proprietary software provides a key element of their own software proposition, particularly in highly regulated markets.
Strategic Roadmap
Our forward-looking roadmap focuses on continued SAAS communication technology developments that enhance our digital messaging capabilities. These developments will further integrate secure, multi-channel delivery and document composition directly into clients' communications workflows.
