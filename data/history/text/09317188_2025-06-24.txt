Introduction
The directors present the Strategic Report for Damoco Bidco Limited (the “Company”) for the year ended 31
January 2024.
Principal activity :
The principal activity of the Company: is that of an intermediate holding company and it provides financing and
management services within the Damovo Group. The principal activity of the wider Damovo Group is to provide
Information and Communications Technology (“ICT”) solutions and services for enterprises in both the private
and public sectors.
Atlanta Topco Limited (“ATL”) is the head of the smallest and largest group for which consolidated financial
statements are prepared and are publicly available. Atlanta Topco Limited and its wholly owned subsidiaries are
more commonly known as, the Damovo Group (“the “Group”).
No significant change in the Company’s principal activity is expected.
Business review
The financial result for the year is a loss of €4,118,284 (2023: loss of €3,018,318) which is in line with Board
expectations.
As at 31 January 2024, the Company had a total net assets position of €43,158,630 (2023: €(31,366,831) of net
liabilities). However, as a significant part of total liabilities represents amounts owing to fellow group undertakings,
the demand for repayment of which is wholly within the control of the Group, the Board is satisfied that this does
not present any liquidity issues for the Company.
On 22 December 2023, the structure of the Damovo Group was revised such that Lares LLC, Lares Holdings,
LLC (together referred a “Lares”) and Damovo Technology Services NV (“DTS”) were transferred from Damovo
Group Trust to ATL Group and became wholly owned subsidiaries of Damoco Bidco Limited. This reorganisation

’ was carried out pursuant to the terms of the senior facilities agreement dated 8 September 2022 with Triton and
Kartesia (“SFA”), for which the completion of the reorganisation was a condition subsequent to the Group
re-financing. Please refer to Note 20 for details.
Principal risks and uncertainties
From the perspective of the Company, the principal risks and uncertainties are the same as the principal risks of
the Group and are not managed separately. Accordingly, the principal risks and uncertainties of the Damovo
Group, which include those of the Company, are discussed in the Atlanta Topco Limited consolidated group
financial statements which do not form part of this report.
The key risk of the Company is the impact any deterioration in financial performance of group undertakings would
have on recoverability of balances due from those group .undertakings and in the value of its investments in
subsidiary undertakings. Due to the current financial strength of its subsidiary undertakings, the Director considers
that the actual risk the Company is exposed to is minimal.
Key performance indicators (KPIs) _
Given the straightforward nature of the Company’s business, being that of an intermediate holding company, the
Company’s directors re of the opinion that an analysis using KPIs is not necessary for an understanding of the
development, performance or position of the Company’s business. Costs of the business are tracked monthly
against an annual budget approved by the directors. :

Going concern (continued)
In September 2022, the Group completed the re-financing of ARES loan of €50,000K with Triton & Kartesia for
€55,000K Senior Facilities Agreement (“SFA”) and aimed to improve the current liability position of the Group.
As part of re-financing, a settlement agreement was also reached with Damoco Bermuda (owners of Damovo
Group before GGHI) and GGHI to settle the legal dispute related to contingent consideration following their sale
of the Damovo in 2018. As part of the settlement agreement, total aggregate deferred consideration and costs of
€14,960K was paid to Damoco Bermuda on completion of the re-financing, with a further amount of €7,034K
comprising principal plus accrued interest and annual interest, at an agreed rate payable, over three years in
quarterly instalments. It was also agreed that GGHI would fund these amounts on behalf of the Damovo Group
pursuant to an equity commitment letter in which it undertook to pay all of the deferred consideration under the
settlement agreement.
However, in 2024 and till date of the approval of accounts, GGHI defaulted on its obligations under the equity
commitment letter. This payment default represents a breach of the settlement agreement and the equity
commitment letter and is a cross-default under the SFA with the new lenders. The payment default triggers certain
enforcement rights for both the previous owners and the current lenders under the relevant contractual
arrangements and enforcement of their respective underlying security in relation to the relevant entities in the
Group. However, at the date of approval of the financial statements, the current lenders have not yet shown any
intent in any enforcement action in respect of the Payment Default to enable all parties to explore options to
address the defaults. The current lenders have a strong and co-operative working relationship with the Damovo
Group and are supportive of the business. The Damovo Board, executive management and the operational finance
teams within the business have regular and extensive engagement with the lenders, who have confirmed that at the
current time, the focus is on developing and executing a viable plan to address the payment default with all relevant
stakeholders. The Damovo Board note that the overall quantum of funding required to resolve the payment default
is not material relative to the Group’s existing committed funding amounts from the lenders. .
During the year and at the date of approval of accounts, the Group was in breach of the net leverage and cash flow
cover covenants in the SFA. The Lenders have reserved their rights in respect of the breach and the issue is being
addressed in the context of the ongoing discussions to resolve the SFA cross-default. The Group maintains

. constructive relationships with its lenders, which resulted on the lenders consenting to full default interests’
capitalization in December 2023, and each quarter since June 2024. The financial statements do not include any
adjustments should the continued support of the lenders not be forthcoming, or a resolution of the payment
default is not found.
The Board of Directors have prepared an overall group strategy to create sustainable positive trading results within
the Group and the Company. Currently, the Group is in an intensive investment and capital expansion phase and
has incurred losses during the current and previous years, which has resulted in accumulated losses as at the
reporting date. However, the Group has been able to generate positive cash flows from its operation for the year
under review.
Given the industry dynamics that the Group operates in and the expectation of being in an investment and
expansion phase for some time, the directors have prepared forecasts of the Group’s performance for the next five ~
years in line with their expectations and are satisfied that the Group’s progress to date is in line with their strategy.
Expected increases in adjusted EBITDA going forward as per management's latest planning will lead to recovery
of accumulated losses before tax over the coming 5 years. :

Opinion

We have audited the financial statements of Damoco Bidco Limited, which comprise the Statement of

comprehensive income, the Balance sheet, the Statement of changes in equity for the year ended 31 January 2024,

and the related notes to the financial statements, including a summary of material accounting policy information.

The financial reporting framework that has been applied in the preparation is applicable law and United Kingdom

Accounting Standards, including Financial Reporting Standard 101 ‘Reduced Disclosure Framework’.

In our opinion, Damoco Bidco Limited's financial statements:

. give a true and fair view in accordance with United Kingdom Generally Accepted Accounting Practice of the
assets, liabilities and financial position of the Company as at 31 January 2024 and of its financial
performance for the year then ended; and

e have been prepared in accordance with the requirements of the Companies Act 2006.

Basis for opinion

We conducted our audit in accordance with International Standards on Auditing (UK) (ISAs (UN)’) and applicable

law. Our responsibilities under those standards are further described in the ‘Responsibilities of the auditor for the

audit of the financial statements’ section of our report. We are independent of the Company in accordance with the
ethical requirements that are relevant to our audit of the financial statements in the United Kingdom, namely the

FRC's Ethical Standard and the ethical pronouncements established by Chartered Accountants Ireland, applied as

determined to be appropriate in the circumstances of the entity. We have fulfilled our other ethical responsibilities

in accordance with these requirements. We believe that the audit evidence we have obtained is sufficient and
appropriate to provide a basis for our opinion.

Material uncertainty related to going concern

In forming our opinion, which is not modified, we draw attention to disclosures made in the Directors’ report and

Note 2.3 in the financial statements concerning the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern. The

Company is an intermediate holding company and therefore is reliant on the Atlanta Topco Limited Group (Group

or Damovo Group) to be a going concern to be able to support it. Therefore the going concern review focuses on

the forecasts for the Group and their ability to continue as a going concern. The events and conditions, along with
other matters as set forth in Note 2.3, indicate that a material uncertainty exists that may cast significant doubt on
: the Group and the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern. The financial statements have been prepared
on a going concern basis, which assumes that the Group and the Company will continue in operational existence
for the foreseeable future. The validity of this assumption as stated in Note 2.3 depends on the outcome of the
actions taken by the directors to return the Group to profitability and the continued support of the Group's
stakeholders and lenders. The financial statements do not include any adjustments relating to the recoverability and
classification of recorded asset amounts and classifications of liabilities that might be necessary should the

Company be unable to continue in existence.

: In auditing the financial statements, we have concluded that the directors’ use of going concern basis of accounting
in the preparation of the financial statements is appropriate.

Our responsibilities and the responsibilities of the directors with respect to going concern are described in the

relevant sections of this report.

2.9 Taxation
Tax expense represents the sum of the tax currently payable and deferred tax.
The current tax is based on taxable profit for the year. Taxable profit differs from net profit as reported
in the Statement of comprehensive income because it excludes items of income or expense that are
taxable or deductible in other years and it further excludes items that are never taxable or deductible. .
The Company’s liability for current tax is calculated by using tax rates that have been enacted or
substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.
Deferred tax is the tax expected to be payable or recoverable on differences between the carrying
amount of assets and liabilities in the financial statements and the corresponding tax bases used in the
computation of taxable profit and is accounted for using the balance sheet liability method. Deferred
tax liabilities are recognised for all taxable temporary differences and deferred tax assets are recognised
to the extent that it is probable that taxable profits will be available against which deductible temporary
differences can be utilised. Such assets and liabilities are not recognised if the temporary difference
arises from the initial recognition of goodwill or from the initial recognition (other than in a business
combination) of other assets and liabilities in a transaction which affects neither the tax profit nor the
accounting profit.
Deferred tax liabilities are recognised for taxable temporary differences arising on investments in
subsidiaries and associates, and interests in jointly controlled entities, except where the Company is able
to control the reversal of the temporary difference and it is probable that the temporary difference will
not reverse in the foreseeable future. Deferred tax is calculated at the tax rates (and tax laws) that have
been enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date. Deferred tax is charged or credited in
the Statement of comprehensive income, except when it relates to items credited or charged directly to
equity, in which case the deferred tax is also dealt with in equity.
