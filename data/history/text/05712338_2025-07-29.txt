Introduction
The directors present their strategic report for Ivy Network Technology U.K. Limited (‘the Company’) for the year
ended 31 December 2024. oe : :
Business review :
The principal activity of the Company is the provision of installation, maintenance and repair services of robotics
equipment and aftermarket repair and logistics services to customers in the information technology sector.
The statement of comprehensive income is set out on page 10.
The Company's turnover for the year is USD$17,412,943 (2023: USD$8,840,814), generating operating profit of
USD$457,054 (2023: USD$647,855 loss). Te . _
Strategy and business model .
The Company's business objective is to provide high quality robotics installation and maintenance services and
end to end repair and logistics which will generate a reasonable return for the Company's shareholders. This is -
achieved through carefully selecting commercial opportunities, diversifying the customer base and configuring

_° “the company’s internal resources and facilities to realise those opportunities in a value accretive manner.” 7
The Company devoted substantial resources to the diversification of its customer base in the financial year
ended 31 December 2024 and expects to see return on this investment in financial year ended 31 December
2025.
Business environment .
The business environment during the period continued to be influenced by global supply chain challenges.
Ongoing geopolitical uncertainties and logistical disruptions have exerted pressure on the availability and cost of
key materials and components. These external factors have impacted lead times and procurement planning,
requiring the business to remain agile in managing operations and supplier relationships.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The Directors of the Company and the execution of its strategy are subject to a number of risks. These risks are
. controlled on a group wide basis. Where relevant, the impact of these risks on the Company is set out below.
Business risk: Management have established formal processes for identifying, evaluating, and managing
: business risk exposures with ongoing reviews of progress against strategic objectives. The business risks
: reviewed include: ;
: * External business risks, including regulator and compliance obligations;
+ Operational risks arising from supplier dependency, fire damage, material damage, etc.;
So. ¢ Legal risks including risks arising from factory leases and supplier contracts; and :
: : * Information risks including the integrity of IT systems and the security of information. . .
Competition risk: The Company's continued ability to trade is predicated on the Company being able to deliver a
superior offering compared to competitors in terms of service price, quality, reliability, and supply among other
factors. An increase in the competitive intensity in the Company's key markets could adversely impact the
Company's trading performance. In mitigation, the Company's commercial management team collate and review
market intelligence to obtain insight into competitor activity so that the Company can respond in a timely and well
considered manner.
Service quality risk: Service quality is of paramount importance. The Company's service processes are subject to
Lo rigorous quality control checks leveraging both human and technical resources to streamline the service
management process. Bespoke information technology processes have been developed, refined, and
implemented to reduce this risk.
Liquidity and cash flow risk: The Company manages its liquidity and cash flow within appropriate limits.
Significant emphasis is placed on frequently updated cashflow forecast to provide early indication of any possible
liquidity issues.
