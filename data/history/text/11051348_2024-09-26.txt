Strategic report .
Introduction
The directors present the Company Strategic Report for Toob Limited for the period ended 31 December 2023.
The Company is currently investing in a full fibre broadband rollout to 300,000 premises and the Company’s strategy
is to provide full fibre availability to one million homes and small businesses.
2023 saw the Company expand its own network to the east and north of the hub of Southampton to include Gosport,
Fareham and Eastleigh and extend its footprint in the north of the County in and around the towns of Camberley,
Farnborough and Aldershot, to allow it to offer services to Fleet, Mytchett and Woking. The Company also
successfully launched its full fibre broadband services under its ISP agreement with CityFibre into towns in the region
served by the CityFibre network.
The Company has achieved notable success in commercialising the network investment it has made and has delivered
strong growth in customer connections both on its own network and that of its wholesale partner CityFibre.
To support this continued network and customer expansion the Company secured additional funding of up to £303M
from funds managed by Ares Management's Infrastructure Debt Strategy as disclosed in note 13 as well as further
equity from Funds managed by the Amber Infrastructure Group supplementing its initial investment.
During 2024 the Company will largely complete the current phase of expansion of its own network to 300,000
premises and will continue with its focus to market its services to customers able to connect to its own network and
also to those customers able to connect to the CityFibre network in the areas that the Company chooses to address.
Principal Activities :
Toob Limited designs, builds, owns, sells and operates its own full fibre network and resells a third-party full fibre
network for the benefit of consumers and as appropriate, small businesses.
Business Review
Toob Limited has a clear strategy and business plan to scale the Company as a full fibre network provider.
It has a very experienced and seasoned management team who have diligently managed the deployment of the Toob
Limited network in its chosen geographies and who continue to successfully commercialise the network investment
by acquiring and retaining connected customers.
The Company is supported by a mixture of equity and debt of up to £395M to deliver their plan to create a sustainable
business focusing on the south of England and to opportunistically take advantage of market consolidation as
opportunities arise.
In its fifth full year of operation 2023 has seen the Company densify and extend its fibre deployment in current and ‘
newly announced towns in the south of England and continue to deliver its rollout in those towns where it is already
established, acquiring connected customers as the network goes live in each geography. The Company has sought to
leverage its successful brand in the region through its ISP agreement with CityFibre, increasing its customer base to
deliver additional business synergies and incremental margin.
The financial statements reflect the deployment of capital in rolling out the planned network together with the
investént in salés, miarketing, and customer service expénsés to support the growing customer base. These cash
outflows are now seeing more material offset from revenues from the growth in connected customers.
The Company is focused on achieving cash breakeven under its existing committed investment and will, as required,
seek to release further investment to continue to grow in additional phases to deliver its strategy.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The directors identify, assess and manage principal risks and uncertainties that might directly affect the Company’s
strategy aiid opérations. Risks generally arise from either éxterial factors driven by the market in which the Compaty
operates or from internal factors driven by the operational aspects of the business. :
Market
Changes in government policy can adversely affect the Company. Toob Limited works with industry groups such as
ISPA and INCA to engage with the government and Ofcom on key topics and consultations to ensure that the interests
of Toob Limited are properly and clearly represented.
The Company carefully and diligently reviews all new areas selected for potential build to understand the current and
possible futuro competitive landscape to identify circumstances where its economic prospects might be impacted:
Liquidity
Cashflow is managed by ensuring there are sufficient funds available to support continuing operations and plans for
future growth. Toob Limited maintains a detailed rolling cashflow forecast to ensure there are adequate funds available
to meet commitments as they fall due. The Company has put in place appropriate equity and debt facilities that
underpin its funding requirements. ;
Interest
Recent funding rounds have increased the leverage of Toob Limited which exposes the Company to greater cost risk :
from any increases in interest rates. The Company has put in place an interest rate cap to mitigate the exposure.
Operations
Toob Limited maintains a practice of dual sourcing for its key materials and equipment and ensures that minimum
inventory levels can mitigate potential supply chain risks. Service interruptions are a risk to brand and reputation so
the Company architects its network to be resilient at appropriate levels of consolidation in its core network and its
Points of Presence and responds prompily to minimise the effect of outages on its customers.
Financial Key Performance Indicators
Financial performance is measured against a detailed annual budget and forecast. The Company finalises its
performance metrics on a monthly basis for the senior management and directors to monitor and manage the operations
of the business.
Future Developments ,

agreements that will allow third party channels to resell the Toob Limited network under various retail brands.

Going Concern
Notwithstanding the net liabilities of £100,308k as at 31 December 2023 (2022: £63,039k), a loss for the year then
ended of £37,271k (2022: £27,700k) and operating cash outflows for the year of £13,792k (2022: £13,825k), the
financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis which the directors consider to be appropriate for .
the following reasons.
As at 31 December 2023 the business had £7,086k cash available and as at 31 March 2024 £1,510k.
The directors have prepared cash flow forecasts for a period of 12 months from the date of approval of these financial
statements (‘base case model’) which indicate that, taking account of reasonably possible downsides, the Company
wili have sufficient funds, through the availability of debt funding from funds managed by Ares Management’s
Infrastructure Debt Strategy entered into on 16 May 2023 as disclosed in note 13 that sets out the repayment terms on
the loan and further equity from Funds managed by the Ambor Infrastructure Group to supplement its initial
investment:
The debt facility is subject to five covenant requirements; total net debt to equity ratio, net debt per premise passed,
net debt per premise connected, total premises passed and average revenuc per customer. Cashflow forecasts under
the base case model produce debt and revenues to satisfy the covenants for at least the next 12 months.
Furthermore, the directors have carefully reviewed the prospects of the Company and its future cash flows for potential
adverse economic impacts. The directors have prepared a severe but plausible downside model (‘downside model’)
iising the’ following assumptions: no new customer ‘sign up, suspension of network infrastructure build “and
maintenance of services for existing customer base only.
This downside model forecasts that the cash available to the business from its debt facility will also satisfy the covenant .
requirements for at least the next 12 months.
Furthermore, the Company has not included the following actions which are in the contro} of the directors that could
be taken to further reduce costs or cash outflows; additional cost-saving measures such as reduction in capital
expenditure and payroll costs.
Consequently, the directors are confident that the Company will have sufficient funds to continuc to mect its liabilities
as they fall due for at least 12 months from the date of approval of the financial statements and therefore have prepared
the financial statements on a going concern basis. Accordingly, these financial statements do not include any
adjustments to the carrying amount or classification of assets and liabilitics that would result if the Company was
unable to continue as a going concern. ~

19 Taxation
The tax expense represents the sum of the tax currently payable and any deferred tax.
The current tax charge is based on the taxable profit for the year. Taxable profit differs from net profit as reported in
the Statement of Financial Position because it excludes items of income or expense that are taxable or deductible in
other years and it further excludes items that arc never taxable or deductible. The Company’s liability for current tax
is calculated using tax rates that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the year end date.
Deferred tax is the tax expected to be payable or recoverable on differences between the carrying amounts of assets
and liabilities in tho financial statements and the corresponding tax bases used in the computation of taxable profit
and is accounted for using the liability method. Deferred tax liabilities are generally recognised for all taxable
temporary differences and deferred tax assets are recognised to the extent that it is probable that taxable profits will
be available against which deductible temporary differences can be utilised. Such assets and liabilitics arc not
recognised if the temporary difference arises from goodwill or from the initial recognition (other than in a busincss
combination) of other assets and liabilitics in a transaction that affects ncither the taxable profit nor the accounting
profit.
Deferred tax liabilities are recognised for taxable temporary differences arising on investments in subsidiaries, except
where the Company is able to control the reversal of the temporary difference and it is probable that the temporary
difference will not reverse in the foreseeable future.
Deferred tax is calculated at the tax rates that are expected to apply in the period when the liability is settled, or the
asset realised. Deferred tax is charged or credited to the Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income,
except when it relates to items charged or credited directly to other comprchensive income, in which casc the deferred
tax is algo dealt with in other comprehensive income:
Deferred tax assets and liabilities are offset when there is a legally enforceable right to set off current tax assets against
current tax liabilities and when they relate to income taxes levied by the same taxation authority and the Company
intends to settle on a net basis.
