STRATEGIC REPORT FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2023
Pursuant to the Companies Act 2006 (Strategic Report and Directors' Report) Regulations 2013, Avanti HYLAS 2 Limited (the
"Company") and the Directors present a Strategic Report. :
Principal Activities
The Company is a part of the overall Avanti Group which has a strategy of commercially exploiting its space assets, namely its
spectrum rights, satellites, intellectual property and ground station assets. The Company owns and operates the HYLAS 4
satellite, and has a wholly owned subsidiary which owns and operates the HYLAS 2 satellite. Revenue is generated from the
commercial exploitation of the Company's space and network assets. These assets include its spectrum rights, satellites,
intellectual property and ground station assets.
Results
The financial position and results for the year ended 31 December 2023 are shown on pages 10 and 11.
Revenue in the year decreased by $1,839k to $28,034k (2022: $29,873k). Cost of sales, staff costs, and operating expenses
increased by $55,240k to $92,999k, mainly due to a $87,685k expected credit loss against intercompany receivables. There was
also an increase in staff costs due to bonuses and pay rises, and an increase in professional fees due to the large number of
ptojects in the year.
EBITDA has decreased from negative $7,886k to negative $64,965k as a result of the factors described above.
The Company made a loss before tax in the year of $92,263k compared with a loss before tax of $283,122k in the previous year,
driven by the $223,255k impairment in the prior year.
Key Performance Indicators ("KPIs")
The key financial performance indicators of the Company and wider group are:

- Bandwidth revenue

- EBITDA

- Operating cash flow
Post balance sheet events
On 2 April 2024, the Super Senior Facility holder exercised a call option over the equity shares in an intermediate holding
company of the Group, Plate Newco 3 Limited, which resulted in the acquisition of 100% of the shares of Plate Newco 3
Limited by Plate New Topco Limited, a company wholly owned and controlled by the Super Senior Facility holder. As a result,
the sole Group debt provider is now also the 100% equity holder of the Group.
The Group from a trading perspective continues with business as usual, only with a stronger balance sheet. There was no change
to the Avanti team, business activities, assets or technical capabilities as a part of the transaction.

Principal Risks and Uncertainties
The Company faces a number of risks and uncertainties that may adversely affect its business, operations, liquidity, financial
position and future performance.
The principal business risks and uncertainties faced by the Company relate to the stability of the markets in which HYLAS 4 and
HYLAS 2 operate, as well as competition from similar operators in the industry. The Company also faces a number of ongoing
operational risks including credit and foreign exchange risk.
To the extent that these risks are under managements control, the directors aim to manage these risks and uncertainties going
forwards in order to maintain and improve on the current level of performance.
Liquidity risk
The Company finances its business through operating cash flow. The Company is also dependent on Plate Newco 3 Limited,
through Plate Bidco 4 Limited, not seeking repayment of the amounts currently due. At the date of approval of these financial
statements amounts due to Plate Bidco 4 Limited amounted to $265m. Plate Bidco 4 Limited has indicated that it does not
intend to seek repayment of the amounts due at the balance sheet date, for the forseeable future. See the Basis of Preparation in
Note 1 for further details.
Foreign exchange risk
The Company has foreign currency transactions and is exposed to foreign exchange risk arising from various currency
exposures, primarily with respect to the pound Sterling and the Euro. In order to mitigate the foreign currency risk, the Group
to which Avant HYLAS 2 Limited belongs monitors the level at which natural hedges occur and continually reviews the need to
enter into forward contracts in order to mitigate any material forecast exposure. The Company's reported results of operations
and financial condition are affected by exchange rate fluctuations due to both transaction and translation risks.
Interest rate risk
The Company’s main source of loan finance is interest free intercompany loans from other entities in the Group. Cash and
deposits earn interest at fixed rates based on banks’ short-term treasury deposit rates. Short-term trade and other receivables are
interest free. As a result, interest rate tisk is not currently a significant consideration.
Credit risk
Credit risk is the risk of financial loss arising from a counterparty’s inability to repay or service debt in accordance with
contractual terms. Credit risk includes the direct risk of default and the risk of deterioration of creditworthiness. The Company
primarily generates its revenue by selling capacity on its satellite to other companies within the Group in order to service third
party customer contracts entered into by those entities. Those entities assess the credit quality of their major customers before
trading commences, taking into account customers’ financial position, past experience and other factors.
