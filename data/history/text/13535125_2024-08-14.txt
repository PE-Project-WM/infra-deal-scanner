Introduction
The Directors present their report for the year 31 December 2023. :
The principle activity of the company is providing Customer Communications Management (CCM) SAAS
technology that digitally transforms customers communication workflows providing document composition,
secure multi-channel delivery (post, email, SMS, portal), storage and retrieval, through an easy to use web
interface.
Business review
Now fully established as the leading Hybrid Mail provider of our type, this year we set out to transform our
business into a fully-fledged Customer Communications Management solution, with the inclusion of document
composition, secure multi-channel delivery and complex workflows, whilst cherishing our Hybrid Mail offering
which remains uniquely at the core of our solution.
Turnover this year, mostly from Hybrid Mail revenues, increased by 33% for the 12 month comparable data from
£21,395,490 for the 12 months ended 31 December 2022 to £28,436,998 for the 12 months ended 31
December 2023, which when comparing our net income per employee productivity ratio to industry peers,
establishes Imail as the market leader in the this market.
This year has seen an increase of £1,033,427 in IT development spend underpinning the development of new
CCM technology whilst enhancing all functionality, scalability and security within the core platform. The roll-out
of our new platform is well underway and two significant financial market customers are in the process of
migrating current Hybrid Mail volumes to our encrypted digital delivery solution, reducing costs whilst improving
recipient user experience. .
: We have made good progress in retaining and developing revenues from existing clients in key target markets,
whilst onboarding significant new business wins, which we'll see the full benefit from as they trade through into
2025.
Alongside providing full end-to-end communication solutions to end-user clients we also augment channel
partners own communication solutions providing them with wholesale access to elements of our CCM platform,
utilising API calls to integrate seamlessly. Our existing channel partners have seen strong organic Hybrid Mail
growth in 2023 supported these functionalities.
The self-development of an integrated all-platform lead generation solution has resulted in notable vertical
target market wins and we expect this to accelerate as our online presence strengthens. Further, in short time
we are now page 1 ranking for key search terms and have an increasing number of 5* start Trust Pilot reviews.

2.6 Revenue
Revenue is recognised to the extent that it is probable that the economic benefits will flow to the
Company and the revenue can be reliably measured. Revenue is measured as the fair value of the
consideration received or receivable, excluding discounts, rebates, value added tax and other sales
taxes. The following criteria must also be met before revenue is recognised:
Sale of goods
Revenue from the sale of goods is recognised when all of the following conditions are satisfied:
. the Company has transferred the significant risks and rewards of ownership to the buyer;
. the Company retains neither continuing managerial involvement to the degree usually
associated with ownership nor effective control over the goods sold;
. the amount of revenue can be measured retiably;
. it is probable that the Company will receive the consideration due under the transaction; and
. the costs incurred or to be incurred in respect of the transaction can be measured reliably.
Rendering of services
Revenue from a contract to provide services is recognised in the period in which the services are
provided in accordance with the stage of completion of the contract when all of the following
conditions are satisfied:
. the amount.of revenue can be measured reliably;
. it is probable that the Company will receive the consideration due under the contract;
. the stage of completion of the contract at the end of the reporting period can be measured
reliably; and i
. the costs incurred and the costs to complete the contract can be measured reliably. :
