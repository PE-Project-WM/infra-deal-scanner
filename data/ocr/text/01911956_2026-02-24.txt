Chatham Street Technologies Limited
Strategic report
for the year ended 30 June 2025
The directors present their strategic report together with the audited financial statements for the year ended 30 June
2025.
Principal activities
The country of incorporation and domicile of Chatham Street Technologies Limited is the United Kingdom.
The principal activity of the company is the ownership of the intellectual property rights (‘IPR’) in the Transform
product, a document processing software solution.
Review of the business and future development
The Company's income continues to be generated solely from Group affiliates in respect of intellectual property
rights (‘IPR’) over the Transform software. Turnover for the year decreased by 15% to £0.8m (2024 - £1.0m) and
the business made an operating profit for the year of £0.8m (2024 - £1.0m). The revenue will be expected to decline
in future years until zero in 2028 according to a royalty model agreed between Group affiliates and the Company.
This is because the value in Transform software declines with a fixed rate delay over time and was replaced by
another IPR owned by fellow group undertaking Bottomline Technologies Limited.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties facing the company fall into the following categories:
Liquidity risk:
The directors consider the company's exposure to liquidity risk to be minimal given there is little outgoing
expenses. The primary outgoing cash flow would expect to be dividend payment which would only be made after
evaluation of cash and Distributable reserves. Further, Bottomline Parent Holdings III, LP provides parental support
to the company should it be required which would cover liabilities as and when they fall due, but only to the extent
that money is not otherwise available to meet such liabilities.
Competition:
With consideration that the main business of Chatham Street Technologies now derives from Inter-company
revenue based on retained IPR, the main risk to the business is external competition to Bottomline products.
Macroeconomic Environment:
The macroeconomic conditions in this fiscal year were characterized by a mix of growth and challenges. The global
growth rates vary across regions with US expected to lead while Europe faces distinct challenges, but together
they highlight how fragile global economic remains in the past year. In the US, impact of tariffs is beginning to hit
industries with expectations of reduced headcount in the coming year; as well as facing labour market shocks and
policy uncertainty. Political instability and European climate disasters also created uncertainty for Europe and global
markets. The UK economic outlook remains challenging. Although inflation has moderated and interest rates have
begun to stabilise, GDP growth projections remain subdued. Persistent cost-of-living pressures, geopolitical
tensions, and potential volatility in financial markets create uncertainty for businesses across sectors. These
conditions may impact customer demand, credit risk, and operational costs. The Company regularly reviews its
forecasts and stress-tests liquidity under multiple economic scenarios to ensure resilience.
Approval
This strategic report was approved on behalf of the Board on 18 February 2026.

The following principal accounting policies have been applied:
Turnover
The Company’s turnover is solely driven by transfer pricing based on a royalty model agreed between Group
affiliates and the Company.
Dividends
Equity dividends are recognised when they become legally payable. Interim equity dividends are recognised.
when paid. Final equity dividends are recognised when approved by shareholders at an annual general
meeting.
Provisions

A provision is recognised when the Company has a legal or constructive obligation as a result of a past event

and it is probable that an outflow of economic benefits will be required to settle the obligation and a reliable

estimate can be made of the amount of the obligation. If the effect of the time value of money is material,
provisions are discounted using a current pre-tax rate that reflects, when appropriate, the risks specific to the
liability. When discounting is used, the increase in the provision due to the passage of time is recognised as

a finance cost.

Foreign currencies

Transactions in foreign currencies are recorded at the average rate ruling during the month in which the

transaction occurs. Monetary assets and liabilities denominated in foreign currencies are retranslated at the

rate of exchange ruling at the statement of financial position date. All differences are taken to the Statement
of Comprehensive Income.

Taxation

Current tax, including UK corporation tax, is provided at amounts expected to be paid to (or recovered from)

the taxation authorities, using the tax rates and laws that have been enacted or substantially enacted by the

date of the statement of financial position. :

Deferred tax assets and liabilities are recognised where the carrying amount of an asset or fiabifity in the

statement of financial position differs from its tax base.

Deferred tax liabilities are recognised for all taxable temporary differences, except:

* when the deferred tax liability arises from the initial recognition of goodwill or an asset or liability in a
transaction that is not a business combination and, at the time of the transaction, affects neither the
accounting profit nor taxable profit or loss.

Deferred tax assets are recognised for all deductible temporary differences, the carry forward of unused tax

credits and any unused tax losses. Recognition of deferred tax assets is restricted to those instances where

it is probable that taxable profit will be available against which the difference can be utilised, except:

« when the deferred tax asset arises from the initial recognition of an asset or liability in a transaction that
is not a business combination and, at the time of the transaction, affects neither the accounting profit nor
taxable profit or loss.

The amount of the asset or liability is determined using tax rates that have been enacted or substantively

enacted by the reporting date and are expected to apply when the deferred tax liabilities/(assets) are settled

or recovered.
