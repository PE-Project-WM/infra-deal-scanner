STRATEGIC REPORT .

FOR THE PERIOD ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2024

The directors present the strategic report on the group for the 9 months ended 31 December 2024. .
Principal Activity

Brsk Limited (the “Company’) and its subsidiaries, together form the “Group”. The Group's principal activities during

the period were to continue to develop and operate Full Fibre (1) optic broadband infrastructure (via Group
Company Brsk Limited) and provide internet connectivity services to residential and business consumers across

the United Kingdom (via Group Company Brsk ISP Limited):

Strategy and business model

The Group's principal activities in 2024 remained the development and operation of full fibre broadband
infrastructure and the delivery of high-speed internet connectivity to both residential and business customers across

the UK. .
Our strategy is focused on deploying full fibre broadband in underserved areas with strong subscriber potential. We

aim to deliver long-term value by:

¢ Expanding our network footprint in both existing and new geographic areas; .

* Increasing penetration in built areas through local engagement and targeted marketing;

¢ Offering a competitive portfolio of broadband products and services;

¢ Enhancing customer acquisition and retention through superior service and community integration.

Our business model is underpinned by continued investment in network infrastructure, partnerships with skilled
contractors, and technological innovation. This hybrid approach—leveraging both internal expertise and external
resources—enables scalable, capital-efficient growth aligned to our strategic objectives.

Business Review

2024 was a transformative year for the Group, marked by operational expansion, financial strengthening, and a
significant strategic merger.

Key highlights:

¢ Network Expansion: Continued roll-out into new areas and improved customer take-up in existing ones.

¢ Funding: Secured £75 million in committed Loan Facility funding, complementing the £150 million facility
obtained in the prior financial year.

* Strategic Merger: Merged with Substantial Group, creating one of the UK's largest and most capital-
efficient alternative networks, with a combined footprint of 2.2 million Premises Passed (2) and a target of
nearly 3 million by end-2025.

This scale positions the Group as a unique retail, wholesale, and consolidation platform in the UK broadband
market — taking second position in terms of Premises Passed at the end of 2024 when compared to all alternative
networks in the UK.

Financial Review (Group)
Turnover for the 9 months ended 31 December 2024 was £11.6m, an increase of £7.3m or 170% from £4.3m in the
12 months ended 31 March 2024. Gross Profit for the 9 months ended 31 December 2024 was £8.8m, an increase
of £7.1m or 418% from £1.7m in the 12 months ended 31 March 2024. These increases are due to an increase in
subscribers in the period as well as an increase in average revenue per user (ARPU).
In the same periods, the loss before tax has increased by £3.1m or 9% to £37.5m from £34.4m, in line with
company expectations as the company incurs cost to build the network and onboard subscribers.
The statement of financial position remains in a net asset position at the 9 months ended 31 December 2024 at
£12.0m, down £7.5m or 38% from £19.5m at the 12 months ended 31 March 2024. During the 9 months ended 31
December 2024 additional equity of £30m was contributed and the group had drawn an additional £52m (March
2024: £27.6m) from its debt facility to fund the continued roll out of the network.
