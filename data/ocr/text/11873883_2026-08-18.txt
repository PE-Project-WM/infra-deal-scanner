Summary
The Directors present their report together with the audited financial statements for the year ended 31
December 2025.

2006 entitled to small companies from preparing a Strategic Report.
REGIS-TR UK Ltd (the “Company” or “REGIS-TR UK’) is a private limited liability company by shares,
incorporated under the laws of England and Wales. The Company was incorporated on 11 March 2019.
On 19 November 20285, foliowing registration in the Mercantile Registry of Madrid, Sociedad de Gestion

Measurement convention

3.2 Current / non-current distinction

Current assets correspond to assets that the Company expects to be realised within twelve months after
the reporting period or are ‘Cash and cash equivalents’.

Current liabilities correspond to liabilities that the Company expects to settle within twelve months after the
reporting period.

Financial instruments comprise of financial assets and liabilities. For the Company, financial assets mainly

3.5 Revenue recognition -
The Company's revenue represents revenue from contracts with customers, including UK EMIR
membership fees and reporting fees (UK EMIR Fees). Revenue is measured based on a consideration
specified in a contract with a customer and excludes any amount collected on behalf of third parties.
Revenue is recognised when (or as) each performance obligation is satisfied in terms of the contract.
UK EMIR membership fees and reporting fees revenue represents revenue earned by providing services
related to repository for reporting trades and transactions across multiple product classes and jurisdictions
on behalf of its customers.
The Company’s performance obligation related to UK EMIR membership fees is recognised as invoiced,
also deferred over the period for these services provided over time, as the Company has right to payment
for an amount that corresponds directly with the value to the customer of the entity's performance to date.
The Company's performance obligation related to UK EMIR reporting fees is recognised when the trades
are executed, as this fee is based on the volume (number of trades) executed by the customer and under
IFRS 15 the Company has a present right to payment for the services provided at that time (point in time).
Furthermore, the Company's performance obligation related to UK EMIR special (variable) fees is based
on efforts inputted, following ad-hoc clients requests, and, therefore, is recognised when the services are
delivered.
Revenue is also earned from intercompany recharges to fellow subsidiary undertakings. Hours spent on
group wide activities by local employees are recharged on a cost plus basis. The revenue is recognised
when the activities are executed. .

exchange rate at the balance sheet date, while non-monetary balance sheet items recognised at historical
cost are measured at the exchange rate on the transaction day. Exchange rate differences are recorded
as Finance income or expense in the period in which they arise.
