Strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2026

Principal activities and review of the business, including subsequent events

Principal activities

Historically, the principal activity of the Company and the Group was the development of low carbon
electricity generation facilities. However, as a result of issues relating to the raising of finance for the
construction of Horizon’s first power station, Wylfa Newydd, on Anglesey, the Group in January 2019
suspended the majority of its activities. Subsequently, in September 2020, following an announcement
by Hitachi, Limited (the Ultimate Shareholder) that it would cease its backing for Horizon entirely and
withdraw from the UK nuclear market, the Group ceased all its activities to develop its projects at both
Wylfa Newydd on Anglesey and Oldbury on Severn in South Gloucestershire.

On 28 June 2024 the Group completed the sale of its sites at Oldbury and Wylfa to Great British Nuclear
(GBN), and as part of the sale the Group agreed to support GBN (which was subsequently renamed
Great British Energy - Nuclear (GBE-N) with estate and site management services from 28 June 2024
until 31 December 2025. This was completed on 31 December 2025 and the Group will now be retained
until 30 June 2027 when the deferred element of the payment for the Oldbury site is due from GBE-N
(subject to the outcome of site conditions) and when warranties on the sale expire.

Review of business

In the year ended 31 March 2026 the Group continued to support GBE-N with the provision of transitional
services under Transitional Service Agreements (TSAs) with GBE-N. Under these agreements Horizon
Nuclear Power Oldbury Limited and Horizon Nuclear Power Wyifa Limited were obliged to provide estate
and site management services to GBE-N for the period from completion to 31 December 2025. The TSAs
ended on 31 December 2025 as intended.

Following the completion of the TSAs the Group undertook the following activities.

e Two Horizon staff were made redundant. One director, who previously worked for Hitachi Europe
Limited, joined Horizon in September 2025 to assist in the management and ultimate liquidation
of the Group.

e Two site vehicles were sold and all assets were removed from the Group's fixed asset register.

e The Group applied to HMRC to deregister its Group Companies from the Construction Industry
Scheme (CIS) and Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings (ATED).

The completion of the TSAs with GBE-N resulted in a change in trade for tax purposes.

In February 2026 the Group issued a dividend of £140,000k to the Ultimate Shareholder. The cash came
: from the proceeds of the land asset sale to GBE-N in June 2024 plus accrued interest from cash pooling

facility with Hitachi Europe Limited.

Strategy

Following completion of the TSAs with GBE-N, the Group will be retained until 30 June 2027 when the

deferred element of the payment for the Oldbury site is due from GBE-N (subject to the outcome of site

conditions) and when warranties on the sale expire. It is the Ultimate Shareholder’s intention that following

payment of the deferred consideration and expiry of the warranty period, the Group will then be liquidated.
