STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
The directors presents the Strategic Report of Vistra Limited (the Company) for the year ended 31 December 2025 , including an
assessment of the risks affecting the Company.
Business review
The principal activity of the Company in the year under review was that of marketing, finance and operational support for group
companies, as well as that of a formation agent and business information provider. There were no significant changes in the nature of
the Company's business during the year.
The Company reported a profit for the year of £299,053 (2024: loss £205,309), representing a 246% improvement compared to the
prior year. This improvement was primarily driven by lower operating expenses, including reduced employee-related costs.
Turnover for the year was £4,560,733 (2024: £4,489,034) 2% up year-on-year. The increase was primarily due to pricing increases.
However, the impact was moderated as the principal customer was subject to a fixed three-year pricing agreement and therefore did
not experience the full 5% price increase. The Company maintained a consistent gross profit margin of 36% (2024: 35%)
demonstrating resilience in pricing and cost management.
The operating profit for the year was £781,176 (2024: operating loss £274,493), a siginificant improvement of 385% compared to the
prior year. The improvement was mainly attributable to a reduction in the Company's cost base, including lower employee-related
costs resulting from a reduction in headcount. The Company's EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Tax, Depreciation, and Amortisation)
also improved significantly to a profit of £885,985 (2024: loss £170,711).
Management considers EBITDA to be a more meaningful indicator of the Company’s underlying financial performance, as it excludes
significant non-cash charges, notably depreciation and amortisation, which had a material impact on statutory results.
The directors will develop the future growth of the Company through both organic growth and acquisition. As Vistra Holdings Limited
and all of its subsidiaries (the "Vistra Group") expands into different geographical markets and business sectors, the Company is
expected to benefit from this worldwide expansion.
Going concern
Vistra Group management meet regularly to assess the performance and going concern of the Company and are satisfied that the
Company will continue its activities for at least 12 months from the date of signing of these financial statements.
The Company prepared a 12 months cashflow forecast, which shows a positive cash balance. The Company also received a letter of
support from Vistra Holdings Limited. Should the company encounter difficulties in meeting its financial obligations as they fall due, it
is the intention of Vistra Holdings Limited to offer appropriate finance support for a period of at least twelve (12) months from the
date of signing the audit report.
In assessing the going concern of the Company, the directors considered the Company's current and expected free cash flows, the
nature and timing of repayment of existing intercompany liabilities. The directors also considered, for a period of at least 12 months
from the date of approving the financial statements, the continuing availability of support from Vistra Holdings Limited, and the
effects of potential economic headwinds. Vistra Holdings Limited has indicated its intention to continue to make available such funds
as are needed by the Company.
Having considered the prospects and viability as detailed above, the directors are confident that the Company will have sufficient
funds to continue to meet its liabilities as they fall due for at least 12 months from the date of approval of the financial statements
and therefore have prepared the financial statements on a going concern basis.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The Company is subjected to the following risks and uncertainties:
Competitive Risk
The Company faces ongoing competitive risk from market saturation, emerging technologies, and shifting customer preferences. Key
threats include pricing pressure, innovation by rivals, and loss of brand loyalty. To mitigate this, the Company has made significant
investment in software and marketing and is continues to monitor market developments. These efforts aim to strengthen our market
position and drive long-term growth.
Credit risk
The Company's activities expose it to credit risk from trade receivables and it has therefore implemented strong controls to manage
and mitigate this risk, including a dedicated credit control team.
Liquidity risk
Proactive treasury planning is in place to manage liquidity risk, which includes regular cash flow forecast updates to assess the level of
working capital required for at least 12 weeks in advance. In addition the Company has a dedicated credit control resource to manage
debtors, assessing any adjustments to the bad provision on a monthly basis.
Foreign exchange .
The Company is exposed to fluctuations in the Euro and Dollar on overseas activities, and against intercompany settlements and
receipts. Steps are taken to mitigate the risk around intercompany transactions in particularly by settling in the transactional currency
and agreeing payment plans and netting of debtors and creditors where possible.
~ Operational risk

Operational risk is managed and mitigated by employing robust procedures and processes, and clear segregation of duties where
appropriate. The professional competence and development of personnel is measured and developed via continuous training, and the
Company is covered under the Vistra Group global professional indemnity insurance policy.
Reputational risk
Reputational risk arises as a result of a failure to manage the other risks identified by the Company. As such, the Company and its
personnel always endeavour to act with integrity and highest professional standards in dealing with clients, regulators and other
stakeholders.
Financial key performance indicators
The Company's key performance indicators are revenue, gross profit, and EBITDA. Revenue, gross profit and EBITDA have decreased
in the year.
