Directors' Report for the year ended 31 March 2026
The Directors present their report and the financial statements of Cable and Wireless (India) Limited (the
‘Company’) for the year ended 31 March 2026.
Incorporation
The Company was incorporated as a private company limited by shares on 21 October 1994 and registered in
England and Wales.
Principal activities
On 31 March 2014, the Company entered into a Business Transfer Agreement with an effective date of.1 April
2014, under which substantially all of its trading operations and related assets were transferred to Vodafone
Global Services Private Limited. Following the transfer, the Company ceased trading activities and has not
undertaken any trading in the current or prior year.
The Company continues to hold significant cash resources generated from the historical business transfer and
subsequent activities. At 31 March 2026, the Company held cash and cash equivalents of £6,249,967 and net
assets of £3,165,023, which continue to provide substantial liquidity and support the Company's ongoing
obligations. The principal activities of the Company are now limited to the management of its remaining assets
and liabilities, oversight of the Indian branch and administration of tax matters in India.
Branches outside the UK
The branch has not conducted any trading activities in the current and previous financial year. Although no
trading activities are undertaken, the Indian branch continues to incur limited administrative costs associated
with branch management, regulatory compliance and tax administration.
Review of the business and future development
The Directors do not anticipate that the business of the Company will change in the foreseeable future. There
have been no subsequent events that would suggest future changes.
Results and Dividends
The income statement is set out on page 10 of the financial statements. For the year ended 31 March 2026, there
was a loss on ordinary activities after taxation of £232,710 (2025: loss of £215,857).
The loss was generated as a result of expenses incurred in the branch for audit, legal and professional charges. In
addition, the Company incurred interest on its loan with another Group company of £238,058 (2025: £247,821).
The income tax credit £53,561 (2025: £71,249) is explained in note 7.
The Directors do not recommend the payment of a dividend for the year ended 31 March 2026 (2025: £nil).

liquidity risk. The Company follows the board approved policies of its parent Vodafone Group Plc, to manage
these principal financial risks. This includes the treasury function of the Vodafone Group, which provides a
centralised treasury service to the Company, and follows a framework of policies and guidelines authorised and
reviewed annually by the Vodafone Group Plc Board.
The Group's internal auditors review the internal control environment regularly. There has been no significant,
change during the financial year to the types of financial risks faced by the Company, or the Company's
approach to the management of those risks. Further details of the Group's risks and policies on financial risk
management can be found in the annual report and financial statements of Vodafone Group Ple for the year
ended 31 March 2026.
The Company has considered macroeconomic and geopolitical risks as part of its going concern assessment.
The Directors will continue to monitor these risks but do not consider them to impact the Company's ability to
continue as a going concern throughout the assessment period.
The Directors have considered all the above risks as part of the Company’s risk and going concern assessment.
In reaching its conclusion, the Directors also considered the findings of the work performed by the Group to
support the statement on the long-term viability of the Group.
The Company is expected to continue managing its remaining assets and liabilities, including oversight of the
Indian Branch and its tax affairs, with no significant change in the outlook for the Company's principal risks
during the going concern assessment period.

The Company classifies its financial assets in the category of receivables. Receivables are non-derivative
