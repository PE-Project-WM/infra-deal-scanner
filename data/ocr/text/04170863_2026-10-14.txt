The directors present the Strategic report for Masergy Communications UK Limited (the “Company”)
for the year ended 31 December 2025. :
7 Business review and principal activities :
The principal activity of the Company is the provision of network services specialising in real-time
application performance across converged enterprise networks for clients of its immediate parent
entity, Masergy Communications, Inc. The Company uses its multi. service network platform to :
develop technology for enterprise customers to manage the security and performance of their critical
applications such as video, voice and data globally. :
Masergy Communications UK Limited is a wholly owned subsidiary of Masergy Communications, Inc.
and provides network and sales services for the parent company. The Company manages operations
in the United Kingdom and has support branches located in Australia, France, Germany, Hong Kong,
India, Japan, Norway, Switzerland, Singapore, and the Netherlands. The profit for the year ended 31
December 2025 amounted to £2,966,638 (2024: £1,981,149). The net asset position of the Company
was £7,307,823 and £4,341,185, as of 31 December 2025 and 31 December 2024, respectively.
Principal risks and uncertainties :
The Company maintains a key relationship with its. Parent, Comcast Corporation Inc., and relies on
ongoing financial support from its Parent. If this relationship were no longer available, it could
potentially negatively impact the business. Comcast Corporation Inc provides a letter of support to
Masergy Communications UK Limited on an annual basis. This letter provides financial support to the
UK Company. ;
Global supply issues
Macro-economic risk factors including localised price inflation and potential stagflation in 2025 are .
being closely monitored by senior management but at this time it is not predicted that the Company
will be majorly impacted over the next 12 months. - :
Financial risks and uncertainties
The Company uses various financial instruments which include cash, trade debtors, trade creditors
that arise directly from its operations. The existence of these financial instruments exposes the
Company to a number of financial risks. :
The main risks arising from the Company's financial instruments are currency risk, liquidity risk and
credit risk. The directors review and agree policies for managing each of these risks and they are
summarized below. These policies have remained unchanged from. previous years.
