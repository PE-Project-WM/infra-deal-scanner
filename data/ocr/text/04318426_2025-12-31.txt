Strategic Report .
The directors present their strategic report for the year end 31 December 2024.
1. Business Review
Clickatell is a global leader in mobile messaging and transaction services. The company operates within a highly dynamic and
innovative market, focusing on enabling customers to alert, interact, and transact with their business partners and communities
via mobile phones.
The company's core services cater to businesses seeking efficient and secure communication channels, leveraging technology
to deliver real-time solutions. Clickatell’s operations align with the group’s overarching mission to lead in chat commerce and
mobile interaction, with a presence in key markets across multiple geographies.
Clickatell is focused on the increasing of revenue and expansion of our product offering. The reduction in revenue aligns with
our expectations as we implement our new digital offering. We anticipated a decline due to the lower price point. However, as
more clients adopt the new product, we expect an initial revenue drop followed by an increase as customer numbers grow.
: 2. Strategy and Objectives
As part of the Clickatell group, the company contributes to the strategic goal of enhancing digital engagement through mobile
platforms. Specifically, the company's objectives are:
. Expanding Market Reach: Strengthen the presence in existing markets and enter emerging territories to support the
group's global growth ambitions.
. Driving Innovation: Develop cutting-edge mobile messaging and transaction technologies to offer unparalleled
solutions to customers.
*  Customer-Centric Approach: Focus on delivering high-quality, secure, and scalable services to meet the evolving
needs of business partners and communities.
. Sustainability and Governance: Align with the group's sustainability objectives, ensuring adherence to regulatory
standards and ethical practices
These strategic priorities not only position the company to achieve its business goals but also contribute significantly to the
group's consolidated performance and reputation as a leader in mobile communications.
3. Principal Risks and Uncertainties
The company faces several risks and uncertainties that could impact its financial performance and operational effectiveness.
The most significant risk is the increased costs imposed by vendors, which have directly influenced the profitability of the
business.
To address this challenge, the group has revised its strategic focus to prioritize margin optimization. Key initiatives include: :
. Streamlining Operations: Identifying cost-saving opportunities across the business to maintain competitiveness.
° Strategic Pricing Models: Introducing pricing strategies that ensure profitability while retaining customer satisfaction.
By adopting these measures, the company aims to mitigate the impact of rising costs, ensuring long-term sustainability and
alignment with shareholder expectations.
4. Future developments
Looking forward, Clickatell aims to establish itself as the leader in the chat commerce industry, leveraging innovative solutions
to redefine business-to-customer interactions. The primary focus will be on maximizing the potential of WhatsApp, the world’s
leading messaging platform, to enable businesses to connect with their customers more efficiently and effectively.
Key initiatives driving this vision include:
. Platform Optimization: Enhancing the functionality of WhatsApp as a core tool for seamless business
communication and transactions.
. Customer Engagement: Providing tailored solutions to businesses to improve their customer experience and
streamline processes.
. Market Expansion: Investing in resources to promote WhatsApp-driven chat commerce solutions across diverse
industries and geographies.
Through these efforts, Clickatell seeks to not only secure market leadership but also redefine how businesses interact with their
customers in a digital-first economy.

1.4 Financial instruments
The Company classifies financial instruments, or their component parts, on initial recognition as a financial asset, a financial
liability or an equity instrument in accordance with the substance of the contractual arrangement.
All the Company's recognised financial assets are classified as financial assets at amortised cost.
Financial assets at amortised costs are those financial instruments which meet the following conditions:
e the financial asset is held within a business mode! whose objective is to hold financial assets in order to collect contractual
cash flows; and
« the contractual terms of the financial asset give rise on specified dates to cash flows that are solely payments of principal
and interest on the principal amount outstanding.
Debt and equity instruments issued by the Company are classified as either financial liabilities or as equity in accordance with
the substance of the contractual arrangements and the definitions of a financial liability and an equity instrument.
All the Company's financial liabilities are classified as financial liabilities at amortised cost.
The Company's financial assets primarily comprise trade receivables, other receivables and cash and cash equivalents. The
Company's financial liabilities comprise trade and other payables.
Trade and other receivables
Trade and other receivables, excluding, when applicable, VAT and prepayments, are measured at transaction price as
determined under IFRS 15. All recognised financial assets are subsequently measured at amortised cost using the effective
interest rate method, less any impairment.
Impairment - Expected credit losses and write offs
Loss allowances are recognised for expected credit losses on trade receivables.
The Company recognises a loss allowance for expected credit losses "ECL" on the recognised financial assets. The Company
always recognises lifetime expected credit losses for trade receivables which are estimated using a provision matrix based an
the Company's historical credit loss experience, adjusted for factors that are specific to the debtors, general economic
conditions and an assessment of both the current as well as the forecast direction of conditions at the reporting date, including
time value of money where appropriate.
Bad debts are written off when all efforts have been exhausted and the company is unable to claim any of the amounts owing.
Bad debts written off are not subject to enforcement. Management assess the credit risk of debtors annually. Debtors are
considered in default when they are more than 60 days overdue.
Where lifetime ECL is measured on a collective basis to cater for cases where evidence of significant increases in credit risk at
the individual instrument level may not yet be available, the financial instruments are grouped on the following basis:
. e nature of financial instruments;
e  past-due status;
e nature, size and country of operation of debtors; and
« external credit ratings where available.
Impairment losses are recognised in profit or loss.

1.5 Tax :
Current tax assets and liabilities
Current tax for current and prior periods is, to the extent unpaid, recognised as a liability. If the amount already paid in respect
of current and prior periods exceeds the amount due for those periods, the excess is recognised as an asset.
Current tax liabilities (assets) for the current and prior periods are measured at the amount expected to be paid to (recovered
from) the tax authorities, using the tax rates (and tax laws) that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the end of the
reporting period.
Deferred tax assets and liabilities
Deferred tax is recognised in respect of temporary differences between the carrying amounts of assets and liabilities for
financial reporting purposes and the tax base used for taxation purposes.
A deferred tax liability is recognised for all taxable temporary differences.
A deferred tax asset is recognised for all deductible temporary differences to the extent that it is probable that taxable profit will
be available against which the deductible temporary difference can be utilised.
A deferred tax asset is recognised for the carry forward of unused tax losses to the extent that it is probable that future taxable
profit will be available against which they can be utilised.
Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured at the tax rates that are expected to apply to the period when the asset is
realised or the liability is settled, based on tax rates (and tax laws) that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the end
of the reporting period.
The deferred tax rate applied to assets is determined by the expected manner of recovery. Where the expected recovery of the
asset is through sale, the capital gains tax rate is applied. The normal tax rate is applied when the expected recovery is through
use. A combination of these rates is applied if the recovery is expected to be partly through use and sale.
