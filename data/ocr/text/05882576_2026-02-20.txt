Strategic Report
Year Ended 31 May 2025
The directors present their strategic report for the year ended 31 May 2025.
Review of the business
Oxygen8 Communications is a provider of customer engagement messaging, voice and payment solutions
enabling the payment of digital services using direct carrier billing, premium messaging and calls.
Tumover and profitability in the year fell with lower mobile payment volumes. Turnover was £34.3m (2024:
£46.4m) whilst gross profit was £4.4m (2024: £4.8m) representing a gross profit margin of 12.8% (2024:
10.4%), a continuation of expanding gross profit margins for the six straight year. Administrative expenses
rose in the year to £4.4m (2024: £3.5m) with a full twelve months costs from Viveri Group contributing towards
higher staff costs together with significant customer event costs and settlement of a fong standing customer
dispute. All of this contributed towards generating an operating loss of £0.2m (2024: £1.3m profit) and a toss
after tax of £0.1m (2024: £1.0m profit).
Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risk and uncertainties of the group relate to the potential loss of key customers, the group's
reliance on its key suppliers, the mobile network operators for connectivity and commercials and changes in
regulation in the markets the group operates in.
Whilst no individual lost customer would overiy impact the performance of the group, a group of similar
businesses in any one market can have a noticeable impact on the level of mobile payment volumes which in
turn directly impacts the profitability of the business.
Any change in the services that we can offer and commercials available from the mobile networks can have
an impact on revenues, however any change in commercials are mitigated by passing on the commercial
impact to our customers. Changes in regulation and taxation would have the potential to have an immediate
financial impact on the group.
The group mitigates its principal risks and uncertainties by closely monitoring changes in regulation and
taxation and working with regulators and industry bodies and maintaining its good relationships with the mobile
network operators.
Development and performance
The UK based businesses made up of Viveri Group, Dynamic Mobile Billing and Spoke (Interactive)
represented 86.0%% (2024: 89.5%) of group turnover whilst our South African business represented 14.0%
(2024: 10.5%). Profitability was again generated by the UK based business predominately Dynamic Mobile
Billing and Spoke (Interactive) with Viveri Group focusing on the media sector currently loss making in its early
stage of development. The South Africa business continues to operate at around break-even.
Despite the poorer financial performance the group has laid good foundations which should start to see the
group win new business In the years ahead. We are starting to see some success in winning new substantial
business and continue to work on new opportunities with new custamers to the group.
Financial key performance indicators
The key performance indicators is absolute gross profit and gross profit margin which is a better reflection of
business performance than tumover, as weil as staff expenses as a proportion of tumover reflecting the
: operational efficiency of the business.

{j) Tax
Current tax represents the amount of tax payable or receivable in respect of the taxable profit (or loss)
for the current or past reporting periods. it is measured at the amount expected to be paid or recovered
using the tax rates and laws that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet
date.
Deferred tax represents the future tax consequences of transactions and events recognised in the
financial statements of current and previous periods. It is recognised in respect of all timing differences,
with certain exceptions. Timing differences are differences between taxable profits and total
comprehensive income as stated in the financial statements that arise from the inclusion of income and
expense in tax assessments in periods different from those in which they are recognised in the financial
statements. Unrelieved tax losses and other deferred tax assets are recognised only to the extent that
it is probable that they will be recovered against the reversal of deferred tax liabilities or other future

Deferred tax is measured using the tax rates and laws that have been enacted or substantively enacted

(k) Turnover and other income
Turnover is measured at the fair value of the consideration received or receivable net of VAT and trade

Foreign currency transactions are initially recognised by applying to the foreign currency amount the

Monetary assets and liabilities denominated in a foreign currency at the balance sheet date are

(m) Employee benefits
When employees have rendered service to the company, short-term employee benefits to which the
employees are entitled are recognised as a liability and an expense. The cost of any unused holiday
entitlement is recognised in the period in which the employee's services are received.
The company operates a defined contribution plan for the benefit of its employees. Contributions are
expensed as they become payable.
(n) Holiday pay accrual
A liability is recognised to the extent of any unused holiday pay entitlement which has accrued at the
balance sheet date and carried forward to future periods. This is measured at the undiscounted salary
cost of the future holiday entitlement so accrued at the balance sheet date.
