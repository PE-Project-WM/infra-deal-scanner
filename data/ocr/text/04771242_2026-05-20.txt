Principal risks and uncertainties
The Company's exposure to the impact of wider economic changes is limited by the essential nature of the
services it supplies and diverse nature of the customer base. No single customer accounts for more than 5%
(2024: 2%) of group turnover and 92% (2024: 88%) of gross profit is recurring which reflects the group's focus
on increasing our contracted revenues.
The Company operates in a market that continually evolves, having seen a structural shift from traditional
products such as fixed line telephony to hosted and cloud connectivity, as well as IT and cyber security. The
Company has mitigated risks to individual products through the continual development of its product offering and
. vendor relationships, and remains proud to serve as a single point of contact for the information technology

needs of its clients, providing communications and software solutions, connectivity, Al and value-added IT
services. The product portfolio is continually reviewed in the context of wider technological and commercial
developments in the industry and is proactively managed to ensure that material changes — such as the
upcoming 2027 Public Switched Telephone Network switch-off — are addressed in a timely manner, supporting
customer retention and transition to appropriate new technology.
The Company is mindful of the current macroeconomic environment, which continues to present uncertainty
across the UK business landscape. Subdued business confidence, particularly within the SME sector, has
contributed to a softening in capex investment and spend, which has acted as a constraint on one-off installation
revenues during the period. The Company continues to see a strong shift in demand towards subscription
revenue and products with customers favouring contracted product offerings over these one-off products as
reflected in the continued shift to contracted revenue (2025 92%: 2024 88%). The Company's recurring revenue
base provides resilience against short-term demand fluctuations and the macroenvironment. The Directors
continue to monitor this environment closely and will seek to mitigate any short term impact through targeted
commercial initiatives, operational efficiency and the ongoing diversification of the Company's product and
service portfolio.
Financial risk management objectives and policies
The Company's principle financial instruments comprise cash at bank, trade debtors, trade creditors and
amounts owed to group undertakings. These instruments provide working capital and acquisition finance for the
business and allow it to continue its growth and the acquisition of strategically complementary businesses.
Credit risk
Credit risk is attributable to trade debtors; the amounts shown in the financial statements are after bad and
doubtful debt provisions. The Group has implemented policies to manage the credit risk and require credit
checks on potential customers. The amount of exposure to any one customer is low due to the diverse base and
high level of direct debit collection, with credit limits subject to regular review.
Liquidity risk
The level of available funds is measured and monitored on a Group level. The Group maintains a mixture of long

: and short term debt finance that is designed to ensure that the business has sufficient available funds for its
operations. The level of available funds is monitored on a regular basis through the use of detailed cash flow
forecasts and comparison of forecast to actuals. The Group regularly monitors its financial covenants for its debt
and remains within its covenants. The term of the facility is fixed until 2031.

Financial key performance indicators
. , The Directors monitor a range of financial and non-financial key performance indicators ("KPIs") to assess the
performance of the Company and to inform strategic decision-making. The principal KPIs are set out below.
Financial KPIs ;
Revenue — Recurring and Non-Recurring
The Company monitors total turnover split between recurring revenue, comprising contracted monthly
connectivity, communications and IT service charges, and non-recurring revenue, principally comprising one-off
installation, hardware and project-based income. This distinction is considered important given the Company's
strategic focus on growing its high-quality recurring revenue base. Recurring revenue grew year on year,
reflecting continued progress in the Company's M&A and organic growth strategy, whilst non-recurring revenue
was impacted by the subdued macroeconomic environment and reduced SME business confidence during the
year, as referenced in the Principal Risks and Uncertainties section.
Gross Margin
Gross margin is monitored at both a product and customer level to assess unit economics, pricing integrity and
the overall quality of revenue. Gross margin remained broadly stable year on year, supported by the Company's
ongoing focus on product mix management and customer-level profitability analysis.
EBITDA and Pro-forma EBITDA
EBITDA is the Directors' primary measure of underlying operational profitability. Pro-forma EBITDA, which
adjusts for the annualised contribution of acquired businesses and certain non-recurring items, is used by the
Board for covenant compliance purposes and to assess the underlying run-rate performance of the Company.
Further detail on financial performance is provided in the Business Review section of this report.
Non-Financial KPIs
Customer Satisfaction
Customer satisfaction is monitored through a number of external and internal measures, including Trustpilot
ratings, Net Promoter Score ("NPS") and Customer Satisfaction scores ("CSATs"). These metrics are reviewed
regularly by the Board and management as indicators of service quality and customer retention risk. Customer
satisfaction scores remained at a consistently high level during the year, reflecting the Company's commitment
to service excellence and its position as a trusted single point of contact for the technology needs of its clients.
Employee Satisfaction
The Company measures employee engagement through its Employee Net Promoter Score ("ENPS"), which is
used to assess workforce sentiment and inform people strategy. Employee satisfaction remained positive during
the year, supported by the Company's continued investment in its people, culture and development initiatives.
The Directors also monitor customer churn, cross-sell and volume up-sell/down sell metrics at a product and
----+- customer level as supplementary indicators of commercial performance and revenue quality. These metrics.
inform both operational decision-making and the Company's longer-term strategic planning. .

Employee engagement statement
The Company recognises that its employees are fundamental to the delivery of its strategic objectives and long-
term success. The Board is committed to maintaining an open and inclusive working environment in which

During the year the Company has taken a number of significant steps to invest in its people and to strengthen

The Company launched the ‘Apprentify scheme during the year, through which 61 employees have been
approved for structured development programmes spanning artificial intelligence, leadership and management.
This initiative reflects the Company's commitment to building organisational capability and supporting employees

remuneration remains competitive and that employees are able to share in the success of the business. During

value creation of the Company and further aligning the interests of management with those of its shareholders.
The Company places significant importance on recognising exceptional performance and fostering a strong

contributions on an ongoing basis throughout the year. In addition, the Company operates the Star Awards, a
peer-to-peer recognition scheme through which employees are able to nominate colleagues for exceptional
contributions, with the opportunity to award a financial reward. The Directors consider peer recognition to be an
important element of the Company's culture, fostering a sense of shared purpose and mutual appreciation
across the organisation.

contribute to and participate in the Foundation's work supporting UK grass roots charities. The Directors believe
