Introduction
. The Director presents his Strategic Report and the audited financial statements for the year ended 30
September 2024.
. Business review
Outra’s vision is to change the way data is bought, managed, analysed and used by brands in engaging
: consumers. Outra started in 2017 by bringing together industry leaders to change the experience for consumers
through the utilisation of data and to drive the success of brands. .
Outra provide products and services focused in two core areas:
1. Data - mixture of proprietary and open-source data primarily focused on residential homes. All our data is
GDPR compliant.
2. Platform - a Data Science Platform built on open-source, cloud-based technology as Software as a Service
° (SaaS).
; Outra’s clients are a range of consumer facing retailers and brands looking to increase the understanding of the
markets they operate in. Outra’s combination of data sets and use of data science is unique in the market.
Outra’s clients can use Outra data and models standalone, and also to enrich their 1st party data, to better
understand their current customers and to identify high-potential prospects. Outra data and models can then
inform business decisions and marketing programmes.
Outra’s significant on-going investment into data scale, data accuracy and data utilisation via proprietary and
: partner technology solutions has enabled us to bring to market audience insight and activation targeting products
with specialised relevance for real-estate, insurance and variety of utilities industry verticals. Increasingly, Outra
audience insight and activation management products are being used across the broadest base of industry
sectors beyond these foundational sectors. ;
During the period since launch, investment has been primarily in building capability in people and technology to
produce best in class products and solutions to win and retain clients. Whilst the Company will continue to invest
in research and development, the business’s primary focus is pivoting to the commercial exploitation of its
products by building, developing and motivating a high-powered sales operation.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Company is in its early stage of development and like many others faces risks and uncertainties which
involve managing growth in a fast-changing marketplace, uncertainties and changes in GDPR and E-Privacy
legislation, recruitment and retention of talented people.
