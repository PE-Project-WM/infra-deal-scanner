. Principle activities .
Associated Utility Supplies Limited ("AUS") provides products and services to. the electricity supply, rail and arboreal
industries. The company has specialist manufacturing facilities and offers services including inspection, training, repair
and calibration. A significant growth area for the business has been the supply and installation of products for the
electricity supply industry as part of a new revenue stream, Resiliency as a Service (RaaS)®.

REVIEW OF BUSINESS
During the course of the reporting period, AUS shareholding was acquired by Osmose Europe Ltd an Osmose group
company. The financial reporting period has been shortened to an 8 month reporting period from 30th April 2025 to 31st
December 2024 to align with the group financial year end.
The company saw a 130% growth in profit compared to the prior reporting period. The profit for the period was £2.6m
(2024: £2.0m profit). Turnover was £13.9m (2024: £14.8m) £0.9m less than the prior period. The average monthly
turnover was £1.7m (2024: £1.2m) an increase of 141% compared to the prior period. Operating profit for the period
increased by £0.7m to £2.8m compared with the prior period (2024: £2.1m profit). .
Our adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation amortisation and impairment of non-financial assets (EBITDA)
increased by 132% from £2.2m to £2.9m. The gross profit margin was 35% (2024: 24%) - 11% higher than the prior
period.
The increase in average monthly revenue and total profit compared with 2024 is reflective of the ongoing development
of Resiliency as a Service (RaaS)® and the growing number of customers who are engaging with the business . The
gross margin associated with the new revenue stream is contributing significantly to the overall gross profit margin
improvement in the period. The underlying core business has continued to perform well and is providing a solid platform
for the new Resiliency as a Service (RaaS)® revenue stream to build upon.
The company remains focussed on controlling overheads, however some costs have naturally increased due to inflation
and also with scaling of operations to accommodate our new work stream. For example an additional off site warehouse
location was leased to accommodate incremental stock holding of material intrinsic to the provision of Resiliency as a
Service (RaaS)®. :
Depreciation, amortisation, and impairment of non-financial assets was £0.09m (2024: £0.11m). Averaged monthly this
was an effective increase of 132% and was mainly due to the acquisition of assets of £0.12m utilised to support the
delivery of Resiliency as a Service (RaaS)®.
. The net asset position of the company is £5.5m (2024: £3.1m).
Current assets have increased from £5.7m to £8.5m, a £2.8m uplift. Cash has reduced from £2.1m to £0.2m but has been
offset by an increase in amounts owed by group undertakings, £2.3m that was nil in the prior period. Trade and other
debtors have increased by £2.1m to £4.1m from £2.0m and is reflective of additional turnover and accrued unbilled
income from Resiliency as a Service (RaaS)® work. There has been no change from the prior period to concentration of
credit risk and our experience of bad debts remains comparatively low.
Stocks have increased by £0.1m from £1.6m to £1.7m. Resiliency as a Service (RaaS)® stock is billed to customers on
acquisition and held on consignment thus not materially effecting overall stock value.
Current liabilities have increased by £0.6m from £2.8m to £3.4m. There is some timing difference associated with
year-end date change that has affected general accruals, £0.1m less and vat liabilities, £0.2m more. Corporation tax
liabilities are £0.2m higher than prior period due to increased turnover and profit, net of tax payments on account. Vat
Liabilities are also higher, £0.2m due to higher turnover (partial quarter 2/3) than the prior period (full quarter 3/3).
Non-current liabilities are materially unchanged from the prior period.
