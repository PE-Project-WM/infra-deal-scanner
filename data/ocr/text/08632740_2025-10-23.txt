Monty UK Global Limited’s turnover decreased by 15% when compared to prior year (2023: 3% decrease),
with a net profit before tax margin of 0.9% (2023: 3.3%). In 2024, the Company experienced a decrease in

transitioned from a commitment-based model to a consumption model effective 2024, which led to the toss
of deals with suppliers who were unwilling to comply with the newly adopted approach. Despite these
challenges, the Company successfully increased its gross profit margin percentage from 11% in 2023 to
12% in 2024, reflecting improved operational efficiency and profitability.
Accordingly, operating profits decreased by 74% (2023: 43% decrease) in line with a decrease in profit after
tax by 79% (2023: 42% decrease). An exchange loss was recorded standing at GBP 1,491,101 (2023:
exchange gain GBP 402,532). We continue to attempt to minimize the impact of foreign currency exchange
tate movement by purchasing and selling our services in the same currencies where possible and improving
our payment terms where possible to minimize loss in value that results from late payment.

Going concern
The company's business activities, the factors likely to affect its future development and position, and the
principal risks and uncertainties faced by the company, are set out in the Strategic Report. The directors
have considered the company's forecasts and funding available to it for a period to 31 October 2026.

services offered for example due to adverse and disruptive changes in technology that shift demand in the
direction of alternatives, other than the core sales stream of the group. © :
The company concluded that this risk did not significantly affect the company’s operations and financial
performance. In fact, the company is meeting its day-to-day working capital requirements, and will be able
to meet medium and long term commitments based on the budgeted figures prepared to 31 October 2026.
The current budget was developed by management to capture probable scenarios for a period to 31
October 2026. This includes downside scenario modelling and reverse stress testing. The projected figures
along with the actual figures to date, show an increase in the company’s profitability in 2025 compared to
2024. The positive cash position of £2,707,223 at the 31 December 2024 year end is sufficient to support
: the’ day to day operations of the company (2023: £4,739,869). Management have reviewed the
recoverability of its receivables and continually review the credit worthiness of its customer base.
Management have concluded that receivables are recoverable and forecasted cashflows support the ability :
to continue to meet its obligations as they fall due. The company is expected to remain profitable and cash ~
generative for the entire Going Concern period to 31 October 2026. This is true for downside scenarios
modelled.
The company is the guarantor for a loan held by its ultimate parent undertaking (see note 22). The ultimate
parent undertaking is in discussions with the financing institution regarding early repayment. The Group's
forecasts demonstrate an ability to repay the loan under this scenario. The Group and the company are
satisfied this does not represent a material uncertainty in respect of going concern.

2.6 Leases

The company as a lessee

The company assesses whether a contract is or contains a lease, at inception of a contract. The company

more representative of the time pattern in which economic benefits from the leased asset are consumed.

The lease liability is initially measured at the present value of the lease payments that are not paid at the

commencement date, discounted by using the rate implicit in the lease. If this rate cannot be readily

determined, the company uses its incremental borrowing rate. The incremental borrowing rate is based on
the rate applicable to working capital loans and overdrafts available to the company.

the lease liability (using the effective interest method) and by reducing the carrying amount to reflect the

lease payments made.

The right-of-use assets comprise the initial measurement of the corresponding lease liability, lease .

- Provision for expected credit losses of trade receivables

The company uses a provision matrix to calculate ECLs for trade receivables. The provision rates are based
on days past due for groupings of various customer segments that have similar loss patterns (i.e., by
geography, product type, customer type and rating, and coverage by credit insurance). Due to low loss
rates, a consistent rate across aging groups was considered appropriate for ECL estimation for the year.
The provision matrix is initially based on the company’s historical observed default rates. The company will
calibrate the matrix to adjust the historical credit loss experience with forward-looking information. For
instance, if forecast economic conditions are expected to deteriorate over the next year which can lead to
an increased number of defaults in the telecommunication sector, the historical default rates are adjusted.
At every reporting date, the historical observed default rates are updated and changes in the forward-
looking estimates are analysed. The assessment of the correlation between historical observed default
rates, forecast economic conditions and ECLs is a significant estimate. The amount of ECLs is sensitive to
changes in circumstances and of forecast economic conditions. The company’s historical credit loss
experience and forecast of economic conditions may also not be representative of customer's actual default
in the future.
Disputed trade receivables are specifically provided for until the counterparty agrees to settle the dispute
or an agreement is reached to discharge the receivable (in which case it is written off). For back to back
payable disputes raised liabilities are not extinguished until agreement is reached with the counterparty or
the entity is discharged from its obligations as a result of the passage of time. Estimates relating to the
historic loss rate incorporated into the credit loss provision include receivables that have been written off
as a result of disputes.
