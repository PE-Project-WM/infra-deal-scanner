Regulatory , . a
_ The Group operates in regulated utility markets in the UK. - . :
The Energy Act 2023 established OFGEM as the regulator for heat networks in Great Britain, implemented . ,
fo, . through the Heat Networks (Market Framework) {Great Britain) Regulations 2025. OFGEM’s authorisation
conditions apply from January 2026. Existing heat networks will be deemed authorised but must register with °
- OFGEM by January 2027. After this transition period, all new heat networks will need authorisation from
OFGEM to operate. The Group do not currently expect this to have a material adverse effect on the Groups 7
: future business prospects, . ; :
- : In July 2025, the UK Government announced that OFWAT will be abolished and replaced by a new single water :
regulator. The government intends to introduce legislation for this change, however there is currently not an .
implementation date set and OFWAT will continue to operate during this transition period. The Group will .
continue to monitor developments in relation to these changes but expects the short term impact to be co,
. limited. :
The UK Government has proposed the Future Homes and Buildings Standard (FHBS) to aid its commitment to
- moving the UK towards its goal of net zero GHG emissions by 2050. The FHBS will require new homes to be
: built with low-carbon heating alternatives rather than natural gas boilers. However, in June 2025, the UK :
government published its “Midstream Gas System: Update to the market” which confirms that gas will
continue to play a vital role in the UK’s energy system during the transition to net zero and will retain a role
. post-2050, using carbon-neutral gas from green or offset sources. Regulations are currently being developed,
_with implementation expected in the coming years following a transitional period. ,
The Group is not aware of any other undisclosed proposed changes impacting the existing regulatory :
frameworks in which it operates. Any such change could have either a positive or negative impact on our
" future business prospects. The Group has carried on investing in both regulated and non- regulated markets, :
diversifying its regulatory risk. ~ . a
