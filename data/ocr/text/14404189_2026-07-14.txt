STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Review of the business
The principal activity of GoFibre Broadband Limited (‘the Company”) is to provide high speed and reliable
broadband connectivity services to residential and commercial customers across Scotland and the North East of
England. The Company aims to become Scotland's largest independent internet service provider.
Overall, the Company has delivered strong growth during the year and has been recognised as one of Scotland’s
fastest growing companies. This performance reflects the continued commercial success of the Company as an
independent broadband provider operating in a highly competitive market. Growth has been supported by continued
network expansion and increasing market demand for high-quality fibre connectivity. Customer numbers have
almost doubled over the period, reflecting both the successful execution of the Company's overall growth strategy
and its increasing brand recognition within its operational footprint. This growth has been underpinned by the
Company’s ambitions and people focused culture, enabling it to expand its customer base while maintaining a
distinctive hyper-local and customer centric approach. This combination has supported both customer acquisition
and the strengthening of long-term customer relationships, positioning the business well for continued expansion.
The Company continued to strengthen its reputation as a trusted customer focused broadband provider, supported
by consistently strong customer satisfaction metrics and independent industry recognition. During 2025, GoFibre
maintained excellent Trustpilot and NPS scores, both being significantly above the industry average within the UK
telecommunication sector. This reflects the continued positive customer feedback regarding service reliability,
installation quality and customer support. In addition, the Company received several industry and business
accolades during the year, including being named Scottish Broadband Provider of the Year.
These achievements reinforce the Company's strategic focus on delivering high-quality broadband infrastructure
alongside a differentiated customer experience, particularly within rural and underserved communities across
Scotland and the North of England.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Company operates within a highly competitive and rapidly evolving telecommunications market and is exposed
to several strategic, operational, financial and regulatory risks which could impact the success of its long-term
objectives. .
A principal risk facing the business is customer acquisition and retention across its service areas. The Company's
ability to achieve sustainable growth is dependent on increasing customer penetration across the network footprint
and maintaining strong levels of customer satisfaction and retention. Competitive pressures from larger, well-
established broadband and telecommunications providers may impact the customer growth, pricing strategy and
market share, particularly in areas where network overbuild and promotional activity exists.
The broadband market continues to experience pricing pressure and evolving customer expectations in relation to
service quality, speed, reliability, and value. The Board therefore continues to closely monitor market conditions,
competitor activity, and customer demand trends to ensure the Company maintains an appropriate balance between
commercial competitiveness, customer experience, and sustainable profitability. :
The business operates within a regulated sector and is therefore subject to ongoing compliance obligations relating
to telecommunications regulation, consumer protection, and government-funded broadband programmes. Changes
in regulatory requirements, market conditions, or government policy may impact the Company's operations,
commercial model, or growth strategy. :
Despite these risks, the Board believes the Company remains well positioned to benefit from increasing demand for
high-quality full fibre broadband services across underserved communities in Scotland and the North of England.
The Company's growing customer base, strong customer satisfaction metrics, recognised service quality, and
strategic market positioning continue to support its long-term growth ambitions.

are required to be recognised as part of the cost of stock or fixed assets.

The cost of any unused holiday entitlement is recognised in the period in which the employee’s services are

received.

Termination benefits are recognised immediately as an expense when the company is demonstrably

committed to terminate the employment of an employee or to provide termination benefits.

Retirement benefits

For defined contribution schemes the amount charged to profit or loss is the contributions payable in the year.

Differences between contributions payable in the year and contributions actually paid are shown as either

accruals or prepayments. .

Rentals payable under operating leases, including any lease incentives received, are charged to profit or loss
‘on a straight line basis over the term of the relevant lease except where another more systematic basis is
