Principal activities
Ecosurety is a high-growth packaging compliance and data business.
We see a future where every brand can access the data and insights they need to make smart and sustainable
packaging decisions which reduce material use, waste and carbon emissions, and encourage better
procurement choices across the packaging lifecycle. Small changes, scaled across our largest members, create
significant improvements. .
Ecosurety enables its clients to save costs, optimise processes and ensure compliance while reducing their
environmental impact. Today, Ecosurety is the trusted packaging compliance partner to global brands, and we
continue to deliver on our purpose: to rid the world of unnecessary packaging.
We operate a UK packaging producer responsibility compliance scheme alongside a fast-growing packaging
data services business. We are proud to serve an impressive range of leading global brand owners and retailers,
with demanding and complex reporting and compliance requirements. Our UK heritage provides the platform for
our expanding international data services capability.
Ecosurety is distinguished by the combination of its proprietary data and analytics platform, deep regulatory
expertise, and the hard-earned trust of its blue-chip client base. We are a certified B-Corp, having improved our
score from 83.7 to 100.2 on recertification in 2024. Our team is committed to using existing and emerging
technology responsibly to push boundaries, support our mission and achieve our growth ambitions for the benefit
of future generations.
Business review
The Directors are pleased with the strong operational and financial performance delivered in 2025. Despite
economic uncertainty and pressures on the UK economy, Ecosurety has strengthened its core compliance
business, accelerated the growth of its data and advisory services capability, and continued to deliver fast and
profitable growth.
Ecosurety operates in the large UK packaging compliance and data services market, which is growing at
approximately 12% per annum, driven by increasing regulatory complexity, rising compliance costs and growing
demand for packaging data insights. We have also gained share with our core PRN (Packaging Recycling
Notes) market — growing from 9% in 2018 to 18% in 2025. This growth reflects a differentiated value proposition
combining deep regulatory expertise, a proprietary data and analytics platform, and high-touch account
management.
The quality of Ecosurety's service and client relationships is evidenced by a Net Promoter Score of 57 in 2025,
which has doubled since 2023. Approximately 60% of net revenue originates from clients who have been with
Ecosurety for six years or more, and net revenue per client has tripled over the 2021-25 period. The Ecosurety
Hub, our client web portal, continues to provide quick and easy access to data, and we have expanded our
services into new Extended Producer Responsibility areas including re-use and refill. We have maintained high
levels of staff engagement and continue to deliver a high-quality service in close collaboration with the
Environment Agency, DEFRA, PackUK and a wide range of industry bodies, trade associations and recycling
partners.

Aims and Objectives
The Ecosurety business is focused on four clear strategic objectives: (i) to protect and grow the core UK
packaging compliance business through service quality, competitive pricing and deep regulatory relationships: (ii)
to invest in and scale our proprietary data and analytics platform to support our clients to reduce costs and make
better strategic decisions, leveraging Ecosurety’s technology infrastructure, data and relationships; (iii) to expand
our compliance, data and advisory services internationally, helping clients go beyond compliance to optimise
their packaging decisions; and (iv) to extend into voluntary extended producer responsibility and reusable
packaging systems, positioning the business ahead of evolving regulation. -
These strategic objectives are underpinned by the company’s values of “Protect”, “Inspire” and “Transform” and
by a commitment to driving measurable environmental outcomes — diverting packaging material from
incineration and landfitl and helping build towards a more circular and sustainable packaging ecosystem.
By continuing to make targeted investments in our proprietary data platform and technology, over the long term
we will develop a global multi-sided platform which will service the requirements of all stakeholders in the
packaging ecosystem, thereby enabling Ecosurety to deliver lasting environmental impact at scale.
Key performance indicators
Ecosurety monitors KPIs closely, focusing on the impact being delivered through packaging recovery note (PRN)
evidence purchases, strong operating margin, cash generation, recycling tonnage growth and other initiatives
beyond compliance.
Employee engagement remained strong in 2025, continuing to reflect a high level of engagement across the
organisation. This was supported by initiatives including our award of Carbon Literate Gold status in 2025.
Further detail is provided in our annual Ecosurety Impact Report.”
Financial KPIs
Turnover for the year increased from £70.5m (in 2024) to £91.7m (in 2025). This growth is attributable to an
increase in the market price of PRNs, which form a key component of our revenue streams, as well as growth in

. data services revenue. In addition to growing the number of clients, the total tonnage of recycling evidence
managed during the year increased, and the average price per tonne of PRNs also grew vs. the prior year.
With the increase in PRN volume and an increase in consultancy services, the total gross profit increased from
£14m in 2024 to £16.6m in 2025.
This, combined with a continuing investment in technology and operating expenses to support the expansion in
data services, led to the delivery of an underlying EBITDA of £8.5m in 2025, compared to £6.6m in 2024.
At year end, the net current assets of the company were at £3.1m in 2025 (2024 - £6.4m) with a cash balance at
the end of 2025 of £18.4m (2024 - £18.4m).

Progress against our impact framework
Last year, we introduced a new impact framework to measure the positive contribution we make to the issues
that matter most to our stakeholders and to us. Below is an overview of progress across our three impact pillars.
4. Influencing better packaging being placed on the market
We launched a new dashboard that provides members with insights into the recyclability and estimated CO2e
emissions of their packaging, using their Recyclability Assessment Methodology (RAM) outputs. The tool helps
members identify opportunities for improvement by packaging type, product, material and supplier, enabling
more informed decisions to reduce environmental impact.
The tool is available to members who provide granular packaging data. For those who do not yet have this level
of data but wish to access the insights, we also offer support to improve their data coverage over time.

2. Driving an increase in quality recycling
After three years of trials across 10 local authorities, covering more than 160,000 households and collecting over
400 tonnes of material, our Flexible Plastic Fund FlexCollect project demonstrated that kerbside collections of
flexible plastic packaging can be delivered successfully at scale.
In September 2025, we launched our blueprint for action, sharing the tools and learnings needed by local
authorities, government and industry to roll out kerbside collections of flexible plastic packaging ahead of the
introduction of Simpler Recycling in 2027.
‘ 3. Driving the adoption of reusable packaging
In July 2025, we introduced Lendable, a free, frictionless reuse scheme designed to remove the barriers that
have historically held reuse back. Focused on food and drink packaging for on-the-go use, the scheme has been
developed in close collaboration with brands and hospitality partners, with plans to implement it in Bristol in 2026.
Social performance
e = Staff satisfaction
We monitor staff satisfaction through a monthly pulse survey. In 2025, our average employee engagement score
was 82%. While this represents a 5% decrease compared to 2024, engagement remains strong overall.
In response to these results and the qualitative feedback gathered through the surveys, we introduced regular
wellbeing check-ins, optional social activities, and a mentoring programme for women and non-binary
employees. We also promoted access to our Employee Assistance Programme (EAP), which provides all staff
with free, confidential, 24/7 support, including mental health support, financial advice, and guidance on physical
wellbeing.
e¢ Customers satisfaction :
We continue to track customer satisfaction through regular Net Promoter Score (NPS) surveys. Our average
NPS for the year was +58, which is classed as excellent and represents an improvement of 17 points from 2024.
Environmental performance

Impairment of financial assets :
At the end of each reporting period financial assets measured at amortised cost are assessed for
objective evidence of impairment. If an asset is impaired the impairment loss is the difference
between the carrying amount and the present value of the estimated cash flows discounted at the
asset's original effective interest rate. The impairment loss is recognised in profit or ioss.
Financial assets are impaired when events, subsequent to their initial recognition, indicate the
estimated future cash flows derived from the financial asset(s) have been adversely impacted. The
impairment loss will be the difference between the current carrying amount and the present value of
the future cash flows at the asset(s) original effective interest rate.
If there is a favourable change in relation to the events surrounding the impairment loss then the
impairment can be reviewed for possible reversal. The reversal will not cause the current carrying
amount to exceed the original carrying amount had the impairment not been recognised. The
