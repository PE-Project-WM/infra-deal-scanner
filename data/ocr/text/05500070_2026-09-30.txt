Strategic Report
for the Year Ended 31 December 2025
The directors present their strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2025 for BroadSoft Ltd ("the
Company").
Principal activities
The principal activities of the Company are:
* Providing a software-as-a-service or SaaS voice over internet protocol (VoIP) offering that enable
telecommunications service providers to deliver hosted, cloud-based Unified Communications, or UC, to their
enterprise customers.
* Providing sales and/or sales-related support services, customer support services, and general and
administrative services to BroadSoft, Inc. and Cisco Systems, Inc.
Review of the business
The turnover for the financial year was £419,813 (2024: £4,295,687 as restated). The loss before taxation for the
financial year was £229,342 (2024: £61,000 profil as restated). The net assets as al 31 December 2025 were
£3,267,527 (2024: £1,550,105 liabilities as restated).
While we are secing an acccleration in the adoption of cloud communications, and increased demand from our
customers, the Company is currently working in parallel towards the full integration of our customer base into
the core Cisco Group legal entities. The decrease in turnover is a direct result of our efforts to streamline our
offerings through these core entities, and to provide our customers with a single buying experience. Some of the
offers sold by BroadSoft Ltd reached end of sale, as equivalent offers are made available to our customers via
the core Cisco Group legal entities.
For the Company's future developments and overall strategy, please refer to the Directors’ Report.
Key financial and performance indicators
There are no specific key performance indicators (KPIs) operating at the Company's level. KPIs operate at the
Cisco Systems, Inc. group business unit level. For this reason, the Company's directors believe that analysis
using KPIs for the Company is not necessary or appropriate for an understanding of the development,
performance or position of the business BroadSoft Ltd.

Impact of Group-wide policies and procedures on employee and stakeholder engagement

group which has an impact on the long-term development of the Company. Primary responsibility for key

policies affecting employees, suppliers and business relationships with stakeholders are implemented by the

Financial risk management
i) Credit Risk

The Company's principal financial assets are cash, trade and other receivables. The Company's credit risk is
primarily attributable to amounts owed by group undertakings (intercompany receivables), with trade
receivables being immaterial. The amounts presented in the balance sheet are net of allowances for doubtful
receivables. An allowance for. impairment is made where there is an identified loss event which, based on
previous experience, is evidence of a reduction in the recoverability of the cash flows. The risk is mitigated by
credit assessment reviews prior to accepting new orders and ensuring the history of payment supports extending
new credit.
The credit risk on cash balances is limited because the counterparties are banks with high credit ratings.
ii) Liquidity Risk :
In order to maintain liquidity to ensure that sufficient funds are available for ongoing operations and future

. developments, the Company is funded via trading relationships with group companies. The risk is mitigated by
periodic review and clearance of these inter-company balances.
The Company has no financing from external banking institutions.
iii) Customer Risk
If we experience a loss of one or more significant customers or have a substantial reduction in orders from one
or more of our significant customers or distribution partners and we are unable to sell directly or indirectly to
new customers or increase orders from other existing customers to offset lost revenue, our business could be
harmed. In order to mitigate this risk and drive growth, our sales strategy anticipates future changes in revenues,
and we actively build and foster relationships with existing and new service partners. The Company has also
introduced its customers to similar offerings within the Cisco group as a result of our efforts to streamline our
offerings.
iv) Competition Risk
Many of our existing and potential competitors have substantially greater financial, technical, marketing,
intellectual property and distribution resources than we have. Their resources may enable them to develop
superior products, or they could aggressively price, finance and bundle their product offerings to attempt to gain
market adoption or to increase market share. If we lack sufficient financial or other resources to maintain or
improve our competitive position, our business and financial condition may be harmed.
The Company is utilising the resources available within the wider Cisco Group to make sure that its product
offerings remain competitive in the market.
'v) Price Risk
Our business in part depends upon the success of service providers who are vulnerable to competition from
alternative IP-based communications providers who offer services free or for low cost, If service providers do
not succeed in growing and maintaining their IP subscriber base for any reason, including failure to effectively
compete with competitors offering free or low-cost services, then our business, financial condition and result of
operations could be harmed.
The Company is utilising the resources available within the wider Cisco Group to make sure that its prices

: remain competitive and affordable to its customer base.

vi) Security Risk
If our security measures are breached or unauthorized access to data on our cloud-based networks is otherwise
obtained, our hosted services may be perceived as being vulnerable causing customers to reduce their use of, or
stop using, our hosted services. This could lead to negative publicity that causes our reputation and brand to be
damaged, our business may suffer, and we could incur significant legal fines and other financial liabilities.
The Company benefits from working with some of the best global businesses with extremely high security
standards, requiring regular and detailed audits of their suppliers.
vii) Legislative Risk :
Use of our BroadCloud offerings can involve the storage and transmission of an end-user's business and
personally identifiable information. Virtually every jurisdiction in which we operate has established its own data

. security and privacy legal framework which we must comply with, including the General Data Protection
Regulation established in the European Union.
The Company has invested heavily in its products, platforms and processes in order to ensure market leading
technology and practice are built-in to the solutions provided to its customers. The Company also follows
detailed procedures for the regular monitoring and risk assessment of ongoing services to make sure that they
are in line with the requirements of applicable regulations.
