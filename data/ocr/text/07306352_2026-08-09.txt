The Directors present their Strategic Report of the Company for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Business review and principal activities
Colt Data Centre Services UK Limited (the “Company” or “Colt DCS”) provides data centre services, within the group
comprising Colt Group Holdings Limited and its subsidiaries (‘the Group”), enabling customers to store their
infrastructure and information in a safe and secure way. .
The Company's operates 2 datacentres in Welwyn Garden City and West London.
In addition to its core data centre operations, the Company has expanded its service offering to include consulting,
management and operational services. These services support other data centres within both the Group and other
related entities. These services include activities such as land sourcing, leasing, development, and operational
services.
The Company’s strategy is based around a clear purpose and vision: to transform the way the world works through
the power of digital infrastructure and to become the most customer centric business in its industry. The Company
aims to achieve operational excellence in the sustainable design, build, delivery and operational management of
hyperscale data centres.
The Directors expect the Company to continue these activities in the future.

Cyber incident on the Group and its impact
The Colt Group suffered a Cyber incident in August 2025, which impacted all business support systems, further
requiring manual processes to be implemented for critical activities. The Colt DCS data centres were not affected by
the incident.
The Colt Group took action to remove the threat actor from our environment, and proactively took some systems
offline, leading to disruption to some elements of our services. The Colt Group brought in specialist third-party
investigation, forensic and cyber support and have implemented enhanced containment and security measures to
further harden our systems as well as help prevent similar incidents in the future.
The Colt Group took systems offline as a precaution and worked to restore services. The Colt Group implemented
additional controls and completed the restoration of the majority its systems in December 2025. Financial reporting
systems were not impacted by the activities of the threat actor and no further threat actor activity was identified within
the Colt Group's network environment since the restoration of systems.
The Colt Group proactively notified the relevant regulators and authorities and will continue to work closely with law
enforcement agencies. The cyber incident caused temporary disruption in the Colt DCS business in the second half
of 2025.
Change and transformation program
Colt DCS continues to execute on a significant change agenda. In prior years, Colt DCS underwent the legal
separation of the Colt DCS businesses into separate legal entities in the Colt Group. Colt DCS is now delivering on
a major transformation programme to separate its systems and services from the rest of the Colt Group, enabling
Colt DCS to focus on its own processes to support growth ambitions, security requirements, and a standalone
business operating model.
