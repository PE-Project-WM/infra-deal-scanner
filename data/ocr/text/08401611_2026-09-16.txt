PRINCIPAL ACTIVITIES
The Company's principal activity is the sourcing, processing and fulfilment of mobile phone products and the
provision of business process outsourcing services to the telecoms and insurance industry. The income statement and
the balance sheet are set out on pages 13 and 14. :
REVIEW OF BUSINESS
The Directors are pleased with the significant progress that has been made to date where the Company continues to
gain market share with new high value contracts with large high-profile banking, retail and telecom providers.
Likewize Services UK Limited is a key member of a group of companies, to which Likewize Services UK Limited
supports as a fulfilment and repair provider for mobile phones. The Company completed 2025 having successfully
transitioned to its core strategic customer base following the customer transitions undertaken in the prior year.
Revenue for the year was £180.4m (2024: £187.6m). The decrease reflects purchasing inventory more strategically,
and this is aligned to key disposition channels. It is worth noting new long terms contracts in period with a large PLC
bank, and multiple contracts with large scale OEM’s were signed in period. The business and industry continue to
see increased volume and demand of used device sales. Operating loss for the year was £1.9m (2024: loss £1.7m).
The result reflects depreciation associated with prior investment in customer service, fulfilment and technology
infrastructure. Operational efficiencies continued to improve during the year, supported by the launch ofa significant
new contract with a major UK network carrier in the fourth quarter. Net assets as at 31 December 2025 were £35.6m
(2024: £19.4m), the increase relating to capital contribution in period.

Financial Risk Management
The Company’s exposure to the risk of changes in market interest rates relates to its foreign denominated receivables
and payables, short-term borrowings, cash and short-term deposits, which have fixed and floating interest rates. All
surplus funds are managed daily to ensure maximisation of interest receivable or reduction of interest payable in the
. medium term.
Inflation risk
The Company is consistently monitoring inflationary risk, the highest of which come through procurement
of goods, and provision of labour. The Company operates with suppliers outside of fixed terms at regular
intervals, which ensures that the Company can adjust selling prices in the same period as any inflationary
increases incurred. Recent inflationary pressures have primarily arisen through labour cost increases. The
Company has not experienced any material adverse impact on demand or sales volumes as a result.
Foreign currency risk
The Company has transactional currency exposures arising from sales and purchases in currencies other than
their functional currency. The Company uses spot currency contracts, or use of a global multi-currency
cashpool to eliminate currency exposures on all transactions once the Company has entered into a firm
commitment for a sale or purchase.
Credit risk
The Company extends credit only to recognised, creditworthy third parties. It is the Company's policy that all
customers who wish to trade on credit terms are subject to credit verification procedures. In addition, trade
debtor balances are monitored on an on-going basis to ensure that the Company’s exposure to bad debts is
minimised.
Liquidity risk
The Company aims to maintain a balance between continuity of funding and flexibility by ensuring that
sufficient borrowing facilities are in place by reference to forecast debt levels.

FUTURE DEVELOPMENTS
Post year-end financial performance has improved for Likewize Services UK Limited, supported largely by
the continued growth of its 2025 large new contracts, and a further increased market growth of used device
collections in period. Likewize Services UK Limited continues to carefully monitor the interest and
inflationary increases within 2026 and the impact that it may have on its supply chain, people and clients. To
date this has not impacted operations or significantly the financial results of the Company.
The Directors are satisfied with the prospects of the business and continue to invest across all areas of the
Company, including the implementation of new insurance contracts with a major UK banking and financial
services customer, while also progressing the implementation of a new ERP platform. The business is
expected to return to higher profitability through a continued focus on cost control, operational efficiencies
arising from recent investments, and a strategic shift towards services with more stable margins
Although there remains uncertainty with sterling and the UK economy, the Company maintains an improved
balance sheet to allow it to take advantage of any new opportunities that arise in the next financial year. The
Company has not experienced any material impact from geopolitical developments including the conflict
involving the US and Iran.
The widespread adoption of artificial intelligence across the economy is seen as a benefit to both the Company
and the wider Likewize Group to which it supports. :

GOING CONCERN
. Inflationary and interest rates have an impact on the Company as with many companies, however the Company has

a large portfolio of key customer contracts with long term contracts, which incorporate protective measures, in
addition to the relevant services that the Company offers, the products are both a key and desirable commodity in
any economic situation which gives high demand for its services. The Company has continued its path to exit lower
margin and riskier businesses relating mainly to traditional new device distribution and accessories and continues
to monitor carefully, at a granular level, of detail all areas of business. This is reflected in management’s going
concern analysis.
The Company is a subsidiary within the Likewize Group of companies ("the Group") whose ultimate parent entity
is Genstar (see Note 23). The Company is under common management of the Group and can benefit from Group
support when needed. This support is in the form of a USD 100m facility provided to Likewize Services UK Limited
from Likewize Netherlands IHB, a fellow Likewize Group company. This facility expires in December 2032, with
an automatic renewal and is repayable on demand. In assessing the actual and potential future impacts the Group
sensitised forecasts and projections have been considered through to end of December 2027 which show that the
Group should be able to meet its liabilities as they fall due. The cash balances and available unutilised facilities
allow more than sufficient headroom even when the business model is stress tested. The Company has prepared a
reasonably possible worst-case scenario for the Company for the next 12 months whilst having supporting potential
of utilisation of its existing equity or Group facility. In this scenario and worse such cases the Company would
have sufficient funding availability, assuming the Group facility remains available for the next 12 months post
signing of these accounts.
As the Company has historically received capital from its parent together with access to a Group cash pool facility,

Likewize Corp, stating that it will provide any necessary funding to ensure that the Company has adequate funding

statements. The Directors have assessed the willingness and ability of the Group to provide such support and, taking
