Introduction
This strategic report for Zzoomm plc has been prepared for the entire business, and references to Zzoomm, The Group or
The Company reflect the whole business.

Strategic overview and outlook
The Group is a fast-growing Full Fibre network operator, providing FTTP broadband in underserved market towns and
small urban areas. The Group has built extensive infrastructure, delivering high-quality service to a growing customer
base, leading to long-term returns. The Group focuses on value, reliability, and customer satisfaction, positioning itself
as a strong challenger brand. The Group ceased large scale network construction at the end of 2024 and growth will now
arise from adding connections rather than increasing the footprint.
In January 2025 Zzoomm announced a merger with the Iris Infra group of companies including Full Fibre Limited and
BeFibre Limited, which completed in March 2025 following regulatory approval. The combined Group has 602,000
properties Ready For Service operating in 90 towns being managed on a single technology platform. At the end of the
year the Group had 86,000 customers and is ranked as the fifth largest Alt-Net in the UK by footprint. The Group
continues to explore strategic options to enhance shareholder value by leveraging its established infrastructure.
The newly created Group Holding / parent company, Altnet Partners Holdco Limited, is owned by Iris Infra Holdco
Limited & ZGL Investment Limited, and the Group continues to be backed by funds managed by Oaktree Capital
Management & Basalt Infrastructure Partners Ill.
The substance of the business combination is that Iris Infra Limited acquired Zzoomm plc in a reverse takeover, and
therefore the Group accounts are presented of Iris Infra Limited for the full period and Zzoomm plc from the date of
acquisition (March 2025). ,
As part of the transaction, the loan of £100m from ING, Hamburg Commercial Bank and Kommunalkredit Austria was
extended by one year to September 2027.

Markets
Approximately 31 rittion properties exist in the UK, with 24 million having access to Full Fibre-lu-the-Premises
broadband. Demand for high-speed, reliable internet continues to grow due to the increasing reliance on online
entertainment, communication, and multiple connected devices. As a result of ongoing investment, Fibre-to-the-
Premises availability in the UK has reached 76%. The consumer market has remained challenging with continued cost of
living pressures and increasing competition.
Network and infrastructure
The group builds and operates its own Full Fibre-to-the-Premises network, supporting up to 150 devices per connection
with symmetrical speeds ranging from 200Mbps to 2Gbps. Residential and most business services use XGS-PON
technology, capable of delivering up to 8.5Gbps to each property. For enterprises, the group provides high-capacity
dedicated services over 10Gbps or 100Gbps bearers. The network is built using a mix of underground ducts, utility poles,
and BT Openreach infrastructure (“PIA”).
Construction
The group has ceased building new network coverage.

Financial Risk Management Objectives and Policies
Liquidity Risk
Liquidity risk is the risk that the Group will not be able to meet its short-term financial obligation as they fall due. As part
of its overall prudent tiquidity risk management, the Group actively manages its operating cash flows and does not
commit to expenditure that it does not have immediate access to settle.
Cashflow Risk :
Cashflow risk is that the fair value or cash flows of financial instruments will fluctuate as a result of market forces outside
the control of the group, including interest rate risk arising from the effect of changes in market interest rates on cash at
bank, overdrafts, investments in debt instruments, borrowings and obligations under finance leases.
The Group has taken out hedging instruments to mitigate the interest rate risk on the borrowings.
