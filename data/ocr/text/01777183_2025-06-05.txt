The board of directors (the “Board”) presents herewith their strategic report, directors’ report
and audited financial statements (‘financial statements”) of Dealogic Limited for the financial
year ended 31 December 2024.
PRINCIPAL ACTIVITIES, REVIEW OF THE BUSINESS AND FUTURE DEVELOPMENTS
The principal activities of the Company are to develop and market content, data, analytics and
software solutions to participants of the global capital markets. The Company intends to
continue to grow organically.
Financial Performance indicators
The Company's key measures of financial performance are Revenue, EBITDA (earnings before
interest, taxation, depreciation and amortisation), Profit after taxation and Net Cash Flow.
Revenue
The Company's total revenue was £132.2 million in 2024 and £118.4 million in 2023. The
increase in total revenue for 2024 as compared to 2023 is £13.8 million or 11.7%.
EBITDA
Earnings before interest, taxation, depreciation and amortisation was £83.4 million in 2024 and
£80.5 million in 2023. The increase in EBITDA for 2024 as compared to 2023 is £2.9 million or :
3.6%.
Profit after Taxation
Profit after taxation was £77.6 million in 2024, and £76.6 million in 2023. The increase in profit
after taxation for 2024 as compared to 2023 is £1.0 million or 1.3%. See the Statement of
Comprehensive Income for further details.
Net Cash Flow
The Company's cash balance is £1.0 million at 31 December 2024 (2023: £4.8 million). The
decrease in net cash flows for 2024 compared to 2023 is £3.8 million.
PRINCIPAL RISKS AND UNCERTAINTIES ;
The principal risks and uncertainties which the Company faces are:

e The Company derives a significant portion of its revenues from customers in the
financial services sector. The Company's business, financial condition and operating
results could be adversely affected by significant changes in that sector;

e Consolidation of customers as well as cost-cutting across our customer base could
impact the Company's available markets and revenue growth; .

e The Company generates a significant percentage of its revenues from recurring,
subscription-based arrangements, and the Company's ability to maintain existing
revenues and to generate higher revenues is dependent in part on maintaining a high
customer retention rate;

(h) Investment in subsidiaries
Investments in subsidiaries are initially recognised at cost. Subsequent to initial

(i) Impairment of non-financial assets
The Company assesses at each reporting date whether there is an indication that an
asset may be impaired. If any such indication exists, or wnen annual-impairment testing
for an asset is required, the Company makes an estimate of the asset's recoverable
amount in order to determine the extent of the impairment loss. An asset's recoverable
amount is the higher of an asset's (or cash-generating unit's) fair value less costs to sell
and its value in use and is determined at the individual asset level, unless the asset does
not generate cash inflows that are largely independent of those from other assets or group
of assets. Where the carrying amount of an asset exceeds its recoverable amount, the
asset is considered impaired and is written down to its recoverable amount. Impairment
losses are recognised in the Statement of Comprehensive Income. .
