Principal risks and uncertainties
The following is a discussion of the key risks facing the Company together with a summary of the Company's
approach to managing those risks.
: Foreign currency risk

The Company's exposure to foreign currency risk mainly relates to the currency exposure on the service of
interest and capital on euro-denominated debt, payment of management fees and other charges to the ultimate
parent Company and other foreign currency-denominated purchases. The Company's policy is to hedge known
currency exposures as they arise. The Company enters into forward currency purchase contracts to fix the
sterling price for future foreign currency-denominated transactions. :
Health and safety risk
The health and safety of all employees, contractors, agency staff and the public is a key risk given the nature of
the company's business. To minimise this risk the Company is committed to creating a culture that views safe
working as the only way of working and to reviewing all our processes and procedures to ensure they implement
this. ‘Training is provided to managers to ensure they understand their responsibility for the safety of employees
that they set to work. In addition, a confidential helpline has been set up for the use of anyone within the
organisation to help eradicate unsafe practices and safeguard our stakeholders.
Taxation, penalty and reputational risk
Taxation risk is the risk that the company suffers losses arising from additional tax charges, financial penalties,
or reputational damage. These risks could arise from failure to comply with procedures required by tax
authorities, the interpretation of tax law, or changes in tax law. The company has mitigated this risk by ~
implementation of effective, well documented, and controlled processes to ensure compliance with tax disclosure
and filing obligations. This is further supported by the use of appropriate advice from professional firms.
Liquidity risk
Liquidity risk is the risk that the proceeds from financial assets are not sufficient to fund the obligations arising
from liabilities as they fall due. The Company's exposure to liquidity risk is reduced by its borrowing facilities in
place provided by its shareholder.
Financial key performance indicators

gross margin of 5% (2023: 15%) and negative EBITDA of £8,673,089 (2023: positive EBITDA: £2,223,006).

Greenhouse gas emissions, energy consumption and energy efficiency action

Reporting boundary and scope

This disclosure covers the UK operations of Voltalia UK Ltd (company no. 07489990) and is prepared in line with

the UK Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting (SECR) framework. It includes Scope 1 (direct) and Scope 2

(electricity) greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions and the required subset of Scope 3 emissions, alongside total UK

energy consumption and an intensity ratio. Methodologies follow the UK Government's Environmental Reporting

Guidelines and GHG Protocol, using the latest Government conversion factors.

Governance and commitment

Voltalia UK aligns with the Group's sustainability strategy and the Voltalia UK Carbon Reduction Plan (CRP),

which is prepared in accordance with PPN 06/21 and commits to achieving organisational het-zero GHG

emissions by 2050. The CRP sets out UK-specific decarbonisation actions across Scopes 1, 2 and the

mandated subset of Scope 3.

Methodology and organisational boundary

e Organisational boundary is financial-control for Voltalia UK Ltd.

e Energy and emissions cover: fuel used in company-controlled vehicles and gas/oil (Scope 1); purchased
electricity (Scope 2, market/location-based as available); and the SECR-required subset of Scope 3
(business travel in non-company vehicles, employee commuting, waste and relevant upstream/downstream
transport, where material).

e Emissions are reported as tCO2e using the Government's annual factors.

Intensity ratio

Voltalia UK reports an operational intensity ratio of tCO2e per full-time equivalent (FTE) (and, where helpful,

tCO2e per £m revenue) to reflect the office- and services-led nature of UK activities. This provides a stable basis

for year-on-year comparison under SECR.

Post balance sheet events
In April 2025, the Voltalia Group approved the closure of the Equipment, Trading and Distribution (“ETD”)
business line. This decision followed a strategic reassessment of the Company's activities and reflected the
reduced viability of ETD operations within the evolving UK market. All ETD activities were wound down in an
orderly manner during 2025.
Subsequent to the closure of ETD, Voltalia Group launched its organisational restructuring initiative, Project

. Spring, with implementation commencing later in 2025. Project Spring introduced a revised operating model for
the UK platform, separating the Engineering, Procurement and Construction ("EPC") and Operations and
Maintenance (“O&M”) functions from Voltalia UK Ltd into a newly incorporated subsidiary, VLT O&S UK Ltd
(company number 16730282).
With effect from 1 December 2025, all EPC and O&M activities have been transferred to VLT O&S UK Ltd,
together with the related employees, operational responsibilities and commercial arrangements. From this date,
VLT O&S UK Ltd functions as a standalone delivery entity, assuming full accountability for the execution of EPC
and O&M services and for the recognition of all associated revenues and costs within its own financial
statements. :
Voltalia UK Ltd continues to operate as a separate legal entity, with a defined focus on Development and IPP
activities. Its responsibilities are limited to asset development, ownership and long-term portfolio stewardship.
The strategic objectives, governance arrangements and performance measures of Voltalia UK Ltd are therefore
distinct from those of VLT O&S UK Ltd, notwithstanding their common group ownership.
The restructuring strengthens operational governance, improves transparency of business-line performance and
aligns the UK structure with the Group's long-term strategic direction. The financial effects of both the ETD
closure and the Project Spring restructuring have been recognised in these financial statements where
applicable. .
