Strategic Report
Year ended 31 March 2025

Fair review of the business

For the year ending 31 March 2025, the business reported turnover of £17.4m and annual recurring

revenue of £18.4m. While market conditions in the first half of the year were challenging due to

reduced budgets, business confidence, deal processes, the company demonstrated resilience and

operational agility, delivering strong performance in the second half. This was achieved through

enhanced product delivery, measurable gains in customer satisfaction, and stronger business metrics.

The focus on operational efficiency allowed gross profit to be maintained, as infrastructure and

telecom costs were reduced. This enabled reinvestment into customer experience and product and

development initiatives. EBITDA for the year was £3.5m, representing 20% of turnover, and

disciplined cash management provided a strong financial foundation for future growth.

During the year, the business accelerated development including the launch of the new Al-enabled

capabilities designed to optimise customer engagement and insight. These innovations support

businesses in understanding and enhancing the customer journey, key touchpoints, intent and

outcome of calls, conversion barriers, integrating this data back into bid and other platforms, in order

to reach a better audience, drive stronger conversions, and optimise all spend.

The company continued to focus on serving medium-to-large enterprises, digital agencies, and

technology partners within its core verticals: travel, automotive, healthcare, property, and financial

services. The business supports a base of over 620 active clients, with an average annual account
: value of £29k. The business continues to focus on growth in both the number and average value of

clients through deeper adoption, improved results, and the introduction of industry-specific,

feature-rich products.

The Group enters the new financial year with momentum and a clear growth trajectory. Building on the

gains achieved in the second half of 2025, the business is targeting £20m in annual recurring revenue

by the end of FY25-26. This will be driven by continued product innovation, strong retention, client

-expansion, and new client acquisition across its core markets.

The Board is confident in the business's position and strategy and is encouraged by the positive

market response to recent product enhancements.

Subsidiaries

Infinity Tracking Inc., whose core activity is the provision of advanced call tracking and call intelligence

solutions to the United States market, continues to make fair progress in building brand presence and

commercial opportunities. The Directors remain confident that the US market presents a significant

long-term growth opportunity and have maintained a strategic focus on developing sustainable

operations within this territory.

During the year, the business achieved commercial self-sufficiency through the generation of

increased volumes of direct work from clients based in the US. While the company continues to

benefit from the operational support of its parent entity, this trajectory reflects improved market

engagement and growing recognition of Infinity's value proposition in the United States.

ResponseTap Limited was acquired on 26 April 2021, remains a wholly owned subsidiary of the

Group. Following the successful integration of its operations, all trading activity was transferred to

Infinity Tracking Limited through a formal hive-up process completed on 31 August 2022. As a result,

ResponseTap Limited is now a non-trading entity within the Group structure.

Research and development
During the year the Group continued to invest in the innovative development of its technology :
platform, the development of its technology practices, the training of its staff and the assessment of
new technologies to meet its customers’ current and future needs. The directors regard the investment
in research and development ahead of market demands as integral to the continuing success of the
business.
Future developments
The external commercial environment is expected to remain competitive but the progress and interest
in Al-enabled technology, especially for customer experience, continues to evolve rapidly. The

- business continues to expand its product offer and is targeting both existing and new markets; and,
the directors remain confident that the company will be able to build on its current level of performance
in the future.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Group's operations expose it to a variety of risks that include but not limited to market
competition, the loss of key customers, supplier performance and pricing, availability of skilled
employees, business continuity and reliance on key IT systems, together with risks associated with
growth of the business and underlying economic environment factors affecting overall business
confidence.
These risks are formally reviewed by the Board at least annually and appropriate processes put in
place to monitor and mitigate their effects. Customer risk is managed by focusing on the diversification
of the customer portfolio and customer communication and retention strategies. Supplier risk is
managed through strong communication with suppliers and regular assessment is made of market
pricing and alternative suppliers.
The Group has established a formal business continuity and disaster recovery plan, and these are
reviewed by the Board at least annually.
The risk to the growth of the business and the underlying economic environment is managed by
aligning the Company's service offering with the requirements of current and future customers and by
winning and retaining customers from a diverse cross-section of size, sectors, and geographic
locations.

Financial risk management
The group is exposed to financial risks, including liquidity risk, credit risk, supplier insolvency and
foreign exchange risk. The group has limited exposure to price risk and interest rate risk. Given the
size of the group, the directors have not delegated the responsibility of monitoring financial risk
management to a sub-committee of the board. The policies set by the board of directors are
implemented by the group's finance department which applies specific risk management policies and
procedures. The company's approach to these risks is discussed below.
Liquidity risk
Liquidity risk is the risk that the group does not have sufficient financial resources to meet its
obligations when they fall due or will have to do so at excessive cost. This risk can arise from
mismatches in the timing of cash flows relating to assets and liabilities. This risk is managed through
effective credit control procedures, including managing credit risk, and detailed financial reviews
regarding the acceptance of any proposed significant financial obligations to ensure that the group can
continue to meet its liabilities as they fall due.
Credit risk :
The group has implemented policies that require credit checks on potential customers before sales are
made. The group regularly reviews the credit limits applied to all Its customers.
Supplier insolvency risk
If a supplier were to be declared bankrupt or insolvent, the group would have financial exposure. Any
tisk is mitigated by the diversification of suppliers and undertaking regular credit reviews of key
suppliers. -
Foreign exchange risk
The group's international operations are subject to foreign exchange risk arising from currency ;
exposures, primarily with respect to the US dollar. Foreign exchange risk arises when future .
commercial transactions. recognised assets or liabilities, or investments are denominated in a ,
currency that is not the entity's functional currency. The group hedges foreign exchange risk arising
from future commercial transactions and recognised assets and liabilities through forward contracts.
Management regularly reviews the group's foreign exchange exposures to ensure there is no material
impact on the group's trading performance.
Corporate and social responsibility
The group believes that corporate social responsibility is integral to our business. Working in an ethical
way is at the core of everything that we do and we are dedicated to being a great employer,
minimising our environmental impacts and making a positive contribution to our community.
We recognise that the success of our business is dependent on the success of our people. We
provide an environment where employees are treated fairly, with dignity and without discrimination. ,
The same can be said for the relationships with our customers and partners; we uphold high business
performance in line with our values of honesty, and fair, open communication.
We are conscious of our impact on the environment and aim to limit this as much as possible. We
endeavour to recycle, reuse and make use of environmentally friendly products wherever possible.
Waste materials are disposed of in an environmentally safe manner in accordance with regulations.
We aim to minimise our consumption of energy and water as far as practical and encourage the use of
public transport and remote working to reduce unnecessary travel.
