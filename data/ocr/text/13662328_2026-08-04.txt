Strategic report
Period ended 31 October 2025
The directors present their strategic report for the period ended 31 October 2025.

: Principal activities : :
The principal activity of Svella Connect Limited during the period was the provision of digital infrastructure
solutions under framework arrangements for major UK telecommunications customers. During the period the
company’s principal contracts came to an end and operational activity has subsequently been substantially
wound down as part of the wider Svella group’s reorganisation. .
Business review
The company reported turnover of £50.9m (year ended 2024: £87.1m) and a pre-tax loss of £10.3m (year
ended 2024: £1.9m) for the 18-month period, reflecting the wind-down of the digital infrastructure operations,
. the demobilisation of resources and the impact of exiting its principal contracts.

Key performance indicators | :
Given the company's operational wind-down during and after the period, the directors do not consider key
performance indicators beyond the financial results to be relevant to an understanding of the development,
performance or position of the company. - .
Future developments ;
Following the period end, the company has continued to wind down its operational activities and is not pursuing
significant new contract opportunities. The directors’ current intention is that the company will be maintained
within the Svella group in order to manage residual obligations and balances, with no expectation of a return to
its previous scale of trading. :
Principal risk and uncertainties 7 :

: The principal risks and uncertainties now relate primarily to the orderly management of the wind-down process,
including settlement of residual obligations, management of the reduced cost base and maintenance of .
appropriate funding support while the company remains within the wider Svella group.

Section 172 of the Companies Act 2006 requires the directors to promote the success of the company for the

These financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis. The company meets its
day-to-day working capital requirements through the financial and other support of its ultimate parent

primarily arising on intra-group balances. Notwithstanding this position and the substantial reduction
in operational activity following the wind-down of the digital infrastructure business, after making

from Svella Plc, the directors have a reasonable expectation that the company will continue to meet
its liabilities as they fall due for at least twelve months from the date of approval of these financial
statements. Accordingly, they continue to adopt the going concern basis of preparation.

3.5 Leases : :
Assets held under finance leases and hire purchase contracts, which confer rights and obligations

- on the company similar to those attached to owned assets, are capitalised as tangible fixed assets
at the fair value of the leased asset (or, if lower, the present value of the minimum lease payments
as determined at the inception of the lease) and are depreciated over the shorter of the lease term
and their useful lives. The capital elements of future lease obligations are recorded as liabilities, and
the interest elements are charged to the profit and loss account over the period of the leases to
produce a constant periodic rate of charge on the remaining balance of the liability.

: Leases that do not confer rights and obligations approximating to ownership are classified as
operating leases. Rental payments under operating leases are charged to the profit and loss
account on a straight-line basis over the lease term, even if payments are not made on such a basis.
Benefits received and receivable as an incentive to sign an operating lease are similarly spread on a
straight line basis over the lease term.

3.6 Finance costs
Finance costs are charged to profit or loss over the term of the debt using the effective interest
method so that the amount charged is at a constant rate on the carrying amount. Issue costs are
initially recognised as a reduction in the proceeds of the associated capital instrument.
