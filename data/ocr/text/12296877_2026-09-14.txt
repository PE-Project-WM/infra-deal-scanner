Financial Risk Management

The company’s activity is exposed to several financial risks, market risk (including

exchange risk, interest rate risk and price risk), credit risk and liquidity risk. The

Company's overall risk management program tries to minimize the potential adverse

effects on its financial profitability.

a) Market Risk
Interest rate exposure
The company’s cash is exposed to interest rate risk, which could have an
adverse impact on its financial performance and cash flows.
Aside from cash, the Company has no other significant interest-bearing assets
and liabilities. The Company's income and operating cash flows are substantially
independent of changes in market interest rates.
Exchange rate exposure ,
Fluctuations in the parity of the currency in which a given economic transaction
is carried out against the accounting currency can have a negative impact on the
financial expense and profit or loss for the year, with a particular impact on the .
financial management of assets, liabilities and profit or loss.
Although the company operates almost wholly within the UK and historically has
had minimal foreign exchange risk, during the year it has entered into hedging
arrangements to mitigate currency risk associated with purchases in foreign
currency. These hedging instruments help reduce the potential impact of
exchange rate fluctuations on financial performance.
b) Liquidity risk
Prudent liquidity management implies maintaining sufficient cash and the
availability of funding. The company’s current contract includes advance
payments from customers which help to maintain a positive working capital
position.
The company has access to credit financing through group’s loan facilities. The
shareholder also provides credit facilities to the company to manage its liquidity.
Additionally, the company has the support of the Gransolar Group where
necessary.

Revenue is measured at the fair value of the consideration received or receivable and

percentage of total estimated costs for each contract. This cost estimated is made at the
start of each project and is updated in accordance with the real costs that are incurred,
