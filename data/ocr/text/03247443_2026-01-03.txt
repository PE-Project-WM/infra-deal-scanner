Strategic Report for the Year Ended 31 December 2024
The directors present their strategic report for ICON Clinical (UK) Limited formerly Pharm Research Associates
(UK) Limited (“the Company”) for the year ended 31 December 2024, which has been prepared in accordance
with the Companies Act 2006 (Strategic Report and Directors’ Report) Regulations 2013.
Principal activities and future developments
The principal activity of the Company is to conduct clinical research to support the pharmaceutical industry. The
directors do not envisage a significant change in the activities of the Company over the coming year.
Prior to 2023, the Company acted as principal party for business activities in Europe, Africa and Asia (“the EAPA
region”), overseeing other group companies across the region. As its activities are regional in nature, local work
was subcontracted to other group companies operating in each territory. Revenue was recognised by the Company
where they were the contracting party or were the party responsible for providing the services to the Sponsors.
Effective 31 December 2022, the Company entered into an agreement with ICON Clinical Research Limited
(“ICRL”), a fellow ICON Group company, whereby ICRL has assumed responsibility as the sole provider of
services for Legacy PRA Sponsor Contracts and Transitional Sponsor Contracts (together “Legacy Contracts”).
Under the Agreement, the Company will provide services under the direction of ICRL for Legacy Contracts and
will continue to perform certain administrative functions such as invoicing and collections on behalf of ICRL.
Further, the Company provides clinical research related services to ICRL on certain global clinical trials which
are contracted with ICRL in exchange for an arm’s length consideration. Passthrough expense/revenue (and
associated payment on account balances) remains in the Company, where the Company remains the contracting
entity, or the party responsible for providing the services to the Sponsors, (being a necessary service provided for
completion of clinical trial). Passthrough revenue is a zero-margin transaction.

Principal risks and uncertainties
One of the prime challenges for us as a group is working with unpredictable project cycles because of timing and
drug stabilisation, the resources and funding available to our clients and the complexities of the studies involved.
ICON’s superior IT infrastructure enables the Company to pool work with its overseas affiliates to meet these
peaks and troughs.
The Company has limited risk as it provides clinical trial related services under the direction of ICRL in exchange
for an arm’s length remuneration.
The Company’s operations expose it to a variety of financial risks that include, exchange rate fluctuations,
liquidity risk and interest rate cash flow risk.
The Company made a profit for the financial year of £64,416,062 in 2024 (2023: £67,087,583) and had net current
assets of £451,846,245 (2023: £385,153,801) including cash of £2,564,282 as at 31 December 2024 (2023:
£2,629,805). Net current assets also includes a net receivable of £329,874,452 (2023: £331,059,382) from the US
operating company. There are no doubts as to the recoverability of this balance.
