Introduction
The Director presents the Strategic Report together with the audited financial statements of NTT Cloud
Communications UK Ltd. (the "Company") for the year ended 31 March 2025.
Principal activity
: The Company's principal activity during the year was to provide a complete range of Unified Communications
(UC), Digital Events and Cloud Voice solutions to businesses. With effect from 1 March 2025 the UK operations
were moved to the Germany entity, so the entity has became a non-trading company.
Business review
The Company showed an overall revenue decrease of 28.3% for the year ended 31 March 2025 compared to
the prior year ended 31 March 2024, which was primarily driven by the decrease in legacy audio revenue
streams and to wind down of trading and transfer of operations to other group entities.
The Director reports a loss after tax of the Company for the year ended 31 March 2025 of £1,904k (2024: £24k).

Principal risks and uncertainties
The Company operates in a competitive market which is a continuing risk to the Company. However, the
Company manages this risk by constantly developing its services, delivering high quality products to its
customers and focusing on careful cost management.
‘ Financial risk management objectives and policies
The Company's activities expose it to several financial risks including cash flow, credit, liquidity and price risk, as
noted below:
Cash flow risk .
Cash flow risk is the risk that the Company's available cash is not sufficient to meet its financial obligations. The
Company mitigates its cash flow risk with careful cash management and forecasting. The Company also has
cash pooling arrangements with its parent company where it can access additional funds when required.
Credit risk
Credit risk is the risk that a counterparty will be unable to pay amounts in full when due. The Company
customers are mostly large blue-chip companies, and the Company limits its risk exposure by conducting a
credit review of potential clients. The Company has no significant concentration of credit risk, with exposure
spread over a large number of counterparties and customers.
Liquidity risk
Liquidity risk is the risk that a counterparty will encounter difficulty in meeting obligations associated with financial
liabilities. The Company aims to mitigate liquidity risk by managing cash generation by its operations, applying
cash collection targets throughout the Company and by performing regular cash forecasting analysis.
Price risk /
The Company manages its price risk by negotiating long term contracts for its telecommunication and other
product contract costs. This is managed by a specialised team based in London and Paris. Foreign exchange
currency exposure risk is also minimised as the majority of transactions are based in GBP.
Future developments 7
As part of a global NTT strategic initiative, it is proposed that the Company will be gradually wound down and
deregistered within 12 months from the date these accounts are signed.

2.4 Revenue
Revenue represents the fair value of the consideration received or receivable for services rendered in
the ordinary course of the Company's activities. Material revenue streams include Cloud Voice
revenue, managed services revenue, professional services revenue and other revenue streams (sale
of hardware and reselling of licenses).
The Company recognises revenue when the amount of the revenue can be reliably measured, it is
probable that future economic benefits will flow to the entity and when specific criteria have been met
for each of the Company's activities described below. The Company bases its estimates on historical
results taking into consideration the type of client, the type of transaction, and the specifics of each
arrangement.
Cloud voice revenue includes the provision of universal calling plans, cloud fax and recording
services. Revenue in respect of Cloud voice is recognized when the related service is provided to the
customer under agreed contractual terms. Cash is received in the month subsequent to the provision

: of the services or products.
Managed services revenue includes the IT practices and services outsourced by the client to the
Company. Managed Services follow a licenses type billing model. The revenue is recognised when
the related licenses are provided to the customer under agreed contractual terms. Cash is typically
received within 30 days of the date of the invoice being issued at the beginning of the following month
as agreed in the standard terms and conditions.
Professional services revenue includes a range of services including: project management, UC
engineering and solution architect. The revenue recognition model of the professional services will
depend on the contract value. For a contract below a certain value the revenue will be recognised on
billing under agreed contractual terms. For contracts above a certain value, revenue is recognised on
a time and material basis as services are rendered and as direct expenses are incurred. There is a
fixed general daily transaction price.
