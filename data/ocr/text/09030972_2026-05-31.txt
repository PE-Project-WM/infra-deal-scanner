In addition, the group is subject to the risks and uncertainties related to the current macroeconomic context. The
group operates in a competitive and dynamic landscape with customers’ needs continuously changing. Whilst the
group operates globally and. generates revenue and costs in various currencies, management seeks to create natural
hedges when possible to protect against exchange rate fluctuations. .

. Competition risk -
Due to the competitive environment that the Kinsta group operates in, the main challenge is to win enough new
customers to grow the business without lowering the price or the quality of services. Management believes this is

. achieved through a superior product and continuous product investment.

Customer credit risk

Customer credit risk is managed by the group's established policies and procedures. All sales opportunities are
subject to internal credit checking criteria, and all clients are subject to constant monitoring. Outstanding debts are
not material and are attributable to non-credit card-paying customers.

Liquidity risk ; ;

‘ The group monitors its cash flow on a long-term and short-term basis, producing more detailed reports enabling
management to ensure sufficient cash in the near, medium, and long term.
Currency risk : . ;
The group is exposed to several currencies, particularly the movement between USD and GBP. However, this is
mitigated through natural hedges as a proportion of revenues and costs are USD.

Cyber security usk , :

The business is heavily dependent on IT; therefore, cyber security is a critical risk. The group mitigates this risk by
having an internal cyber security team whose responsibilities are to ensure the business has compliant policies and
follows best practices alongside educating all staff on the risks related to cyber security. The group also has
insurance policies which protect the business against unavoidable exposure to cyber security.

Macro-economic context :

: The group is subject to risks with uncertainties related to the economic, financial, regulatory, and broad market
context, which may affect the group's performance. The financial situation of the group is influenced in the first
instance by the various factors that make up the macroeconomic framework, including the increase or decrease in
GDP, the level of confidence of consumers in enterprises, interest and exchange rates, availability of credit,
consumption, cost of goods and employment rate, in the context in which it operates. This is monitored and
reviewed by staying current with the latest market and political information and managing financial planning

. wherever possible. .
The business has assessed the impact of the Russia-Ukraine war and has determined that there has been no
significant impact on any areas of the business. : ,
