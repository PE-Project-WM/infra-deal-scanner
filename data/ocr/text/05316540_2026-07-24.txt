STRATEGIC REPORT FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2024 ‘
Pursuant to the Companies Act 2006 (Strategic Report and Directors’ Report) Regulations 2013, Avanti Space Limited (the “Company") and the
Directors present a Strategic Report.
Principal Activities
The Company is part of the overall Avant Group (the "Group", defined as Plate Newco 3 Limited and its subsidiaries) which has a strategy of
commercially exploiting its space and network assets, namely its spectrum rights, satellites, intellectual property and ground station assets. The
principal activity of the Company is the provision of communication services and it is expected to be so for the foreseeable furure. The
company generates revenue from the commercial exploitation of the HYLAS 1 satellite.
Results
Bandwidth revenue for the year of $23,878k represented a decrease of $5,936k from the prior year revenue of $29,814k. This is mainly due to a
. decrease in BIU revenue of $6,427k.
EBITDA (Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortisation) has decreased to $17,461k compared to $23,503k in the prior year.
The company made a profit before tax in the period of $4,730k compared with $10,722k in the previous period, representing a decrease in the
profit before tax of $5,992k.
The major assets held by the company are the HYLAS 1 satellite and associated ground infrasteucture, with a net book value of $13,465k (2023:
$21,913k) on which there are no impairment indicators.
Key Performance Indicators ("KPIs")
The key financial performance indicators of the Company and wider group are:
- Bandwidth revenue
- EBITDA
- Operating cash flow
Parent company
On 2 April 2024, the Super Senior Facility holder exercised a call option over the equity shares in an intermediate holding company of the
Group, Plate Newco 3 Limited, which resulted in the acquisition of 100% of the shares of Plate Newco 3 Limited by Plate New Topco Limited,
a company wholly owned and controlled by the Super Senior Facility holder. As a result, the sole Group debt provider is now the same as the
100% equity holder of the Group.
The Group from a teading perspective continues with business as usual, only with a stronger balance sheet. There was no change to the Avant
team, business activities, assets or technical capabilities as a part of the transaction.
Principal Risks and Uncertainties
The Company faces a number of risks and uncertainties that may adversely affect its business, operations, liquidity, financial position and future
performance.
The principal business risks and uncertainties faced by the Company relate to the stability of the economy and competition from similar
operators in the industry. The Company also faces a number of ongoing operational risks including credit and foreign exchange risk.
To the extent that these risks are under management's control, the directors aim to manage these risks and uncertainties going forwards in order
to maintain and improve on the current level of performance. :
Liquidity nsk
The Company finances its business through operating cash flow. The Company is also dependent on Plate Newco 3 Limited, through Avant
Communications Infrastructure Limited, not seeking repayment of the amounts currently due and providing additional financial support to the
Group if necessary. At the date of approval of these financial statements amounts due to Avanti Communications Infrastructure Limited
amounted to $16.4m. Avanti Communications Infrastructure Limited has indicated that it does not intend to seek repayment of the amounts
due at the balance sheet date, for the forseeable future. See the Basis of Preparation in Note 1 for further details.

Global economy
The global economy remains fragile and it continues to be difficult to predict customer demand. The Group is susceptible to decreased growth .
rates within high growth markets and/or continued economic and market downtum in developing markets. The effects could lead to a deciine

Foreign exchange risk
The Company has foreign currency transactions and is exposed to foreign exchange risk arising from various currency exposures, primarily with
respect to the pound Sterling and the Euro. In order to mitigate the foreign currency risk, the Group to which Avanti Space Limited belongs
monitors the level at which natural hedges occur and continually reviews the need to enter into forward contracts in order to mitigate any
material forecast exposure. The Company's reported results of operations and financial condition are affected by exchange rate fluctuations due
to both transaction and translation risks.
Interest rate risk
The Company’s main source of loan finance is interest free intercompany loans from other entities in the Group. Cash and deposits carn
interest at fixed rates based on banks’ short-term treasury deposit rates. Short-teem trade and other receivables are interest free. As a result,
interest rate risk is not currently a significant consideration.
Credit risk
Credit risk is the risk of financial loss arising from a counterparty’s inability to repay or service debt in accordance with contractual terms. Credit
risk includes the direct risk of default and the risk of deterioration of creditworthiness. We assess the credit quality of major customers before
trading commences, taking into account customers’ financial position, past experience and other factors.

Business Model and Bandwidth Seriices revenue
The business model of the Group, of which the Company is a subsidiary, is the commercial exploitation of its space assets, namely its spectrum rights,
satellites, intellectual property and ground station assets. The Bandwidth Services revenue earned by the Company is from the sale to third party
customers and other group companies of bringing into use services using the HYLAS 1 satellite.
Contract assets represent the excess of revenue recognised over amounts invoiced. Contract liabilities represent any unearned balances remaining from
amounts received from customers pursuant to prepaid contracts.
Bringing into use
Revenue from BIU projects is recognised as the performance obligations in the specific contract are satisfied. Performance obligations typically include
the delivery of a ‘Technical Report and a specified time On-Station after which the orbital slot is considered to have been brought into use. The
transaction price allocated to the performance obligations is typically determined as a percentage of the total contract value:
. 33.33% Technical Report Revenue recognised at a point in time on delivery
. 66.67% On-Station Revenue recognised over a period of time relating to the time On-Station
Lessor Income from Operating Leases
Revenue from leases is recognized on a straight-line basis over the lease term, reflecting the pattern in which economic benefits are derived. The
Company has assessed that it retains no significant risk in the underlying assets associated with these leases.
ESA grant
Capital grants are held on the balance sheet under ‘Deferred Income’ and released to the income statement on a straight line basis over the anticipated
life of the associated asset.
Foreign currency
Transactions entered into by the Company in a currency other than the currency of the primary economic environment in which it operates (the
“functional currency”) are recorded at the rates ruling when the transactions occur. Foreign currency monetary assets and liabilitics are translated at the
rate ruling at the reporting date. Exchange differences arising on the retranslation of unsettled monetary assets and liabilities are recognised immediately
in the income statement.
The functional currency and presentation currency of the Company at the balance sheet date is the United States dollar.

items recognised in other comprehensive income or directly in equity. In this case the tax is also recognised in other comprehensive income or directly
in equity respectively.
Current tax .
The charge for taxation is based on taxable profits for the year. Taxable profits differ from profit as reported in the income statement because it
excludes items of income and expenses that are taxable or deductible in other years and it further excludes items that are never taxable or deducuble.
Current tax assets and liabilities are measured at the amount expected to be recovered from or paid to the taxation authorities based on tax rates that
have been enacted or substantially enacted by the reporting date.
The current tax charge/credit also includes amounts payable to or receivable from other group companies in respect of current year group relief
received or surrendered.
The Group's overall UK current tax charge is nil as the taxable profits have been fully relieved through the surrender of group relief. However the

_ Company has a current tax charge for the period as this reflects amounts payable to other Group companies for the utilisation of their taxable losses via
the group relief mechanism.
Deferred tax
Deferred tax is recognised on differences between the carrying amount of assets and liabilities in the financial statements and the corresponding tax
bases used in the computation of taxable profit, and is accounted for using the balance sheet liability method.
Deferred tax Liabilities are generally recognised for all taxable temporary differences, and deferred tax assets are generally recognised for all deductible
temporary differences to the extent that it is probable that future taxable profits will be available against which those deductible temporary difference
can be utilised.
The carrying amount of deferred tax assets is reviewed at each reporting date and reduced to the extent that it is no longer probable that sufficient
furure taxable profits will be available to allow all or part of the asset to be recovered.
Deferred tax assets and liabilities are measured at the tax rates that are expected to apply in the period in which the liability is settled or the asset
realised, based on tax rates that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the reporting date. The measurement of the deferred tax liabilities and
assets reflects the tax consequences that would follow from the manner in which the Company expects, at the reporting date, to recover or settle the
carrying amount of its assets and liabilities.
Deferred tax assets and liabilities are offset when the Company has a legally enforceable right to offset current tax assets and liabilities and the deferred
tax assets and Liabilities relate to taxes levied by the same taxation authority.

Impairment policy
Assets that are subject to amortisation and depreciation are reviewed for impairment when events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying
amount may not be fully recoverable. The impairment review comprises a comparison of the carrying amount of the fixed asset with its recoverable
amount, which is the higher of fair value less costs to sell and value in use. .
Fair value less costs to sell is calculated by reference to the amount at which the asset could be disposed of. Value in use is calculated by discounting the
expected future cash flows obtainable as a result of the asset’s continued use, including those resulting from its ultimate disposal, at a market based
discount rate on a pre-tax basis.
An impairment loss is recognised in the Income Statement whenever the carrying amount of an asset exceeds its recoverable amount. The carrying
amount will only be increased where an impairment loss recognised in a previous period for an asset either no longer exists or has decreased, up to the
amount that it would have been had the original impairment not occurred.
For the purpose of conducting impairment reviews, CGUs (cash-generating units) are identified as groups of assets and liabilities that generate cash
flows that are largely independent of other cash flow streams. The assets and liabilities include those directly involved in generating the cash flows and
an appropmiate proportion of corporate assets. For the purposes of impairment, individual satellites are treated as individual CGUs.
Leased assets
‘An assessment of whether an arrangement is, or contains, a lease is made at inception of a contract. A contract is accounted for as a lease by the
Company if it conveys a right to control the use of a specified asset for a period of time in exchange for consideration.
At the commencement date, the Company recognises a right-of-use asset and a lease liability in the Statement of Financial Position. The lease liability is
measured at the present value of the outstanding lease payments, discounted at the rate implicit in the lease, or at the incremental borrowing rate if that
rate is not easily determinable. The right-of-use asset is measured as the value of the lease liability, lease payments made before commencement date,
initial direct costs of use, and end of life cost estimates.
After the commencement date, the right-of-use asset is depreciated over the shorter of either the estimated useful life or the term of the lease on a
straight-line basis. The Liability is accounted for by reducing the carrying amount for lease payments made, and increasing the carrying amount to reflect
interest on the outstanding liability. The interest element of these obligations is charged to the Income Statement over the relevant period. Assets that
are deemed to be of low value (defined by the Group as less than £4,000) or leases with a term of 12 months or less are charged to the Income
Statement on a straight-line basis over the period of the lease.
