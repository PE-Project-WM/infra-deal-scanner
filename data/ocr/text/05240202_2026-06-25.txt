FOR THE PERIOD ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025

The directors present their Strategic Report for Ground Control Technologies UK Ltd (the ‘Company’) for the
43 month period ended 31 December 2025. .
Review of business
The Company is a leading Internet of Things (‘loT’) and Machine-to-Machine (‘M2M’) end to end platform
provider. The Company provides SCADA, telemetry, monitoring and tracking platforms for loT and M2M
assets, generally in remote or challenging environments, and operates globally. The Company provides a one-
stop solution combining secure infrastructure, resilient software platforms and multiple network connectivity.
Working in partnership with multiple major satellite and cellular networks and key hardware manufacturers, the
Company provides managed service solutions tailored to the lot and M2M connectivity needs of a range of
customers from global blue chip organisations, government agencies and small enterprises.
In August 2017 the Company was acquired by Horizon Capital LLP who planned to back the management
team to execute a buy and build growth strategy to significantly accelerate the growth of the business as part
of a group. This transaction provided the Group with access to significant funding in order to deliver these
plans. In 2018 the Group acquired Rock Seven Mobile Services Ltd and YB Tracking Ltd (together “Rock
Seven’) in the UK and in 2019 the Group acquired Ground Control Systems (“Ground Control”) in the United
States of America. In August 2021 the Group rebranded the business with a new look and feel under the
Ground Control name and relaunched the groundcontrol.com website.
On 24 September 2025, Collecte Localisation Satellites (CLS) SAS, incorporated in France, acquired the
Company, immediately expanding its global reach and strengthening its position in the fast-growing satellite

. loT market. The Company's rugged hardware and its Cloudloop platform slot into a much larger ecosystem of
Satellite services, giving the business access to greater investment, wider distribution channels and deeper
technical capabilities. : .
During the period the Company's installed base of satellite and cellular loT and M2M continued to grow as
active device connectivity subscriptions increased. Connectivity and services revenue grew by 13%
(compared to a decline of 3% in 2024) as a result of the increased active device subscriptions. Connectivity
and services revenue represented 66% of total revenue. The key enabler of recurring revenue growth is the
sale of hardware solutions. Hardware revenue grew by 9% (compared to 29% in 2024).
The Company continued to invest substantially in securing a high-growth future. Significant progress was
made developing and launching new products and new product variants, substantially strengthening the sales
and marketing operation, and establishing its own IP networking capability. This will allow the Company to
maximise the opportunity presented by new products and achieve top tier partner status with key suppliers in
the sector, further diversifying supply base. risk. The Company finished the year in a strong position and well
placed to continue and accelerate growth. ;
Principal risks and uncertainties :
The principal risks facing the Company and planned mitigation are:

. * Supplier relationships and supply chain - relationships with the leading satellite network operators are
important to the Company's on-going success. The Company is an important channel and route to
market for them and these co-dependent relationships are managed and protected via frequent and
open dialogue and contact. The global supply chain of communication technology products has been
disrupted by several events including the pandemic. The Company has recruited a supply chain
manager and invested in inventory planning systems to manage this risk;

Strategy ,

The Board undertook major strategic review in 2021 which reflected the increasingly global nature of the
Company. The strategy was refined in 2023, increasing the focus on five growth pillars which will be delivered
over time through both organic growth and mergers and acquisitions (‘M&A’) activity:

* Platform: expand and continuously develop the Company's leading Cloudloop® platform, supporting
an expanding range of core services, enabling value-added services layers, and creating sticky high-
value connectivity with partners across our complete range of products, solutions and market;

* New brand positioning: supporting the strategy of the integrated Company and its broad geographic
footprint and service offerings;

* Sales acceleration. growing sales capabilities, verticalising and focusing on key markets, and
partnering with systems integrators and other channel partners;

* Broader connectivity: repositioning the Company as connectivity agnostic, adding new connectivity
partner relationships and new technologies and increasing prominence with key satellite and cellular
network partners;

* Products: continuing to leverage the Company's flexible development capabilities, creating exclusive
hardware to drive connectivity and services, focusing on recurring revenue enhancers, looking
towards higher bandwidth loT/M2M applications, data hosting, management and analytics.

In the short term the focus of the business is to continue to deliver organic growth, with greatest emphasis on
solutions driving high recurring revenue and maximised customer lifetime value, whilst evaluating mergers
and M&A opportunities and completing the remaining operational consolidation. The longer-term strategy of
the Company remains to become a global category leader in loT and M2M solutions:

+ Positioned as a connectivity-agnostic service delivery platform;

* Providing customers with market-leading solutions for high-availability, mission-critical applications in
remote or challenging environments; and

* Challenging the service provider/solution landscape as a disrupter.

Business model :
The Company's business model is focused on innovating to provide bespoke solutions to our customers and
partners, strong ongoing customer support and delivering profitable growth.
The Company benefits from high levels of recurring turnover providing confidence in future performance and

levels.
Future outlook .
The future outlook for the industry and the Company remains positive as the Company holds an excellent
position in its respective markets and is expected to grow. Analysis of the loT and M2M markets by external
experts forecasts continued rapid market expansion. This is driven by growth in the number of end users, as
the use of communications technology and automation in consumer, industrial and governmental applications
continues to expand. This increases the need for both resilient private infrastructure and the platforms the
Company offers to enable customers to manage their growing estate of devices.
The Board has performed a rigorous assessment of the business's forecasts and projections at a group level

support in an appropriate form should the need arise. Accordingly, the Company continues to adopt the going
