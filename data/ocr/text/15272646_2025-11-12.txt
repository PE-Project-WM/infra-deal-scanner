Strategic Report
For the Period Ended 31 December 2024

The Directors present their Strategic Report and the audited financial statements of CoreWeave UK Limited (the

"Company’) for the period from incorporation on 9 November 2023 to 31 December 2024. .

Principal activity and business review

The Company provides data hosting services to other entities within CoreWeave Group ("CoreWeave").
. CoreWeave is a modern cloud infrastructure technology enterprise which offers it's customers access to the

CoreWeave Cloud Platform that consists of proprietary software and cloud services that deliver the automation

and efficiency needed to manage complex Al infrastructure at scale, and other services.

During the year the Company made significant investments in data center assets totaling $576.2 million and

expansion of its workforce to 53 employees to operationalize two data center locations to enable the provision of

data center hosting services totaling $31.7 million of revenue. The significant data center investments were

enabled by the ultimate parent, who provided short term operating liquidity to the Company prior to a planned

additional capitalization of the Company in 2025.

The result for the year, and the Company's financial position at the end of the period, is shown on pages 8 to 9.

The Company made a loss after tax of $594,315.

Key performance indicators (KPIs)

Number of Employees: 53

Gross Data Center Assets in Service: $576.2 million

Future developments

It is the intention of the Directors to continue to develop the existing activities of the Company in support of

CoreWeave’s operations.

Principal risks and uncertainties

The Company provides data hosting services to the other entities within CoreWeave and is therefore exposed

to risks and uncertainties via it's reliance on other CoreWeave entities. The risk and uncertainties of

CoreWeave are noted in the company’s public filings with the US Securities and Exchange Commission.

Specific to the Company's operations, the Company is exposed to the following primary risks and uncertainties.

Reliance on ongoing support from CoreWeave entities

The Company solely provides data hosting services to CoreWeave entities and is reliant on other CoreWeave

entities to obtain the capital and liquidity necessary to invest in such data hosting infrastructure as well as for the

operating revenues to support ongoing operational expenses.

Currency risk

The Company primarily transacts with other CoreWeave entities in US Dollars and primarily purchases assets in

US Dollars, however the Company is exposed to currency risk primarily from its leases and power purchase

arrangements, and employee salaries and benefits, which are primarily denominated in British Pounds.

2.8 Pensions
: Defined contribution pension plan
The Company operates a defined contribution plan for its employees. A defined contribution plan is a
pension plan under which the Company pays fixed contributions into a separate entity. Once the
contributions have been paid the Company has no further payment obligations.
The contributions are recognised as an expense in profit or loss when they fall due. Amounts not
paid are shown in other creditors as a liability in the Statement of Financial Position. The assets of
the plan are held separately from the Company in independently administered funds.
2.9 Share-based compensation
Where share based compensation is awarded to employees by a related entity, the fair value of
such compensation at the date of grant is charged to profit or loss over the vesting period. Non
market vesting conditions are taken into account by adjusting the number of equity instruments
expected to vest at each reporting date so that, ultimately, the cumulative amount recognised over
the vesting period is based on the number of options that eventually vest. Market vesting conditions
are factored into the fair value of the options granted. The cumulative expense is not adjusted for
failure to achieve a market vesting condition.
The fair value of the award also takes into account non-vesting conditions. These are either factors
beyond the control of either party (such as a target based on an index) or factors which are within
the control of one or other of the parties (such as the Company keeping the scheme open or the
employee maintaining any contributions required by the scheme).
Where the terms and conditions of options are modified before they vest, the increase in the fair
value of the options, measured immediately before and after the modification, is also charged to
profit or loss over the remaining vesting period.
Where equity instruments are granted to persons other than employees, profit or loss is charged
with fair value of goods and services received.
The Company will make no payment to the ultimate parent for the value of share based
compensation provided to employees or persons other than employees, therefore an increase in
equity has been recorded for the value of expense recognized in the period.
2.10 Operating lease agreements

remain with the lessor are charged to profit or loss on a straight-line basis over the period of the

These provisions are a best estimate of the cost required to return leased properties to their original

2.12 Tangible fixed assets

estimated useful lives, using the straight -line method.
Depreciation is provided on the following basis:

Leasehold Improvements - Over the lease term on a straight-line basis

Fixtures and fittings - Over 5 years on a’straight-tine basis

Computer equipment - Over 3 years on a straight-line basis

Data center equipment - Between 6 to 12 years on a straight-line basis
Within data center equipment, graphic processing units are depreciated over a 6-year useful life.
The assets’ residual values, useful lives and depreciation methods are reviewed, and adjusted
prospectively if appropriate, or if there is an indication of a significant change since the last reporting

Gains and losses on disposals are determined by comparing the proceeds with the carrying amount
and are recognised in profit or loss.

19. Share-based compensation

Employees of the Company participate in the share compensation scheme of its parent CoreWeave, Inc.

In July 2019, the parent company adopted a stock option plan (the “2019 Stock Option Plan” or the “2019

Plan’). The purpose of the 2019 Plan is to provide incentives to attract, retain, and motivate eligible
: persons whose potential contributions are important to the success of the group by offering those eligible

persons an opportunity to participate in the group’s future performance through the grant of awards of

common stock.

Stock Options

CoreWeave, Inc. has provided share-based compensation awards in the form of Stock Options to certain

employees of the Company, granted in 2023 under the ultimate parent company’s 2019 Stock Option

Plan. The awards contain a service vesting condition, but no other vesting conditions.

The fair value of the awards was calculated as of grant date and is being expensed over the service term

using a graded vesting method. Given that the Company does not settle the share-based compensation

awards with the employee, the awards are being treated as equity settled.

Restricted Stock Units

CoreWeave, Inc. has provided share-based compensation awards in the form of Restricted Stock Units

(‘RSUs’) to certain employees of the Company, granted in 2024, under the ultimate parent company's

2019 Stock Option Plan, as Amended in December 2023.

The awards contain a service vesting condition, as well as a non-market liquidity event condition. The

service-based vesting condition has varying terms, but is generally satisfied over four years. The

performance-based vesting condition is satisfied upon the occurrence of a qualifying liquidation event for

the ultimate parent, which is defined as the earlier to occur of {i) an acquisition of the ultimate parent or

(ii) the effective date of a registration statement of the ultimate parent filed for the IPO of the ultimate

parent's common stock.

The fair value of the awards was calculated as of grant date and is being expensed over the service term

: using a graded vesting method. Given that the Company does not settle the share-based compensation

awards with the employee, the awards are being treated as equity settled.

The total share-based payment expense recognised in profit and loss for the year was $1,586,429 with a

corresponding credit to equity.
