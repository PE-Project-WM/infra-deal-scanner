REVIEW OF THE BUSINESS” —

NSSLGlobal continued to deliver strong profitability and contract momentum across its core mobility
markets of maritime, government and defence sectors.

NSSLGlobal reported revenues of £94.3 million (2024: £101.6 million) in 2025, down 7% (2024: up
by 8%) due to the delivery of a large £12m project in 2024 that was not repeated in 2025. Gross profit
had a corresponding 5% decrease for the same reasons (2024: 8% increase) to £37.9m (2024:
£39.7m).

EBITDA amounted to £17.0 million (2024: £20.7 million). Operating profit was £15.4 million (2024:
£18.5 million) which was 17% (2024: 27% higher) lower than 2024, while the profit after tax was £11.6
million (2024: £14.0 million).

Operating margin for the year decreased slightly to 16% (2024 - 18%) and was largely related to a
small improvement in gross profit margin offset by higher staff costs due to an increase in employee
numbers increasing operating costs by 10% (2024: 5% decrease).

The largest customer segment for NSSLGlobal is the Government and Defence sector which
accounted for 64% of revenue in 2025. NSSLGlobal has long-standing customer relationships with
several European government institutions and continually works to customize and grow its services ;
to these customers. The company experienced healthy sales throughout the year including a number
of new multi-million governmental projects in 2025 giving it a strong order backlog into 2026 and
beyond. NSSLGlobal remains a significant service provider to the UK Ministry of Defence and
German Armed Forces through a number of long-term framework and communication agreements
and recent contract and project wins for both satellite and LTE communication, ICT and entertainment
services.

On the Commercial maritime side, NSSLGlobal continued to roll out its Fusion'’“©° service to existing
and new clients. This includes a hybrid of the best of GEO, LTE and LEO based satellite
constellations. It combines the trusted benefits of a global, high availability GEO service with the low
latency and high capacity from LTE and LEO satellites. At the core of this service is NSSLGlobal’s
Smart@Sea platform providing secure and seamless solutions to the end user, as well as additional
cyber, Crew entertainment, ITSM functionality.

: In 2025 NSSLGlobal! won a significant eight-year critical infrastructure project for a large European
utility company which includes Satlink hubs and sites across Europe. This new infrastructure project
represents an entry into a new market area which will increase the total addressable market for
NSSLGlobal in the future. In addition a new multi-million contract was signed with the FCDO Services,
a provider of critical security and integration solutions for UK government diplomacy and crises
response teams.

NSSLGlobal’s sales and bid pipeline remain strong including eleven multi-million tender opportunities
across both the government, enterprise and maritime sectors that are in the 2026 opportunity pipeline
for implementation from 2026 onwards.
The Group continues to have strong operational cash flow. Cash management is important and has
been achieved by focusing on blue chip customers, strong credit control policies and fast resolution
of issues. Stock has decreased significantly during 2025 due to the completion of some large projects
and focus on stock management.
The Directors believe that we are well positioned as a group to continue to provide solid financial ;
performance in the years to come, whilst our continued investment and focus on our own engineering
solutions and skillset will differentiate ourselves from our competition and provide us with new growth
opportunities.

RISKS AND UNCERTAINTIES
The Group faces a number of risks and uncertainties that may adversely affect our business,
operations, financial position or future performance, many of which are wholly outside our control.
The satellite industry continues to undergo structural changes following the introduction of LEO
constellations, offering lower-cost and higher throughput alternatives to traditional GEO-based VSAT
solutions. This development has put sustained pressure on airtime margins across the industry.
In 2025, elevated military activity led to increased airtime usage, resulting in continuing high airtime
volumes that partially offset margin pressure.
NSSLGlobal is in a transition phase, shifting its business mix from airtime towards higher value-added
services. These services are more labour-intensive, resulting in structurally lower margins compared
to traditional airtime. However, these offerings enhance NSSLGiobal long-term relevance and
stickiness for its mobility customers.
We continue to see a skilled labour shortage across Europe, in particular in the UK, Norway and
Germany and, whilst we are hiring, it is not as quick as we budgeted so continues to be a priority for
NSSLGlobal to enable us to grow.
The directors have also considered the exposure of the company to price risk, credit risk, liquidity risk
and cash flow risk. Those that are considered to be material include a significant exchange rate
fluctuation risk particularly in GBP/USD and in USD/EUR due to the increasing complexity in the
intergroup currency transactions as well as the difference in supplier (usually USD) and customer
currencies (often GBP or EUR). The Board has sought, where necessary, to mitigate contract risk
with a forward FX hedge.
FUTURE DEVELOPMENTS
In 2026, we will continue with our long-term strategy of securing and growing our core mobility
markets alongside critical infrastructure, with a focus on developing our engineering skillset and .
global resources and offices whilst ensuring that we continue to develop solutions and services
geared around the requirements of our key customers who remain, as ever, at the core of our
business.
NSSLGlobal 2026 Budget forecasts revenue and operating profit to be tower than in 2025, due to
reduced event traffic. However given the markets that NSSLGlobal is in, and the current political
events recently, including in the Middle East and Ukraine, the demand for increased European
spending on defence may result in significant variability to its forecast in event traffic. NSSLGlobal
also has a solid pipeline for Project Sales and large Tenders particularly in the governmental market
but if won, revenues for these will not be seen until 2027. .
The Group will also continue to pursue its strategy of NSSLGlobal branded services and value-added
solutions by being a leading network operator and systems integrator with core technical
competences in digital and internet-based services.
The Group continues to finance all its customer, internal investments, and acquisitions from its strong
operational cash flow. Cash management is important and has been achieved by focusing on blue
chip customers, strong credit control policies and fast resolution of issues. Dividend policy is set to
maintain cover for operational investments and working capital requirements.

Energy Efficiency Actions
During the 2025 reporting period, NSSLGlobal Limited continued to implement initiatives to improve energy efficiency
and modernise its operational infrastructure. A Building Management System (BMS) was installed to optimise climate
control within the facility, alongside the installation of new heating and cooling equipment. External condensers were
also relocated to ground level to improve accessibility and support more efficient maintenance. ,
The organisation has begun planning the phased removal! of legacy gas-fired central heating radiators, supporting a
gradual transition away from fossil fuel-based heating and contributing to reduced gas consumption. Energy efficiency
improvements were also achieved through the completion of the transition to LED lighting, with the final installation of
high-efficiency fittings throughout the warehouse completed during 2025.
Employee engagement continues to support the organisation's sustainability objectives. An online environmental
training module was completed by all existing staff during 2025 and is now included as part of the mandatory induction
process for new employees.
NSSLGlobal Limited remains compliant with the Energy Savings Opportunity Scheme (ESOS), with the most recent
update completed in December 2025. Energy performance improvements continue to be guided by the organisation's
five-year energy management plan maintained within its reporting framework.
For more information on our sustainability initiatives, the Carbon Reduction Management Plan is available on the
company website.

Methodology
The methodology used for determining energy and carbon emissions comes from a number of sources of
greenhouse emissions: .
. Natural gas used for heating the buildings occupied (Scope 1)
. Electricity used for lighting; heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC), and the operation of office
and warehouse equipment (Scope 2)
* Transport activities, consumption use by company cars and vans, as well as by grey fleet operated for
business travel purposes (Scope 1 and Scope 3)
Energy consumption for the Redhill and Newcastle offices, including both gas and electricity, is based on verified .
invoice data covering the full 12-month FY25 reporting period. Energy generated from renewable sources at the
Redhill and Newcastle sites has been taken into account when calculating the adjusted intensity ratios.
Energy consumption for the Cornwall and Portsmouth offices has been estimated based on occupied floor area
(47 m? and 160 m? respectively) and has been included within the SECR statement.
Transport emissions from company vans and pool cars have been calculated based on the quantity of fuel
consumed. Emissions from the use of personal vehicles for business travel (grey fleet) have been calculated
using mileage claims, with fuel type and engine size information obtained from the company’s expenses system.
Greenhouse gas emissions have been calculated using the UK Government conversion factors for company
reporting (2025) published by the Department for Energy Security and Net Zero (DESNZ).
