The Directors present their Annual Report and audited financial statements of Spirent Communications

Principal activity

The Company's principal activity is that of a holding company In addition, it also sells hardware, software
and services for testing, assurance and positioning solutions

Acquisition of Spirent Group by Keysight Technologies Inc. (“Keysight”)

On 28 March 2024, the Boards of Keysight and Spirent announced that they had reached agreement on '
the terms of a recommended cash offer for the entire issued ordinary share capital of Spirent which was
estimated at £1 16 billion Subsequently, Keysight announced that, to secure regulatory approval, it would
be required to divest Spirent's high-speed ethernet, network security and channel emulation business
(“Divestco”)

The acquisition of the Company and Group by Keysight was completed on 15 October 2025 and
subsequently on 16 October 2025, Keysight sold the Divestco business assets to Viavi Solutions Inc
Following the finalisation of the acquisition, Spirent’s shares were delisted from the London Stock
Exchange, effective 17 October 2025

Business review

Spirent Communications pte delivered a strong performance over the pertod, with solid year-on-year
growth in turnover, reflecting sustained market demand and effective execution of its strategy
Performance was driven in particular by the Positioning division, which continued to bulfd momentum
following the launch of PNTX in 2024 and the subsequent introduction of PNTXe in 2025 These new
products have been well received by customers, strengthening Spirent’s competitive positioning and
supporting increased customer adoption across key markets

The Company also benefited from disciplined commercial execution and continued focus on innovation,
enabling tt to respond effectively to evolving customer requirements and market opportunities

In addition, during the year the Company continued to operate as the principal holding company for the
Spirent group, providing strategic oversight and support to its subsidiaries In this capacity, the Company
received income from subsidiary undertakings during the period

White the Company reported an operating loss for the year, this was principally driven by exceptional non-
recurring costs associated with the acquisition of Spirent by Keysight Technologies These costs were
one-off in nature and related primarily to transaction and advisory activites undertaken as part of the
acquisition process

The balance sheet remained strong throughout the year, supported by the Company's solid financial
Position and disciplined capital management Cash balances reduced during the period, primarily
reflecting higher expenditure on exceptional acquisition-related costs imcurred during the year Despite
this reduction, the Company continues to maintain a robust liquidity position and remains well placed to
support its ongoing strategic priorities and future growth opportunities ‘
Overall, the combination of innovation-led growth, expanding customer engagement and operational
discipline has positioned the Company well for continued success
