Introduction
The company was incorporated on 5 June 2015 and commenced to trade on 22 July 2015. iManage provides
software and cloud solutions primarily through direct sales efforts and, indirectly, through channel partners.
Revenues are derived primarily from cloud subscriptions, perpetual licenses and maintenance and support fees.
The company also derives revenues from professional services, which include implementation, training and
other data migration consulting engagements.
Business review
Built on close to 30 years of experience, iManage helps leading organisations manage documents and emails
more efficiently, protect vital information assets, and leverage knowledge to drive better business outcomes.
iManage employs award-winning Al-enabled technology, an extensive partner ecosystem, and a customer-
centric approach to provide support and guidance you can trust.
iManage’s commitment to product excellence and customer success has driven tremendous growth. iManage
has also built a vibrant innovation ecosystem of 150+ implementation partners, 200+ technology partners, and
300+ tech partner integrations that provide expert services including guidance and training.
A deep partnership with Microsoft enables iManage customers to maximize their Microsoft 365 subscription with
deep integrations into Office, Purview, Teams, Copilot and more. The alliance also ensures the global reliability
and scalability of our cloud-native platform built on Microsoft Azure.
iManage is more than a market-leading document and email management solution. iManage is a trusted vendor
delivering comprehensive productivity, governance and Al solutions on a reliable, scalable, and highly secure
knowledge work platform. iManage drives increased productivity, security, better governance, and greater
business agility for our customers.
On 16 June 2023, iManage EMEA Limited acquired 18.1% of the share capital of Co-Flo Enterprise Holdings
B.V. for consideration of £1,626,810. On 1 August 2024, iManage EMEA Limited acquired an additional 555
ordinary shares in Co-Flo for EUR 2.5 million, increasing its total investment to 33.6%. On 24 September 2025
Co-Flo entered into a loan agreement with iManage where Co-Flo obtained a loan in the amount of €2.5 million
(‘Term Loan’). The Term Loan is a three-year loan with an annual interest rate of 6.5%.
The company's profit after tax amounted to £509,476 (2024: £3,583,509). No dividend has been proposed or
paid during the year. The commercial environment in which the company operates remains competitive, but the
director believes that the company is well placed to maintain its current position in the future.
Based on the comprehensive assessment of current global economic and political conditions, the company is
deemed to have no going concern issues. The company is expected to continue its operations smoothly and
meet all its obligations without any financial distress.
Future outlook
Building on nearly 30 years of expertise, the company is well-positioned for continued growth and innovation.
The company will continue to expand its cloud-native platform, invest in Al-enabled innovation and strategic
partnerships to enhance customer experience and drive operational efficiency. The director remains confident in
the company’s ability to maintain its position and deliver long-term value to stakeholders.

Principal risks and uncertainties and financial risk management and objectives
The company uses cash, an intercompany loan, trade debtors and trade creditors to raise finance for its
operations. These expose the company to a number of financial risks which are described in more detail below.
Liquidity Risk
The company ensures sufficient liquidity by producing and reviewing regular cashflow forecasts.
Credit Risk
The company continually monitors its positions with, and credit quality of, its customers and the financial
institutions with which it does business. The company is exposed to credit loss in the event of non-payment by
customers and continually re-evaluates the need for a reserve.
Currency Risk
The financial position and operating results of foreign operations are consolidated using the local currency as the
functional currency. The company recognizes gains and losses on transactions that are denominated in a
. currency other than the respective entity's functional currency. Foreign currency transaction gains and losses
. also include amounts realized on the settlement of intercompany loans with foreign subsidiaries that are of a
short-term period.
Competition Risk
The company operates in a competitive market for document, email, and knowledge management solutions. The
company continues to invest in product innovation and customer relationships to maintain its market position.
Regulatory and Compliance Risk :
The company operates across multiple jurisdictions subject to evolving data protection and industry regulations.
The company monitors regulatory developments and maintains compliance programs to manage this exposure.
Talent and Key Personnel Risk
The company's success depends on attracting and retaining skilled employees in a competitive talent market.
The company invests in employee development and monitors retention closely as a key performance indicator.
Financial key performance indicators ;
The principal performance measure used to monitor the business are sales and net asset growth. For the year
ended 31 December 2025 revenues were £87,352k (2024: £74,682k). This was due to continuing expansion of
new customer base. For the year ended 31 December 2025 net assets were £7,503k (2024: £6,994k). This was
due to retained earnings from profitable operations. This reflects the company’s continued financial resilience
and supports its strategic objectives for sustainable growth.
Non-Financial key performance indicators
The employee retention rate is a critical indicator of organizational health, workforce stability, and the
effectiveness of human capital management. It provides stakeholders with insight into the company’s ability to
attract, develop, and retain talent-factors that directly influence long-term performance and operational continuity.
For the year ended 31 December 2025 retention rate was 91.98% (2024: 91.4%).

Energy Efficiency Actions Taken by iManage EMEA in 2025
The Belfast office is powered by local, renewable electricity with zero carbon emissions.
The London office also utilises renewable electricity, with two-thirds directly supplied by a solar plant in Devon,
UK and one-third covered by a Renewable Energy Guarantee of Origin (REGO).
As a result, there were zero market-based emissions associated with purchased electricity for iManage EMEA in
2025.

Impairment of financial assets :
At the end of each reporting period financial assets measured at amortised cost are assessed for
objective evidence of impairment. If an asset is impaired the impairment loss is the difference

estimated future cash flows derived from the financial asset(s) have been adversely impacted. The
impairment loss will be the difference between the current carrying amount and the present value of
the future cash flows at the asset(s) original effective interest rate.

impairment can be reviewed for possible reversal. The reversal will not cause the current carrying
amount to exceed the original carrying amount had the impairment not been recognised. The
impairment reversal is recognised in the profit or loss.

On 1 August 2024, iManage EMEA Limited acquired an additional 555 ordinary shares in Co-Flo
Enterprise Holdings B.V. for consideration of EUR 2,500,000, increasing its total investment to 33.6%.
In accordance with FRS 102, the Company evaluates its investments in associate annually for indicators
of impairment. The recoverable amount of the investment in associate has been determined based on a
value in use calculation, using a discounted cash flow model covering a five-year explicit forecast period
plus an exit-based revenue multiple. The Company has updated its assumptions as at 31 December
2025, reflecting the latest budget and forecast cash flows and recognised an impairment of £0.95m
against the carrying value of the investment. This reflects the early stage of the associate's development
and the inherent uncertainty in long-range cash flow forecasts.
Sensitivity analysis
As disclosed in the accounting policies note 3, the pre-tax discount rate and revenue multiple on exit to
determine terminal value are key sources of estimation uncertainty. Management has performed a
sensitivity analysis on each of these assumptions; reasonably possible changes, applied individually,
could result in the following impacts:
- A 5% increase in pre-tax discount rate would result in further impairment charge of £0.71m;
- A 3x decrease in revenue multiple to determine terminal value would result in further impairment
. charge of £1.43m.
