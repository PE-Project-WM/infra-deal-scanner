Introduction .
The Directors present the Strategic Report alongside the audited consolidated financial statements for the year
ended 30 June 2025.
Business Overview
Tymit Ltd (Tymit”, “we”) provides information technology consultancy services, data processing, hosting and
related services, as well as credit granting through non-deposit-taking finance houses and other specialist
consumer credit providers. Tymit is authorised and regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), with
registration number 796994.
Tymit develops and maintains technology platforms that support buy-now-pay-later (BNPL) solutions and credit
cards featuring embedded installment functionality. Our largest platform currently supports the Frasers Plus
programme, which enables customers of the Frasers Group and its affiliates to spread purchase payments over
up to 36 months (or 48 months for selected products), while earning loyalty points.
Frasers Plus
Tymit's technology is used by Frasers Group Financial Services for its Frasers Plus programme, and this
continued to experience significant growth during the reporting period. During the 12 months ended 30 June
2025, Tymit’s platform processed nearly 3 million purchase transactions totalling £320 million, up from 750,000
transactions and £77 million the previous year. This strong growth highlights our capacity to scale operations
without significant increases in operating costs, which is a testament to our technical expertise and platform
resilience.
Whilst Frasers Group Financial Services manage the customers, credit decisioning and operations and have
accountability for the credit loan book, Tymit's team of highly skilled technology professionals continues to
develop and support the advanced infrastructure underpinning the Frasers Plus programme. Maintenance and
support of our technology platform represent Tymit’s core revenue stream. As such, we are committed to
maintaining the highest standards in transaction processing, payment facilitation, and customer service. These
processes directly impact customer satisfaction and, by extension, the performance of our largest client.
Frasers Group Investment
Frasers Group became a shareholder in Tymit in early 2022 and has since increased its investment alongside
the growth of the Frasers Plus programme. As at 30 June 2025, Frasers Group held 44.1% of Tymit’s share
capital and invested £16 million in Convertible Loan Notes. Frasers Group have the option to require this loan to
be repaid or converted into additional equity, and in this scenario upon conversion, these notes would result in
Frasers Group exceeding 50% of Tymit’s share capital. After the balance sheet date, Frasers Group, in effect of
a few transactions described in Post Balance Sheet Events section, increased their shareholding in Tymit to
75.7%. On 22 May 2026, Frasers Group Plc transferred all the shares held in Tymit Ltd to FGFS Holdco Ltd, a
fully owned subsidiary of Frasers Group. Between 15 May 2026 and 22 May 2026, FGFS Holdco Ltd purchased
all the shares held by all other Tymit Ltd shareholders from those shareholders. As a result of this transaction,
FGFS Holdco Ltd holds 100% of Tymit’s share capital.
. Tymit Credit Cards ;
Tymit previously offered credit cards with embedded installment features. The onboarding of new customers
ceased in early 2023, and in July 2024, we formally decided to exit the credit card business. In November 2024,
all existing Tymit Credit customers were notified that their cards had been deactivated for new purchases.
Customers were granted a three-year period to repay their outstanding balances. Since then, outstanding
balances have steadily declined. As at 30 June 2025, the total balance of customers not more than 90 days in
arrears was £1.1 million. : . :

liabilities and obligations under the related debt funding agreements, assuming beneficial ownership of the
receivables portfolio. Tymit was appointed as servicer of the receivables until a subsequent sale of the portfolio
to another lender or a debt purchaser. Accordingly, Tymit no longer recognises these receivables, associated

Principal Risks and Uncertainties
Technology Risk
As a provider of critical financial technology infrastructure, Tymit is a potential target for cyber-attacks aimed at
disrupting payment processing or extracting financial data. While we have implemented robust security
measures - details of which are confidential for security reasons - we continue to face increasing cyber threats.
Additionally, system failures due to external factors (e.g., power outages, natural disasters) can result in data
loss and transaction processing disruption. To mitigate such risks, Tymit has established fallback systems and
maintains a dedicated incident response team prepared to act swiftly when needed.
Regulatory Risk
As an FCA-regulated firm, Tymit must remain responsive to changes in the regulatory landscape. New rules,
licensing requirements, or taxation policies could impact our operations and cost structures.
We proactively engage with our legal, regulatory, and tax advisors to assess the impact of any new
developments and ensure full compliance across all aspects of our business.
Risk of Key Personnel Leaving
Tymit’s success depends on retaining knowledge and accumulated experience of its key personnel. We face the
risk of our key technology development specialists being offered jobs by our competitors and effectively losing
talent. Talent may also be fost due to illness and accidents. Whereas we make sure that our level of salaries
ensures retention of key personnel, the risk of people leaving to competition cannot be ignored. We have created
repositories of processes and procedures and of knowledge of our most experienced people which should help
us continue providing good service in case of loss of talent. : :
Risk Related to Dependence on a Single Customer
A significant majority of Tymit revenues are generated by servicing Tymit's major client — Frasers Group
Financial Services Ltd (“FGFS”), a subsidiary of Frasers Group. Reliance on a single customer means that our
revenues may fluctuate depending on the demand of that customer and such fluctuations would not be offset by
any other revenue streams. Any potential financial difficulties of FGFS will have a direct impact on Tymit’s cash
flows and our ability to pay our employees and our vendors on time. We believe that the growth of the Frasers
Plus programme secures our future cash flows, however it also increases Tymit’s dependence on FGFS.
