Review of the business
Principal activities
Target Servicing Limited (‘Target Servicing’) is a subsidiary of Target Group Limited, hereafter referred to
together with Elderbridge Limited as ‘the Target Group’ or ‘Group’. The principal activities of Target Servicing
are the provision of transformational outsourcing, business process management and managed services to the
financial services sector. We enable clients to transform performance by delivering a world class combination of
customer experience, regulatory compliance and productivity through our digital technology and process
improvement capabilities. Our services are delivered in highly regulated mission critical environments, and our
platform supports over £20bn of business. We have over 40 years’ experience and are trusted by over 25 financial
institutions, including some of the top 20 global banks.

Financial review
Turnover of £26,789k (2024: £33,892k) represents a decrease of 20% (2024: decrease of 12%). Gross margin
increased to 34% for the year compared to -5% for the year ending 31 March 2024. Operating loss decreased from
£3,851k for the year ended 31 March 2024 to an operating loss of £2,830k for the year ended 31 March 2025.
This follows delivery of numerous initiatives, which are continuing, to right-size Target Servicing to align to a
reduced client base and focus on its core operating strengths to help win new business.
The macro-economic climate has continued to have an impact on our financial performance: the nature of
portfolios we service are predominantly closed books and therefore year-on-year our associated revenue has been
declining. Alongside limited new business which has overall impacted our top line revenue as such we have
focussed on the continuation of our business plan and strategy which is focussed on the simplification of the
company, driving efficiencies and a move to a product-based approach.
The execution of our strategy is the key driver for the significant improvement reported in gross margin and
longer-term stability of the Group. Target Group has continued to transform the way the business operates through
a strategy of simplifying and rightsizing the organisation, increasing flexibility and streamlined ways of working
; for our people; improving service offerings to clients; and harnessing the global scale of our parent organisation
Tech Mahindra (“Tech M”). Target Group has integrated with Tech M’s Business Process Services arm and is
working to deliver joint service offerings to global clients and building a global service pipeline.
Target Servicing is a wholly owned subsidiary of Target Group Limited, with the ultimate parent company being
Tech Mahindra Limited. In March 2025, Tech Mahindra Fintech Holdings Limited completed an equity injection
into Target Group Limited for £4m, which has been used to provide ongoing support for the business to deliver
on its new strategy alongside its regulatory commitments. The capital injection ensured Target Group had positive
net assets at 31 March 2025 and the ongoing support gives the Directors of Target Group confidence that the
companies of the Group can continue to operate as a going concern.

Financial risk management objectives and policies
- . Target Servicing’s activities expose it to several financial risks including FX and interest rate risks, credit risk, :
and liquidity risks.
FX and interest rate risks
Target Servicing has limited exposure to exchange rate fluctuations. All treasury matters are now coordinated via
the relevant group functions of our multinational listed parent Tech Mahindra.
There is minimal interest rate risk to Target Servicing and Target Servicing currently has no external debt.
Credit risk
Target Servicing’s principal financial assets are bank balances and cash, trade and other receivables and
investments.
Target Servicing’s credit risk is primarily attributable to its trade receivables. The amounts presented in the
balance sheet are net of allowances for doubtful receivables. An allowance for impairment is made where there is
an identified loss event which, based on previous experience, is evidence of a reduction in the recoverability of
cash flows. :
Target Servicing has no significant concentration of credit risk, with exposure spread over many clients. Most of
our clients are blue chip investment, retail banking, finance and insurance companies, and government bodies
which represent a low credit risk.
Liquidity risk : ;
To maintain liquidity to ensure that sufficient funds are available for ongoing operations and future developments,
Target Servicing uses a mixture of long-term and short-term debt finance, when required. Currently there is an
overdraft arranged for use of the wider Target Group with our bankers, HSBC, which is being used.
Regulatory risk :
Target Servicing is classified as an Article 3 MiFID exempt firm and also holds the status as ISA Plan Manager
with HMRC in respect of the Hartmoor Financial brand. Our compliance function reviews our regulatory
requirements on an ongoing basis to ensure compliance with all relevant permissions held.
The Target group’s compliance function reviews the group’s regulatory requirements on an ongoing basis to
ensure compliance with all relevant permissions held.

Financial instruments
Financial assets and financial liabilities are recognised when the company becomes a party to the contractual

Remediation : .
Target Servicing Limited is regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA), whose role includes protecting the
rights of consumers. During the year, a review of compliance with FCA regulatory requirements, internal policies and
procedures, and customer loan terms and conditions has continued. This review has been ongoing for several years,
previously limited in scope to Target Group Limited and Elderbridge Limited. The objective of this review is to assess
and quantify any customer detriment arising from non-compliance with FCA rules or best practice.
Based on external legal advice and internal analysis available as at 31 March 2025, management considers it probable
that for certain aspects of the review, there will be an outflow of funds from Target Servicing Limited. A provision has
been recognised for expected remediation payments to impacted customers. As the likelihood of recovering such
amounts is either uncertain or considered remote, no corresponding asset has been recognised.
Detailed modelling conducted during the year has led to issues being identified relating to the use of certain regulatory
permissions of Target Servicing Limited, and a corresponding provision of £1.1 million has been recognised.
Management expects the remediation programme to conclude during the financial year ending 31 March 2026, by which
point greater certainty over final settlement values will be achieved. .

Contingent liability - Remediation
As part of the review leading to the remediation provision, management considers that there are instances of
significant uncertainty where there is a possibility that Target Servicing Limited could be subject to further economic
outflows in the future with regards to the remediation project.
The degree of uncertainty is significant, and it is not yet possible to reliably estimate the likelihood, value or timing
of the future outflows. There are circumstances in which Target Servicing may be subject to an economic outflow
with some, or all, of the value being reimbursed by a third party.

exposure for certain elements of the potential costs noting that management do not expect a Target entity to be liable.
