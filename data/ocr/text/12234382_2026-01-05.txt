Business Strategy .
Digital Media Innovations UK Limited (“the Company”) is part of the global brand Notified (“we”, “us”, or “our”).
Notified is a leading technology and services partner to corporate storytellers, including investor relations (“IR”), public
relations (“PR”), and corporate communications professionals. Notified is the only technology company offering a suite
of end-to-end communications solutions and professional services for both IR and PR professionals. Our integrated
suite of communication tools and services empowers PR and IR professionals to take control of their corporate narrative,
using the most modern technologies and tools available to tell their story powerfully, effectively, and flawlessly.
Notified helps organizations effectively reach and engage customers, investors, employees, and the media — from IR
websites and shareholder communications to marketing and regulatory press release distribution (via
GlobeNewswire®), while measuring the associated impact of earned media and communication effectiveness.
Notified’s suite of award-winning communications solutions and its dedicated customer support and delivery teams are
relied upon by more than 10,000 global customers, from growing businesses and newly public companies, to some of
the world’s most recognizable brands.
In 2024 we continued our history and commitment of innovation with the following unique partnerships and solutions
launched or in our pipeline:

« Exclusive partnership with CLEAR: First ever press release distribution platform to include identity-
verification technology and accompanying watermark to enhance trust and reduce risk of fraudulent or
misleading press releases.

* Significantly enhanced GlobeNewswire Analytics platform to provide more actionable insights. The
advancements offer organizations deeper, more intuitive insights into the performance and ROI of their press
releases, making it easier to measure impact and optimize communication strategies.

« Al-powered Automated Media Insights: Enhanced the Media Contact Database with Al-powered capabilities
that summarize contact information, enabling users to easily identify and engage the most relevant media
professionals for their needs. This advancement streamlines media outreach and improves targeting efficiency.

« GlobeNewswire Press Release User Experience Enhancements: Introduced new features to increase the impact
and value of press releases as well as message comprehension, including a Quote Spotlight service to highlight
key content and a side bar content feature to provide additional context.

« Centralized IR Platform: IR Hub allows customers to log into one platform to manage all their major IR
communications programs. Platform is purpose-built to save time and streamline complicated processes.

During 2024, Notified also expanded its international footprint and capabilities across Europe and APAC in the

Business performance and key performance indicators
The directors consider revenue growth to be the primary key performance indicator of the business. Revenue is
generated from two distinct divisions: (i) Public Relations and Investor Relations (“PR/IR”) services and (ii)
Experiences.
During FY21, the Experiences platforms performed exceptionally well, driven in part by the impact of COVID-19 and
the accelerated adoption of virtual, remote and online webcasting solutions. Demand normalised in FY22 and FY23,
resulting in a decline in Experiences revenue over this period. In FY24, the Experiences business was sold globally to
a third party. :
PR and IR revenue increased by 5% from FY21 to FY22, by 11% from FY22 to FY23, and declined marginally by 1%
in FY24. Notwithstanding the modest reduction in FY24, management expects continued growth in this revenue stream
over the medium term. Whilst the disposal of the Experiences business has reduced total reported revenues, the directors
expect future growth in PR/IR revenue to more than offset this change. :

’ fixed assets. This generated a loss on disposal of fixed assets of £8k and a profit on disposal of intangible assets of

amortised and therefore no further charges of this nature are expected. There was also a £600k reduction in the
intercompany cash pool creditor during the period.
Market Opportunity
Notified are a leading global provider of technology solutions for PR and IR professionals. Our global content
distribution network reaches over 158 countries and over 35 languages. Notified have been a leader in these solutions
since our acquisitions of Nasdaq Public Relations Solutions and Digital Media Services and INXPO, Inc. in 2018 and
NSO Group AB, d.b.a. Notified in 2019 (in 2021, the segment formerly known as Digital Media was renamed Notified).
The opportunities in the markets where Notified participate are mature and stable. However, both PR and IR are in the
midst of massive disruption caused by technological innovations, economic pressures, and consumer behaviours. PR
and IR professionals must adapt, embrace new technologies and behaviours, and find partners to help them.
Our single-sign-on end-to-end workflow provides us with a competitive advantage as well as a significant opportunity
to cross-sell to our existing client base as clients look to consolidate all aspects of the communications workflow.
GlobeNewswire is one of three industry leaders in the hard-to-enter press release distribution space, leaving Notified
well positioned to expand, consolidate, and disrupt into high growth, emerging sub-industries. Notified have a diverse
blue-chip customer base and are trusted by leading global organizations to deliver communications. Notified have a
global reach, operating in 16 countries and serving clients in 85 countries. Notified have an exclusive partnership with
Nasdaq offering Nasdaq-subsidized IPO packages that include IR website, earnings calls, IR day, and regulatory
releases.

Financial risk management objectives and policies
The financial risks and uncertainties of the business have been reviewed by the directors and are assessed as follows:
Price risk
The Company is exposed to price risk as a result of its participation in a competitive marketplace. The need to remain
competitive may lead to price erosion. However, the directors believe that our single-sign-on end-to-end workflow
: provides us with a competitive advantage as well as a significant opportunity to cross-sell to our existing client base as
clients look to consolidate all aspects of the communication workflow.
Credit risk
The Company’s principal financial assets are bank balances and cash, trade and other receivables. The amounts
* presented in the balance sheet are net of allowances for doubtful receivables. The Company has no significant
concentration of credit risk, with exposure spread over a large number of customers.
Cash flow risk
In the current uncertain economic environment, the directors are of the opinion that the Company is well placed to
respond to the challenges faced. In addition, the Company has a strong balance sheet position and maintains good
margins and a healthy cash flow.
Liquidity risk .
The Company manages cash flow to ensure that sufficient funds are available for ongoing operations and future
developments. The Company is able to obtain additional cash reserves from Group led by West Technology Group
LLC (West Group) resources and is confident of its ability to meet its obligations as they fall due.
Foreign exchange risk
The Company does not hedge transactions in foreign currencies but maintains bank accounts in foreign currencies in
order to partially match cash inflows and outflows in those currencies.
The remaining exposure relates to’ intercompany trading with other affiliated companies, and customer and supplier
invoices. No specific risk management is applied to these foreign exchange exposures.

Cash Pool Arrangements : : .
The Company participates in a multi — currency cash pooling arrangement administered by West Group Treasury. Under
this arrangement, surplus cash balances are automatically swept into the pool and deficit balances are funded from the
pool, with interest charged or credited at a rate equal to the Interbank Offered Rates. Participation in the pool provides
the Company with efficient access to short-term liquidity while optimizing the management of surplus funds within the
West Group.
Going concern .
In accordance with their responsibilities as directors, the directors have considered the appropriateness of the going
concern basis for the preparation of the financial statements.
In arriving at their assessment, the directors considered the anticipated performance of the Company, the accessibility
to funds and the support available from its parent companies for the 12 months from signing date to 31 December 2026
as follows:

« Performance — the directors reviewed the forecasted income statement for the Company for the remainder of
2025 and also the full year 2026. Despite an EBITDA loss in 2025 the Company anticipate profitable EBITDA
in 2026 and beyond. The directors considered the current market conditions and future trade for the Digital
Media business in which Digital Media Innovations UK Limited operates, and believe the opportunities are
large and growing.

« Funding — the directors considered the funding available to the Company and its ability to meet its liabilities
as they fall due. The Company is part of a cash pooling arrangement with other European group entities owed
by Digital Media Innovations Holdings Limited. The West group treasury function ensures that a minimum
balance is always maintained, and the Company has access to this. The other entities in the arrangement are
cash generative and at the date of approval of the financial statements there was a significant surplus in the
pool.

e Group support - the Company operates within a large multinational group which is profitable and cash
generative. The ultimate holding company as the signing date, Orbit Private Holdings I Limited, has provided
a letter of support guaranteeing that it would meet any third party liabilities or intercompany debts that the
Company was not able to meet plus it would not recall any intercompany loans for a period of at least 12
months from date of approval of these financial statements if the Company was unable to meet the demand.

using the tax rates and laws that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.
Deferred tax is recognised in respect of all timing differences that have originated but not reversed at the balance sheet
date where transactions or events that result in an obligation to pay more tax in the future or a right to pay less tax in
the future have occurred at the balance sheet date. Timing differences are differences between the Company's taxable
profits and its results as stated in the financial statements that arise from the inclusion of gains and losses in tax
assessments in periods different from those in which they are recognised in the financial statements.
Unrelieved tax losses and other deferred tax assets are recognised only to the extent that, on the basis of all available
evidence, it can be regarded as more likely than not that there will be suitable taxable profits from which the future
. reversal of the underlying timing differences can be deducted.

When the amount that can be deducted for tax for an asset (other than goodwill) that is recognised in a business
combination is less (more) than the value at which it is recognised, a deferred tax liability (asset) is recognised for the
additional tax that will be paid (avoided) in respect of that difference. Similarly, a deferred tax asset (liability) is
recognised for the additional tax that will be avoided (paid) because of a difference between the value at which a liability
is recognised and the amount that will be assessed for tax. The amount attributed to goodwill is adjusted by the amount
of deferred tax recognised.
Deferred tax liabilities are recognised for timing differences arising from investments in subsidiaries and associates,
except where the Company is able to control the reversal of the timing difference and it is probable that it will not
reverse in the foreseeable future.
Deferred tax is measured using the tax rates and laws that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the balance
sheet date that are expected to apply to the reversal of the timing difference. Deferred tax relating to non-depreciable
property measured using the revaluation model and investment property is measured using the tax rates and allowances
that apply to sale of the asset. In other cases, the measurement of deferred tax liabilities and assets reflects the tax
consequences that would follow from the manner in which the Company expects, at the end of the reporting period, to
recover or settle the carrying amount of its assets and liabilities.
Where items recognised in other comprehensive income or equity are chargeable to or deductible for tax purposes, the
resulting current or deferred tax expense or income is presented in the same component of comprehensive income or
equity as the transaction or other event that resulted in the tax expense or income.
Current tax assets and liabilities are offset only when there is a legally enforceable right to set off the amounts and the
Company intends either to settle on a net basis or to realise the asset and settle the liability simultaneously.
Deferred tax assets and liabilities are offset only if: a) the Company has a legally enforceable right to set off current tax
assets against current tax liabilities; and b) the deferred tax assets and deferred tax liabilities relate to income taxes
levied by the same taxation authority on either the same taxable entity or different taxable entities which intend either
to settle current tax liabilities and assets on a net basis, or to realise the assets and settle the liabilities simultaneously,
in each future period in which significant amounts of deferred tax liabilities or assets are expected to be settled or
recovered.
