GROUP STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 MARCH 2025
Introduction
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2025.
Principat activities
The principal activity of the Group continued to be that of a Global Financial Technology (“FinTech") Group
company.
VoxSmart develops data analysis software for the financial services industry, enabling financial institutions to
create business value from their financial communications data.
With offices in 5 global cities (London, New York, Singapore, Madrid, and Melbourne) VoxSmart designs,
develops, and deploys its proprietary technology to over 100 customers worldwide, including many of the largest
financial institutions in the world.
During the year, the Group continued its focus on research and design, new solution development and customer
onboarding and integration. The Group invested in, and released, many new features and enhancements in its
Enterprise Risk and Compliance Management (“ERCM”) solutions throughout the year including making
: substantial advancements in the areas of Cloud Computing (“SaaS”), Artificial Intelligence (“Al”), Machine
Learning (“ML”) and Natural Language Processing (“NLP”).
Business review
The results for the period are set out on page 12.
Growth with Purpose
This year, the Group surpassed a significant milestone of $10 million in Annual Recurring Revenue (“ARR”). This
achievement is a testament to the Group’s relentless focus on customer value, the differentiated data platform,
and the increasing demand for modern compliance solutions in financial markets globally.
Key performance highlights from the year include:
. Revenue Growth: ARR grew 37% year-over-year, driven by both new customer acquisition and
expansion within the Group's Tier 1 Banking, Inter Dealer Broker, Asset Management and Energy firm's base.
. Customer Retention: Net Revenue Retention ("NRR”) of 131%, reflecting strong upsell momentum and
impressively low churn (1.4%).
. Operational Efficiency: The Group improved gross margin to 84%, up from 82% last year, aligning with
top SaaS benchmarks globally.

Simplifying Compliance in a Complex Regulatory World :
The Group's mission is to ‘simplify everything compliance.’ In a space crowded with fragmented tools and rising
global regulatory demands, the Group is building a single, dependable platform built for institutions that need
clarity and control. :
This year, the Group successfully delivered:
. A modern, Al-enabled platform: With new release pipelines and modular architecture, setting the stage for
faster, safer product delivery in the remainder of 2025 and beyond. This accelerates time to market, unlocking
new revenue streams and end to end product experiences while reducing delivery risk, improving scalability,
security, and ensuring future product rollouts are seamless and cost-efficient.
. Al Driven Compliance: The Group’s proprietary Al Intents Engine processes millions of voice and
messaging interactions monthly across mobile, fixed lines, top messaging apps, and trade communications,
spotting anomalies in real time while slashing false positives by around 50% to boost compliance accuracy and
efficiency.
. Cross-Jurisdictional Coverage: Spanning EMEA, North America, and GCC, the Group’s platform delivers
real-time compliance alignment with evolving regulations like MiFID !1, Dodd-Frank, MAR, and VARA, ensuring
customers stay ahead no matter where they operate.
. Reducing the average time to value from 9 to under 3 months, including big and complex deployments.
This enabled us to unlock growth much earlier, strengthening Group expansion.
By embedding A\ at its core and unifying essential compliance functions, the Group is slashing complexity and
vendor fragmentation, while delivering seamless, globally localised compliance that adapts effortlessly to any
country or regulatory mandate.
This is compliance made simple, scalable, and truly inclusive.
Market Expansion: Designed for Global, Regulated Growth
During this period, the Group has established key commercial footholds:
° New Strategic Customers: The Group signed 8 new (enterprise) contracts — including Tier 1 banks and
hedge funds.
. Channel Leverage: The Group built out GTM partnerships with IPC, FIS, and leading regional
consultants in key markets.
. Mid-Market Momentum: Strong upsell and cross-sell growth within mid-market accounts — a segment
often overlooked by legacy vendors.

, Crucially, >20% of the Group’s new business pipeline is from inbound referrals and partner integrations,
signalling both market pull and product-led growth efficiency.
Markets & Customers: Scaling Beyond the UK
This year marked the Group's first full year with a dedicated focus on South America and the Middle East, where
early traction validates our product market fit beyond continental Europe:
. New Logos: The Group added 8 enterprise customers, including 2 top-financial institutions and 4 top-
customers in Latin America.
. Use Case Expansion: From voice surveillance to crypto surveillance monitoring, customers are
expanding their use of the platform to manage an increasingly diverse set of regulatory risks.
. Channel Partnerships: Strategic integrations with leading compliance and messaging platforms have
amplified the Group's reach and go-to-market efficiency.

Group strategy and future outlook
Strong Foundational Support & Strategic M&A Readiness
As the group continues its planning for the remainder of 2025, specific focus areas include:
. Global Sales Acceleration: Doubling go-to-market headcount with regional leadership hires, dedicated
marketing support and increased top-of-funnel! activity.
. Al Governance Suite: Expanding use cases into transaction-level forensics and proactive and
preventative compliance alerts.
. Buy vs. Build: Evaluating bolt-on and tuck-in acquisitions in analytics, voice surveillance as well as KYC
& IDV to accelerate roadmap and revenue growth.
The Group remains deeply committed to building not just a great company, but a long-term, defensible platform
that delivers recurring value to institutions navigating regulatory complexity.
While macroeconomic uncertainty persists, compliance remains non-negotiable. The Group is confident that the
business is positioned counter-cyclically — as firms seek scalable, intelligent ways to manage risk.
The strategy of the Group is to continue to build market leading solutions, use cases and applications into its
products to help financial institutions participants manage risk, regulatory compliance, and operational
effectiveness.
The Group has been a market leader with solutions for the Enterprise Risk & Compliance Management
(“ERCM”) sector for over a decade having pioneered many innovations and initiatives such as compliant Mobile
Voice, SMS and WhatsApp Capture, Transcription & Translation and Triggered Actions for financial institutions.
Following substantial investment throughout 2024/25 in Research and Development, prior years external
acquisitions and new product innovation, the Group’s strategy is to enter a multi-year phase of significant and
sustainable revenue growth to achieve the Group’s profitability goals by 2025/26.
The Group is targeting >50% year-on-year annual recurring revenue growth by capitalising on the ever-
expanding global market for financial technology and in particular Cloud Computing (“SaaS”), Artificial
Intelligence (“Al”) and Machine Learning (“ML”) Technology. The Group will achieve this through continued
acquisition of new accounts, expansion of existing accounts and the consistent release of new solutions, use
cases and services.
Recent financial markets competition, high profile regulatory fines and continued geo-political pressures give the
Group’s management team strong confidence in their ability to execute the strategy particularly as global
financial institutions continue to invest heavily (Smultibillion) in Data Analytics and Al solutions across the Front-
office, Back-office and Compliance.

Currency risk
The Group trades internationally and is exposed to movements in exchange rates. The Group’s Finance team
monitors and minimises as far as possible the net exposure on a regular basis.
Credit risk
The Group's principal credit risk relates to the recovery of amounts from trade debtors. Debts are actively
chased by the Finance department, supported by the Sales team managing the customer relationship.
Liquidity risk
The business monitors cash flow as part of its day-to-day control procedures. The Group’s Finance team
considers cash flow projections on a monthly basis and ensures that appropriate facilities are available to be
drawn upon, as necessary.
Data Security and Cyber Threats
The Group mitigates these risks through robust governance structures, alignment to ISO 27001, continuous
monitoring, incident response planning, and regular staff training. Investment continues in our security
Capabilities to reflect the evolving nature of threats. The Board maintains oversight of this area through quarterly
tisk reporting.
Going concern
The group made losses of £7.7m in the year (2024: £9.1) and had net assets of £3.6m at 31 March 2025 (2024:
£5.6m). During the year, the group has onboarded new customers, continued developing market leading
specialist technology.
As turnover for the year has been lower than expected with deals taking longer to close, the Group decided to
seek further funding.
In October 2024, the company secured a 3-year, £5.5m, Revolving Credit Facility from HSBC. This has been
used to finance the repayment of the previous NatWest facility as well as related fees.
The directors of VoxSmart Limited have received a letter of financial support from its principal investor,
Toscafund Asset Management LLP, confirming that it intends to provide financial support directly or arrange
such support to meet liabilities.
As a result, the directors believe the group and company have enough resources to continue as a going concern
for a period of at least 12 months from the date of approval of these financial statements.

2.5 Turnover
Turnover is measured as the fair value of the consideration received or receivable, excluding
discounts, rebates, value added tax and other sales taxes.

necessary to reflect the substance of the arrangement and in relation to two or more linked
transactions when necessary to understand the commercial effect.
When cash inflows are deferred and represent a financing arrangement, the fair value of the
consideration is the present value of the future receipts. The difference between the fair value of the
consideration and the nominal amount received is recognised as interest income.
Software licensing
Turnover from software licensing arrangements which do not contain any requirement for the

the time the software license is delivered.

completion, cost incurred and cost to complete can be estimated reliably. The stage of completion is
