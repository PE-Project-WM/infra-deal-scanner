Talogy Limited
Strategic Report
For the year ended 31 December 2025
Introduction ;

Talogy Limited is a UK trading subsidiary of Talogy International Holdings Limited a company registered in the
UK. The ultimate parent is Lifelong Learner Enterprises, LLC, registered in the US.
Until January 2024, the two principal activities of the Lifelong Learner Group (LLH/Group) were the delivery of
high stakes testing services (PSI) and talent assessment and development products and services (Talogy).
These were provided from offices across the US, UK, Europe, Middle East, and Asia Pacific. In January 2024
LLH sold the PSI business to reduce debt and to focus on the growth and development of the Talogy
business.
Talogy is a global partner in talent assessment and development, helping organisations attract, develop, and
retain talent. Through integrated, end-to-end solutions, Talogy combines assessments, technology, and
consulting services to support clients across the talent life cycle. Talogy's offerings span early careers through
senior leadership and include personality, cognitive ability, values, situational judgment, simulations, and job-
relevant experiences designed to be fair, accessible, and predictive. These solutions are delivered through a
flexible, enterprise-ready platform and are complemented by consulting services that support leadership
development, coaching, and broader talent transformation initiatives.
Business Review and key performance indicators -
The Company's revenue increased by £1.1m to £8.9m in 2025. Core revenue from licence and service
activities rose by 14% to £8.8m, primarily driven by the transition of UK customers to Talogy Limited from its
sister UK entities as the Talogy group continues to streamline its UK operations. In addition, Talogy Limited
earns royalties based on revenue from its associated UK companies; in 2025, royalty income increased by
57% to £137,000.
In 2025 the Company onboarded a new Executive Leadership Team. This team completed an operational
review of the business and that review, coupled with their perspectives on the market and macroenvironment
drove actions to reposition the business. These actions included resizing the cost base, streamlining
operations, and sharpening strategic focus to better align with evolving market conditions. It also included
centralising the UK payroll within Talogy Limited through the transfer of the employees from Talogy
International Holdings Limited on 1 April 2025. At the same time, the Company accelerated targeted
investment in its people, technology, and digital capabilities to enhance agility, improve efficiency, and support
more informed decision-making.
These measures are intended to strengthen operational resilience and establish a solid foundation for
sustainable long-term growth. As market conditions stabilise, the business is well positioned to capitalise on
emerging opportunities with a more focused, efficient, and technology-enabled operating model. Reflecting
these actions, gross profit margin improved to 65.7% (2024: 63.9%) and operating profit before non-recurring
administrative items increased by 42% to.£2.1m.
The Company's full-year pre-tax operating profit after non-recurring administrative items was £0.6m (2024:
£12.3m). The primary driver of the reduction in operating profit was the 2024 group management review of

: intercompany indebtedness across the Group, during which certain balances were determined to be non-
payable. As a result, the Company forgave net intercompany balances, with £9.7m recognised as a credit to
non-recurring administrative expenses in the statement of income and retained earnings in 2024. In 2025,
the Company discharged intercompany balances with other Group entities that were wound up during the
year as part of the ongoing rationalisation of the Talogy business. This resulted in a charge to non-recurring
administrative expenses of £1.5m in 2025.
No dividends were paid during the year.

Future Developments
The primary aim of Talogy Limited is to establish itself as a leading provider of technology-enabled tools and
services, particularly in the areas of assessment, selection and people development, while continuing to grow
its client portfolio. We deliver innovative, integrated solutions for HR professionals globally, alongside the
infrastructure and tools required for assessment development and delivery across multiple channels. The
Company has consistently invested in and enhanced its products, offering robust and effective solutions that
support HR professionals, strengthen existing relationships, and attract new clients.
Principal risks and uncertainties
People
As a professional services organisation, the Group’s performance depends on the selection, development and
retention of highly skilled employees. To support retention, the Group invests in personal development
through a structured training programme and provides opportunities for individuals to undertake challenging
assignments internationally. Consistent hiring quality is maintained through the use of the Group’s own tools
and expertise in selection and assessment processes, The Group also maintains a network of associates to
manage peaks in workload; these associates are carefully selected and their output closely monitored to
ensure consistent standards of delivery.
Information Technology
The Group's ability to deliver its online products globally relies on robust network infrastructure. The risk of
service disruption is mitigated through the use of off-site, third-party hosting, which provides resilience across
power, network and hardware, alongside adherence to best practice security measures. This is distinct from
the provision of internal IT services for employees. IT security risks are further mitigated by maintaining ISO
27001 certification for Information Security Management Systems and by implementing appropriate privacy
controls in line with the European General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).
Compliance & Security
Online tools and mandatory training relating to data compliance and security risks remain a key component of
employee development. A rigorous due diligence process is undertaken when establishing new relationships,
with annual reviews conducted to ensure the Group not only remains globally compliant but also adheres to
best practice standards.
Litigation
The Group operates in an environment where data protection and fairness in selection are subject to close
scrutiny by both candidates and clients. Specialist legal and data protection advice is obtained to ensure that
operational procedures and client offerings comply fully with local regulations, thereby reducing the risk of
litigation.
Cashflow risk
The Group is exposed to working capital risk, which is actively managed through robust debtor management, _
strong client relationships and clearly defined responsibilities for the pursuit of outstanding payments. Cash
flow is regularly reviewed by management. The Group also benefits from the support of its parent company,
which can provide funding where necessary to address any shortfall and support the requirements of
individual entities.
Economic Risks .
Prudent management has enabled the Group to respond swiftly to uncertainties arising from economic
changes. Its technology infrastructure and capital management approach support flexible working
arrangements globally and facilitate multi-channel delivery of services. This agile, diverse and collaborative
model places the Group in a strong position to navigate periods of economic uncertainty.

1.4 Going concern
The Company is dependent on the continued financial support of its parent company. The parent
company has confirmed that it intends to provide financial support to the Company and will not seek
repayment of amounts owed to it for a period of at least twelve months from the date of approval of
these financial statements.
The parent company's ability to continue providing financial support is dependent on the successful
refinancing of borrowing facilities that mature in April 2027. As at the date of approval of these financial
statements, the refinancing is in preparation but has not been completed. The directors believe the
parent company will be able to refinance the debt facility and continue supporting the Company for a
period of at least twelve months, however, were the refinancing to be unsuccessful, the directors
acknowledge there would be a material uncertainty which may cast significant doubt over the company's
ability to continue as a going concern. .
Notwithstanding this uncertainty, the directors are confident that the company will continue in operational
existence for the foreseeable future. The Company therefore continues to adopt the going concern
basis in preparing its financial statements.
