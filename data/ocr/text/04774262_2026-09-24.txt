Strategic report

The director presents the Strategic Report for RETN LTD ("the Company") for the year ended 31 December 2025. :

Business review

The Company is part of the RETN Networks Group of companies (‘the Group’). Its main activity is providing

wholesale telecom services such as IP Transit, Leased Capacity (100G/400G wavelength), Ethernet services in

Europe, Asia and certain other geographic locations.

The Company is focused on the market in the United Kingdom (‘UK’) and global accounts. The Company has

diversified its customer base through an expanded product suite and a consolidated marketing push in its European

markets, whilst at the same time consolidating its position in its existing markets. The Company serves international

carriers, large regional carriers, global and regional enterprises, content and media players, and other corporates with

requirements in the regions where Group has its network coverage. :

The Company generated net profit of £154,231 for 2025 compared to net profit of £44,340 for 2024. Implementing :

strategic cost-effectiveness measures directly expanded profit margins by optimizing operational workflows and

reducing overhead costs. :

Future developments .

The Directors remain confident in the Company's long-term prospects and believe it is well positioned to benefit from

the continued growth in demand for high-capacity, secure and resilient digital connectivity. Increasing data

consumption, cloud adoption, artificial intelligence workloads and international network requirements continue to

support demand for the Company's services across its markets.

Looking ahead, the Company will continue to focus on executing its long-term strategy by expanding its network

footprint, strengthening its product offering and enhancing the quality and resilience of its services. Investment will

remain focused on strategic infrastructure, automation and digital technologies that improve operational efficiency,

customer experience and long-term scalability.

The Company remains committed to strong governance practices, with a particular focus on monitoring legal and

regulatory developments, including sanctions. To ensure ongoing compliance, the Company utilises advanced

software solutions from globally recognised. providers, enabling it to stay current with evolving legal requirements

and sanctions regulations. .

Although the Directors remain mindful of the broader macroeconomic, geopolitical and competitive environment,

they believe that the Company's diversified customer base, international network, recurring revenue model and :

experienced management team provide a strong platform for sustainable long-term growth.

Principal risks and uncertainties
Price risk
The Company operates in a complex global environment characterised by geopolitical uncertainty, economic volatility
and evolving market conditions. Key risks include geopolitical developments, macroeconomic pressures and potential
operational disruptions, which may affect customer demand, investment activity and the overall business environment.
In addition, the telecommunications market remains highly competitive. Continued investment and network expansion
by large international and regional connectivity providers have increased competitive pressure, contributing to
ongoing price erosion across certain services and markets. The Company actively monitors market developments and
continues to focus on service quality, network resilience, geographic expansion and long-term customer relationships
to maintain its competitive position.
Cash flow and foreign exchange risk .
The Company’s activities expose it primarily to the financial risks of changes in foreign currency exchange rates and
interest rates. The Company does not use foreign exchange forward contracts and interest rate swap contracts to hedge
these exposures. The majority of the Company’s revenues are generated in EUR and USD, which are to a large degree
naturally hedged with the costs, which are also incurred in its majority in EUR and USD. Some portion of General
and Admin costs and financing costs are borne in GBP.
The Company’s credit risk is primarily attributable to its trade receivables. The amounts presented in the balance sheet
are net of allowances for doubtful receivables. An allowance for impairment is made where there is an identified loss
event which, based on previous experience, is evidence of a reduction in the recoverability of the cash flows.
The Company is exposed to cash flow risk arising from the timing of customer receipts, supplier payments and
ongoing capital investment requirements. Management actively monitors cash flow forecasts and working capital,
maintains appropriate liquidity, and seeks to align operating cash inflows with funding requirements to ensure the

‘Company can meet its financial obligations as they fall due.

Compliance with laws and regulation
The Company operates in a complex legal and regulatory environment. As a result, there is a risk that failure to comply
with applicable laws, regulations, and industry standards may lead to significant legal, financial, and reputational
damage. This includes risks related to regulatory changes, litigation, data protection, environmental regulations,
sector-specific legislation and international sanctions.
The Company takes a proactive approach to managing compliance risk and implements continuous monitoring of
regulatory developments across all jurisdictions it operates. In addition, the Company fosters a culture of compliance,
ethical behaviour, and accountability at all levels. This commitment ensures that the Company remains vigilant and
responsive to potential risks and maintains the highest standards of governance. \

: The Company has a rigorous proactive sanctions compliance policy in place, confirming compliance of customers,
suppliers and other partners both at an onboarding stage and at regular intervals after onboarding. This process checks
for compliance with the sanctions of, the United Kingdom, the European Union, and the United States of America.
Further the Company’s operating policies are developed with the guidance of leading legal specialists in international
sanctions.

Going concern
RETN LTD isa part of the Group of companies owned by RETN Networks Ltd. The RETN Group is a leading
Eurasian data services provider with unique network assets operating an extensive network connecting 45+ territories
in three continents. Total network coverage exceeds 42,400 km with deep metro network in selected cities. The
‘ operations of RETN subsidiaries are intrinsically connected with each other. Any subsidiary can provide the service
to its customers only by having access to the shared network and Group-wide services. To serve the customers of any
subsidiary, the access to one or more subsidiaries’ network may be required. Due to such an operating structure, the
Company is dependent on the access to all Group’s assets and services. The Group has prepared forecasts indicating
that the Group will continue in operational existence under different scenarios, and continue growing, for the
foreseeable future, and at least for twelve months from the approval of these financial statements.
The Company has a strong customer and geographically diverse customer base, access to Group’s infrastructure and
continued support from its shareholder. Therefore, the Director believes that the Company is well placed to manage
its business risks successfully despite the current uncertain economic outlook and therefore continue to adopt the going
concern basis of accounting in preparing the annual financial statements.

-the contract contains an identified asset, which is either explicitly identified in the contract or implicitly specified by

A financial asset not carried at fair value through profit or loss is assessed at each reporting date to determine whether

An impairment loss in respect of a financial asset measured at amortised cost is calculated as the difference between

For financial instruments measured at cost less impairment an impairment is calculated as the difference between it

Non-financial assets
The carrying amounts of the Company’s non-financial assets, other than stocks and deferred tax assets, are reviewed
at each reporting date.to determine whether there is any indication of impairment. If any such indication exists, then
the asset’s recoverable amount is estimated. .
The recoverable amount of an asset or cash-generating unit is the greater of its value in use and its fair value less costs :
to sell. In assessing value in use, the estimated future cash flows are discounted to their present value using a pre-tax
