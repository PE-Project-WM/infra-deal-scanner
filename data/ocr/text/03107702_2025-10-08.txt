Strategic Report for the Year Ended 31 December 2024
The directors present their strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2024.
Principal activity
The company provides infrastructure solutions for the delivery of broadband services. This involves the
manufacture of fibre cable and the procurement of telecom broadband equipment for sale in the UK and abroad.
The company operates as part of the wider CommScope group ("Group") selling to external customers and
related parties.
Review of the business and outlook
The company has seen a year on year growth in tumover of 7.1% (2023: decline of 45.2%). Company's external
' sales are subject to volatility and are dependent on capital project. activity within the marketplace. The
distribution business to external operators remained stable in 2024. However, manufacturing outputs in North
Wales have further declined. The Company continues to counteract this through identifying efficiencies and cost
management.
The increase in turnover is driven by cost plus income from affiliated companies through the services the
company's employees provide to other group companies. The number of such employees has increased
following a global plan driven by CommScope Group’s general manager mode that was implemented on 1
January 2024 to re-align employees to their respective business segment legal entities. 41 employees transferred
into the company on this date and a number of external contractors. On the 1 October 2024 an additional 19
employees transferred into the company. The company incurred expense of £3,048,000 as a result of the
employee transfers, recognised in the administrative expenses. .
The company's gross profit has increased and operating margin has reduced to 33.83% and -1.66 % respectively
(2023: 15.52% and -0.81%). Gross profit margin has improved through additional £10m of cost plus income
from affiliates where the expenses are recognised in administrative expenses and £1m of improved gross profit
for trade sales of manufactured or externally procured product. Also, fluctuations in GBP to EURO and GBP to
USD exchanges rates throughout 2024 have resulted in a favourable impact on the operating margin by £0.7m.
For part of the business, the company buys finished goods in EUR from affiliates for resale to the customer in
GBP. The company continues to look for ways to reduce costs and improve cash flow. .
The Directors’ are satisfied with the performance of the company's investment.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties facing the business are the pace of technological development and changes
of strategy by the operators, often at short notice. The company addresses these with its focus on technological
and market solutions and by maintaining close working relationships with the operators, and offering robust,
efficient, simplified and future proof products and services. The company does not consider there to be any sales
price risks.
Technological risk, credit risk, liquidity risk and cash flow risk
The company is competing in a technologically intensive industry. It is important that we remain competitive in
this field and so invest appropriate resources in this area. The company has been benefiting from growth in the
UK connectivity markets, although this has slowed down signifiantly in the last 12 months.
The company's credit risks are covered by a process of credit checks and an experienced accounts receivable
department.
Liquidity and cash flow risk are managed by the group treasury function.
Russia-Ukraine and Israel-Gaza conflicts
The crisis in Russia and Ukraine began in February 2022 and in Israel and Gaza in October 2023 and continues
as of the date of this annual report. CommScope Group suspended all the services in Russia and Belarus, which
has not had and is not expected to have any impact on the financial statements or operating results of the
Company. The conflict in Israel and Gaza has not had a material impact on the financial statements or operating
results of the Company. The attacks on the ships on Red Sea have increased freight costs and transport times for
products coming from Asia to Europe although this impact has eased during the second half of 2024.
Future developments
The Company's ultimate owner announced on 18 July 2024 to sell its OWN segment as well as the Distributed
Antenna Systems business unit of its NICS segment to Amphenol Corporation. This transaction was completed
at the end of January 2025 and as part of this sale the Company acquired a defined benefit pension liability. :

The directors acknowledge their responsibilities for preparing the Annual Report and the financial statements in

Company law requires the directors to prepare financial statements for each financial year. Under that law the

give a true and fair view of the state of affairs of the company and of the profit or loss of the company for that
period. In preparing these financial statements, the directors are required to:
* select suitable accounting policies and apply them consistently;
* make judgements and accounting estimates that are reasonable and prudent;
* state whether applicable United Kingdom Accounting Standards have been followed, subject to any material
departures disclosed and explained in the financial statements; and
* prepare the financial statements on the going concern basis unless it is inappropriate to presume that the
company will continue in business.
The directors are responsible for keeping adequate accounting records that are sufficient to show and explain the
company's transactions and disclose with reasonable accuracy at any time the financial position of the company
and enable them to ensure that the financial statements comply with the Companies Act 2006. They are also
responsible for safeguarding the assets of the company and hence for taking reasonable steps for the prevention
and detection of fraud and other irregularities. .
