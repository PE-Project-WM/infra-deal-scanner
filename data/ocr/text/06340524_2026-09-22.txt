Methodology
GHG emissions are calculated in line with GHG Reporting Protocol - Corporate standard and reported in line with
the UK Governnient's Guidance on Streamlined Energy and Carbon Reporting and mandatory GHG reporting
guidance. Our Scope 1, Scope 2, and Scope 3 energy use and GHG data for 2025 has been independently
produced by an external consultancy with expertise jn this area based on information. provided by MediaTek.
Energy and-carbon emissions comes from a number of sources:
* Natural gas used for building space heatirig (Scope 1)
* Electricity used for lighting, heating, ventilation and air conditioning (HVAC), and the operation of office
equipment (Scope:2) .
* The use of vehicles for business travéel— grey fleet only (Scope 3)
The company does not own or lease any vehicles, consistent with FY25 reporting, and therefore the company does
not report Scope 1 mobile combustion energy use or emissions. Scope 1 emissions only include natural gas usage’
and Scope-2 emissions are related to purchased electricity across all operational sites.
Only the Cambourne office in Cambridge.consumed natural gas during FY25. Consumption. data was obtained from .
monthly utility invoices, covering the full 12-month reporting period. This data was based on actual meter readings.
For Scope 2 electricity emissions, morithly electricity invoices covering the full 12-month reporting were provided for:
both the: Cambourne office and the County Gate site. At the Cambourne site, all electricity usage is attributable
solely to the company. At the-County Gate site in Kent, electricity is invoiced for the entire building, which is shared
with other tenants. Electricity consumption attributable to the company was apportioned based on occupied floor
area, with the company occupying 30.79% of the total building space. Accordingly, electricity consumption for this
site was estimated using this proportion.
For grey fleet travel under Scope 3 emissions, annual mileage. data extracted from the finance system was used to
calculate emissions and energy consumption. The Directors: excluded records relating to train tickets, parking fees,
or other non-mileage expenses unrelated to grey fleet travel. Mileage was estimated by the company using the.
standard reimbursement raté of £0.45 per mile. Emissions ard energy consumption were then calculated using the.
relevant UK Government conversion factors. Where vehicle type or fuel information was: unavailable, average
vehicle emission factors. were applied.
