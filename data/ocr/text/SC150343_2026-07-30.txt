Fair review of the business
Commsworld is a leading UK Telecommunications Network Provider and Internet Service Provider (ISP). It
operates in the business to business market and its client base includes small, medium and large organisations in
both the private and public sectors. The business has a broad portfolio of Cloud, Security and Voice solutions,
supported by in-house service & support capabilities.
The quality of service provided has been a key factor in helping the company secure large contracts in both public
[| and private sectors. Our support staff are ITIL qualified and we have {SO9001 and ISO27001 accreditations. The

Financial risk management objectives and policies
The company uses various financial instruments which include cash, and various items, such as trade debtors and
trade creditors that arise directly from its operations. The main purpose of these financial instruments is to raise
finance for the company's operations. The existence of these financial instruments exposes the company to a”
number of financial risks, are described in more detail below.
Liquidity risk
The company seeks to manage financial risk by ensuring sufficient liquidity is available to meet foreseeable needs.
The objective is to ensure a mix of funding methods offering flexibility and cost effectiveness to match the needs of
the company. Currently the company has no loans or overdrafts other than loans with its parent company and
makes use of finance leases where possible.
Interest rate risk
The company has no external debt through bank borrowing or overdrafts and thus its exposure to interest rate risk
is limited. The company does have balances held in deposit accounts at times, however, the interest received from
these are immaterial.
Currency risk
The company is exposed to foreign exchange risk. The company's policy is to minimise the use of complex financial
instruments and foreign exchange is not hedged. Foreign exchange differences on retranslation of these assets and
liabilities are taken to profit or loss. The overall impact of foreign exchange is not material to the company.
Credit risk
The principal credit risk arises from its trade debtors. In order to manage credit risk the directors set limits for
customers based on a combination of payment history and third party credit references. Credit limits are reviewed
by the credit controller on a regular basis in conjunction with debt ageing and collection history,

Turnover
Turnover is recognised at the fair value of the consideration received or receivable for goods and services 1
provided in the normal course of business, and is shown net of VAT and other sales related taxes. I
For hardware installations, revenue is recognised at the point of service delivery.
For network service call charges, revenue is recognised in the month in which the calls occur. As customers |
ate billed one month in arrears the final month of the years network service call charges are included in i
accrued income at the balance sheet date. Training and maintenance contracts are recognised over the life of i
the contract. i
For network service charges, revenue is recognised in the month in which network service Is delivered. As i
certain customers are billed in advance for network service charges, {he element of revenue relating to the i
next financial year is recognised within deferred income at the balance sheet date. |
|
|
The company has secured several large, multi-year contracts, where the end customer is a public sector body. |
