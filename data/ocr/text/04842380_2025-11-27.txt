The director presents their Strategic Report for the year ended 31 March 2025.
Principal activity
Merit Data & Technology Limited's main activities are to support the b2b information and media industry
through the following:
= Data Product services: we gather, organise and enrich the data that feeds some of the world's
leading b2b intelligence brands.
= Marketing Data services: we research prospect and customer data to make it rich, compliant
and relevant for defined target audiences.
= Marketing Automation: we create the plans, audience segments and digital assets to get the
most out of marketing automation tools.
= Customised software services: we supply direct, flexible, and cost-effective access to a wealth
of programming talent to deliver client projects.
Review of the business and future developments
, A key performance indicator for the Company is turnover which decreased to £11,237,453 (2024:
£12,764,310). Gross profit decreased to £2,962,209 (2024: £3,880,012), representing a decrease in
gross profit margin from 30% to 26%. ,
The Company's administration costs increased to £4,977,065 (2024: £4,294,774). The Company
recognised loan interest receivable in the year of £15,924 (2024: £29,186) and incurred interest charges
The Company's loss before taxation was £2,333,026 (2024: loss of £661,036), resulting in a loss after
taxation of £2,333,026 (2024: loss of £661,036).
At 31 March 2025 the Company had net liabilities of £3,625,908 (2024: net liabilities of £1,292,882) and :
net current liabilities of £4,033,163 (2024: £1,497,729). Non-current assets increased to £407,255
(2024: £204,847).

_ Current assets decreased to £1,941,079 (2024: £2,762,630) being the impact of lower trade debtors
attributable to reduced revenue and a reduction in cash balances. Current liabilities increased to
£5,974,242 (2024: £4,260,359) as a higher level of intra-group funding was outstanding at the year-
end.

Whilst the director continues to pursue growth in revenues, they do not expect the nature of the business
to change significantly in the foreseeable future.

. = — in line with the hours used under the contract for services;

determined had no impairment loss been recognised for the asset in prior years. A reversal of an
impairment loss is recognised immediately in profit or loss.

q) Contingent liabilities
A contingent liability is either a possible or uncertain obligation or a present obligation that is not
recognised because of a transfer of economic benefits is not probable. A contingent liability also arises
if a present obligation exists but the amount required to settle it cannot be reliably estimated, although
this is not currently applicable to the Company. Contingent liabilities are not recognised unless they
have arisen in a business combination. They are disclosed unless the probability of an outflow of
resources is remote.
