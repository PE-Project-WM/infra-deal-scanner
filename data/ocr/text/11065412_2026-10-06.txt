GROUP STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
Principal Activities
Cudo Ventures Limited provides Al infrastructure and cloud compute services to enterprise customers. The
Group secures data centre capacity, owns and operates GPU compute and contracts that capacity to customers
under multi-year agreements.
Business review and Key performance indicators :
The principal key performance indicators used by management are turnover and profit after taxation. Turnover
rose to $74.8m (2024: $15.1m) and gross profit to $8.7m (2024: $1.5m), driven by a 24-month cloud compute
contract that runs into 2027. Administrative expenses rose to $11.4m (2024: $6.3m) as average headcount
grew from 28 to 46. The loss after tax narrowed to $3.0m (2024: $4.3m). Cash at year end was $8.4m (2024:
$1.4m).
Principal risks and uncertainties
The main risks are customer concentration, the availability of data centre capacity and hardware, access to
capital to fund growth, and recruiting the people needed to deliver committed projects.
Future developments
The directors expect the Group to continue growing its contracted compute capacity. Since the year end the
Group has entered into a significant multi-year customer contract and is progressing a funding round to support
it
