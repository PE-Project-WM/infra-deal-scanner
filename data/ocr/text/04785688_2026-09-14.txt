Introduction
The Directors present the Strategic Report and audited financial statements for the year ended 31 January 2026.
- Principal activity and business overview .

1Spatial Group Limited (the “Company") is a subsidiary of 1Spatial Limited (formerly 1Spatial plc). The Group
provides location master data management software, solutions and related services to enterprise and
government customers across its operating geographies. The Group’s purpose is to enable. customers to make
more confident and informed decisions through improved data governance and assured location data.
Business Review :
The Company delivered a strong financial performance during the year, supported by continued demand for its
software, support and maintenance services and consultancy offerings.
Revenue increased to £16.8 million (2025: £14.0 million), whilst gross profit increased to £9.0 million (2025: £7.7

: million). Adjusted EBITDA increased to £5.4 million (2025: £4.5 million). Operating profit increased to £3.2 million

. (2025: £2.0 million) and profit before taxation increased to £3.0 million (2025: £1.9 million).

. The Company's results reflect continued investment in its software platform and technology capabilities whilst
maintaining a disciplined focus on profitability and cash generation.
Strategic, integration and other non-recurring items of £185,187 were incurred during the year and relate to costs

: that are not considered part of the Company's normal recurring operations.
Following the year-end, the acquisition of the Company's ultimate parent undertaking by VertiGIS Limited
became effective. The Directors consider this acquisition to be a non-adjusting post balance sheet event and do
not currently expect a significant change to the Company's principal revenue-generating activities as a result of
the transaction. .
Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties facing the Company are: : :
People , :
The Company's success depends upon its ability to attract, retain and develop employees with the skills and
experience required to support customers and deliver-future growth. © .
Cybersecurity and data protection © ~
The Company relies upon the availability, integrity and security of its systems, software and data. Cybersecurity
; incidents could result in operational disruption, financial loss or reputational damage.
Technology and innovation - :
The markets in which the Company operates continue to evolve rapidly. Failure to respond effectively to
technological change or changing customer requirements could impact future growth and competitiveness.
Customer and delivery risk .
The Company is exposed to risks associated with customer demand, project delivery and the successful ~
execution of customer contracts. Delays or delivery challenges could adversely affect financial performance.

Based on our risk assessment, we considered the areas most susceptible to fraud to be management override

of controls, revenue recognition for services contracts open at the year end. and point in time license revenue

recognised pre year end.

Our procedures in respect of the above included:

* Testing a sample of journal! entries throughout the year, which met a defined risk criteria, by agreeing to

bias by management; and

¢ With regards to the risk of fraud in revenue recognition for services contracts open at year end, our
