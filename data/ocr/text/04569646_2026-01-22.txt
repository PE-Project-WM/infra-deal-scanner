3 Principal Activities 7 :
The principal activity of Last.FM Limited (“Last.FM”) is providing music services through the internet.
: ‘Last.FM provides personalised music data and recommendation services. Using a systemcalled —
. “scrobbling", Last.FM builds a detailed profile of each registered user's musical taste by recording details of -
: the songs the user listens to, either music streamed across the internet or music played back locally on a :
. user’s music devices. This information is transferred ("scrobbled") to the Last.FM database, either via the
music player itself or via a plugin installed within the user's music player. The data is then displayed on the
: registered user's profile page and compiled to create reference pages for individual artists and music
. recommendations. : _
Last.FM's site offers several social networking features, detailed music information, and allows users to keep
track of which songs they are listening to through the “scrobble” technology. ;
