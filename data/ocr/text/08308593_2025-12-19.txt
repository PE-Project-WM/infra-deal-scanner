Strategic Report

For the year ended 31 December 2024

The directors present their strategic report on PCCW Global Investments Limited (the “company’).

Principal activities, business review and future development

The company ceased trading since January 2016 and remained substantially dormant until 31 March 2024. The
company commenced operations following a group reorganisation, under which it purchased certain assets and
liabilities from other group companies. effective from 1 April 2024. The assets and liabilities transferred included
accounts payable, tangible assets, accruals and prepayments. The assets and liabilities were purchased for
consideration, which has been recorded as part of the intercompany balance.

The principal activities of the company are the provision of telecommunications services.

The company reported total revenue of £2,020,618 for the financial year (2023. nil) and an operating profit of £56,645
(2023: loss of £1,860). The main operating costs comprised depreciation of tangible assets. The company is focused
on the provision of telecommunications services.

Key performance indicators (KPIs)

Given the straightforward nature of the business, the company's directors are of the opinion that analysis using KPIs is
not necessary for an understanding of the development, performance or position of the business.

Principal risks and uncertainties :

The principai risk to the business is a catastrophic failure of network conneciivily. This risk has been mitigated through
the implementation of a dual-site architecture to ensure service continuity. The directors remain committed to ongoing
investment in infrastructure and technology to further minimise this risk.

Financial risk management

The management of the business and the execution of the company's strategy are subject to a number of risks. The
key financial risks affecting the company are managed at the Group ievel. These risks include credit, liquidily, and
operational financial risks.

Geopolitical risks

The company monitors global geopolitical Asks which may impact the supply chain, customer demand, of cross-border
operations. While the company does not have direct operations in conftict zones, it is exposed to secondary effects
such as increased logistics costs and global market volatility. The board continues to monitor the situation and adapt its
tisk mitigation strategies accordingly.
