1. STRATEGIC REPORT -

1.1, Business Review
NHOA UK Ltd (the “Company’} is a wholly owned subsidiary of NHOA Energy S.r.l. NHOA Energy offers (a) ,
utility-scale storage solutions designed to support the transmission and distribution grids in dealing with
increasing penetration of intermittent renewable sources, and (b) distributed storage solutions, on
industrial scale, to address the sustainability, affordability, flexibility and reliability needs of the industrial
and conventional power generation sectors.
NHOA Energy is 100% indirectly-owned by NHOA S.A. The ultimate controlling shareholder is TCC Group
Holdings (or “TCC’), one of Asia's pre-eminent investment-grade industrial conglomerates.
NHOA Energy, directly and through its subsidiaries, operates across four geographies (Americas, EMEA,
Asia and Australia & Pacific) under the coordination of four regional hubs (Milan - the global HQ - for EMEA,
Houston for the Americas, Taipei for Asia and Perth for Australia & Pacific).
NHOA UK Ltd was incorporated on 16 February 2023 as a general commercial company to develop and

. execute contract in the UK BESS market.

During 2023, NHOA UK Ltd signed two contracts with EKU Energy for the delivery of two IS0MWh battery
storage systems in Basildon and Loudwater. Execution of both projects was completed during FY2025,
following which the Company transitioned to the operation and maintenance phase under the related
long-term service agreements (LTSAs), generating recurring service revenue.
In the First Half of 2024, NHOA UK Ltd entered into an agreement with Statkraft for the supply and long-
term service of a 113MWh battery storage system in Coylton, Scotland, under the NOA Stability Pathfinder
Phase 2 Programme. This project aims to deliver advanced grid stability solutions, including synthetic
inertia and enhanced power quality, and is expected to complete in 04 2026, at which point the related
long-term service agreement is expected to commence. :
In the first Half of 2026, the Company has signed a new EPC contract for the Old Allen Road BESS project,
a 100 MW / 243.52 MWh battery energy storage system to be installed adjacent to the Bradford West
National Grid substation (UK), with energisation scheduled for 1 December 2027. The project is being
executed jointly with IQA Operations Group Limited (Elecnor's UK subsidiary) through an unincorporated
consortium, with NHOA UK acting as Leader.
The Company's involvement in these projects aligns with the UK's objectives for enhancing grid resilience
and achieving Net Zero by 2030.

2.6. Stationary Storage Market
Battery energy storage has firmly established itself as a core component across generation,
transmission, distribution, mini-grid, and off-grid technologies. In 2025, the global energy storage market
. surpassed 100 gigawatts for the first time, setting a new record. Despite policy headwinds, annual
capacity additions continued to rise in both China and the US, a sign of the market's underlying resilience.
Growth was also evident in Australia, Saudi Arabia, and Sub-Saharan Africa. Looking ahead, the sector is

75.3. Cash Flow Heqge Derivatives and Held-for-Trading Derivatives

Cash Flow Hedge Derivatives

The Company applies cash flow hedge accounting in accordance with IFRS 9 to mitigate exposure to

. variability in cash flows of highly probable forecast transactions, using forward contracts, swaps or

options as hedging instruments. At inception, the Company formally documents each hedging

relationship, including the risk management objective and strategy, the hedging instrument and hedged

item, the nature of the hedged risk, the hedge ratio and the method used to assess hedge effectiveness,

which is assessed both prospectively and retrospectively.

The effective portion of gains or losses on the hedging instrument is recognised in Other Comprehensive

Income (OCi) and accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve, while any ineffective portion is recognised

immediately in profit or loss. Amounts accumulated in the cash flow hedge reserve are reclassified to

profit or loss in the period(s) in which the hedged forecast transaction affects profit or loss, or included

in the initial carrying amount of the asset or liability where the hedged transaction results in a non-

financial item.

Hedge accounting is discontinued prospectively when the hedging instrument expires, is sold,

terminated or exercised, or when the hedge relationship no longer meets the qualifying criteria —

including once the hedged forecast transaction has occurred. Any cumulative gain or loss remaining in

the cash flow hedge reserve at that date is reclassified to profit orloss in the same period(s)as the hedged

transaction, unless the transaction is no longer expected to occur, in which case it is reclassified

immediately.

Held-for-Trading Derivatives

Once hedge accounting is discontinued, any derivative instrument still outstanding is reclassified as

held-for-trading and accounted for at Fair Value Through Profit or Loss (FVTPL): it continues to be

remeasured at fair value at each reporting date until maturity, with all fair value changes recognised

immediately within Net Financial Income and Expenses and no further impact on OCI or the cash flow

hedge reserve.

7.5.10. | Revenues recognition

Revenue from contracts with customers is recognized based on IFRS 15 guidelines, which ensure that
economic benefits are probable and amounts are reliably measurable. IFRS 15 outlines a five-step model
for revenue recognition:

1, Contract Identification: A contract is valid when both parties approve, their rights and
obligations are clear, and payment terms are defined. The company must be confident that the
consideration will be received.

2. Identification of Performance Obligations: These are promises to deliver distinct goods or
services to the customer.

3. -Determination of Transaction Price: The total contract amount is established, considering the
full contractual duration.

4. Allocation of Price to Obligations: Price is allocated proportionally to the fair value of each
performance obligation. :

5. Revenue Recognition: Revenue is recognized when performance obligations are fulfilled, net of :
discounts, rebates, and taxes.

Revenue is recognized over time when probable economic benefits and reliable measurements are

present, regardless of payment timing. Revenue is measured at fair value, excluding taxes.

For goods, revenue is recognized upon transferring ownership risks to the buyer, typically at delivery. Fair

value is net of returns, allowances, and discounts. NHOA UK provides a two-year warranty on its products,

and based on historical costs, no warranty provision is deemed necessary.

NHOA UK Ltd. was incorporated on 16 February 2023, with operations commencing in
September/October 2024. As the Company moves beyond its first year of operations, it continues to be
exposed to a range of financial risks, including credit risk, liquidity risk, and market risk, the profile of

obligations. For NHOA UK Ltd., credit risk primarily arises from financial assets such as contract assets

Subsequent to the financial year end, NHOA UK was selected by Varco Energy for the 244 MWh Old Allen
Road BESS in the UK. On June 23, 2026, Varco Energy announced the award of supply contracts for its
100 MW / 244 MWh Old Allen Road Battery Energy Storage System in West Yorkshire.

integration and grid stability in the UK. Scheduled to begin operations in 2027, the project strengthens
NHOA Energy Group’s presence in the UK market and builds on its experience in grid-stability initiatives.
