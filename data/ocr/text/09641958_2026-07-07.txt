Strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2026
Principal activities and review of the business, including subsequent events
Principal activities

: Historically, the principal activity of the Company was the development of low carbon electricity generation
facilities through its subsidiary Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa Limited, towards the construction and
operation of a new nuclear power plant. However, as a result of issues relating to the raising of finance
for the construction of Horizon’s first power station, Wylfa Newydd, on Anglesey, the Group in January
2019 suspended the majority of its activities. Subsequently, in September 2020, following an
announcement by Hitachi, Limited (the Ultimate Shareholder) that it would cease its backing for Horizon
entirely and withdraw from the UK nuclear market, the Company has now ceased all its activities to
develop its project at Wylfa Newydd on Anglesey.
On 28 June 2024 the Company (through its subsidiary Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa Limited) completed
the sale of its site at Wylfa to Great British Nuclear (GBN), and as part of the sale the Company (through
its subsidiary Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa Limited) agreed to support GBN (which was subsequently
renamed Great British Energy — Nuclear (GBE-N) with estate and site management services from 28
June 2024 until 31 December 2025.
Review of business
In the year ended 31 March 2026 the Company (through its subsidiary Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa
Limited) continued to support GBE-N with the provision of transitional services under a Transitional
Service Agreement (TSA) with GBE-N. Under this agreement Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa Limited was
obliged to provide estate and site management services to GBE-N for the period from completion to 31
December 2025. The TSA ended on 31 December 2025 as intended.
In February 2026 the Company issued a dividend of £123,908k to its shareholder Horizon Nuclear Power
Limited (the Shareholder). The cash came from dividend received from Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa
Limited from the proceeds of the land asset sale to GBE-N in June 2024 plus accrued interest from cash
pooling facility with Hitachi Europe Limited.
Strategy :
Following completion of the TSA with GBE-N, the Company will be retained until 30 June 2027 when
warranties on the sale expiré. It is the Ultimate Shareholder’s intention that following expiry of the warranty
period, the Company will then be liquidated.
Subsequent events following the reporting period end
No subsequent events to report.
