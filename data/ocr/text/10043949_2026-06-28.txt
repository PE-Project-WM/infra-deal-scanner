STRATEGIC' REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
The directors present their strategic report together with the audited financial statements for the year ended 31
December 2025.
Principal Activities
Accelercomm Limited (the “company”), and its subsidiary, together (the “Group”) is a leading independent
developer of innovative, groundbreaking technology that delivers cost effective, market leading digital
communications that achieve robust, error resilient, ultra-high performance. The Company strives to
accelerate the next generation of wireless communications used around the globe. It's primary activity
is the development of Baseband Processor architecture for satellites, an inherently complexed area of
engineering, for which there is no licensable solution in the world.
The Company is actively engaged in the development of technology and IP which develops and provides IP
cores capable of delivering channel coding performance for 5G applications, with the main target market being
chip manufacturers and hardware equipment vendors in the mobile industry. The primary markets the Company
addresses are both in relation to base stations in the 4.5G and 5G mobile communications sector,
as well as defense and satellite.
Business Review .
Accelercomm has growth ambitions to become the number one supplier of channel coding, demodulation and
channel estimation solutions in the world. The Group hopes to meet this target by supplying best in class
hardware and software technology, measured in terms of throughput; latency and efficiency at the system
level on a global scale.

Principal Risks and Uncertainties
The group takes a proactive approach to the management of the various risks that is faces. A principal risk that
the group faces is the difficulty involved in the development and implementation of Baseband Processors for
satellites. Currently there is no licensable solution available worldwide and it has taken 200-250 person years to
build a working Baseband Processor. This is also affected by the large number of iterations and design variations
required in producing such Processor.
Power and Spectrum remain finite, which drives efficiency and performance requirements, but also makes it :
challenging to exponentially increase signal processing speeds.
Therefore, the development, deployment and testing of these broadband processors is expensive, time
consuming and inherently difficult, meaning that investment in a Company whose focus is Baseband Processors
technology continues to pose a significant risk to capital. This Directors recognize the importance of maintaining
strong relationships with customers, suppliers and other business partnerships. The Group operates in a highly
competitive and international market and the Directors recognise that sustained investment in technology, product
development and delivery to customers will aid the future development and growth of the Group and solidifying
their market position.

Financial Risk Management
The Group finances its activities with a combination of cash and short term deposits and cash investments from

The directors are responsible for preparing the Directors’ report and the financial statements in accordance with

must not approve the financial statements unless they are satisfied that they give a true and fair view of the state
of affairs of the company and of the profit or loss of the company for that period.

. select suitable accounting policies for the company's financial statements and then apply them

and to enable them to ensure that the financial statements comply with the Companies Act 2006. They are also

Results And Dividends

Going Concern

In preparing the financial statements, the Directors have considered the ability of the Group and Company to
continue as a going concern and have reviewed cash flow forecasts to inform their decisions. As the Group and
Company is still early in its life cycle, it has recorded a loss for the period. During this stage, the Group and
Company will remain in a net cash outflow position in the near term.
Cash-flow forecasts have been prepared identifying all contractual cash commitments for the next 12 months post
approval of the financial statements and these have been compared to current cash holdings and expected
revenues and other cash inflows. Consequently, the Directors are satisfied that the financial statements should
be prepared on the going concern basis.
Based on the cash flow forecasts, the Group and Company requires additional funding or an increase in revenue.
The Directors expect the funding to be successfully concluded when the cash is required. The completion of future
funding rounds is contingent on future investment decisions at the date of approval of the financial statements.
The Directors have a high degree of confidence in securing new customer contracts during the period of 12 months
post approval of the financial statements, the finalisation of such contracts is contingent on future events at the
date of approval of the financial statements.
The Group and Company is dependent on the completion of future equity funding rounds or securing new
customer contracts that have yet to be finalised, both of which are not guaranteed. These circumstances indicate
the existence of a material uncertainty which may cast significant doubt over the company’s ability to continue as
a going concern and therefore, it may be unable to realise its assets and discharge its liabilities in the normal
course of business.
