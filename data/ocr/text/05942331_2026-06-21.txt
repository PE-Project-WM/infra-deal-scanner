Strategic report
For the year ended 31 December 2025
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Principal activities
The principal activity of the group continued to be that of the provision of visual communication and other

: workplace services. The group's services and products focus on the development, project management,
implementation, maintenance and customisation of video conferencing and other workplace systems. The group
specialises in providing turn-key end-to-end solutions and maintenance services tailored to the special needs of
individual clients.
Business review of performance and position
In the year ended 31 December 2025 York Telecom Limited saw a 32.9% increase in revenue and a 24.2% increase
in gross profit. The company has focussed on adding operational efficiencies which have improved gross margin.
Administrative expenses in 2025 were £4,924,362 (2024: £4,056,925) as the company has been investing in
upskilling and expanding their team.
York Telecom Limited wil! continue to be reliant on support from York Telecom Corporation.
Strategy :
Group and company strategy is fivefold: nurture existing clients, acquire new customers, enter new markets,
introduce new products or service and support York Telecom customers on a global basis. Historically the
company has been successful at retaining existing customers, so additional focus is being given to acquiring new
customers. Also, the company is looking to diversify its portfolio by increasing the volume of services being
introduced and sold.
Principal risks and uncertainties
Supply chain - As the group and company does not manufacture itself it is reliant on third parties who may
choose to cease trading through partners. The group and company has mitigated this risk by working with a
variety of different manufacturers and has worked with clients to adapt solutions to work with the products
that are available. The majority of products come from within the EMEA region, so it is expected that risk of any
new tariffs will be minimal.
New technology - New technology could be introduced to the market that would cause some of the existing
video services to become obsolete. The group and company works with new suppliers of technology to
integrate them with their services.
The economy - A low level of economic activity may cause other industries to be at risk, but the group and
company believes that as it emphasises cost savings and efficiency associated with its technology it can mitigate
this risk.
Brexit - The group and company believes that it has mitigated the impact of Brexit as the group and company
works with a network of manufacturers, distributors and logistics companies to deliver outside the UK.
Key performance indicators
The group focuses on two key indicators of financial performance: Revenue and Operating Profit. Revenue shows
the success or otherwise of identifying opportunities, winning opportunities and delivering opportunities.
Operating Profit measures the success or otherwise of managing a profitable business.

Financial instruments
Cash flow risk
The group have controls around credit risk and work closely with suppliers where it is known there may be a need
for extended credit terms due to the nature of the project undertaken. The group seek deposits from customers as
appropriate.
Liquidity risk :
The group is exposed to some liquidity risk as it may need to pay suppliers for goods before the group has received
payment from customers. Where the group is aware of this possibility in advance the group will negotiate
extended terms with suppliers. Historically on occasion the group has received short term funds from its parent
company York Telecom Corporation.
Interest rate risk
The directors do not consider the group to be exposed to interest rate risk as the group has no variable interest
arrangements.
Currency risk
The group buys and sells products and services in sterling, US dollars and euros. To date risk exposure has been
maintained as we align purchases and sales, but this position is monitored. Where historic support has been
provided by York Telecom Corporation this support was provided in US dollars which means the group has risk
when there is an adverse movement in sterling to US dollars. The exchange movement in 2025 related to the
historic support from York Telecom Corporation, to date the exchange loss has not been realised.
- Credit risk

The group deals with a large portfolio of customers so is not exposcd to a concentration of credit risk. The
customers with whom the group has extensive dealings tend to be large multinationals with strong credit ratings.
New customers are subject to credit reviews and where there are concerns, deposits are taken.
Price risk
The group produces bespoke pricing for most jobs and the quotes are typically valid for only thirty days, therefore
there is little exposure to risk of supplier prices changing adversely that are not covered by an equivalent change
in sales price. Competition may result in a decline in margins as the group must respond to market trends.
