Engaging with stakeholders , :
The success of our business is dependent on the support of all of our customers, suppliers and the associates
employed by Snap-on. Building positive relationships with stakeholders that share our values is important to us.
We work together in support of shared goals to deliver long-term, sustainable success for all involved. :
Snap-on Business Solutions Limited is comprised of a number of varied business units and product lines, all of . .
which are engaged with a unique set of stakeholders. Our governance framework delegates authority for local = -
decision making at the business unit level. This is based upon business unit size and provides approval levels of
revenue and expense spend at each management level which allows the individual business unit leaders to take
account of specific product line needs in their decision-making process. :
The leadership teams of each business unit are encouraged to make decisions with a long-term view in mind
and with the highest standards of conduct in accordance with Company policies. In order to fulfil their duties,

: the leaders of each business unit and the company itself take care to assess the potential consequences on all
stakeholders of the decisions and actions for which they take. Major decisions are carefully discussed with
affected groups and senior leadership to ensure full comprehension and support prior to the undertaking.
Reports are regularly made to divisional management by the business units about the strategy, performance and
key decisions to be taken which provides management with assurance that proper consideration is given to
stakeholder interests in decision-making. Management is well informed about the views of the business unit
leaders through regular discussion and key performance indicator reporting. This information is used to assess
the impact of decisions on each business unit, séparately and as a whole group, as potential alternatives may be

FINANCIAL RISK MANAGEMENT OBJECTIVES AND POLICIES

The company's activities expose it to a number of financial risks including credit risk, foreign exchange risk and

liquidity risk. : .
Credit risk : .
The Company’s principal credit risk relates to the recovery of amounts owed by trade debtors. In order to |
manage credit risk the directors review the Automotive OEM client receivables on a regular basis to ensure that.
balances are within agreed terms and recoverable in full. All credit balances are reviewed by the credit control:
department on a more frequent basis in conjunction with debt ageing and collection history, and debts are .
actively chased. : :

Foreign exchange risk
The Company’s activities expose it primarily to the financial risks of changes in foreign currency exchange :
rates. Where possible the company seeks to match purchases and sales in foreign currencies. Where appropriate :
the ultimate parent company uses forward exchange contracts to reduce risk. : : :
Liquidity risk .

In order to maintain, liquidity to ensure that sufficient funds are available for ongoing operations and future
developments, the company uses short-term debt finance. : .

: Interest rate risk . :

_ The Company has exposure to interest rate risk on intercompany loan balances. This is mitigated through the

Treasury management which is undertaken as part of the overall risk management strategy of Snap-on
Incorporated, the ultimate parent company. . : .
