Strategic report
The directors present their strategic report and the financial statements for the year
ended 30 September 2024.
Principal activity and review of the business
The principal activity of the company is that of a voice and data telecommunications
service provider, primarily in the financial services sector. The company experienced
stable turnover during the year but significant reduction in profitability, although it
remains profitable. The company’s key financial and other performance indicators
during the year were as follows:

Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties facing the company are as follows:
Market risk
The company provides voice and data communication services mainly to the financial
services community and therefore to a certain extent its existing revenue and future
revenue growth are linked to the state of the financial markets.
Competitive risk
The company operates in a competitive market and therefore its margins and its ability
to sustain its rate of growth could be affected by increased price competition and other
competitive factors.

Going concern

; The financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.

The directors have reviewed the current financial performance and position of the
company and are confident it will not lead to the company no longer being a going
concern. The company is in a net liabilities and net current liability position. However,
in the event that it is not possible for the company to meet its liabilities as they fall due
and also due to the company being commercially interdependent on IPC Corp., IPC
Corp. has agreed to provide financial support to the company in meeting its liabilities as
and when they fail due for at least 12 months from the date of approval of the financial
statements, being the going concern period.

In assessing IPC Corp’s ability to provide such support, the directors of the company
have obtained and reviewed IPC Corp’s going concern assessment as well as related
budgets and forecasts. IPC Corp’s cash flow outlook shows sufficient liquidity to fund
operations through September of 2026. The directors of the company also note that
IPC Corp has successfully restructured its debt by paying down $100 million of debt
along with extending the maturity date and electing to pay principal payments in kind
during the next four quarters, and that IPC Corp’s projected financial information
indicates that it will be able to meet all its obligations including its interest and principal
repayments on its outstanding credit facilities, as well as meet all its financial covenant
requirements under the new credit facilities until 01 September 2026. .
The directors therefore consider it appropriate to prepare financial statements on a
going concern basis.

Actions taken to increase Energy Efficiency
The main energy sources of greenhouse gas emissions for IPC Network Services Limited
are the direct electricity consumption at its UK office (Scope 2), which was moved in to
in November 2020. It is noted that 100% of the energy purchased by the building comes
from renewable sources. The indirect electricity consumption related to power usage of
cabinets stored in data centres is a significant source of emissions being tracked in
conjunction with our suppliers as part of the wider ESG program and relate to Scope 3
emissions.
Following the Covid-19 pandemic, IPC Network Services Limited reconsidered business
travel needs for its employees. While business travel rose to a higher level than during
the previous reporting period, the company intends to increasingly rely on
videoconferencing and other remote teamwork tools to replace in person meetings and
interactions, where relevant.
IPC Network Services Limited intends to monitor, manage and reduce its energy ‘
consumption and related emissions in a more proactive way, as part of a broader
Environmental and Social Governance (ESG) strategy. The intent is to focus not only on
Scope 1 and 2 emissions, but to work together with business partners to tackle Scope 3
emissions — for example, to achieve energy efficiency gains in data centres used by the
company for its cabinets.
