STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 AUGUST 2025

Introduction
The directors present their strategic report for the year ended 31 August 2025.
Business review
The principal activity of the Company is the undertaking specific and end-to-end telecoms infrastructure
projects.
Turnover has increased by 14% to £17.5m (2024: £15.4m) in the year to 31 August 2025 — attributed to the final

. phase of a projects lifecycle and closure. Gross profit balanced to £4.9m (2024: £5.0m) which resulted in an
operating profit of £844K (2024: £1.6m). The Company continues to perform in line with our overall strategy
which included the addition of strategic long term framework agreements with both existing and new clients.
Novo Technologies provides high quality full turnkey services within the mobile and fixed line
telecommunications network and infrastructure sector including acquisition, design, construction and
maintenance of sites.
Whilst the business has seen a decrease in profitability due to the closure of a Fibre related project, it has
realised a significant increase on profitability against turnover in terms of the wireless division. This in line with
its client focus approach on quality, project efficiency and integrated management systems.
The overarching strategy of the company includes expanding its service offerings and increasing profitability
through cost efficiencies whilst continuing to invest in our employees training needs to meet the identified
business risks both financially and operationally to mitigate any potential threats.
Principal risks and uncertainties
Health, safety, and environmental risk
The company is subject to stringent health, safety and environmental regulations. Accordingly, the provision of
adequate training is a top priority and every precaution is taken to minimise the risk in this area. -
Revenue risk
The company has relationships with customers and has entered into key trading contracts of various lengths.
The company continues to focus on building relationships with existing and potential new customers, and
always performs to a high standard in order to minimise the risk of losing a trading customer. The company is
constantly seeking to develop new revenue streams and increase the number of customers to manage this risk.
Competitive risk
The company operates in a competitive market and the principal risk is losing sales from key customers. The
company manages the risk of competition through providing quality services and maintaining excellent customer
service.
Asset risk
The company’s principal financial assets are stock and debtors. Stocks are only purchased for contracts as
required, and stock levels are continually monitored for any obsolescence or reduction in value. Trade debtors
are considered below in credit risk.
Credit risk
The company’s credit risk is primarily attributable to its trade debtors, and amounts presented in the statement
of financial position are net of allowances for doubtful receivables. The company does not consider it has any
significant concentration of credit risk, with any exposure spread over many customers. The company manages
this credit risk through strong credit control procedures and review processes.

2.11 Current and deferred taxation
The tax expense for the year comprises current and deferred tax. Tax is recognised in profit or loss
except that a charge attributable to an item of income and expense recognised as other
. comprehensive income or to an item recognised directly in equity is also recognised in other
comprehensive income or directly in equity respectively.
The current income tax charge is calculated on the basis of tax rates and laws that have been
enacted or substantively enacted by the reporting date in the countries where the Company operates
and generates income.
Deferred tax balances are recognised in respect of all timing differences that have originated but not
reversed by the reporting date, except that:
e The recognition of deferred tax assets is limited to the extent that it is probable that they will
be recovered against the reversal of deferred tax liabilities or other future taxable profits; and
e Any deferred tax balances are reversed if and when all conditions for retaining associated tax
allowances have been met.
Deferred tax balances are not recognised in respect of permanent differences except in respect of
business combinations, when deferred tax is recognised on the differences between the fair values
of assets acquired and the future tax deductions available for them and the differences between the
fair values of liabilities acquired and the amount that will be assessed for tax. Deferred tax is
determined using tax rates and laws that have been enacted or substantively enacted by the
reporting date.

2.14 Stocks

immediately in the Statement of Income and Retained Earnings.
