Principal activity :
The principal activity of the company is the supply of high reliability microwave components to the defence and
space industries. This includes the supply of satellite equipment, ferrite switches, subsystems for advanced . :
radars and payload electronic instruments to various customers for Earth observation and telecommunications

Review of the business and future developments
The loss for the financial year, after taxation, was £1,537,000 (2024: loss of £5,192,000). The significant
reduction in the loss reflects a strong increase in revenue of £6.8 million (54%), which more than offset increases
in cost of sales (£2 million) and administrative expenses (£1 million). As a result, the entity achieved both a gross
: profit and an ‘operating profit during the year, demonstrating improved trading performance and strengthened
margins. However, the entity continues to report a net loss due to substantial interest payable, which exceeds
operating profits. These financing costs arise primarily from group treasury cash pooling arrangements and
represent a group-level funding decision impacting the entity's results. While operating margins have improved, it
. _femains insufficient to fully absorb. the financing costs and deliver net profitability. :
Inventory increased by 68% from £1,777,000 to £2,986,000, reflecting business growth driven by the award of a
. high-value engineering contract (QKDSat) and increased demand within the ISC division, resulting in higher
levels of raw materials and work in progress, alongside associated cash inflows. This is reflected in an increase
in current liabilities, primarily due to £2,200,000 of advance payments received from the European Space Agency
to fund future development activities. Overall, the increases in current assets and current liabilities are aligned
with the expansion of operations and have had a limited net impact on the entity's net current position. ,
Turnover increased by £6,864,000 compared with the prior year, representing growth of 54%, from £12,600,000
to £19,464,000. This increase reflects strong performance on ongoing projects during the year, particularly within
the defence business, where demand for components and subsystems used in radar applications has grown
significantly. In addition, the establishment of a new business unit focused on Quantum Key Distribution (QKD)
for space applications has contributed to current year revenues and is expected to support further revenue
- growth in future periods.

Strategy / : :
The company is part of the Honeywell Group, and therefore its strategy is aligned to the Group strategy for the :
Aerospace Technologies ("AT") Strategic Business Group.
Across all its segments, the company's strategy is to build a unique position by offering its customers a software
driven and connected solution. .
Aerospace is a leading global supplier of products, software and services for aircrafts that it sells to original
equipment manufacturers (OEM) and other customers in a variety of end markets including: air transport,
regional, business and general aviation aircraft, airlines, aircraft operators, and defense and space contractors.
Aerospace products and services include auxiliary power units, propulsion engines, environmental control’
systems, integrated avionics, wireless connectivity services, electric power systems, engine controls, flight safety,
communications, navigation hardware, data and software applications, radar and surveillance systems, aircraft
. lighting, management and technical services, advanced systems and instruments, satellite and space
components, aircraft wheels and brakes, repair and overhaul services, and thermal systems. Aerospace also
provides spare parts, repair, overhaul, and maintenance services (principally to aircraft operators); and sells
licenses or intellectual property to other parties. Honeywell Forge solutions, are leveraged by our customers as
: tools to turn data into predictive maintenance and predictive analytics to enable better fleet management and
make flight operations more efficient. . :
