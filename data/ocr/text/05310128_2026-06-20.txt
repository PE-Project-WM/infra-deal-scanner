Telefonica O02 Holdings Limited (the “Company”) is a member of the Telefénica group of companies (the
“Telefonica Group’). The immediate parent company is Telefénica, S.A. headquartered in Spain and listed on the
Spanish stock exchange as well as the New York and Lima stock exchanges. In December 2025, Telefonica, S.A.
announced its intention to initiate the procedure for the voluntary delisting in the New York Stock Exchange, a
process that became effective in January 2026. Telefénica, S.A. also intends to request the voluntary delisting
from the Lima Stock Exchange.
The Company has taken advantage of the exemption under s401 of the Companies Act 2006 not to prepare
group financial statements as it is a wholly owned subsidiary. These financial statements present information
about the Company as an individual undertaking and not about its group.
The results of the Company are included in the consolidated financial statements of Telefdnica, S.A.. which may
be obtained from its website at https://www.telefonica.com/en/ or from its registered address, Gran Via 28,

Financial performance and Development
During the year the purpose of the Company was to act as an intermediate holding company within the
Telefonica Group, holding the investment in VIED O02 UK Limited (VMED O2 UK), the joint venture entity as
described below. The Company's main activities during the year were mainly to continue to maintain operational
oversight of VMED O02 UK.

the amount of £1.847m (2024: £52m impairment gain) the Company generated a loss before tax of £1.653m in

The Company has not declared nor paid dividends in 2025 (2024: £425m) .

VMED 02 UK. Overview of financial year
VMED 02 UK, (O02 and Virgin Media joint venture) is an integrated communications provider of mobile,
broadband internet, video and fixed-line telephony to residential customers and businesses in the UK. On 12 May
2025, VMED O2 UK entered into a contribution agreement with Jet Holdco Limited (formerly Daisy Topco
Limited) and Jet Comms Pikco Limited (formerly Daisy Pikco Limited) (Daisy Pikco) to contribute certain Daisy
Group companies (Daisy Group) and certain VMED O02 UK companies including VMBL to create a new B2B
telecommunications business (O2 Daisy). On 1 August 2025, VMED O02 UK acquired 70% of O2 Daisy.
This year VMED 02 UK moved from focusing on integration and synergies to establishing the long-term
operating model and investing in the fundamentals. This meant reshaping the business to focus on three
customer types: consumer, B2B and wholesale, ensuring VMED O2 UK is able differentiate itself with compelling
propositions in what has been, and will continue to be, a challenging market.
As at 31 December 2025, VMED 02 UK total gigabit footprint stood at 18.8 million homes serviceable and serves
5.8 million fixed-line customers, with 5.7 million customers taking a broadband internet product. Mobile network
serves 46.7 miltion connections, including 36.3 million retail mobile connections.
Total Revenue decreased 5.3% to £10,113.1 million (£10,680.5 million in 2024). This decrease is primarily due to
nexfibre construction revenue which decreased from £750.1 million in 2024 to £224.3 million in 2025, reflecting
reduced network construction activity.

Key Financial Performance Indicators ;
The performance of the Company is dependent on the financial performance of its investment in VMED O02 UK.
Key Performance Financial Indicators (KPIs) used in 2025 include Revenues, Adjusted EBITDA, P&E additions
and Adjusted Free Cash Flow.
Revenue: VMED O2 UK derives its revenue from residential and B2B communications services, including mobile,
broadband internet, video and fixed-line telephony services.
Adjusted EBITDA; As VMED O02 UK uses the term. this is defined as profit (loss) from continuing operations
before net income tax benefit (expense), other non-operating income or expenses, share of results of
investments accounted for by the equity method, net finance income (costs), depreciation and amortisation,
share-based compensation, impairment, restructuring and other operating items. Adjusted EBITDA is a measure
used by VMED O2 UK's chief operating decision maker to evaluate operating performance and is also a key
factor that is used by the internal decision makers to (i) determine how to allocate resources and (ii) evaluate the
effectiveness of the management for purposes of annual and other incentive compensation plans.
P&E Additions; are defined as tangible and intangible asset additions, excluding ROU asset additions and
spectrum licence additions. VMED ©2 UK believes this is a key metric to understand VMED O2 UK's total capital
expenditure and allows for a more meaningful comparison of trends from period to period.
Adjusted Free Cash Flow (AFCF); Net cash provided by the operating activities. plus expenses financed by an
intermediary, less capital expenditures, principal payments on amounts financed by vendors and intermediaries,
and principal payments on certain finance leases. VMED ©2 UK believes that the presentation of AFCF, provides
useful information to the investors because this measure can be used to gauge our ability to service debt and
fund new investment opportunities.

3.1. Impairment of investments - Critical accounting estimate
Indicators of impairment of investments are assessed at each year end to determine whether an impairment test
shall be performed and, in such case, whether a loss must be recognised in the income statement or a previously
recognised impairment loss must be reversed. The decision to recognise an impairment reversal (or a loss)
involves estimates of the reasons for the potential impairment (or recovery), as well as the timing and amount.
The impairment of the investments is assessed in Note 10.
An impairment exists when the carrying value of an investment exceeds its recoverable amount. which is the
higher of its fair value less costs to sell and its value in use. The value in use calculation is based on a discounted
cash flow model. In assessing value in use, the estimated future post-tax cash flows deriving from the use of the
asset are discounted to their present value using a post-tax discount rate that reflects current market
assessments of the time value of money and the risks specific to the asset, provided that the result obtained is
the same that would be obtained by discounting pre-tax cash flows at a pre-tax discount rate.
The recoverable amount is most sensitive to the discount rate used in the discounted cash flow model as well as
the expected cash inflows and the growth rate used for extrapolation purposes.
The decision to recognize an impairment loss involves developing estimates that include, among others, an
analysis of the causes of the potential impairment, as well as its timing and expected amount. Furthermore,
additional factors are taken into account, such as the situation of the macroeconomic environment, regulatory
and competitive developments, technological obsolescence, the discontinuation of certain services as well as
other changes in circumstances, that highlight the need to assess a potential impairment.

b. Fair value interest rate risk
Interest rate risk is the risk that fair value of a financial instrument will fluctuate because of changes in
market interest rates.
The Company's interest rate risk arises primarily from the effects of movements in interest rates on the interest
received on cash and cash equivalents. The majority of the Company's cash and cash equivalents are deposited
with a central Telefonica group company that pays interest at variable market rates.
