The Company's activities expose it to a number of financial instrument risks including interest .

Foreign currency risk ,
At the year erid the Company’s net position had a few forward currency contracts to cover USD
and EUR purchases. ’ .
The Company uses forward currency contracts to eliminate the currency exposures on all

borrowing or lending within the ASN group. , .
