BUSINESS REVIEW
The Group is wholly owned by Airspan Networks Inc. (“Parent”), registered in Delaware, USA, and
ultimately owned by Airspan Networks Holdings LLC (“Airspan “TopCo”), Airspan TopCo and all
subsidiaries are referred to as the “Airspan Group”. The Group is a designer and producer of wireless
network equipment for 4G and SG networks for both mainstream public telecommunications service
providers and private network implementations. The Group provides Radio Access Network (“RAN”)
products based on Open Virtualized Cloud Native Architectures that support technologies including 5G new
: radio (“SG NR”), Long-Term Evolution (“LTE”), and Fixed Wireless standards, operating in licensed,
lightly licensed and unlicensed frequencies.
The financial year was significantly influenced by a court-supervised Chapter 11 restructuring process
undertaken by the wider Group’s ultimate parent, TopCo. The purpose of the restructuring was to strengthen
TopCo’s balance sheet, simplify its capital structure and provide a more sustainable platform for future
operations. During this period, management remained focused on preserving customer relationships,
maintaining operational continuity, supporting employees and managing liquidity prudently. The Group
continued to deliver products and services to customers throughout the year, with no material interruption
to core programmes. Management also continued to prioritise product development, operational efficiency
and selective commercial opportunities in key markets. Following completion of the restructuring process,
the Directors believe the Group is better positioned to pursue profitable growth and long-term value
"creation.
The market for the Airspan Group’s wireless systems includes mobile carriers, other public network
operators and private and government network operators for command and contro] in industrial and public
safety applications such as smart utilities, defence, transportation, mining and oil and gas. Its strategy
applies the same network technology across all addressable sectors.
The Airspan Group’s main operations are in Slough, United Kingdom; Mumbai and Bangalore, India;
Airport City, Israel; and the Company’s corporate headquarters are in the United States (“U.S.”) in Plano,
Texas.
The Group has historically incurred losses from operations that have been financed by Airspan Networks
Inc., the parent company, by intercompany loans or equity share purchases.
FINANCIAL OVERVIEW ,
The Group reports its financial statements and the notes to the accounts in United States dollars (“$”).

PRINCIPAL RISKS AND UNCERTAINTIES FACING THE GROUP AND COMPANY
Technology Risk
The Group relies on technology for day-to-day operations that has potential system vulnerabilities, failure
due to hacking, phishing and ransomware and potential data loss. All modern companies operate with this
risk and the Group addresses this risk with a robust attention to system vulnerabilities and education of
users. The Group has strict controls in place and regular communications to staff members to remain
cautious and vigilant to the threats.
Supply Chain Risk :
The Group is reliant on key suppliers for components that are from a single source. An interruption of supply
: from these key suppliers could disrupt or halt production for products that are designed using these key
components.
Key Staff Risk
The Group is reliant on the wealth of expertise of its staff, and in some cases particular staff members, to
research, develop and improve the product line. Losing these key employees could risk the development of
the products and cutting-edge technologies.
Funding Risk
The Group is reliant and funded through its parent company, which has provided the financial support for
the Group to grow to where it is now. The directors believe there is a reasonable expectation that the Group
will continue to be supported and meet its strategic targets.
