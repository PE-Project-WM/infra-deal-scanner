Directors’ Report
The directors present their report and the audited financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2025. This
directors’ report has been prepared in accordance with the special provisions relating to small companies under Part
15 of the Companies Act 2006, including exemption from presenting a strategic report. The company is included in
Kajima Europe Limited consolidated accounts which are prepared under IFRS and are available upon request from
Companies House. As a result, the company is able to apply FRS101.
Principal activity
The principal activity of the company for the year under review was the identification and development of sites suitable
for the location of solar farms and battery storage facilities. Kajima Partnerships Limited holds a 90% interest and
LCA2 Limited holds a 10% interest in the company
: Business review

During the year the company continued to develop a number of suitable sites identified in previous periods. During
the development stage it was determined that four projects (Long Lane, Northwick, C2G and Church and Haise) are
no longer viable, and the board took the decision to expense them to the profit and loss. The Directors current
expectation is that one site (2024: nine) will be explored for potential development over the course of next year - Moss
Farm, Formby. There are another three sites — Renew East, Hanningfield and Shore Top Farm, across various locations
in England that have the potential to be developed.
The company holds weekly calls between directors and investment, finance and operation team’s representatives to
ensure deadlines are met and progress is achieved.
On the 22 December 2023 the company entered into a contract with a potential buyer for the sale of the entire issued
share capital of the subsidiary undertaking, TPS PSC Limited. Completion of the sale occurred on 28 March 2025.
Going concern ,
Having reviewed the financial facilities available to the company, the company forecasts and expected cashflows, the
Directors consider that the company will be able to settle its liabilities as they fall due and accordingly the financial
statements have been prepared on a going concern basis. After successful sale of subsidiary TPS PSC Limited, the
company reported profit after tax of £1,090,392 (2024: £2,565,133) for the year. The company continues to have the
support of the parent company, Kajima Partnerships Limited, which has provided a letter of support which covers a
period of not less than twelve months from the date of signing these financial statements. Events such as the ongoing
Ukraine/Russian war followed by tariff war have created uncertainty to the global macroeconomic outlook. The recent
conflict in the Middle East will increase the negative impact in terms of higher energy, materials and service prices.
Whilst the group has not faced immediate nor direct consequences of the recent events, the Directors continue to
monitor secondary impact that may materialise in future. The business has no legal or contractual commitments to
external stakeholders to continue the development of individual projects.
Future Developments
The company continues to identify new sites for development with the intention to sell the sites once planning
permission is granted. .
Results
The company reported profit for the year, after taxation, amounted to £1,090,392 (2024: £2,565,133). This is a result

. of the sale of a successful project TPS PSC Limited and the impact of the impairment of intercompany loans of
£1,740,485 (2024: £81,162), The company has net liabilities at the year-end of £681,798 (2024: £227,810 net assets).
A dividend of £2,000,000 (2024: £431,978) was paid in the year.
During the year Kajima Partnerships Limited made new loans to the company totalling £680,000 to fund the ongoing
project development. The company repaid a total of £3,760,586 (2024: 1,420,769) during the year of the total loans
provided by the shareholders.

Key sources of estimation uncertainty
The estimates and underlying assumptions are continuously reviewed on an ongoing basis. Revisions to

The estimates and judgements that have a significant risk of causing a material adjustments to the carrying
amounts of assets and liabilities within the next financial year are addressed below:
a) Inventory recoverability
Inventories as reported in Note 11 are stated at lower of cost and net realisable value. The assessment of the
net realisable value of WIP necessarily requires judgement and estimation. The directors review the work-in-
progress inventory on a quarterly basis and make judgements on the viability of projects to each specific WIP
