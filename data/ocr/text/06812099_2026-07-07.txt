Strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2026
Principal activities and review of the business, including subsequent events
Principal activities
Historically, the principal activity of the Company is to support its parent company, Horizon Nuclear Power
‘ Wylfa Limited, in the delivery of its lead project Wylfa Newydd. However, as a result of issues relating to
the raising of finance for the construction of this project, the Group in January 2019 year suspended the
majority of its activities. Subsequently, in September 2020, following an announcement by Hitachi, Limited
(the Ultimate Shareholder) that it would cease its backing for Horizon entirely and withdraw from the UK
nuclear market, the Company ceased all its activities to develop its project at Wylfa Newydd on Anglesey.
On 28 June 2024 the Group completed the sale of its sites at Oldbury and Wylfa to Great British Nuclear
(GBN), and as part of the sale the Group agreed to support GBN (which was subsequently renamed
Great British Energy — Nuclear (GBE-N) with estate and site management services from 28 June 2024
until 31 December 2025.
The Company continues to support Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa Limited and Horizon Nuclear Power
Oldbury Limited.
; Review of business

In the year ended 31 March 2026 the Company continued to support Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa
Limited and Horizon Nuclear Power Oldbury Limited with the provision of transitional services under
Transitional Service Agreements (TSAs) to GBE-N. Under these agreements Horizon Nuclear Power
Oldbury Limited and Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa Limited were obliged to provide estate and site
management services to GBE-N for the period from completion to 31 December 2025. The TSAs ended
on 31 December 2025 as intended.
Following the completion of the TSAs the Company made two Horizon staff redundant. One director, who
previously worked for Hitachi Europe Limited, joined the Company in September 2025 to assist in the
management and ultimate liquidation of the Group.
In February 2026 the Group issued a dividend of £7,010k to its shareholder Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa
Limited (the Shareholder). *
Strategy

, Following completion of the TSAs with GBE-N, the Company will be retained until 30 June 2027 when
the deferred element of the payment for the Oldbury site is due from GBE-N (subject to the outcome of
site conditions) and when warranties on the sale expire. It is the Ultimate Shareholder’s intention that
following payment of the deferred consideration and expiry of the warranty period, the Company will then
be liquidated.
The Company will continue to support Horizon Nuclear Power Wylfa Limited and Horizon Nuclear Power
Oldbury Limited until liquidation.
Subsequent events following the reporting period end
No subsequent events to report ,
