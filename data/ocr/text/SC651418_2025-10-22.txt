20. Critical judgements in applying the Group’s accounting estimates
Carrying value of Investment and Intangibles
The Group is progressing well, albeit at an early stage of its expansion strategy and is well placed for strong growth in
future years. An assessment has been made based on projected revenues, using peer analysis and DCF methods, which
supports the carrying value of investment / software licence intangible at the balance sheet date and at the date of
signing. The value is dependent upon achieving significant growth in our revenues, which is in line with the Board’s most
recent assessment of the most likely outcome on the conversion of our customer pipeline.
The business is at a relatively early, but fast growth phase of its development. As is normal for high growth technology
businesses, judgement was applied in the forecasts used to value the business initially, and in supporting the value of
goodwill carried in these financial statements. The critical input to those valuations is the rate of growth in the revenue
streams arising from the Company's technology and datasets as well as an assessment of the future strategic value of
the Likezero technology to businesses operating in our target markets. The Directors have applied growth assumptions :
which lead to significant revenue growth over the next 6 years at an average annual rate in excess of 50%. This is in line
with their expectations based on discussions currently underway to both expand existing and secure new annual recurring
licencing agreements. In making these judgements, the directors were also informed by discussions with the Group's
retained financial advisors who specialise in advising growth technology businesses in the scale up phase of their
‘development. :
