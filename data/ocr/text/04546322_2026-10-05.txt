STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
Introduction
The directors present their Strategic Report for Telesign Mobile Limited (the Company’) for the year ended 31
December 2025.
Business review
As a mobile network operator (‘MNO’), Telesign Mobile Limited has access to direct connections in a diverse set
of geographies as well as access to data within the Signalling System 7 ('SS7') network that enables the
Company to offer a unique set of capabilities to its customers. The market for Short Messaging Service ('SMS')

. delivery services continues to grow and evolve as companies understand the potential for delivering content
directly to consumers via mobile phone. Historically, the Company focused primarily on SMS two factor
authentication. In recent years, the Company expanded service offerings to provide its customer base with a
variety of communication services via SMS delivery.
The Company's focus on organic growth continues, represented by continuous business with four external
customers in the current year with the primary customer comprising 86% (2024: 89%) of the Company's external
revenues. The Company's organic growth strategy includes hiring of employees in the local area and a focused
promotion in the high growth communications platform as a service market. The Company's total revenue
decreased in 2025 to £19.0m from £40.4m in 2024 with the intercompany revenue portion making up £13.3m in
2025, which is a £16.6m decrease from £29.9m in 2024.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Company's operations expose it to a variety of financial risks that include credit risk, liquidity risk and trading
tisk. The Company has in place the relevant controls that seeks to limit the adverse effects of the risks on the
financial statements. Additionally, the Company has seen inflationary pressures in the current year as well as
exposure to fluctuating foreign exchange rates, impacting both revenues and costs.
Credit risk
New customers are only accepted once all the relevant credit checks have been performed. The Company
regularly reviews the credit positions of existing customers to mitigate the risk of bad debt.
Liquidity risk
The Company is financed with appropriate cash levels and financing to match the needs of the business. This is
primarily from the parent company, Telesign Corporation via a cost plus transfer pricing arrangement, as
required.
Trading risk
The Company's primary customer represents 86% of the Company's external revenues, which may present a
tisk of over-reliance on key customers within the customer base. Certain customers of the Company trade
globally with a presence in Russia, which may give rise to potential risks associated with the ongoing Russia-
Ukraine War, the Company monitors these risks on a weekly basis. Procedures have been implemented to
ensure that any new developments that may have an effect on the Company's operations are dealt with and
additional assurance is gained through external advisors. The Company continues to focus on diversifying its
customer base as part of the Company's strategic financial goals.

° the amount of turnover can be measured reliably; and

2.8 Pensions
Defined contribution pension plan
The Company operates a defined contribution plan for its employees. A defined contribution plan is a
pension plan under which the Company pays fixed contributions into a separate entity. Once the
contributions have been paid the Company has no further payment obligations.
The contributions are recognised as an expense in profit or loss when they fall due. Amounts not paid
are shown in accruals as a liability in the Balance Sheet. The assets of the plan are held separately
, from the Company in independently administered funds.
2.9 Taxation
Tax is recognised in profit or loss except that a charge attributable to an item of income and expense
recognised as other comprehensive income or to an item recognised directly in equity is also
recognised in other comprehensive income or directly in equity respectively.
The current income tax charge is calculated on the basis of tax rates and laws that have been
enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date in the countries where the Company
operates and generates income.
