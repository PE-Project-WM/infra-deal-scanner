Principal risks and uncertainties
. Financial Risks
Market and economic trends
Company operations have the potential to be impacted in the longer-term by trends in other markets and the
wider economic environment. In order to mitigate the risk of a downturn in one market having a significant
impact on the Company, management try to ensure market diversification wherever possible. The Company's
customer base is highly diversified across a wide range of sectors, both private and public.
Price risk
Pricing continues to be a risk in terms of regulatory changes, competition and inflationary pressures. This risk is
mitigated by offering new services that offer additional value and cross selling products supplied via the
acquisitions. The growth strategy provides greater scale and therefore buying economy opportunities.
Liquidity :
The Group reviews its funding requirements closely on a regular basis, preparing both short and longer term
cash flow forecasts that apply sensitivities that may impact the cash requirements of the business. Future
outlook, remains strong withheadroom on banking facilities therefore the Directors consider that these risks have
; been sufficiently mitigated. The company is funded by intercompany arrangements with Thetis Bidco Limited
directly. :
Interest rate risk
The group manages financial risk of increased interest rates by preparing short term and long term sensitivity
forecasts to ascertain the Company's requirements for interest payments. The company is funded by
intercompany arrangements which are non-interest bearing.
Credit risk
To manage credit risk, credit limits are set for customers based on a combination of payment history and third
party credit references. Credit limits are reviewed on a risk basis in conjunction with debt ageing and collection
history. The Company has a highly diversified customer base, across a number of different public and private
industries.

Fraud & Cyber-Attacks
Fraudulent use of services is a constant threat and, although primary responsibility falls on the end user, this risk
is mitigated through proactive monitoring of customer accounts. Wavenet deploys DDoS mitigation at the edge
of its network, reducing the impact of volumetric attacks on proprietary infrastructure and improving quality of
service for customers. .
Fraud protection is never taken for granted. The Company operates systems that track changes in caller data
records, comparing historical usage patterns alongside real-time lookups against known suspicious global calling
locations. These regular checks enable the Company to respond to emerging threats in a timely manner.
The Company has invested significantly in leading-edge Managed Detection & Response ("MDR") and Endpoint
Detection & Response ("EDR") solutions, supported by regular penetration testing and vulnerability assessments
delivered through Wavenet's Security Operations Centre ("SOC") and external partners.
Wavenet is committed to data security and maintains robust systems that are compliant with ISO 27001:2022,
Cyber Essentials Plus, and UK GDPR, with regular audits conducted by UKAS-approved external certification
bodies. All data is held within secure, UK and EU-based data centres with restricted physical access, layered
technical controls including firewalls, multi-factor authentication, and privileged access management. The
Company maintains an in-house Network Operations Centre ("NOC") and Security Operations Centre ("SOC")
providing 24/7 monitoring and threat response.
The Group also maintains formal risk assessment processes to identify, assess, and manage information
security and product risk, including supply chain and third-party risk, in line with industry best practice.
Worldwide events . :

The workforce is located in the UK, as are the vast majority of the customer base. There are a de-minimis
amount of goods and services that are purchased from US and EU countries, hence currency exposure is
minimal.
The Company has minimal exposure to climate change risk.
