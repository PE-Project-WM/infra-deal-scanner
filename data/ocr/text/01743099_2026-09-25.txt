Strategic Report

Business Overview
Telefonica UK Limited (the Company), trading under the O2 brand, is a wholly-owned subsidiary of VMED 02 UK Limited (VMED O02) (Virgin
Media 02) (the Group) and is one of the main operating businesses of Virgin Media O2. VMED O2 UK Limited is a joint venture (the Joint
Venture or JV) of Liberty Global Ltd. (Liberty Global) and Telefénica, SA (Telefénica), both referred to as “Shareholders”.
The principal activity of the Company during the year was, and will continue to be, a mobile network operator (MNO), providing mobile
telephony services to consumer customers in the United Kingdom (UK). On 1 August 2025, the provision of certain mobile telephony
services to business customers was transferred to Virgin Media Business Limited, a subsidiary of O2 Business Holdings Limited (formerly
O2 Daisy Limited) which heads the O2 Business Group (formerly O2 Daisy group) (O2 Business), along with the sale of certain assets.
Further details are reported in the “Business” section on page 7. The Company remains a principal employer within the Group.
Virgin Media O02 combines the second largest fixed network and one of three MNOs in the UK. As of 31 December 2025, our total gigabit
footprint stands at 18.8 million homes serviceable and served 5.8 million fixed-line customers with 5.7 million customers taking a broadband
internet product. In addition, as of 31 December 2025, Virgin Media O2 served 46.7 million mobile connections, including 36.3 million retail
mobile connections.
Further details of the Business Overview including ‘Who We Are’, ‘Key Trends That Influence Us’ and ‘Our Group Strategy and 2025
Progress’ during the financial year ended 31 December 2025 are included in the 2025 Consolidated Annual Report of VMED O2 UK Limited
published on the website https://news.virginmediao2.co.uk,
For the purposes of this Annual Report, unless the context otherwise requires, the terms ‘we’ and ‘our refer to Telefonica UK Limited.
Unless otherwise indicated, operational and statistical data, including subscriber statistics and product offerings, are as of 31 December
2025.

Consumer Mobile
As one of the MNOs in the UK we serve a significant proportion of UK consumers with our mobile telecommunications and related
services.
We utilise a multi-brand approach to operate in different segments of the market. We provide services directly to consumers, and also use
strategic and targeted wholesale relationships through our Mobile Virtual Network Operator (MVNO) partnerships (see “Business and
Wholesale” on page 7 for further information).
Consumer Mobile Services and Products
We offer a wide range of mobile telecommunications and value-add services and products to our consumer customers. Our approach
focuses on the needs of our customers with the aim of maintaining and increasing our large, loyal customer base to drive value.
a) Mobile Services
Consumer customers can access mobile voice, messaging and data services supplied through either a SIM card or an embedded SIM
(eSIM). Customers can purchase a SIM card either on its own as a SIM only (SIMO) mobile service or together with a handset. Mobile
services for both SIMO and handset options are offered under two payment models: contract and prepaid. Contract customers have
access to the “Custom Plan” proposition (as part of “O2 Refresh”), which allows customers to spread the cost of their phones over
different terms, flex their data up and down, or upgrade. O2 prepaid customers can purchase a handset by paying in full upfront.
We also provide data-only mobile broadband (MBB) services over our mobile network. Customers can purchase data-only plans together
with a tablet device, or as a SIMO service purchasing data access devices separately, for example a laptop dongle or a mobile WiFi device.
“02 Family” is our multi-SIM proposition with the aim to simplify billing processes and provide discounts based upon the number of
connections registered. For each family connection registered, the primary account holder receives one bill rather than multiple bills
throughout the month.
“O2 Essential Plan” is a social tariff designed to provide low-cost mobile connectivity to those who need it. As of 31 December 2025, the
proposition offered 10GB of mobile data, plus unlimited calls and texts for £10.00 per month for account holders who receive certain
support payments — including Universal Credit, Pension Credit, Income Support, income-based Jobseeker’s Allowance and income-based
Employment and Support Allowance. Our O2 Essential Plan is currently exempt from our price rises.
“O2 Satellite” was announced in October 2025 and is a partnership with Starlink Direct to Cell to boost mobile coverage through Starlink’s
satellite technology. The O2 Satellite service complements O2’s existing mobile network and works automatically in areas with no
traditional mobile coverage, with the initial launch of the service taking place in February 2026.
b) Hardware Sales
In addition to handsets, tablets and data access devices offered to our mobile customers, we offer a range of stand-alone products,
including smart-tech (such as smart watches and fitness devices) and accessories (such as handset cases, screen protectors, chargers,
cables, portable batteries, headphones and speakers) as well as other technology products (such as games consoles and virtual reality
headsets). .
Our “Switch Up” proposition allows customers on certain tariffs to trade in their current phone for a new one every 11 months, regardless of
the customer’s remaining contract length.
As part of our “Like New” online offers, customers can select from a choice of quality assured pre-owned handsets. Customers can
purchase refurbished handsets as part of our contract and prepaid payment models.
Our “O2 Recycle” scheme allows consumers to exchange old phones and other devices utilising the value towards the cost of a new
device or receive cash payment or bill credit.

Business
The Company provided a range of telecommunications services to UK businesses, outlined below.
In 2025 Virgin Media 02 made a strategic decision to combine certain aspects of the Group’s business-to-business (B2B) operations being
the large customer base and scaled fixed and mobile networks with the agile platforms, broad product set and deep B2B expertise of
Daisy Group. Virgin Media O2 see this as a significant opportunity in the B2B market, driven by greater scale, efficiencies, and a combined
set of products. The combined business completed on 1 August 2025 with the creation of O2 Business Holdings Limited (formerly O02 Daisy
Limited) which heads the O2 Business Group (formerly 02 Daisy group) (02 Business), ultimately owned by Virgin Media O2 (70%) and Jet
Holdco Limited (formerly Daisy Topco Limited) (30%) (02 Business transaction).
As part of the O2 Business transaction, the Company contributed and sold certain assets to a fellow group company Virgin Media Business
Limited (VMBL) at an amount equal to their estimated fair market value. The consideration was satisfied as follows (i) £82.3 million intra-
group loan between VMBL and the Company, (ii) the issue by VMBL of an unsecured promissory note with a principal amount of £50.0
million, repayable on demand at the option of either party; and (iii) the issue by VMBL of a second unsecured promissory note with a
principal amount of £1,004.7 million. This resulted in a gain on sale of certain assets of £1,003.6 million recognised within the Profit and Loss
Account.
As part of the completion steps of the above transaction, the Company distributed by way of a dividend in specie to O2 Holdings Limited
the Company’s rights, title and interest in the receivables arising under the two promissory notes, with an aggregate value of £1,054,657
thousand. In addition, certain employees were transferred from the Company to VMBL under the Transfer of Undertakings (Protection of
Employment) Regulations (TUPE Regulations).
For further information on the transaction, see note 4 of the Notes to the Consolidated Financial Statements of VMED O02 UK Limited for the
year ended 31 December 2025 published on its website.
Direct Business
Until 1 August 2025 when the Company divested its B2B operations, the Company offered connectivity and related services to the Large
Enterprise, Public Sector (LEPS) in addition to Small-Medium Businesses (SMBs).
LEPS
We had a dedicated sales and customer success team focused exclusively on the LEPS market in the UK. We serviced a range of private
sector organisations including retail, construction, professional services, hospitality, financial services and manufacturing. Our combined
service management and sales teams enabled a single customer facing organisation with dedicated Customer Success Managers
focused on maximising customer satisfaction, delivering on our customers’ business objectives and retention.
We offered a wide range of propositions to LEPS customers, reflecting the complex requirements they had to connect their people and
data, operate in a secure and compliant way, and support the transformation of their businesses. Within the public sector, we had secured
and retained places on all the relevant government-approved frameworks, so organisations could buy public sector Information and
Communication Technology (ICT) services as flexibly and efficiently as possible.
Small or Home Office (SoHo) and SMB
We operated in the SoHo and SMB segments, broadly defined as companies with up to 250 employees, via a direct team as well as a
range of indirect partners.
a) Direct SMB
We had a dedicated sales, commercial and marketing team focused exclusively on the SoHo and SMB markets in the UK. The teams were
responsible for offering mobile connectivity propositions, including mobile hardware. :
b) Partnerships
Our partnerships sales channel comprised a number of relationships with partners, who sold O2 branded mobile connectivity and
hardware propositions to business customers either directly or through their network of resellers. These partners, many of whom had
relationships with O2 spanning decades, represented an important part of our SMB activity and were a significant route to market. They
offered us reach into different market segments, giving choice to business customers and often delivering a compelling service wrap and
product range. The partners complemented our direct routes to market by giving SMB customers choice as to where they procured their
telecommunications needs.

Products and Propositions
Mobile
Our mobile offering provides our customers with connectivity, devices, management and enterprise managed mobility tools to help
mobilise and manage their workforce, tailored to their needs. Our flexible approach to tariffs gives the customer the best fit for their
organisation, with voice, data, roaming and bolt-ons to ensure complete flexibility, control and peace of mind. We offer the latest
smartphones, tablets and accessories to help our customers of all sizes work smarter, faster and more efficiently, with flexible financing
and leasing options to spread the cost.
Data and Al
Our “O2 Motion” products provide customers with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (the UK GDPR)-compliant insights into the
movement, demography, mobility patterns and behaviour of millions of people. This is a big data solution that provides insight for smart
cities, transport, environment, economic, housing and planning projects at all scales from national down to street and building level, helping
organisations make the right operational and investment decisions, as well as evaluating their impact over time. Our rigorous processes
ensure customer privacy is maintained, and we are continually audited by regulators and external experts in data privacy and security to
ensure compliance with all data protection and privacy laws.
Private Networks
We have a 4G and 5G Private Networks offering which provides our customers with secure, reliable and low latency connectivity delivered
on-site, and dedicated for the customer to manage business-critical processes and drive digital transformation.
We offer the ability for customers to rapidly deploy Private 5G networks with our plug-and-play suitcase-size offering which includes
integrated edge computing and can be used to pilot and trial the technology in a customer environment.

Wholesale
MVNO
We aim to be the partner of choice in the MVNO market, and serve more MVNO subscribers than any other operator. Our mobile network’s
coverage, capacity and speed, combined with our wholesale platforms, enable MVNOs to provide services to their customers with high-
quality and reliable connectivity.
a) Tesco Mobile
Tesco Mobile, a 50:50 joint venture between Virgin Media O2 and Tesco subsidiaries, is the UK’s largest MVNO by connections. Tesco
Mobile was founded in 2003, and in 2024 we agreed a 10-year renewal of our partnership.
b) Sky Mobile
Sky entered into an exclusive, multi-year MVNO agreement with O2 in 2015, enabling Sky to complement its fixed-line and TV services with
mobile connectivity. This relationship was extended on a long-term basis in 2021, forming part of a broader multi-faceted relationship with
Sky.
We believe our proven track record of highly successful MVNO partnerships positions us to capture the next phase of wholesale growth,
as new entrants look to enter the market. By combining our high-capacity mobile networks with partner-driven retail reach, we remain at
the forefront of enabling choice, innovation, and competition in the UK connectivity market.
Mobile Network-as-a-Service
Under the 02 brand, we power essential customer communications for businesses across the UK, delivering more than ten billion Short
Message Service (SMS) messages annually. These messages enable everything from appointment reminders and delivery updates to
secure authentication. Beyond messaging, we help businesses expand digital sales by offering carrier billing, allowing customers to pay
directly through their mobile account.
Internet of Things (IoT)
We offer loT products and services, which include M2M activities. These provide automated data communications between devices
{including vehicle fleets, payment terminals and traffic lights) via our mobile network, to help businesses reduce costs and grow revenue
through greater convenience and improved customer experience.
In 2013, the UK government awarded 02 a 15-year contract to deliver communications services (including the supply of communication
hubs) to homes in the central and southern regions of the UK. Since then, we have connected smart meters, both gas and electricity, in
more than 11 million homes, enabling reduced energy usage and CO2 emissions by providing greater visibility of energy consumption.
Networks
We are one of three MNOs in the UK, and operate a mobile access network based on 2G, 3G, 4G and 5G standards.
Mobile Network Deployment
Our current focus is evolving our network to 5G technology. As of 31 December 2025, we had 87% 5G outdoor UK population coverage, an
increase of 12% compared to the end of 2024. Additionally, we continue to strengthen our 4G network, with over 99% outdoor population
coverage on our 4G network, as well as over 89% 4G geographic (landmass) coverage.
As part of our network evolution, we also completed the withdrawal of 3G services across the majority of the UK in 2025, with
decommissioning finalised in March 2026. This has enabled us to deliver enhanced speeds and reliability through 4G and 5G for
customers. The move follows the joint statement in December 2021, with all UK MNOs committing to switch off 2G and 3G by 2033.
Network Sharing Arrangement with Vodafone
Our long-standing mobile network sharing agreement with Vodafone enables us to deliver improved customer experience and operational
efficiency across the UK. This arrangement combines our mobile sites into a single national grid and includes both active and passive
infrastructure sharing. *
Active Radio Access Network sharing includes 2G, 3G, 4G, and 5G infrastructure (excluding London and certain other major cities), with
each operator managing different regions. O2 oversees the Eastern half of the UK, Northern Ireland, and most of Scotland, while Vodafone
manages the Western half of the UK, including Wales.

Customer Service
Customer satisfaction is central to our long-term success and remains a core strategic priority across the organisation. We commit to

Risk Appetite
Inherently, there are risks associated with all activities; therefore risk management applies to all areas of the business. To do this
intelligently and proportionately, and to commit the correct amount of resources, risk management considers the context of the
organisation, including its strategy, objectives, risk appetite and available resources.
We apply a risk-based governance design with increased oversight as risks are considered more. significant. Risk governance materiality
thresholds and three risk levels have been established to support this approach, ensuring appropriate oversight and proportionate focus
on risks:

* The ‘Priority’ risks are reported periodically to the Executive Management Team;

* The ‘Enhanced Oversight’ risks are reviewed by divisional senior management teams at agreed frequencies; and

* ‘Locally Managed’ risks have proportionate, local oversight by the accountable business area with escalation as appropriate.

Risk Management Process

The Risk Management Process operates Group-wide, is applicable to all employees, and drives a consistent enterprise-wide approach to
risk management. Our risk management approach includes the following steps:

a) Identify and Document

Risks are identified and captured at all levels across Virgin Media ©2, with input drawn from various sources and forums. These include:
strategic planning activities; operational teams; Governance, Risk, and Compliance functions; horizon scanning and monitoring of emerging
risks; and regular risk-focused review meetings. Risk identification is an ongoing activity embedded within Virgin Media O02, considering
both internal and external factors that could impact the successful delivery of our strategy, operations, and transformation activities.

b) Assessment

All risks are assessed to determine their importance, prioritisation, and risk treatment. The combination of impact and likelihood provides
the profile (ranking) of the risk. in addition, and separate from this profile, we also consider proximity, which indicates how quickly the risk
may materialise and helps determine appropriate prioritisation and response.

¢) Treatment

Once a risk has been identified, documented and assessed, the risk owner determines the most appropriate treatment. In doing so, they
consider the organisation’s risk appetite, potential benefits and opportunities, and the resources required. When the chosen treatment is to
take steps to reduce the likelihood and/or impact of the risk, the risk owner sets a measurable and achievable target and date. The main
tisk treatment options are as follows:

i, Avoid: When we choose not to proceed with the activity which would give rise to the risk. This is usually applied in cases where
the risk exposure is unacceptably significant and outside the business’ risk appetite, or outweighs the opportunity being explored.

ii, Mitigate: When we take actions to reduce the likelihood and/or impact of the risk, thus reducing our exposure. Changing the
impact can sometimes be through sharing or transferring the risk exposure to third-parties. This is typically applied where the risk
exposure is higher than we want to accept.

ii, | Accept: When we agree to accept the current exposure of the risk, taking no further action to reduce it, whilst acknowledging it
could impact us. This is typically applied where either: the risk exposure is within our risk appetite, the cost-benefit-appetite
analysis determined acceptance, or there are significant barriers to mitigation.

iv. Defer: When we want to reduce the risk but are not able to proceed yet. This is differentiated from ‘accept’, as further exposure
reduction is intended at some point. This is typically where a risk requires resources or solutions which are currently unavailable.

v. Monitor: When we are not taking new actions to reduce the exposure, but controls are in place and being operated to prevent
the risk exposure increasing. This is differentiated from ‘accept’, as the business is operating controls to help prevent exposure
increasing. This is sometimes applied where the risk is within appetite but a potential increase in exposure would be outside of
appetite.

d) Monitor, Review and Report
Aligned to our risk governance model, we use ongoing monitoring and review to:

¢ Keep risk information accurate and up to date, ensuring the risk register remains dynamic and accurate for management
oversight;

* Manage triggers that could crystallise risks; and

+ — Ensure risk treatment is on track.

In July 2024, Ofcom announced that mid-contract price rises linked to inflation would be banned from January 2025, with new contracts
issued by communications providers specifying any price changes upfront in pounds and pence.
Effective April 2025, we changed the mechanism for the annual price rise in compliance with these new rules and applied an annual price
rise to the vast majority of mobile airtime plans of £1.80, or £0.75 for Watch and MBB products. In October 2025, we began communicating

increase of £0.75, effective each April from 2026.
We have been clear in our public position: as a business we make decisions to invest millions of pounds every year into our network, with
UK consumers benefitting from an extremely competitive market. We appreciate that price changes are never welcome, but we are fully
transparent with our customers, offering a 30 day right to exit when announcing any changes to the pricing set out in existing customers’
terms and conditions, in line with current regulations.
We have also engaged with Ofcom, Government, and the press and consumer bodies in respect of our price rise activity. In February 2026,
we signed the Government’s voluntary Telecoms Consumer Charter which clearly sets out a shared commitment between government
and industry to deliver even greater outcomes for consumers. Amongst a range of new and existing initiatives, we committed to not
changing the amount by which a customer’s monthly subscription price increases in-contract in future.
