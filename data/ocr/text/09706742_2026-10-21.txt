Introduction ,
The principal activity of Genius Sports Group Limited ("the Company") is that of being an intermediate holding
company, holding investments on behalf of its parent entities. . : .
Genius Sports Limited, a company incorporated in Guernsey is the ultimate parent entity. Genius Sports Limited
is publicly listed on the New York Stock Exchange. Genius Sports Limited and its subsidiary entities, including
oo the Company, (together "the Group") operate as Genius Sports Group. : .
Genius Sports Group is a leading official data, technology and commercial partner that powers the global
écosystem connecting sports, betting and media. The Group is a global leader in digital sports content,
technology and integrity services. The Group's technology is used in over 150 countries worldwide, empowering
sports to capture, manage .and distribute their live data and video, driving their digital transformation and
enhancing their relationships with fans.
Business review .
Genius Sports Group Limited holds certain investments on behalf of its parent undertaking but does not trade in
its own right. The toss for the year was $18,604k (2024: $8,871k). This arises primarily from general overheads
of $12,823k (2024: $11,319k), M&A activity costs of $2,320k (2024: $nil), litigation costs of $17k (2024: $235k)
and a foreign exchange gain of $2,438k (2024: $600k loss) and the difference between the intercompany
interest payable and receivable of $7,501k payable (2024: $3,895k receivable). The net assets position is
commented on below under key performance indicators.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The key risks facing the Company are outlined below. ;
, Financial risk management
The Company's debt financing exposes it to a variety of financial risks. In the course of its business, the
Company is exposed to interest rate risk, liquidity risk, foreign currency risk, technology risk and rights risk.
Interest rate risk .
Interest rate risk arises due to the fluctuations in interest rates, which the Company is exposed to through its
borrowing and lending. This is mitigated through using fixed interest rates. :
Liquidity risk .
Liquidity risk represents the risk that the Company will not be able to meet its financial obligations as they fall
due.
The Company's approach to managing this risk is to ensure, as far as possible, that it will always have sufficient
liquidity to meet its liabilities when due, under both norma! and stressed conditions, without incurring
unacceptable losses or risking damage to the Company's reputation. Should the Company ever have difficulty
maintaining liquidity, it will be supported by its ultimate parent entity.
: Foreign currency risk

Foreign currency risk arises both where sale and purchase transactions are undertaken in currencies other than
the respective functional currency of the Company (transactional exposures). The Company does not have any
significant foreign currency exchange transactions other than intercompany balances, therefore this does not
represent a significant risk to the wider group.

Technology risk :
Our future business and financial success will depend on our ability to continue to anticipate the needs of
‘ customers and potential customers, to achieve and maintain broad market acceptance for our existing and future
products and services, to successfully introduce new and upgraded products and services and to successfully
implement our current and future geographic expansion plans. To be successful, we must be able to quickly
adapt to changes in technology, industry standards and regulatory requirements by continually enhancing our
technology, services and solutions. Developing new services and upgrades to products and services, as well as
integrating and coordinating current products and services, imposes burdens on our product development team,
management and researchers. These processes are costly, and our efforts to develop, integrate and enhance
our services may not be successful. In addition, successfully launching and selling a new or upgraded product or
service puts additional strain on our sales and marketing resources. Expanding into new markets and investing
resources towards increasing the depth of our coverage within existing markets impose additional burdens on
our research, systems development, sales, marketing and general managerial resources. If we are unable to
manage our expansion efforts effectively, in obtaining greater market share or in obtaining widespread adoption
of new or upgraded products and services, we may not be able to offset the expenses associated with the launch
and marketing of the new or upgraded product or service, which could have a material adverse effect on our
financial results. .
Rights risk
If we lose any official accreditation from one of our league or federation partners, we could lose our exclusive
rights to collect certain data, streams or other properties and our services would be less attractive to customers.
Our revenue may decrease as a result, which could have a material adverse effect on the results of our
operations. We must use all efforts to retain and acquire key sports data, streaming and other properties and to
protect and enforce our rights. If we are unable to do so or otherwise unable to provide products and services
that customers want, then customers may become dissatisfied and use our competitors’ services. If we are
unable to continue offering innovative services, we may be unable to attract additional customers or retain our ~
existing customers, which could harm our business, results of operations and financial condition.
Non-financial risks
The Company has very limited exposure to non-financial risks given that its main purpose is to hold investments.
. However, the Company does administer certain rental properties and as such does consider the risks posed by
environmental factors. These risks are limited given the small number of leaseholds held by the Company.
As the Company's main purpose is to hold investments, the principal risks and uncertainties that directly impact
the group headed by Genius Sports Limited ("the Group") may also indirectly impact the Company. For instance,
an uncertainty noted in a group entity could result in an indicator of impairment of the Company's investment.
Alternatively, a group uncertainty could cast doubt over the recoverability of one of the Company’s intercompany
receivable balances.
Accordingly, the below risks are Group risks which are of particular relevance to the Company.
Regulatory risk
There is a risk that the Group’s business model is impacted by future changes in regulations in the online sports
betting industry. The Group regularly reviews activity in this area through proactive discussions with key industry
officials, professional advisors and regulatory bodies where appropriate. The Group is a member of the World
Lottery Association, the European Lotteries and the Toto Association, and is a subscriber to the Gambling
Compliance publications.
A large number of the Group's current customers are online sportsbook operators who market and sell to their
customers on a worldwide basis. Jurisdictions continue to regulate the online gambling market through the
continuation or introduction of licensing regimes. The Group liaises regularly with its customers regarding their
active management of this risk.

Skills risk : .
The success and future growth of the Group is in part dependent on the continued performance and delivery of ©
certain directors, managers and key staff. .
The Group seeks to recruit, develop, manage and deploy highly skilled talent throughout the organisation. The
Group utilises reputable recruiters to fill senior vacancies and operates an employee referral programme to
encourage employees to recommend individuals whose work quality and skills are known to them.
The Group invests in the recruitment of highly skilled individuals and operates a proactive system of training and
performance management. The ability of the Group to attract and retain talented employees requires it to
maintain competitive employment packages and personal development opportunities. A system of succession
planning processes are in place to ensure key roles are identified and career development opportunities
established. :
Systems and infrastructure
The Group is- dependent on its IT technical infrastructure and systems for the management of its core
‘operations. Risks of system outages, loss of connectivity and software errors are regularly reviewed and a
business continuity plan has been established in conjunction with service level agreements with appropriate third
parties. Continuity of access to data and integrity of data is maintained through the implementation of rigorous
application testing and use of a variety of systems for the monitoring of core customer facing applications and
feeds.

2.15 Financial instruments
The Company has elected to apply the provisions of Section 11 “Basic Financial Instruments” of FRS

: - Financial instruments are recognised in the Company's Statement of Financial Position when the ~

Financial assets and liabilities are offset, with the net amounts presented in the financial statements,
, when there is a legally enforceable right to set off the recognised amounts and there is an intention to

Basic financial assets
Basic financial assets, which include trade and other debtors, cash and bank balances, are initially
measured at their transaction price (adjusted for transaction costs except in the initial measurement
of financial assets that are subsequently measured at fair value through profit and loss) and are
subsequently carried at their amortised cost using the effective interest method, less any provision for
: impairment, unless the arrangement constitutes a financing transaction, where the transaction is
measured at the present value of the future receipts discounted at a market rate of interest.
Discounting is omitted where the effect of discounting is immaterial. The Company's cash and cash
equivalents, trade and most other debtors due with the operating cycle fall into this category of
financial instruments. .
Impairment of financial assets . ,
At the end of each reporting period financial assets measured at amortised cost are assessed for
: objective evidence of impairment. If an asset is impaired the impairment loss is the difference
between the carrying amount and the present value of the estimated cash flows discounted at the
asset's original effective interest rate. The impairment loss is recognised in profit or loss.
Financial assets are impaired when events, subsequent to their initial recognition, indicate the
estimated future cash flows derived from the financial asset(s) have been adversely impacted. The
impairment loss will be the difference between the current carrying amount and the present value of
the future cash flows at the asset(s) original effective interest rate.
If there is a favourable change in relation to the events surrounding the impairment loss then the
impairment can be reviewed for possible reversal. The reversal will not cause the current carrying
amount to exceed the original carrying amount had the impairment not been recognised. The
impairment reversal is recognised in the profit or loss.
Financial liabilities :
Financial liabilities and equity instruments are classified according to the substance of the contractual
arrangements entered into. An equity instrument is any contract that evidences a residual interest in
the assets of the Company after the deduction of all its liabilities.
Basic financial liabilities, which include trade and other creditors, bank loans and other loans are
initially measured at their transaction price (adjusting for transaction costs except in the initial
measurement of financial liabilities that are subsequently measured at fair value through profit and
loss). When this constitutes a financing transaction, whereby the debt instrument is measured at the
present value of the future payments discounted at a market rate of interest, discounting is omitted
where the effect of discounting is immaterial.

18. Contingent liabilities
Panda . . :
On 5 October 2023, Sportscastr Inc. (d/b/a Panda Interactive) ("Sportscastr”) filed a claim against Genius
Sports Limited (and various subsidiaries including the Company) in the United States District Court for the
Eastern District of Texas. Sportscastr is claiming Genius Sports Limited is infringing patents held by
Sportscastr relating to the provision of synchronized live data and content: within live video streams.
Sportscastr is seeking an order prohibiting any infringement and monetary relief against Genius Sports
Limited. On 14 February 2025, Sportscastr amended the complaint to add antitrust allegations under
federal and Texas state antitrust laws involving the distribution of official, live professional sports data. The
parties have notified the Court that all matters in dispute have been settled in principle. The parties are
working on finalising and executing a settlement agreement.
dMy
On 12 September 2023, a claim was filed in the Court of Chancery of Delaware against dMY (the special :
. purpose acquisition company ("SPAC") that merged with the Genius legacy business to create Genius
Sports Limited) and the directors of dMY. The claim relates to matters pre-merger. Genius Sports Limited
would be liable for damages and costs awarded. On 10 October 2025, the parties informed the Court that
they have reached a settlement of all claims, which must be reviewed and approved by the Court.
Following Court approval, and in satisfaction of its indemnification obligations, Genius Sports Limited will
make a payment on behalf of the indemnified defendants towards the agreed settlement. The settlement
contribution is included within other current liabilities as of 31 December 2025. The agreed settlement was
paid on 9 January 2026.
Revolving credit facility
The Company is a party in the Group's revolving credit facility agreement of $180m. As of 31 December
2025, the Company had no outstanding borrowings under the Credit Agreement. The Company is subject
to cross guarantees on its bank accounts and assets under the credit arrangement.

Sage & Thompson
On March 24, 2026, Christopher Sage and Terry Thompson (the “Piaintiffs”) filed a claim against Genius
Sports Limited, along with various other Defendants including DraftKings Inc, FanDuel Inc. and the NFL in
the Philadelphia Court of Common Pleas in Philadelphia, Pennsylvania. The claim alleges the Sportsbook
Defendants intentionally and defectively designed their online sports gambling platforms to be highly
. addictive to consumers and that those defective online sports gambling platforms could not have been
created without the official data supplied by Genius Sports Limited through its exclusive partnership with
the NFL. The Plaintiffs are seeking injunctive and monetary relief against all Defendants. Genius Sports
Limited is defending all claims. This litigation is currently ongoing and Genius Sports Limited can provide
no assurances regarding the outcome of the claim and the impact it may have on Genius Sports Limited's
business and reputation. . :
