Directors’ report
The Directors present their report and the audited financial statements of the Company for the year ended 31 December
2025.
Results and dividends
During the financial year, the Company reported a profit of £96,374 (2024: £134,338).
No dividend has been declared or paid to Telekom Malaysia Berhad, the Company’s immediate and ultimate holding
company (2024: Nil).
Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties facing the Company are summarised as follows:
+ Normal competitive pressures from competition.
+  Digitalisation of products & services is key to ensure we can compete in the future. The Company has to
ensure it has the correct digital strategy.
However, these risks are expected to have a minimal adverse impact on the Company taking into consideration that the
expenses incurred to provide the services will be marked up and recharged to TM Technology Services Sdn Bhd (“TM
Tech”), a wholly owned subsidiary of Telekom Malaysia Berhad.
Financial risk management .
The Company’s activities expose it to the following financial risks:
Foreign exchange risk
The Company operates mainly in the United Kingdom and is exposed to foreign exchange risk which arises when
: . commercial transactions, recognised assets and liabilities are denominated in a currency that is not the Company’s :

functional currency. The currencies giving rise to this risk are primarily the United States Dollar and the Euro. In the
opinion of the Directors, the United States Dollar and Euro are likely to fluctuate with Great British Pounds within
tolerable ranges, and accordingly, the Company considers exposure to foreign exchange risk to be low. The Company
has not issued any financial instruments or entered into any contracts for foreign hedging purposes.
Credit risk
The Company’s potential credit risk exposure primarily relates to amounts receivable from its related company. The
amount earned from the related company accounted for 100% (2024: 100%) of the Company’s turnover during the year.
Management reviews the recoverable amount of each individual trade receivable regularly to ensure that adequate
impairment losses are recognised for irrecoverable debts. In this regard, the Directors consider that the exposure to credit
risk to be low.
The Company’s bank balances are deposited in internationally reputable banks. As such, no significant credit risk is
currently identified or anticipated. .
Liquidity risk
The Company follows a prudent liquidity risk management strategy to maintain a sufficient cash balance to meet its
financial commitments so as to enable the Company to meet its liabilities as and when they fall due. All of the Company’s
financial liabilities have a maturity period of less than 12 months.

(d) Revenue recognition
Revenue is measured at the fair value of the consideration received or receivable and represents the amount

financing transaction, the fair value of the consideration is measured as the present value of all future receipts
using the imputed rate of interest.
The Company recognises revenue when (a) the significant risks and rewards of ownership have been transferred
to the buyer; (b) the Company retains no continuing involvement or control over the goods; (c) the amount of
revenue can be measured reliably; (d) it is probable that future economic benefits will flow to the entity and (e)
when the specific criteria relating to each of the Company’s sales channels have been met, as described below.

(k) Taxation
Taxation expense for the period comprises current and deferred tax recognised in the reporting period. Tax is

(i) Current tax
Current tax is based on assessable profit for the year. Taxable profit differs from profit as reported in
the profit and loss account because it excludes or includes items of income or expense that are taxable
or deductible in other years and it further excludes items that are never taxable or deductible. The
Company's liability for current tax is calculated using tax rates that are enacted or substantively enacted
by the balance sheet date.
(ii) Deferred taxation
Deferred tax is recognised as a liability or asset if transactions or events that give the Company the
obligation to pay more tax in future or a right to pay less tax in future have occurred at the balance
sheet date. Deferred income tax is determined using tax rates (and laws) that have been enacted, or
substantially enacted, by the balance sheet date and are expected to apply when the related deferred
income tax asset is realised or deferred income tax liability is settled.
Deferred tax is recognised on all timing differences at the reporting date. Unrelieved tax losses and
other deferred tax assets are only recognised when it is probable that they will be recovered against the
reversal of deferred tax liabilities or other future taxable profits.

17 __ Post balance sheet events
Between the end of the financial year and the date of this report, certain material events have occurred, as disclosed
below. In the opinion of the Directors, no other items, transactions, or events of a material nature have arisen which are
likely to significantly affect the Company’s operations, results of those operations, or state of affairs in future financial
years.
(i) Dividend .
On 8 January 2026, the Company declared an interim dividend amounting to £500,000 to Telekom Malaysia
Berhad, the Company’s immediate and ultimate holding company. The dividend was paid on 21 January 2026.
(ii) Impact of the Middle East Conflict
The Middle East Conflict which started on 28 February 2026 and caused the closure of the Strait of Hormuz, has
significantly disrupted many business operations around the world.
The impact on business operations has not been a direct consequence of the conflict, but a result of the indirect
effects globally, notably surging energy prices, high shipping insurance premiums and supply chain bottlenecks
at critical chokepoints, including the Strait of Hormuz.
The conflict is not an adjusting post balance sheet event. However, up to the date of these financial statements,
the Company has not seen a significant impact of the conflict on the revenue, earnings, cash flow and financial
condition. At this juncture, it is challenging to predict the full extent and duration of its impact on business and
the economy. The Company will continue to monitor the development of these events and will implement
appropriate measures to mitigate the impact of conflict to the Company’s business.
