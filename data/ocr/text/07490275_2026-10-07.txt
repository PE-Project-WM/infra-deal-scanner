Introduction , : :
Torstone Technology Group (the ‘group’) is a global provider of cross-asset securities and derivatives
, post-trade processing and risk technology. The group's Saas Platform enables financial firms to .
reduce their costs, achieve greater control, minimise risk, and drive operational efficiency. The Group
. consists of Torstone Technology Limited (the ‘company’), a UK entity, headquartered in London, with
, . branches in Hong Kong, Singapore, and Tokyo, and a service entity, Torstone Technology (Canada)
Inc., in Canada. :
: Business review ;
North America continues to offer significant opportunities for us; our pipeline in both Canada and the :
US has grown significantly. Europe is our most mature market and we continue to see good .
opportunities to expand our market share and geographic coverage. Asia remains of strategic .
importance to us and for those clients looking to reduce operational costs by consolidating on a
global, cross asset platform. The acquisition by Fidelity National Information Services, Inc. (‘FIS' or the ,
‘parent’) offers significant upside for product investment, product enhancement with the FIS product
eco-system and global reach. . .
From a product perspective we have continued to invest in building our core back office product. We
have also invested significantly in our middle office product, which offers opportunities to expand our :
client base and expand our product offering to existing clients. :
We continue to invest in our people, ensuring we have a well-trained and motivated workforce,
including building a leadership program to help the next stage of our growth. Headcount decreased
from 62 in Dec 2024 to 54 at year end, as a result of the slow down in revenue growth. We have,
: however, largely retained a significant level of capacity to manage the anticipated growth from the FIS
. acquisition. . : :
During the year, Fidelity National Information Services Netherlands B.V. contributed through the ,
: issuance of 1 share of £0.10 each at a premium of £8 million. oe
The group recorded a loss after tax of £3.2m (Dec 2024: loss £4.8m), on the back of the revenue slow
down. . :
Principal risks and uncertainties , .
The management of the business and the execution of the Company strategy are subject to several
; . risks. The principal risks for the group continue to be the market environment and competition. :
. The group can compete effectively by using its repository of deep industry knowledge and applying ¢
structured thinking to provide practical solutions and building a reputation in the industry for delivering
: on time and on budget. To retain and recruit the right employees the Company monitors and provides
compensation & benefits at market rates. Business risk for the Company primarily arises from
: economic activity and the appetite for technology change, however we have seen a continued uptick
in‘interest from the market globally for example due to the T+1 regulatory changes in North America,
which is reflected in strong sales pipeline growth. The acquisition by FIS offers a significant increase
in our ability to deliver product and penetrate markets and clients we previously found challenging,
"e.g. Tier 1 clients and the US market.
. The group's operations expose it to a variety of financial and operational risks, which the group :
: monitors in a regular Risk Committee. The policies set by the Risk Committee are implemented by the.
group's various departments and monitored regularly by the Risk Committee. The integration in the
__ FIS risk management framework has significantly broaden the risk management tools and oversight
: available to the group. . . .
