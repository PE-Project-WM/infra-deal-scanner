Strategic Report 1
For the Year Ended 31 March 2025
The directors present their Strategic Report for the year ended 31 March 2025. |
Strategy and objectives |
The Company , . |
Viasat Technologies Limited (the "Company" or "Viasat Technologies") is a private limited company formed
under the laws of England and Wales. Viasat Technologies is a wholly owned subsidiary of Viasat, Inc.. The
Company has a high-capacity Ka-band satellite, known as ViaSat-2, which was placed into service in the fourth
quarter of fiscal year 2018. The satellite is intended to be used by the Company primarily to supply satellite
capacity on a wholesale basis to group companies.
Concentration of risk
The Company and Viasat, Inc. are party to a capacity purchase agreement (the “Capacity Purchase
' Agreement"), pursuant to which the Company has agreed to provide high-speed broadband Ka-band transmit
and receive communications capacity on the ViaSat-2 satellite to Viasat, Inc. within a specified coverage area.
Turnover under this agreement represented 90% of total turnover for fiscal year 2024 and approximately 100% -
: of total turnover for fiscal year 2025.
Principal risks and uncertainties ;
Financial risk management objectives
Credit risk
The Company's principal financial assets are bank balances and other receivables. The credit risk on liquid
funds is limited because the counterparties are banks with high credit ratings assigned by international credit
tating agencies. Additionally, the Company's income is from its parent undertaking which reduces its exposure
to credit risk.
Liquidity and cash flow risk
In order to maintain tiquidity to ensure that sufficient working capital is available for ongoing operations and
future developments cash reserves are maintained. In addition, the Company has the continued support of its
ultimate parent company, Viasat, Inc..
interest rate risk
The Company's interest rate risk is mitigated by fixed interest rates on its external and intercompany
borrowings.
. Price risk
The Company is exposed to price risk but this is mitigated by the controls operated over purchasing in the wider
Viasat group.
