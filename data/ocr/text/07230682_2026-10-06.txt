Strategic Report
For the year ended 31 December 2025
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Principal activities
The principal activity of the company is that of a holding company for the group.
The principal activities of the group are the provision of services for the design, installation, deployment,
integration and ongoing support of in-building ‘smart’ technologies. These include network connectivity
solutions (both wired and wireless), electronic security systems, audio-visual solutions, Internet of Things (loT)
systems (including environmental, energy and occupancy monitoring), and building and workplace
management software platforms.
Fair review of the business
The group continues to maintain a strong position within the UK smart building solutions market,
demonstrating resilience during a year of transition and targeted investment.
The company achieved a profit for the year of £600,000 (2024: £600,000) after dividends received from its
subsidiary. At the year end the company has net assets of £853,759 (2024: £853,759). .
The review of the trading performance for the year can be found in the financial statements of the trading
subsidiary Line Management Group Limited.
The group has continued to strengthen its strategic position and operational capabilities. Service offerings
have been expanded across long-standing client relationships, supporting additional revenue opportunities.
The company also secured a significant long-term contract renewal, entering into a new five-year global
framework agreement to provide voice support services, offering enhanced visibility of future revenues.
The group continues to invest in innovation and sustainability. During the year, it achieved “Works with WELL" .
accreditation for its Sense IQ platform and regained Silver status in the EcoVadis sustainability rating, placing
it within the top 15% of organisations globally for environmental, social and governance (ESG) performance.
The data centre sector has continued to emerge as a key growth area, with the group acting as a strategic
partner to clients and delivering long-term value through its specialist capabilities. In parallel, the group's
smart technologies revenue stream is forecast to grow significantly, with current projections indicating an
increase of over 250% in 2026.
Looking ahead, the directors remain confident in the group's prospects. While 2025 reflects a period of
transition, the underlying fundamentals of the business remain strong, supported by a solid pipeline of
opportunities, increasing demand for smart building technologies, and a clear strategic direction. The group is
well positioned to deliver sustainable long-term growth.
Global services
Since 2010, the group has been the UK and European representative of LGAP, a global alliance of like-
minded service providers with combined annual sales exceeding $400m. With over 38 offices worldwide,
LGAP members support major corporate clients across North America, South America, Asia Pacific, Europe,
South Africa, Hong Kong, India, China, Australia, New Zealand and Japan. This partnership continues to
provide access to international opportunities, including data centre developments, enterprise office fit outs and
IT integration projects.
The data centre market has continued to expand during the year, with the company increasingly acting as a '
strategic delivery partner on large-scale projects, delivering long-term value to global clients through this
. alliance.

The group continues to evaluate opportunities to strengthen its presence within Europe and will maintain close
collaboration with LGAP. partners to ensure consistent support for global clients.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The directors are responsible for identifying, evaluating and managing the principal risks facing the group. The
Risk Register is reviewed regularly to ensure an effective and responsive risk management framework.
Key risks include:
Market conditions
Exposure to economic uncertainty and fluctuations in client investment is mitigated through maintaining a
diversified service offering and client base, while capitalising on growth areas such as smart buildings and
data centres.
Customer base
The group continues to maintain a diverse client base and strengthen long-term relationships through
expanded service delivery.
Competition
The group operates in competitive markets and maintains a disciplined approach to pricing, cost contro! and
bid selection to ensure appropriate risk-adjusted returns.
The group remains focused on cost and efficiency in order to remain competitive during the construction
phase but undertakes a rigorous bid evaluation process in order to ensure it only competitively bids for project
work where the gross margin is commensurate with risk, and is well matched to its post-construction support
services model.
Commercial risk management
All contracts are subject to robust review and risk assessment to ensure alignment between risk and expected
return.
Supply chain
Supply chain risks are managed through strong supplier relationships, due diligence and diversification.
Financial risk management
Financial risks, including credit, foreign exchange and inflationary pressures, are actively managed through
established policies and oversight by the finance, commercial and procurement teams.
Liquidity risk management
Cash flow is closely monitored to ensure obligations are met as they fall due, supported by effective project-
level financial control.
Credit risk management
Credit procedures remain robust, with ongoing monitoring of client exposures and prudent contract selection. .
Foreign exchange currency exposure .
Foreign exchange risks are assessed and managed where appropriate, including the use of forward contracts |
when beneficial. . |
Future business prospects '
_ Demand for smart, connected and sustainable buildings continues to grow as industry standards become
: more defined through organisations such as RIBA, WiredScore, NABERS and the International WELL Building
Institute. These frameworks are accelerating the adoption of integrated digital technologies that enhance
building performance and user experience.
