The Directors present their strategic report together with the audited financial statements for Telent Technology
Services Limited (the “Company”) for the year ended 31 March 2026.
INTRODUCTION
The Directors are pleased to announce that the Company is now part of M Group, the leading provider of
infrastructure services in the UK and Ireland operating across Energy, Water, Technology & Communications,
Transport, and Defence, Industrial & Public Sector. M Group’s scale, expertise and impact will give the
Company access to a wider client portfolio and growth opportunities, and the Company is committed to be a
meaningful part of M Group supporting the offering of Intelligent Infrastructure solutions that monitor
performance, enabling predictive maintenance to boost efficiency, cut costs, increase uptime, and support
carbon reduction by reducing site visits.
The sale was approved by the Company's shareholder on 15 April 2025 and completed on 31 July 2025.
The M .Group consolidated financial statements are available here: https://mgrouplitd.com/who-we-
are/investors/annual-report/.
Principal Activities :
The Company is a leading technology company and specialist in the design, build, support and management
of the UK's critical smart infrastructure. We deliver services through two business units aligned to our target
sectors:
Network Services which provides services including the design, installation, integration, and ongoing operation
& support of operational technology. This includes critical networks, safety & security systems, command &
control systems and national smart infrastructure focussing on Government, Telecom Network Operators,
Public Safety and Defence sectors.
Transport which provides services including the design, installation, integration and ongoing operation &
support of operational technology. This includes critical networks, safety & security systems, control and
automation systems and national critical infrastructure, focussing on the Rail, Metro and Highways/Urban
Roads sectors
Objective and business strategy
The Company is a market leader delivering operational technology services and solutions to the UK and Ireland
critical national infrastructure market. We have been providing services for over 30 years and are well
established in the sectors we serve.
We aim to develop relationships with our customers, delivering tailored solutions, service excellence, technical
leadership and collaborative innovation, and thereby becoming their technology partner of choice, and securing
recurring revenue streams. We drive innovative solutions to improve our customers’ efficiency and operations
capability. The core of our technical strategy remains both our people and new technologies and we continue
to invest in both to ensure we offer the best solutions for our customers.
Continued growth is forecast for our target markets in the coming years, driven by significant ongoing
investment in public and private networks (fixed and mobile), 5G and digital transformation to deliver data
insights, enhanced performance and sustainability across our vertical markets. In addition to focused organic
growth, we will undertake selective acquisitions which enhance our market position or provide additional
capabilities.
We develop domain expertise and customer alignment through market sector facing business units, supported
by strong central management and business support teams. The structure of our business units is described
on page 4-6.

Review of the business (continued)
Network Services
The strategic aims for the business unit remain unchanged, focussing on our longstanding core market areas:
Telecom Network Operators, Public Safety, Government and Higher Education. Additionally, we made strong
progress within our newer markets: Power and Utilities, and Defence. Our opportunity pipeline across all focus
market areas has remained high, giving us a strong growth plan for the next five years and beyond. We grew
our order book, with order intake well exceeding our sales in the year.
For our Telecom Network Operator customers, we deliver Fixed Infrastructure, Fixed Technology and Mobile.
Investment continues in all three areas.
In Fixed Infrastructure, our main customer has remained Openreach. The year ended strongly, with the final
quarter representing the largest phase for their fibre build programmes across the UK, ensuring an increasing
proportion of premises were connected to fibre. We also continued to support their repair and maintenance
activities in the London region, the largest area within the UK. This contract has been extended to 2035 and
our performance remains strong.
Our Fixed Technology work remained mainly with Virgin Media O02 and BT. We continued to support Virgin
Media O2’s core network, the majority being Juniper and Cisco networking equipment. We also continued to ~
support the System X legacy voice Public Switched Telephone Network ("PSTN") estate for BT and secured a
direct support contract with VMO2 for their estate. Both contracts run through to 2028, covering the nationwide .
network until the PSTN is switched off.
Our Mobile business continued to grow, developing our end-to-end offering from site acquisition through design
to full construction. This has supported key customers, particularly EE in modernising their Radio Access
Network. We continued with programmes including building infill sites for 5G coverage and replacing and
upgrading vendor equipment on existing sites. At half year, after the acquisition of the Company by M Group,
the management of this work moved into the existing M Group Mobile team, enabling sharing of best practice
and recognising efficiencies.
In the Public Safety market, the new Emergency Services Network build continued to gain momentum. Most
of our work was delivered for IBM, who have a contract with the Home Office for end user services work,
including-new technology in control rooms and vehicles. We also secured extensions and new work packages
from our fire, ambulance, police and maritime customers across the UK and the Republic of Ireland. Our largest
contract award was with Thames Valley Police, where we are designing and deploying a new SDWAN and
LAN, followed by ongoing support.
Within the Government sector, we secured a further extension to our Parliamentary Digital Services managed
service contract and additional work with Cisco for the Greater Manchester Combined Authority.
In Higher Education, we continued to support our university customers and made significant progress on our
largest current project with the University of Bristol, with a large amount of Access Points installed and sites
going live on to the new modern network, rolling out connectivity across the campus.
In the Power and Utilities market, our main contract remained with New Nuclear Build, who are responsible for
the build of the new nuclear power station at Hinkley Point C. During the year, the design largely completed,
with proof of concepts and testing undertaken. The large-scale purchase of materials and equipment started,
ready for installation work to start later this year. We were successfully awarded the contract for Sizewell C,
the next new nuclear power station within the UK. This new contract covers the provision of services similar to
that being delivered at Hinkley Point C.
The Defence market remains a strategic priority. Throughout the year we continued to grow the pipeline of
opportunities both directly with the MOD and Government defence organisations, as well as the large prime
contractors within the market. We received a significant award for technical support for the RAF, for the next
5 years. The SCIDA (Site Coordination, Installation and Design Authority) is a key strategic win for the
business, establishing a direct relationship with the MOD.

Transport ,
Over the past year, our Transport business has continued to broaden both its customer base and technology
and service offerings. We are a key strategic partner to many of our customers including Transport for London
(‘TfL"), National Highways, Global Media, Network Rail, Train Operating Companies, local and regional
authorities, and numerous Tier 1 civils and signalling companies across the Transport sector. Additionally, we
provide a route to market for several Global Technology Partners and SMEs.
Our Transport business has three focus areas: Asset Management, Highways, and Rail Projects.
Asset Management
We have continued to deliver strong growth across Asset Management by generating increased revenue from
new sectors such as fire and ‘out of home advertising’ alongside excellent performance supported by
productivity improvements in our core contracts.
Within Traffic Signals, we successfully retained all existing maintenance whilst adding the maintenance of
Newcastle and the North East of England. .
Our CCTV product suite remains a differentiator and we have continued to invest in its development. This
includes ongoing customer trials of Al and Computer vision across our customer base and our remote download
product about to launch with TfL.
Within the Airports sector we secured two key technology contracts: Heathrow Traffic Signal Maintenance and
Arbitex for Liverpool John Lennon Airport, creating a platform for growth. We are focused on replicating the
successful technology partnership model with TfL, Rail and Traffic across the airport sector.
We expect the growth story of the last 6 years to continue across Asset Management, supported by expansion
in the airport sector, increasing our out of home advertising work with Global, delivery of CCTV and analytics
technology across new and existing markets and increasing our footprint with existing customers with new
contracts and services.
Highways
The Company continues in its role as service provider for the National Roads Telecommunications Service
(‘NRTS2") for National Highways. This ten-year contract covers the design and build, maintenance, and
operation of digital connectivity across the Strategic Road Network. During ‘this year National Highways
commenced a re-tender of this contract for up to 9.5 years, starting in 2028. The Company is well positioned
for this work and are in the final phases of the submission process.
In addition, we deliver maintenance and project activities across 13 of National Highways regions providing a
national footprint to deliver operational communications. During the year, we extended our contracts in the
South West and across the M25, to continue its delivery of technology maintenance across the road network.
We also secured further operational technology contracts to supply new digital CCTV cameras, and operational
technology assets, for use on the Strategic Road Network as part of a major technology renewals investment
programme by National Highways. The next phase will involve the rollout these new systems across the
network and the Company is well positioned for this work which will be tendered in the coming year.
We successfully completed the mobilisation activities as the network connectivity provider for National
Highways IT services, secured through our place on the IT Commercial Framework (ITCF). Building on this
platform, the Company has delivered improved network capacity and performance, underpinning the rollout of
new sites across the network. Together with NRTS, this positions us as the largest provider of fixed connectivity
services for both IT and Operational Technology services on behalf of National Highways.

Rail Projects
From legacy assets to the latest technologies, we are driving outcome-based improvements for all stakeholders
through the delivery of solutions and services that help underpin the decarbonisation of railways and deliver
improvements in operational performance, network resilience and safety.
Our breadth of activity includes everything from station communications (and associated technologies), end-
to-end operational communications (from transmission networks to radio systems), digital communications, and
software applications that control the traction power through to station management.
For Network Rail, with our Traction Power Control Management System, we are driving greater reliability and
efficiency in the management of electrical assets while improving the safety of rail colleagues through our
remote isolation application. Over the next year, we will continue the rollout of this technology to 13 of the 15
regions, covering the majority of the electrified rail network in England, whilst continuing to support the
applications we have developed.
This year we completed the complex transformation of Transport for Greater Manchester's core fibre-optic
network that underpins its Metrolink Tram operations. The new Metrolink Core Network (MCN) replaced the
legacy network and was fully tested & commissioned over a 3 month period without disruption to tram services
or the 45 million passengers that use the network annually.
We continue to be Network Rail's only third-party fibre maintainer. Building on this skillset, we are collaborating
with the industry to provide support for the third-party communication initiatives championed by Network Rail
and the UK Government.
We continue to innovate and are proud to continue to be an integral part of the Rail industry Association's
Unlocking Innovation programme alongside Network Rail and UK Rail Research and Innovation Network.
Within the Operational Communications market, we continue to revolutionise the surveying and design of fibre
and radio networks. Today we are delivering faster, as well as more safely and sustainably than ever before.
By utilising train mounted cameras that capture HD video of the railway, machine learning and digital mapping
tools have reduced the need to physically walk the track at night by 80%. This approach delivers more accurate
and detailed outputs while reducing our carbon footprint.
We were again recognised by Young Rail Professionals as leading employer in the UK Rail sector, by securing
Employer of the Year for the second consecutive year.
in the year ahead we aim to expand our Light Rail customer base, introduce mobile coverage to 23 railway
tunnels and secure our first Maritime (Ports) customer.

Section 172 statement
This Section of the strategic report has been adopted from the Group. The Directors believe that the disclosures .
below are appropriate to understand the range of factors set out in section 172 (a)-(f).
Section 172 (1) of the Companies Act 2006 requires each Director of a company to act in the way he or she
considers, in good faith, would most likely promote the success of the company for the benefit of its members
as a whole but having regard to a range of factors set out in section 172 (a)-(f) in the Companies Act 2006. In
discharging our section 172 (1) duty, we have regard for these factors taking them into consideration when
decisions are made.
In addition, we also have regard to other factors which we consider relevant to the decisions being made.
- Those factors for example include the interest and views of our clients and their end users, regulatory bodies,
and our relationship with our lenders. We delegate authority for day-to-day management of the Company to
the Executive Directors and engage management in setting, approving and overseeing execution of the
business strategy and related policies.
What our stakeholders have told us matters most to them
Effective engagement of stakeholder groups supports the principles of Section 172 (1) of the Companies Act
2006, which sets out that Directors should have regard to stakeholder interests when discharging their duty to
promote the success of the company. Our success depends on forging positive relationships with the people,
communities and organisations that have an interest in our business and may be impacted by the decisions we
make. The Directors identify principal decisions as those with significant long-term implications for the Group
and its stakeholders, distinct from routine business decisions.
People

performance can be delivered through a high level of engagement ensuring our people share the Company's

actions and long-term people strategies.
