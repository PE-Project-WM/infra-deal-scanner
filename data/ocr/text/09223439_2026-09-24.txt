Principal activity
The principal activities of the Company during 2025 were the sale of Starlink satellite broadband
telecommunications equipment and services, the finalisation of the disposal of SkyMesh Pty Limited
("SkyMesh’”), its former Australian subsidiary and maintenance of its continuing investments in SKM
Telecommunication Services Pty Ltd ("SKM") and QCL TopCo Limited and QCL MidCo Limited
(together "Quickline” or “QCL”).
Review of business
FY2025 was a year of significant change for the Company, with the disposal of SkyMesh, alongside
broader strategic restructuring initiatives aimed at simplifying the business, strengthening liquidity,
reducing debt exposure, and returning value to shareholders. The business repositioned itself away
from a traditional operating model towards a more focused investment structure centred on retained
interests in both SKM and QCL, combined with the continued development of its Starlink distribution
activities.
The most significant strategic event at the start of the year was the completion of the SkyMesh disposal
on 23 December 2024 to SKM, an acquisition vehicle established by Salter Brothers Asset
Management, for an total consideration of up to AUD$50:20 million, equivalent to approximately £25.03
million. The transaction marked a major milestone for the Company and fundamentally reshaped its
balance sheet and required operational framework.
The structure of the disposal consideration reflected both immediate liquidity generation and longer-
term value participation. the Company receiving an initial cash payment of AUD$30.0 million,
representing approximately £14.96 million or 59.8% of the total headline value. In addition, the Company
received 13.3 million shares in SKM, equating to roughly 26.5% of the transaction value, allowing the
Company to retain meaningful exposure to future upside within the Australian broadband market.
Deferred consideration up to a maximum amount of AUD$6.88 million, approximately £3.43mwas also
payable after one year, which was to be adjusted for Pathfinder Implementation Debts not recovered
and associated costs, alongside profit-linked and performance related adjustments.
The transaction materially improved the Company's financial position. Proceeds from the disposal
enabled the Company to fully repay its outstanding Santander credit facility, substantially reducing
leverage and improving balance sheet flexibility. The strengthened liquidity position also allowed
management to pursue capital return initiatives as outlined below while continuing to support the
Company's remaining strategic investments and operations.
A shareholder return programme was subsequently implemented through a tender offer which
completed in April 2025. The tender offer process received overwhelming shareholder support with
99.994% of votes cast in favour at the General Meeting held on 19 March 2025, where the resolutions
authorising both the capital reduction and market purchase of shares were approved. This exceptionally
high approval level reflected strong investor alignment with management's restructuring strategy and
confidence in the Board’s direction following the disposal. Under this initiative, the Company returned
approximately £6.1 million to shareholders through the repurchase of 15.25 million ordinary shares at
40 pence per share. The tender represented approximately 26% of the Company’s issued share capital
prior to the transaction and demonstrated management's commitment to disciplined capital allocation
following the SkyMesh disposal. Taken together with the c.£26.2 million returned to ordinary
shareholders in a prior period through the cash redemption of bonus B shares, total capital returned to
shareholders since inception amounts to approximately £32.3 million, equivalent to approximately 55
pence per ordinary share based on the ¢.58.8 million ordinary shares in issue prior to the tender offer,
or approximately 74 pence per ordinary share based on the c.43.6 million ordinary shares in issue
following completion of the tender offer.

In November 2025 the Company announced that it was in discussions with SKM regarding the
performance of SkyMesh post its disposal by the Company. Those discussions were concluded at the
date of these financial statements. The Company had previously indicated that the outcome of the
‘ negotiations with SKM could impact not only whether the Company was entitled to any deferred
consideration but could also impose material financial obligations on the Company to SKM. Post
significant financial support from several BBB sharehoiders, the Company engaged a team of UK and
Australian specialised advisors, lawyers and technical experts, as the Company disputed both the
rationale for such a claim and the underlying merit in the numbers. Given the material retained stake
the Company has in SKM, it was important that an appropriate and timely resolution was reached in the
interests of both the Company and SKM shareholders. On 30 June 2026, the Company concluded a
Deed of Settlement and Release with SKM and Salter Brothers Asset Management Pty Ltd, which
resulted in the withdrawal of all dispute notices and mutual releases, and cash consideration to the
Company of AUD$4.2 million payable in three instalments (AUD$2.0 million by 3 July 2026, AUD$1.0
million by 31 December 2026 and AUD$1.2 million by 31 December 2027), together with a retained
holding of 5,328,232 SKM shares, board representation and a contingent AUD$5.0 million entitlement
on a qualifying change of control. The Company fully recognises the pragmatic approach taken by the
SKM Board and recognise the importance of fully supporting the SKM Board and business through its
next growth chapter. The Company also recognises the strength of the SKM Board and will continue to
support them in any way possible to help realise value for all shareholders. The Company will continue
to keep shareholders updated on developments in SKM.
The Quickline investment remained strategically important during the year. The Company held a 2.81%
equity interest (2.20% fully diluted for warrants issued) in QCL TopCo Limited with a carrying value of
£2.2 million, together with 4.5% unsecured B Loan Notes 2041 issued by QCL MidCo Limited carried at
approximately £4.1 million as at 30 November 2025. These loan notes generated recurring accrued
interest income of around £15,000 per month. Despite very challenging market conditions Quickline
~ remains fully funded and have now passed over 250,000 full fibre premises.
The distribution of Starlink was the Company’s principal business activity in the period. Post the disposal
of Skymesh we undertook a detailed review of the operations and with support from Starlink it was
agreed that the contracts should be novated to Brdy Telecom which occurred on 30 April 2026 by way
of an Asset Purchase Agreement (“APA”). The basis of the APA was total cash consideration of £0.4m,
of which £0.27m had been received by completion (a £0.02m deposit received in advance and £0.25m
on completion) and £0.13m deferred until October 2026. Under the Novation the minimum purchase
commitment, all ongoing operational costs and all cross guarantees transferred to the buyer, and the
Company is indemnified against the liabilities of the transferred business without limitation of time. The
Company also retains 10% of net proceeds on a qualifying trigger event within 12 months, together with
Adjusted EBITDA milestone payments, being 10% of the Adjusted EBITDA generated during the Trigger
Event Period, being the 12 months from completion on 30 April 2026 to 30 April 2027, where that
Adjusted EBITDA is £0.1m or more, and 10% of the Adjusted EBITDA generated during the following
12 months to 30 April 2028 where that Adjusted EBITDA is £0.2m or more, each payable within six
months of the end of the relevant period.
Post the disposal of Skymesh and a strategic review undertaken the New Zealand business was placed
into liquidation by special resolution of its shareholders on 15 November 2025 and the liquidators issued
their final report as at 1 May 2026. There were no realisations of substance and no distribution to
creditors, and the closure removed any further costs or exposure for the Company and on-going monthly
costs were reduced by circa £4k per month. While this may reduce operational diversification, it supports
the broader strategy of improving efficiency and preserving shareholder value.
Cost control and operational simplification were recurring strategic themes throughout the period. The
Company undertook streamlining operations, reduced corporate complexity, and aligned expenditure
with its reduced investment footprint following the disposals. These initiatives were particularly important
given the Company’s significantly smaller market capitalisation and transition away from being a large-
scale operating broadband provider, with central costs decreasing from an FY25 average of £75k per

By late 2025, the Company’s market capitalisation was £8.1 million, reflecting both the scale reduction
following the SkyMesh disposal and the market's reassessment of the Company's revised business
model. Despite the smaller valuation, the Company retained meaningful strategic assets, including
investment holdings and cash-generative positions, which management remains focused on monetising
over time. Given the reduced scale of operations and costs in FY2025, a decision was made to cancel
the Company's AIM listing and to re-register the Company as a private company. The Board formally
announced the proposed cancellation in November 2025, with shareholder approval sought through a
General Meeting on 8 December 2025. The cancellation became effective on 18 December 2025, with
private company re-registration. The rationale for the proposed delisting was closely linked to the
Company's reduced operational scale and changing strategic priorities following the SkyMesh
transaction. Management effectively concluded that the costs and regulatory burden associated with
maintaining a public market listing were increasingly disproportionate relative to the Company's size,
simplified structure, and investment-led focus. Following cancellation of admission to trading on AIM on
18 December 2025 the Company’s shares are no longer publicly quoted; a matched bargain facility is
provided through JP Jenkins.
The Company's strategic repositioning going forward is focussed on the retained stakes in SKM and
Quickline, which provide exposure to potential future value creation without requiring the same
operational intensity as direct ownership.
However, the restructuring also introduces notable risks. The Company is now materially smaller and
more concentrated, with performance increasingly dependent on a limited number of strategic holdings.
The success of the retained SKM and Quickline interests, have a disproportionately large impact on
shareholder outcomes.
Liquidity and marketability risks may also increase following the AIM cancellation. While the transition
to a private company structure has reduced costs and administrative burdens, it simultaneously removes
the benefits of public market liquidity, ttansparency, and investor accessibility. Existing shareholders
may therefore face reduced ability to trade holdings or independently establish market value after the

: Company’s delisting.

Overall, FY2025 represented a decisive inflection point for the Company. Management executed a
major disposal, materially strengthened the balance sheet, eliminated debt, returned significant capital
to shareholders, simplified operations, and repositioned the business around a smaller number of
strategic investments. The Company now enters its next phase as a more focused and potentially lower-
cost organisation, albeit one carrying greater concentration risk and increased reliance on successful
execution within a narrower strategic framework.
We are profoundly grateful for the steadfast stewardship during the period of our Chairman, Michael

. Tobin CBE, and Non-Executive Directors Paul Howard and Philip Moses, whose expert guidance has
been instrumental throughout this period of significant transformation. We are also very appreciative of
the financial support from significant shareholders during this period and our banking partner Santander.
