Strategic Report
The Directors present their Strategic Report of the Company for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Business Review
The Company has a profit for the financial year of £1,798,000 (loss for 2024: £125,000). The Directors do not recommend the
payment of any dividends (2024: £nil)
The Company's key financial and other performance indicators during the year were as follows:

The company’s turnover decreased by 3.23% to £66.5m, reflecting movements in partner revenue and customer utilisation of
mobile services. Gross profit decreased to £5.5m as cost of sales remained elevated due to market conditions, although this
remained broadly in line with the Directors’ expectations. Despite a reduction in turnover and gross profit, profit before tax
increased to £3.3m, principally due to lower administrative expenses, with favourable foreign exchange movements
representing the primary driver, alongside reduced purchased service volumes. However, profit for the financial year remained
below its full potential, and the Directors continue to monitor administrative cost pressures closely while maintaining
investment in the Company’s core service capabilities.
Carrier Billing, an interface developed by the Company, continues as a key contributor to the company's overall growth as the
use of apps, digital goods and services available to purchase from app stores increases with the use of Smartphones and the
convenience and ease of use which the service delivers. The Company continues to maintain its sales pipeline of services to
the core Deutsche Telekom AG (DTAG) operating companies. The cross charging of the aforementioned carrier billing
services and intergroup professional services to Deutsche Telekom headquarters also matches with the Company's plans.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The Company is exposed to a variety of risks, primarily market and economic risk. Given the nature of the business,
macroeconomic impacts experienced by consumers can lead to a change in usage behaviour adversely affecting services
provided by Deutsche Telekom (UK) Limited (DTUK) to DTAG's operating companies such as Carrier Billing. This necessitates
market leading and highly cost-effective products and services produced by DTUK. It is therefore necessary to continually
develop and adapt new products and services that can support these operating companies in tough economic times.
Delivery Risks
The Company's primary business is to support intragroup companies with service and platform offerings, thus limiting its
external exposure, however the Company is exposed to changes in services from its partners and technology changes in the
market. The Company manages the risks through close interworking with Deutsche Telekom AG group and agreeing
adaptations or shifts in product and service delivery with our internal providers and external partners. The Company adapts
its contracts and revenue agreements as required to accommodate the adaptations and the market needs as applicable.

Market and Economic Risk
Various scenarios are modelled to establish the financial outcome on a worst-case basis of deterioration in the markets in
which we operate. Specifically, the main factors are partner service offering alterations, trends, foreign exchange, inflation and
interest rates.
Financial Risk Management
The Company manages its own cash deposits with financial institutions after consideration of advice from Deutsche Telekom
AG Group Treasury on the credit rating and overalt financial profile of those financial institutions. The Company's primary
transactional currency is Sterling. Due to the nature of the Company's business the Company has an exposure to foreign
currency risk. In order to manage the risk of exposure to the variability of foreign exchange rates on transactions in other
currencies, the Company is monitoring the affected cash deposits on a regular basis and foreign currency deposits are
converted into Sterling on a regular basis. .
Post Balance Sheet Events
The Directors are not aware of any other post balance sheet events that would impact the 2025 financial statements.
Going Concern
At the balance sheet date, the Company has net current assets of £27,536,000 (2024: £24,283,000). The balance mainly
consists of receivables owed by Group undertakings from day-to-day trading reflecting rolling balances with standard group
invoicing repayment terms, Following a review by the Directors these assets are expected to cover any future liabilities payable
in the next 12 months from the approval of these financial statements. The Company has a letter of financial support from the
parent company Deutsche Telekom AG and will rely upon its support.
The Directors do not expect any obligations to arise for at least 12 months from the approval of the financial statements, other
than the Company's own direct obligations. With this expectation and positive net assets, the Board of Directors consider that
it remains appropriate for the Company's financial statements to be prepared on a going concern basis.

2.7 Impairment of non-financial assets
Non-financial assets not ready to use are not subject to amortisation and are tested annually for impairment.
Assets that are subject to amortisation are reviewed for impairment whenever events or changes in
. circumstances indicate that the carrying amount may not be recoverable. An impairment loss is recognised for
the amount by which the asset’s carrying amount exceeds its recoverable amount. The recoverable amount is
the higher of an asset's fair value less costs of disposal and value in use. For the purposes of assessing
impairment, assets are grouped at the lowest levels for which there are largely independent cash inflows (cash-
generating units), Prior impairments of non-financial assets (other than goodwill) are reviewed for possible
reversal at each reporting date.
2.8 Fixed assets investment
Investment in subsidiary is held at cost less accumulated impairment losses. The Company assesses at the end
of each reporting period whether there is objective evidence that a financial asset or group of financial assets
is impaired. A financial asset or a group of financial assets is impaired and impairment losses are incurred only
if there is objective evidence of impairment as a result of one or more events that occurred after the initial
recognition of the asset (a loss event) and that loss event (or events) has an impact on the estimated future cash
flows of the financial asset or group of financial assets that can be reliably estimated.
2.9 Financial assets

not at fair value through profit of loss (‘FVTPL’), transaction costs. Transaction costs of financial assets at FVTPL

Deferred income tax is recognised on temporary differences arising between the tax bases of assets and

income taxes levied by the same taxation authority on either the same taxable entity or different taxable entities
where there is an intention to settle the balances on a net basis.
