REVIEW OF BUSINESS
The results for the year and financial position of the company are shown in the financial statements pages 14
and 15. The Company is the principal trading subsidiary of the Arria NLG Limited group of companies.
PRINCIPAL ACTIVITIES
The principal activities of the Company are the development of intellectual property in the field of Artificial
Intelligence (“Ai”) known as Natural Language Generation (“NLG”) in the form of software and software-as-a-
service technologies. Arria NLG Limited (Arria or Arria NZ) owns, develops, and licenses its core, patented NLG
technologies, which are made available via the Arria NLG Platform, NLG-driven products and NLG services to its
clients and partners. The Company is the main trading entity of the group, which sells the Arria NLG products to
clients.
STRATEGY FOR REVENUE AND AMRR GROWTH BEYOND 2022

driving ambition to be the global leader in the development and deployment of mission critical, core industrial,

as the global standard for NLG so that one day soon every interaction people have with their devices will
incorporate Arria’s NLG capabilities.

The principal risks and uncertainties to which the Group is exposed to will also impact the company:
Dependence on equity and debt financing
The Group is dependent on investors to fund its operations. Further information on the Group’s reliance in the near
term on attracting additional equity and loan investment is included in the Directors’ Report in the section on Going

Cybersecurity risks
The Group and its subsidiaries rely on certain key systems and technologies to ensure continued operation, exposing
the Company to a significant risk as the systems are vulnerable to damage or interruption from a variety of sources
including, but not limited to, computer viruses, data breaches and computer intrusions and attacks. Furthermore,
employees or contractors, as well as third parties, may break in, sabotage or vandalise the Group’s systems or .
misappropriate information including information of clients and their users. Any unexpected data breach, malfunction
or attack on systems or technologies may have a materia! detrimental effect to the Company's business, results of
operation and financial condition.
Utigation alleging infringement of intellectual property rights
The Group’s business and prospects may be seriously harmed if it is unable to adequately protect and enforce its
intellectual property rights or avoid actions by others to enforce their rights. To date, no such claims have been
presented by or against the Company or its subsidiaries.
The Group's business and prospects are highly dependent on its intellectual property, primarily the NLG technologies
protected by withholding source code, imposing restrictive covenants and assignment of inventions on employees and
consultants, asserting copyright in software and building a portfolio of US patents on key innovations. Efforts the
Company makes to develop and protect exclusionary intellectual property rights and to avoid running afoul of the
exclusionary rights of others may not be successful. The Group may not be able to identify all unauthorised uses or
take effective action against infringement, and it may inadvertently infringe the exclusionary rights of others. As a
result, the Group or its subsidiaries may assert or become subject to litigation and claims, including claims of
misappropriation of trade secrets or infringement of patents, copyrights, trademarks, and other intellectual property
rights of third parties that would be time-consuming and costly to resolve and could lead to unfavourable judgments
or settlements.
Functional currency :
The Company has determined its functional! currency as US dollars (USD). This is a change from Sterling as reported in
2019 to align with financing provided by ultimate parent Arria NLG Limited. An assessment was performed by
managementto determine that the functional currency change has no material impact on the presentation of financial
statements for the Company.
Brexit
The UK’s decision to leave the EU is likely to result in a short to medium term period of economic and political
uncertainty and complexity. There is a risk that this uncertainty could reduce demand in the Group’s UK market and
have a negative impact on foreign exchange transactions. The Company, having considered the Brexit review believes
the Group’s strategy, geographical diversification and primarily raising capital in USD will provide resilience until the
outcome and wider impact of Brexit is determined.

Going concern
Notwithstanding net current liabilities of $ 35,974 k as at 30 September 2020 and a loss for the year then ended
of $ 12,632 k, the financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis which the directors consider
to be appropriate for the following reasons. :
The Company relies on funds provided to it by its ultimate parent, Arria NLG Limited (‘Group’) which will continue
to provide financial and other support to the extent necessary to enable the Company to continue to trade and
meet its financial obligations for the foreseeable future and for at least twelve months from the date of the
approval of the audit report for the financial statements for the year ended 30 September 2020.
As at 30 September 2021, Arria NLG Limited incurred a loss of $55.2 million and used cash of $20.7 million in
operating activities. The Group is forecasting to continue incurring losses and have net cash outflows from
operations as the business develops, continues to invest in its products and seeks to generate revenue from its
existing and upcoming products. The Group had cash available of $ 27 million as at 30 September 2023 but will
require additional funding in the 12 months from the date of this report which has not been secured.
However, the intention of Arria NLG Limited to continue to provide this support is dependent on successful
undertaking of an Initial Public Offering (‘IPO’) to raise funds to enable the Group to continue its activities and
operations and support Arria Data2Text. In the current economic environment, there may be some uncertainty
as to whether the IPO will be completed, which could also impact the cashflows of the Group. In the event that
the IPO does not proceed or does not raise sufficient funds at the required time, the Group will need to secure
alternative funding.
Based on these indications the directors believe that it remains appropriate to prepare the financial statements
ona going concern basis. However, due to the uncertainty in relation to the outcome of the IPO and in the event
that Group does not raise the required funding, whether the Group will be able to secure sufficient alternative
funding, indicates the existence of a material uncertainty related to events or conditions that may cast significant
doubt on the company’s ability to continue as a going concern and, therefore, that the company may be unable
to realise its assets and discharge its liabilities in the normal course of business. The financial statements do not
include any adjustments that would result from the basis of preparation being inappropriate.

of $ 12,632 k, the financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis which the directors consider

to be appropriate for the following reasons.

The Company relies on funds provided to it by its ultimate parent, Arria NLG Limited (‘Group’) which will continue
. to provide financial and other support to the extent necessary to enable the Company to continue to trade and

meet its financial obligations for the foreseeable future and for at least twelve months from the date of the

approval of the audit report for the financial statements for the year ended 30 September 2020.

As at 30 September 2021, Arria NLG Limited incurred a loss of $55.2 million and used cash of $20.7 million in

operating activities. The Group is forecasting to continue incurring losses and have net cash outflows from

operations as the business develops, continues to invest in its products and seeks to generate revenue from its

existing and upcoming products. The Group had cash available of $ 27 million as at 30 September 2021 but will

require additional funding in the 12 months from the date of this report which has not been secured.

However, the intention of Arria NLG Limited to continue to provide this support is dependent on successful

undertaking of an Initial Public Offering (‘IPO’) to raise funds to enable the Group to continue its activities and

operations and support Arria Data2Text. In the current economic environment, there may be some uncertainty

as to whether the IPO will be completed, which could also impact the cashflows of the Group. in the event that

the IPO does not proceed or does not raise sufficient funds at the required time, the Group will need to secure

alternative funding.

Based on these indications the directors believe that it remains appropriate to prepare the financial statements

ona going concern basis. However, due to the uncertainty in relation to the outcome of the IPO and in the event

that Group does not raise the required funding, whether the Group will be able to secure sufficient alternative

funding, indicates the existence of a material uncertainty related to events or conditions that may cast significant

. doubt on the company’s ability to continue as a going concern and, therefore, that the company may be unable

to realise its assets and discharge its liabilities in the normal course of business. The financial statements do not

include any adjustments that would result from the basis of preparation being inappropriate.
