Strategic Report .
For the Year Ended 31 December 2025
The directors are pleased to present their Strategic Report for the company for the year ended 31
December 2025.
Principal activities .
myPOS Payments Ltd (the “Company”) is an electronic money institution authorised and
regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) UK to provide e-money and payment
services, under the Electronic Money Regulations 2011 (“EMRs”).
myPOS Payments Ltd is a wholly owned subsidiary of myPOS World Ltd and a part of a wider
myPOS Group. The Group is a leading provider of point-of-sale payment acquiring and
technology-related payment solutions to small and medium sized merchants across the European
Economic Area (“EEA”), the UK and Switzerland. :
Business review
Review of the business
Under its e-money license, myPOS Payments Ltd continued to provide account related services to
its customers during the course of the year. myPOS Payments has direct membership with the
card schemes and has been providing issuing, acquiring and account related services to its UK,
Gibraltar and Swiss customers directly.
The Company made an operating profit of €30,977 with a pre-tax loss of €205,423 after finance
costs and one-off costs relating to a reorganisation, compared to a pre-tax loss of €2,996,923 in
the prior year. The loss in 2024 was primarily driven by a one-off bonus of €3,241,568 paid to key
staff as part of acompletion bonus relating to the acquisition of myPOS Group by AI Circle Bidco
Limited in 2024. The loss in current year was driven by reorganisation casts relating to one off
: employee payments. :
Turnover was €18,275,076 (2024: €14,778,626}, 2 23.65% incrcasc compared to prior ycar. This
increase reflects the fact that the company has continued to grow steadily during the year, gaining
fnew customers, improving pricing strategies and realising the benefits of direct memberships with
the card schemes, which lead to growth in revenue, gross profit and net assets. The directors of
the Company are satisfied with the Company's performance for the period. The Company has
achieved the majority of its strategic objectives and continues to operate in an effective manner.
The net asset position of the Company is €3,415,118 (2024: €3,700,197). Current assets have
increased from €49,491,343 to €59,652,681, an increase of 20.53% or €10,161,338. Of this,
€7,830,953 relates to an increase in cash and cash equivalents due to acquisition of new customers
during the year. Additionally, there was also an increase in trade and other receivables amounting
to €2,349,906 mainly due to an increase in receivables from the card schemes at year end.
At the year end, total number of active accounts with the Company stands at 33,630 (2024: 19,170).
The average number of employees of the Company for the year is 12 (2024: 21). .

and industry leading standards.

Regulatory and Compiiance Risk :
The Company operates under strict regulatory frameworks from the FCA. Key areas of focus
include Anti-Money Laundering and Financial Crime, Safeguarding of Customer Funds, Consumer
Protection and Regulatory Reporting. Compliance failures could lead to financial penalties,
reputational damage, or restrictions on operations. The Company maintains robust policies,
monitoring mechanisms, and compliance training to ensure adherence to regulatory requirements.
Counterparty and Key Stakeholder Risk
The Company relies on strategic relationships with banks, card schemes and key partners to
Operate its business. Failure to meet stakeholder expectations or comply with contractual
obligations could result in service disruptions, financial penalties, or loss of critical partnerships.
The Company proactively engages with stakeholders and monitors adherence to requirements.
Operations and Technology Risk
The complexity of the Company's operations, combined with digital transformation initiatives,
presents risks related to system resilience, data integrity, third-party dependencies, and change
management. The Company continuously strengthens its technology infrastructure, operational

Company faces increasing threats from cyberattacks, data breaches, and information security risks.
It continuously enhances its security controls, invests in cybersecurity infrastructure, and adheres
to regulatory requirements to safeguard customer and company data.

Financial instrument risks ,

Liquidity risk :
The Company manages its cash and borrowing requirements in order to maximise interest income
and minimise interest expense, whilst ensuring the Company has sufficient liquid resources to meet
the operating needs of the business. The Company does not consider it has a high level of liquidity
risk in view of the level of capitalisation required by the FCA and the policy of the Directors is to
not to take on obligations unless there is a source of finance to satisfy those obligations. The
Company has implemented systems and controls such as cash flow forecasting, budgeting, liquidity
management and improvement in pricing strategies to mitigate such risks.

Foreign currency risk

The Company’s principal foreign currency exposures arise from trading with overscas companies.
Company policy permits but does not demand that these exposures be hedged in order to fix the
cost in Euro.

Credit risk

At present the Company’s primary credit risk is with its trade debtors and balances with banks. .
The Directors have assessed this risk and consider it to be at a low level in view of the financial
strength of the counterparties.
