Review of business
Headquartered in Hull, MS3 Networks Limited ("MS3") is a wholesale Fibre to the Premises ("FTTP”)
developer and operator. Operating within a uniquely competitive market, MS3 has established itself
as an important regional connectivity provider, challenging a long-standing loca! near-monopoly.
Following a decade of B2B focus, the Company secured investment in 2021 to support the rollout of
a large-scale residential fibre network, which now covers the majority of the city of Hull. Hull occupies
a distinctive position within the UK telecommunications landscape, with limited BT Openreach
availability and a single incumbent provider, KCOM, maintaining a near monopoly over broadband
services in the area.

f investment from Asterion Industrial Infra Fund Il. By year end, the network extended across five

8 Share-based payments
Certain employees of the company were issued 420,000 B shares on 23 December 2021. These
B shares are subject to employment conditions, including a requirement to sell the shares back
to the company at the lower of fair value or nominal value should the employees choose to leave
employment prior to the end of the vesting period. The employment conditions cease upon the
earlier of an exit event or 31 December 2027. Should the employees remain to the end of the
vesting period, they can benefit as a shareholder from any exit event.
These B shares are accounted for as share-based payments.
