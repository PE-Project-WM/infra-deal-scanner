Strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2026
Principal activities and review of the business, including subsequent events
Principal activities
Historically, the principal activity of the Company was the development of low carbon electricity
generation facilities, which currently includes the development of its land holdings at Wylfa towards the
construction and operation of a new nuclear power plant. However, as a result of issues relating to the
raising of finance for the construction of Horizon’s first power station, Wylfa Newydd, on Anglesey, the
Group in January 2019 suspended the majority of its activities. Subsequently, in September 2020,
following an announcement by Hitachi, Limited (the Ultimate Shareholder) that it would cease its
backing for Horizon entirely and withdraw from the UK nuclear market, the Company has now ceased
all its activities to develop its project at Wylfa Newydd on Anglesey.
On 28 June 2024 the Company completed the sale of its site to Great British Nuclear (GBN), and as
part of the sale the Company agreed to support GBN (which was subsequently renamed Great British
Energy — Nuclear (GBE-N) with estate and site management services from 28 June 2024 until 31
December 2025. This was completed on 31 December 2025 and the Company will now be retained
until 30 June 2027 when warranties on the sale expire.
. Review of business

In the year ended 31 March 2026 the Company continued to support GBE-N with the provision of
transitional services under a Transitional Service Agreement (TSA) with GBE-N. Under this agreement
the Company was obliged to provide estate and site management services to GBE-N for the period
from completion to 31 December 2025. The TSA ended on 31 December 2025 as intended.
Following the completion of the TSA the Company undertook the following activities.

e Its site vehicle was sold and all assets were removed from the Company’s fixed asset register.

e The Company applied to HMRC to deregister from the Construction industry Scheme (CIS)

and Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings (ATED).
The completion of the TSA with GBE-N resulted in a change in trade for tax purposes.
In February 2026 the Company issued a dividend of £123,910k to its shareholder Horizon Nuclear
Power Wylfa Holdings Limited (the Shareholder). The cash came from the proceeds of the land asset
sale to GBE-N in June 2024 plus accrued interest from cash pooling facility with Hitachi Europe Limited.
Strategy
Following completion of the TSA with GBE-N, the Company will be retained until 30 June 2027 when
warranties on the sale expire. It is the Ultimate Shareholder’s intention that following expiry of the
warranty period, the Company will then be liquidated.

Current and deferred income tax
The current income tax charge is measured on the basis of tax laws enacted or substantively enacted
at the reporting period end date in the United Kingdom. Management periodically evaluate positions
taken in tax returns with respect to situations in which applicable tax regulation is subject to
interpretation. It establishes provisions where appropriate on the basis of amounts expected to be paid
to the tax authorities.
Deferred income tax is recognised, using the liability method, on temporary differences arising between
the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements. However,
the deferred income tax is not accounted for if it arises from initial recognition of an asset or liability in
a transaction other than a business combination that at the time of the transaction affects neither
