the Companies Act 2006 (Strategic Report and Directors' Report) Regulations 2013. :
Principal activities :
Twilio provides software and cloud-based communications to enable developers and businesses to rapidly
build and deploy communication solutions that meet their specific needs. ,
The principal activities of Twilio UK Limited (the "company") are the provision of marketing services and
developing brand awareness of Twilio in the United Kingdom. The company also provides intra group research
and development services.
: Review of the business
“ The results for the year and state of affairs of the company are set out on pages 14 and 15. The profit on
ordinary activities after taxation amounted to £1,086,083 . This amount has been transferred to reserves.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties of the company are intrinsically linked to the other Twilio group companies.
The company has no significant borrowings. :
Cash flow risk, credit risk, liquidity risk and market risk
Cash flow risk
Financial risk of changes in foreign currency exchanges are mitigated by short payment terms by Twilio Ireland
Limited and access to their cash pool where required. The company does not use derivative financial
instruments for speculative purposes or to hedge risks. Cash flow risk is largely mitigated due to arrangements
in place with its immediate parent company Twilio Ireland Limited, a company incorporated in the Republic of
Ireland.
: Credit risk
: The company's credit risk is primarily attributable to its intercompany receivable balance with Twilio Ireland
Limited. This is not considered a risk due to the solvency and liquidity of Twilio Ireland Limited.
Liquidity risk
In order to maintain liquidity to ensure that sufficient funds are available for ongoing operations and future
developments, the company relies on support from Twilio Ireland Limited if required.
Market risk
The company's long-term success is dependent on its ability to attract and retain talented employees. The
company continues to invest in attracting and retaining high-caliber individuals.

Financial key performance indicators
The company's financial KPI for measuring success is revenue growth and the non-financial KPI for
measuring success is number of employees.
The company's business activities expanded during 2025. Turnover increased by 5% from £ 54,388,047 in
2024 to £ 56,937,816 in 2025. Under the company's cost-plus operating model, revenue is directly aligned
with overall expenditure. This growth in revenue reflects a corresponding increase in operating expenses,

Twilio has a science-based greenhouse gas emissions reduction target validated by the SBTi. The company
