Introduction
Tela Technology Limited ("Tela") is one of the UK’s leading converged unified communications providers and is
amongst a select few businesses that is and continues to successfully diversify into the managed technology
services arena from the mobile centric space. It is well placed to capitalise on the wealth of up sell and cross sell
IT, Security, Connectivity, Hosted Telephony, 5G, and IOT (Internet-of-Things) opportunities in future years.
Established in April 2012 (with a rich pedigree of over 37 years), Tela is one of 3 ‘Vodafone Total
Communications Key Partners’ and one of its Top 3 ‘Strategic Partners’, having won the Communications
Business Digital Transformational Solution award in 2017. Tela has also won the Unified Communications
Partner of the Year 2013 award and has been twice in the Sunday Times Technology Track Fastest Growing UK
100 Businesses (61 in 2013 and 82 in 2012) having completed 15 acquisitions in the last ten years.
Tela has excellent partnerships with Ringcentral, Xelion, Samsung, Google, Gamma, ITS, Microsoft, Bitdefender
and a whole host of other stakeholders. Furthermore, it has BSI Governmental accreditations in ISO 9001 and
27001 (Security) and supports businesses in their Cybersecurity accreditations. Tela is also regulated by the
FCA (Financial Conduct Authority) and has the Safe Contractor accreditation vital in Health and Safety.
Tela received the 2022 and 2023 Vodafone's independent “Customer Value Management” award in recognition :
of the proactive work carried out with new and long-standing customers. This is following Tela’s customer
obsessive and customer first approach recognised by Vodafone and achievement of an industry leading
Customer NPS (Net Promoter Score) of 89. Tela also received a Vodafone award in 2022 and 2023 for its
commitment to “Sustainability”, with its green, corporate responsibility, and ethical credentials in conjunction with :
transparent processes leading the way in the telecoms and IT industry. In 2024 and 2025 Tela achieved the
prestigious Vodafone & Ringcentral award of being the best partner in 2024 and 2025 with a 250% growth from a
standstill start. Tela also won Samsung Partner of the Year Award 2022 and in August 2023 won the Microsoft
Infinigate Modern Workplace partner of the year against stiff competition from significantly larger Managed
Service Partners (MSP’s). This truly demonstrates the values by which Tela has successfully risen amongst its
far larger peers to be a business respected by its competitors and revered by its customers.
Tela supports over 45 charities across the UK and continues to invest 5% of its EBITDA into organisations which
provide essential services to its communities across the UK. .
Tela transforms business performance by helping customers embrace current and emerging technologies.
Through our ‘Communication Made Simple’ solution, Tela aims to change the way people live, by changing the
way they work. Tela’s solutions and services empowers customers to be flexible by leveraging their investment in
IT and infrastructure, giving them the freedom to work effectively and efficiently, both in and out of the office. We
provide Mobility Consultation, IT Managed Software Solutions (e.g. Microsoft Office 365), Desktop Support,
Cybersecurity, |OT and Telecom's expertise across the UK on a 24 x 7 x 365-days basis. The business has
offices in London, Birmingham, Bromsgrove, Kidderminster, Worcester, Bristol, Warrington, Glasgow and
Aberdeen.

was at £23.5m (2024 - £21.7m) with annual gross profit of £10.8m (2024 - £9.0m) reflecting a gross profit margin

Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks for the Company going forward are perceived to be:
1) The Economic Climate
The Company has continued to demonstrate a strong and robust cash position year on year, with only
Shareholder debt and long-term customer provisions recorded as liabilities in the Balance Sheet. The Company
continues to write down Goodwill (non-cash item) from its historic acquisitions aggressively at circa £1m per
annum which has contributed to the continued Profit and loss account deficit and profit before tax. However, the
Company has strong processes and controls which will help to identify and mitigate any impact if there was to be
any further economic downturn. :
2) Lack of Diversification
The Company is continuing to invest in its Connectivity, Hosted Solutions VOIP business and IT division which
showed strong growth, and this is expected to continue accelerating. From its onset in 2012 the business has
transitioned from a 95% mobile mix to a 50% mobile and 50% other services mix. These new services have
offset the competitive margins in mobile. !T in 2026 will represent 25% of Tela’s turnover.
3) Regulatory Changes
The Company actively monitors regulatory development on an on-going basis and where applicable will
implement strategies and policies to address any commercial pressures or compliance requirements.
4) Embracing New Technologies ,
The speed of various advancements and customer expectations are regularly monitored by the Company. Tela
is able to change and adapt to these influences and offer new products to its diverse customer set across the
UK. We have pushed ahead with Microsoft Teams integration / collaboration, Co-Pilot and an Enterprise grade
Hosted Telephony system powered by Ringcentral and Vodafone known as Vodafone Business Unified Solution
(VBUC).

2.3 Foreign currency translation
Functional and presentation currency i
The Company's functional and presentational currency is GBP.
Transactions and balances
Foreign currency transactions are translated into the functional currency using the spot exchange
rates at the dates of the transactions.

: monetary items measured at historical cost are translated using the exchange rate at the date of the

Other financial assets
Other financial assets, which includes investments in equity instruments which are not classified as

Impairment of financial assets
At the end of each reporting period financial assets measured at amortised cost are assessed for
objective evidence of impairment. If an asset is impaired the impairment loss is the difference
between the carrying amount and the present value of the estimated cash flows discounted at the
asset's original effective interest rate. The impairment loss is recognised in profit or loss.
Financial assets are impaired when events, subsequent to their initial recognition, indicate the
estimated future cash flows derived from the financial asset(s) have been adversely impacted. The
impairment toss will be the difference between the current carrying amount and the present value of
the future cash flows at the asset(s) original effective interest rate.
If there is a favourable change in relation to the events surrounding the impairment loss then the
impairment can be reviewed for possible reversal. The reversal will not cause the current carrying
. amount to exceed the original carrying amount had the impairment not been recognised. The  .
impairment reversal is recognised in the profit or loss.
