Principal Activities
The principal activities of the Company are providing Secure Customer Engagement for Human and Al agents, through a
suite of patented products. Our products help protect sensitive customer data and can be utilised on any customer
engagement channel (e.g. live voice agent, self-service IVR, web chat, email, social media and Al bots) and using any device
the customer chooses.
In addition, our solutions, which enable our clients to ‘Engage, Secure and Transform’ their customers, are delivered
primarily through our multi-vendor cloud platforms and our own hosted platform. Eckoh’s products and solutions are
developed by our core development team based in the UK.

Financial risk management
