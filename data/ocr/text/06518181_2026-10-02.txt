GROUP STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025

The directors of BackOffice Associates Europe (UK) Limited acknowledge their duty to prepare a consolidated
strategic report for the year 2025, in accordance with Chapter 414A of the Companies Act of 2006. The report
covers the BackOffice Associates Europe (UK) Limited business which has operations in the EMEA (Europe,
Middle East & Africa) region. A list of the subsidiaries within the group is shown in note 12 of the financial
statements.
Fair review of business
BackOffice Associates Europe (UK) Limited, the "company", is a wholly owned subsidiary of BackOffice
Associates LLC which trades as Syniti, a limited liabitity company organised in the United States of America.
Syniti is a worldwide leader in information governance and data migration solutions. The company is an
investment holding company and with its subsidiaries, the “group” engages in the business of selling software,
developed internally or by affiliates, and selling and delivering consulting services. The group collaborates with
its affiliates in the Americas and the Asia Pacific region as part of a global organisation.
The group licenses its own and third party software under both term and perpetual licenses to resellers and end
user customers, and hosts software on a cloud based platform. The group's core platform is the Syniti
Knowledge Platform, encompassing data migration, data quality and master data management (the "Core
Products"). Core products are sold to enable the data aspects of clients’ overall digital transformation programs,
which frequently include implementation or upgrade of ERP platforms such as SAP®, integration or spin out of
mergers/acquisitions and divestitures, or overall data quality and master data management initiatives. The
group's products and services are sold directly to clients, through SAP or through large scale system
integrators. In addition to the Core Products, the group also sells Syniti Match software solution.
The group had 227 employees as of 31 December 2025, primarily billable consultants. This is an increase of 22
at year end from the prior year. To meet fluctuating demand, the group also deploys consultants who are
employed by other BackOffice entities, primarily from India and the United States.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The business is highly competitive. Large system integrators and small providers of similar goods and services
compete on price, terms, technology, delivery schedules, company reputation, and the specific nature of the
service offerings. The sales cycle tends to be long, and significant efforts can be incurred which do not result in
customer acquisition.
Projects involve many unknowns at the time of setting the contract price or commencing work. Negotiations
may result in fixed price contracts, milestone approvals, expense caps, or time and materials arrangements with
potential penalty clauses. In some cases, the group may commence work or may expand the scope of work
prior to obtaining formal approval of terms and conditions, resulting in significant delays between the time
resources are expended and receipt of customer payment. In all cases, delays or cost overruns may
unfavourably impact margins and cash flows.

, The group employs highly skilled consultants. The inability to hire and retain consultants may unfavourably
impact turnover. ~
Changing technology may influence customer demand or change the competitive position of the group,
requiring additional investment in product development, partnering with other companies, or adjusting schedules
and prices. Finally, the group conducts business in several currencies, primarily the Pound Sterling, the Euro,
the Swiss Franc and the US Dollar. In general, inflows of Swiss Francs and Euros exceed outflows, which are
primarily payroll and related costs denominated in Pound Sterling and Euros. As BackOffice Associates LLC
(the parent company) carefully manages its global cash positions, the group believes that its risk of loss due to
fluctuating exchange rates is minimal.

Key performance indicators and results
Key performance indicators used to measure results include turnover, gross margins and earnings before
interest, taxes, depreciation and amortisation (“EBITDA"). The group delivered turnover of £57.7 million versus
a 2024 amount of £44.6 million, an increase of 29%. Cost of sales increased from £29.6 million to £38.4 million
with project margins remained consistent at 33%. This margin level was primarily due to the continued use of
technical resources from lower salary cost countries and continued efficiency of utilisations. Consulting

: resources were deployed from within the group, from the U.S. parent company, its affiliates, and unrelated third
party subcontractors.
EBITDA as measured above has increased from £5.7 million profit in 2024 to a £8.9 million profit in 2025.
Measures of the financial condition of the group also include cash flow and other balance sheet movements.
The group continued to make significant efforts to manage its debtor balances during 2025, substantially
reducing its unbilled and overdue receivable amounts. The group also measures new customers won, project
profit margins, staff utilisation and new business bookings, which are leading indicators for our service business.
Future developments and other information
The group has planned growth in 2026. To support these initiatives, the company and its ultimate parent
company have made and will continue to make investments in sales personnel, marketing events, infrastructure
and supporting functions.

impairment can be reviewed for possible reversal. The reversal will not cause the current carrying
amount to exceed the original carrying amount had the impairment not been recognised. The
