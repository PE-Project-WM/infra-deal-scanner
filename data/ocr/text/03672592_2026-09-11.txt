Introduction
The Directors present their Strategic Report for FE Fundinfo Limited ("the Company") for the year ended 31
December 2025.
Business review and future developments
The principal activity of the Company is to provide services to subsidiaries of the FE Topco Limited consolidated
group ("the Group") whose purpose is to provide leading financial data to connect the investment industry in the
UK, Europe and Asia Pacific on one integrated platform. There are no plans to change the principal activity of the
Company. .

Evolving and emerging risks
UK businesses continue to face many macro uncertainties, including geopolitical events such as regional
conflicts and government changes across the world. These uncertainties continue to impact inflation, the future
trajectory of interest rates, supply chains, labour supply and how businesses structure and operate.
Other risks include the deployment of artificial intelligence within business operations and delivery of client
solutions. The technology is a market disrupter and determining how, when and where to adopt the technology
gives rise to a variety of risks, as well as opportunities.
These continue to be monitored when taking operational and strategic decisions. These risks are not adjusting
events and are considered as part of the Company's going concern assessment.
Risk Management
The Board of Directors and management follow established risk management processes that continually review
operational, financial and strategic risks.

Principal risks and uncertainties
The principal risks and uncertainties facing the Company are summarised below:
The Board of Directors and management follow established risk management processes that continually review
operational, financial and strategic risks.
Strategic risk - This is the risk of mismanaging our supplier relationships and not effectively completing due
diligence on business acquisitions. Supplier relationships are closely managed through supplier meetings and
prompt payment of supplier invoices. Ail prospective business acquisitions have established internal teams, as
well as third parties, in place to support diligence processes. This extends to dedicated integration teams post
completion of acquisitions.
Financial risk - This is the risk of having insufficient financial resources to meet obligations as they fall due or not
being able to pay suppliers on time. These risks are proactively managed on a regular basis through ensuring
regular settlement of intra group positions due to the Company and regular payment of suppliers.
People risk - People are critical to ensuring that quality services are provided on a timely basis to customers.
Retention of key staff, employee development and employee wellbeing are critical to ensuring the Company and
Group remains a place where employees wish to work. Employees are encouraged to share their views through
employment engagement surveys and regular town halls disseminate key Group information and celebrate the
success of employees. The Group also operates an employee learning academy to support personal
development and offers competitive incentive and benefit schemes to support retention. .

2.11 Share-based payments

Where share options are awarded to employees, the fair value of the options at the date of grant is
charged to the Statement of Comprehensive Income over the vesting period. Non-market vesting

each reporting date so that, ultimately, the cumulative amount recognised over the vesting period is

fair value of the options granted. The cumulative expense is not adjusted for failure to achieve a
market vesting condition.
The fair value of the award also takes into account non-vesting conditions. These are either factors
beyond the control of either party (such as a target based on an index) or factors which are within the
control of one or other of the parties (such as the Company keeping the scheme open or the
employee maintaining any contributions required by the scheme).
Where the terms and conditions of options are modified before they vest, the increase in the fair
value of the options, measured immediately before and after the modification, is also charged to profit
or loss over the remaining vesting period.
Where equity instruments are granted to persons other than employees, profit or loss is charged with
fair value of goods and services received.
