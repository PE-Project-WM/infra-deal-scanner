Strategic report for the year ended 31 March 2026
Principal activities and review of the business, including subsequent events
Principal activities
Historically, the principal activity of the Company was the development of low carbon electricity
generation facilities, which currently includes the development of its land holdings at Oldbury towards
the construction and operation of a new nuclear power plant. However, as a result of issues relating to
the raising of finance for the construction of Horizon’s first power station, Wyifa Newydd, on Anglesey,
the Group in January 2019 suspended the majority of its activities. Subsequently, in September 2020,
following an announcement by Hitachi, Limited (the Ultimate Shareholder) that it would cease its
backing for Horizon entirely and withdraw from the UK nuclear market, the Company ceased all its
activities to develop its project at Oldbury on Severn in South Gloucestershire.
On 28 June 2024 the Company completed the sale of its site to Great British Nuclear (GBN), and as
part of the sale the Company agreed to support GBN (which was subsequently renamed Great British
Energy — Nuclear (GBE-N) with estate and site management services from 28 June 2024 until 31
December 2025. This was completed on 31 December 2025 and the Company will now be retained
until 30 June 2027 when the deferred element of the payment for its Oldbury site is due from GBE-N
: (subject to the outcome of site conditions) and when warranties on the sale expire.
, Review of business
In the year ended 31 March 2026 the Company continued to support GBE-N with the provision of
transitional services under a Transitional Service Agreement (TSA) with GBE-N. Under this agreement
the Company was obliged to provide estate and site management services to GBE-N for the period
from completion to 31 December 2025. The TSA ended on 31 December 2025 as intended.
: Following the completion of the TSA the Company undertook the following activities.
e {ts site vehicle was sold and all assets were removed from the Company's fixed asset register.
« The Company applied to HMRC to deregister from the Construction Industry Scheme (CIS)
and Annual Tax on Enveloped Dwellings (ATED).
. The completion of the TSA with GBE-N resulted in a change in trade for tax purposes.
In February 2026 the Company issued a dividend of £16, 100k to its shareholder Horizon Nuclear Power
Limited (the Shareholder) (2025: £nil). The cash came from the proceeds of the land asset sale to GBE-
N in June 2024 plus accrued interest from cash pooling facility with Hitachi Europe Limited.
Strategy
Following completion of the TSA with GBE-N, the Company will be retained until 30 June 2027 when
the deferred element of the payment for its Oldbury site is due from GBE-N (subject to the outcome of
site conditions) and when warranties on the sale expire. It is the Ultimate Shareholder's intention that
following payment of the deferred consideration and expiry of the warranty period, the Company will
then be liquidated.

Impairment
At each reporting period end date and throughout the year the Company reviews the carrying amount
of its tangible and intangible assets to determine whether there is any indication that those assets have
suffered an impairment loss. In this event, the recoverable amount of the asset is estimated to
determine the extent of the loss, and any impairment loss is recognised immediately in the statement
of comprehensive income for the financial period.

: Trade and other receivables
Trade and other receivables are non-derivative financial assets with fixed or determinable payments
that are not quoted in an active market. They are initially recognised at fair value and if there is objective
evidence that the Company will not collect the full amount of the receivable, this is recognised through
the statement of comprehensive income. They are included in current assets, except for maturities
greater than 12 months after the end of the reporting period. These are classified as non-current assets.

Current and deferred income tax )

the tax bases of assets and liabilities and their carrying amounts in the financial statements. However,

accounting nor taxable profit or loss. Deferred income tax is determined using tax rates (and laws) that
have been enacted or substantially enacted by the reporting period end date and are expected to apply
when the related deferred income tax asset is realised or the deferred income tax liability is settled.
Deferred income tax assets are recognised only to the extent that it is probable that future taxable profit
will be available against which the temporary differences can be utilised. .
