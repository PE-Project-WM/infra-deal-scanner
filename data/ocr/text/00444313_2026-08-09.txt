Enigma Industrial Services Limited (EIS) presents its latest set of statutory accounts for the 12-month period ending .
31 December 2025, with December remaining as our permanent year-end accounting date.
. Business Review

The principal! activities of EIS remain the provision of access solutions, thermal insulation, protective coatings, and
other related hard / soft services, with 2025 proving to be an excellent trading year for EIS in which the business
delivered its highest tevels of profitability since its inception in October 2018. .
Revenues in 2025 at £72.6M were marginally ahead of the previous year by circa £0.5M or 0.75%. Whilst only a
small increase, 2025 was our fifth consecutive year of annual revenue growth, tangible evidence that our strategy of
delivering consistent, organic growth on an annual basis is working. These revenue levels were achieved against a
backdrop of very modest Construction sector growth in year which was broadly communicated at approximately
1.8% for the year as reported by the Office for National Statistics [ONS].
These levels of sustainable revenues reflect well on Enigma’s ability to take advantage of the growth opportunities
available across both our diverse client base and the extensive industrial markets we choose to operate in. The
different mix in revenue streams, markets and associated margins contributed to an Operating Profit of £3.5M, an
increase of £0.36M or 11.5% compared to 2024. As a result, Enigma’s EBITDA for the 12-months to 31 December
2025 now stands at £6.1M, up circa £0.5M or 9.4% on the corresponding period fast year.
In addition to the increase in reported Operating profit and EBITDA, we were delighted to deliver a profit before tax
number of £3.1M, up £0.5M or 19% on last year's £2.61M and weli ahead of our original budget commitment of
£2.5M. The ability to deliver favourably against budget reflects well on our capacity to react to ever-changing market
conditions as we flex aur offering in order to respond positively to a range of client demands.
The above revenue and profitability profile resulted in a Capex spend in year of £4.25M, including £2.7M of
scaffolding kit. Not only does this level of spend demonstrate our investment in our core fixed asset whilst
maintaining sensible levels of utilisation, it also demonstrates our commitment to improving our wider infrastructure.
The business invested nearly £1M in the upgrade and replacement of our HGV fleet, as well as nearly £0.5M in
improving our extensive branch and office working space. It is this continuous investment in our infrastructure that
provides the cornerstone of sustainable revenue growth in future years and a continuous service improvement to
our customers.
It is again noteworthy that the 2025 financials provide further tangible evidence that at the heart of EIS remains a
strong core business with robust underlying gross margins and flexible Working Capital commitments. Gross
margins at 27.7%, up 2.2% on the previous year, showcase a return to previous years margin percentage run rates
with a more profitable mix of work delivered in 2025 compared to 2024.
Cash and Working Capital continue to be key metrics for EIS, and we are delighted to report that 2025 has been
another good year of cash management with exposure days competitively positioned compared to the average of
the last five years. We finished the year with a closing bank balance of £3.7M [£3.9M in 2024], further
demonstrating our available liquidity.
Given the improvement of EIS’ key performance metrics: :

+ Another year of top-ine growth;

: + Improving gross margins;

+ Record highs reported for both Operating Profit and EBITDA;

+ Robust Working Capital;

+ Net assets of c£34.1M, £3.7M of which is cash,
the Management Team remain confident that EfS is well placed to succeed going forward.
This confidence is tempered by management's cautious view of 2026 which will be largely determined by the level
of construction sector growth combined with the easing of Bank of England base rates. Given the levels of political
instability around the world neither of these things are guaranteed, albeit that most forecasts have 2026 as a year of
modest growth at circa 2.8%. .

Principal Risks and Uncertainties

The key risks and uncertainties affecting the company are predominantly driven by market factors, contractual

delivery, commercial terms and conditions, Health & Safety regime, financial risks, and key personnel.

EIS Limited has the personnel, experience, resources, and flexibility to respond to all of the above as and when

required. This confidence was demonstrated over recent years when dealing with and subsequently surpassing

previous operational and financial activity levels. The above risk factors are considered on a quarterly basis (or as

and when relevant events dictate) by the Enigma Board.

Enigma is exposed to a variety of financial risks which the Board manages with the objective of minimising any

potential adverse effect on the company’s performance. Inflation, liquidity, and credit risk are all relevant, with

appropriate policies and procedures in place to minimise and mitigate risk.

2025 has seen us complete our first year working with Cynergy Bank, our Working Capital provider. Cynergy

continue to provide funds based on the provision of applications and invoices, doing so at competitive rates and with

increased flexibility. This move has proved hugely positive for Enigma and we look forward to a rewarding

partnership going forward.

The sectors in which we operate are exposed to changes in the economic climate, the UK Government's policies,

and employment laws as well as changing market practices and behaviours. Whilst the last few years have proved

challenging, and with more challenges to come in 2026 and beyond for both the global and UK economy in general,

the UK Construction sector remains on a reasonably secure footing. Given recent political fall-out and the change of

a UK Prime Minister, the UK construction industry will have to adapt accordingly. Notwithstanding the inevitable

change this will bring, the commitment to build more house across the UK is one that is highly likely to be
. maintained, whatever the political back-drop.

The EIS board will therefore continue to mitigate risk in a number of ways, notably by continuing to foster long-term

relationships with our key clients, maintaining flexibility in our cost base, ensuring that we are not overly reliant on

any one of our extensive supply chain, and by continuing to engage regularly with all other key stakeholders across

the business.

Key Financial and Non-Financial Performance Indicators

Enigma is managed via our network of regional offices, with each region having monthly targets to achieve across a

range of financial and non-financial KPI's. The basis of these KPI's is agreed as part of our annual business plan

process and cover financial, commercial, operational and safety targets against which performance is monitored

- monthly. :

Other information and explanations

EtS has delivered another set of extremely competitive results in 2025, and whilst revenues are only marginally up

on the previous year, Profit Before Tax as well as EBITDA are well ahead of the previous year. A strong balance

sheet and strict Working Capital management provides the assurance necessary that EIS remains a robust going

concern with every expectation that it will deliver another solid set of results in 2026 and beyond. This assertion is

wholly supported by an order book that boasts secured revenues of circa £130M, of which £60M specifically relates.

to 2026.

: To this aim EIS will continue to focus on its core clients operating in the industrial, industrial related, construction,
and house building sectors. Our objectives remain to operate safely, provide outstanding service to our customers,
maintain strong relationships with our suppliers and to engage openly and honestly with our employees in order to
drive continuous growth for the company.

The stand-alone nature of the EIS business provides for greater levels of autonomy and flexibility, a flexibility that
has been hugely beneficial to EIS since it became privately owned in October 2018. This is particularly true in terms
of cost base and Working Capital commitments. The EIS board remain confident that the future offers continuing
opportunity for progressive growth with the aim of consistent and manageable top line growth supported by robust
margins that are cash backed, all of which were delivered in 2025.
