STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE NINE-MONTH PERIOD ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025
The director presents his Strategic Report for Windcave Limited (the "Company”) for the period ended 31
December 2025.
Review of the business .
The financial statements cover the nine-month period from 1 April 2025 to 31 December 2025, following a
change in the Company's accounting reference date. The comparative figures relate to the twelve month period
ended 31 March 2025 and are therefore not directly comparable.
Turnover for the period amounted to £14,435,235 (31 March 2025: £17,109,597). The reduction reflects both
the shorter reporting period and continued competitive conditions within the payment institution sector albeit the
9 month results imply a 12.2% growth rate if pro-rated for a full 12 month period.
The Company reported an operating profit of £462,932 for the period (31 March 2025: £777,255). Operating
performance was influenced by ongoing investment in platform stability, regulatory compliance, and information
security, alongside competitive pricing pressures. ’
At the period end, the Company remained compliant with its regulatory capital requirements and maintained
safeguarding arrangements in line with applicable regulations. The director considers the Company's
performance and financial position during the period to be satisfactory,
Business model and strategy :
The Company generates revenue primarily from transaction based fees calculated by reference to payment
volumes processed through its payment gateway platform. Additional income arises from terminal leasing,
hardware sales, and related support services.
The Company's strategy is focused on maintaining a secure, resilient, and compliant operating platform,
supporting existing merchant relationships, and managing costs appropriately within a regulated environment.
The Company does not pursue growth strategies that would compromise regulatory compliance or operational
Stability.
Principal risks and uncertainties .
The director has identified the following principal risks and uncertainties which could affect the Company's
future performance:
Regulatory and compliance risk
The Company operates in a highly regulated sector and is subject to supervision by the FCA. Non-compliance
with regulatory requirements could result in restrictions or penalties. The Company mitigates this risk through
established compliance procedures, internal controls, and regulatory monitoring.
Technology and cyber security risk
The Company's operations depend on the availability and security of its systems. Cyberattacks, system failures,
or data breaches could disrupt services and impact customer confidence. Mitigation measures include system
monitoring, security controls, and thirdparty assurance processes. :
Competitive market risk
The electronic payments industry is characterised by intense competition, which may place downward pressure
on margins. The Company seeks to mitigate this risk by focusing on service reliability and maintaining
longstanding merchant relationships. :
Liquidity and operational risk
The Company manages significant volumes of merchant funds and payables. Liquidity risk is managed through
cash flow monitoring, safeguarding arrangements, and support from the wider group where appropriate.

2.5 Revenue
Revenue is recognised and measured at the fair value of the consideration received or receivable.
Contracts with customers
Revenue from contracts with customers -is recognised when control of goods or services is
transferred to the customer, at the consideration expected to be exchanged for those goods or
services. Amounts disclosed as revenue are net of discounts, rebates, expected returns and duties
and taxes paid.
In determining the transaction price for the sale of goods or services, the group considers the effects
of variable consideration, significant financing components, non-cash consideration and
consideration payable to customers, if any.
Sale of services includes transaction and usage fees recognised when the underlying transactions
are processed through Windcave platforms, establishment of new connections, customisation,
upgrade of existing services, and provision of technical support on an ongoing basis. Provision of
services is recognised on either an ongoing basis or at a point in time, depending on the contractual
terms.
The Company either processes transactions through payment gateway solution with customers’ third
party merchant service provider, or clears and settles the transactions directly.
The sale of hardware is recognised at a point in time when control is transferred to the customer.
. Lease revenue in relation to operating leases on the Company's terminals is recognised in the
Statement of Comprehensive income on a straight line basis over the lease term.

20. Financial risk management
The Company's principal financial liabilities are creditors falling due within one year. The main purpose of
these financial liabilities is to finance the Company's operations. The Company's principal financial assets
include debtors and cash equivalents.
The Company is exposed to credit risk and liquidity risk through its use of financial instruments which
result from its operating and investing activities. The Company's risk management is coordinated by the
director and strategic management at the Company's immediate parent and focuses on actively securing
the Company's short to medium term cash flows by minimising the exposure to financial risks. The
Company's director ensures adequate working capital requirements through group financing
arrangements. The Company did not make use of derivative financial instruments to hedge certain risk
exposures during the current financial period.
The director reviews and agrees policies for managing each of these risks. The most significant financial
risks to which the Company is exposed to are described below.
(a) Credit risk ‘

customer contract, leading to a financial loss. The Company is exposed to credit risk from its operating
activities, including deposits with banks and financial institutions and other financial instruments.
The Company transacts only with recognised and creditworthy parties. It is the Company's policy that all
customers who wish to trade on credit terms are subject to credit verification procedures. In addition,
receivable balances are monitored on an ongoing basis with the result that the Company's exposure to
bad debts is not significant.
