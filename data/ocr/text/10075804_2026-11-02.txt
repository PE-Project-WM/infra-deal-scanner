General Information .
P.|. Works is a leading provider of Al-driven and automated network management solutions
globally, with an award-winning product portfolio and field-proven services expertise. The group's
mission is to help mobile operators design, optimize, monitor and automate their networks efficiently
and cost-effectively. P.!. Works solutions support multi-vendor and multi-technology mobile :
networks and address network performance, customer experience, energy efficiency and
operational efficiency. The group continues to invest in Al-driven automation for 5G and next-
generation networks and contributes to industry standardization initiatives including ETSI, 3GPP.
and the O-RAN ecosystem.
- P.I. Works UK Ltd. operates as the UK subsidiary of the P.I. Works group, whose principal
operations are based in Turkiye. The company's registered office is in Marlow, Buckinghamshire.
The wider group also has a presence in the USA, Singapore and Romania.
The company operates with a lean UK-based team and is supported by the wider P.1. Works
: group for product development, engineering and service delivery. :
Technology
.P.I. Works’ product portfolio includes EXA, VantagePM, Experia and Configurex for network
automation, optimization, performance management, customer-experience analytics and
configuration management. The company also provides managed services, professional services
and training services. Its solution capabilities include Service Operations Center, Special Event
. Management, VoLTE Optimization, loT Optimization, HetNet Planning & Optimization, Spectrum :
. Refarming, Driveless Tuning and Smart Energy Saving.
Mobile networks continue to evolve towards 5G-Advanced, Open RAN and increasingly
autonomous operations. These environments require flexible resource allocation, multi-vendor
interoperability, real-time analytics and closed-loop automation. Al-driven Self-Organizing Network
capabilities help operators improve network performance and customer experience while controlling
: operational and capital costs.
P.I. Works’ EVO Platform and SMO capabilities provide a unified framework for automated
management across traditional and Open RAN environments. By correlating network and customer-
experience data, the platform supports orchestration, proactive optimization and efficient use of
network resources, helping operators progress towards greater network autonomy and energy
efficiency.
Customers .
The company's customers include major telecommunications operators, including MTN group
companies, Beeline Uzbekistan, Telefonica UK and others. The company provides network
. automation and optimization solutions together with professional and managed services.

1. Summary of significant accounting policies .
Basis of preparation se
The financial statements have been prepared under the historical cost convention and in
accordance with FRS 102, The Financial Reporting Standard applicable in the UK and Republic of
Ireland. .
Turnover .
Turnover is measured at the fair value of the consideration received or receivable, net of
discounts and value added taxes. Turnover includes revenue earned from the sale of goods and
from the rendering of services. Turnover from the sale of goods is recognised when the significant
risks and rewards of ownership of the goods have transferred to the buyer. Turnover from the
rendering of services is recognised by reference to the stage of completion of the contract. The
stage of completion of a contract is measured by comparing the costs incurred for work performed :
to date to the total estimated contract costs.
intangible fixed assets .
Intangible fixed assets are measured at cost less accumulative amortisation and any accumulative .
impairment losses.
Tangible fixed assets .
. . Tangible fixed assets are measured at cost less accumulative depreciation and any accumulative
. impairment losses. Depreciation is provided on all tangible fixed assets, other than freehold land,
Y at rates calculated to write off the cost, less estimated residual value, of each asset evenly over its
expected useful life, as follows: .
_ Fixtures, fittings, tools and equipment over 3 years
Debtors
Short term debtors are measured at transaction price (which is usually the invoice price), less any
. impairment losses for bad and doubtful debts. Loans and other financial assets are initially
4 recognised at transaction price including any transaction costs and subsequently measured at
: amortised cost determined using the effective interest method, less any impairment losses for bad
: and doubtful debts. .
Creditors .
Short term creditors are measured at transaction price (which is usually the invoice price). Loans
and other financial liabilities are initially recognised at transaction price net of any transaction
costs and subsequently measured at amortised cost determined using the effective interest
method. :
Taxation .
A current tax liability is recognised for the tax payable on the taxable profit of the current and past
periods. A current tax asset is recognised in respect of a tax loss that can be carried back to
recover tax paid in a previous period. Deferred tax is recognised in respect of all timing
differences between the recognition of income and expenses in the financial statements and their
inclusion in tax assessments. Unrelieved tax losses and other deferred tax assets are recognised
only to the extent that it is probable that they will be recovered against the reversal of deferred tax
liabilities or other future taxable profits. Deferred tax is measured using the tax rates and laws that
have been enacted or substantively enacted by the reporting date and that are expected to apply
to the reversal of the timing difference, except for revalued land and investment property where
the tax rate that applies to the sale of the asset is used. Current and deferred tax assets and
liabilities are not discounted.
