Energy Efficiency Action
At Ericsson Limited we are committed to monitor and decrease our overall carbon footprint to minimise our impact to the environment. By

reduce the overall energy demand. In 2025, we have participated in the Energy Savings Opportunities Scheme (ESOS) to reach 95% of
