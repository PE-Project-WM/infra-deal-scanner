Business Review :

Wireless Logic Limited (“Company”) is a leading global Internet of Things (“loT”) and Machine to Machine

(“M2M") solution provider that simplifies and automates loT connectivity and management for any device, ,

anywhere. We are dedicated to bridging the physical and digital worids with seamless, secure, and scalable

solutions. With more than 18 million devices connected in 165 countries and direct partnerships with over 50+

mobile and satellite operators, the company offers global coverage and end-to-end loT services that accelerate

the success of loT projects. Our loT services are meticulously designed, tested, deployed, and managed to meet

the specific needs of each customer device fleet. : .

From EPOS systems in retail stores, to smart energy grids and drones delivering essential equipment to remote .

parts for the world, the company has expertise across numerous sectors and use cases, including agriculture,

manufacturing, healthcare, security, transport, energy, utilities and smart cities. It works with a diverse client 7

base that includes global OEMs, system integrators, solution providers, application developers, blue-chip

enterprises, and start-ups. :

Conexa, a purpose-built platform and dedicated loT network, enables customers to securely connect and .

manage assets across any network and number of deployments. This simplifies operations, accelerates time to

market, lowers the total cost of ownership, and ensures ultra-reliable connectivity.

Through in-house development and strategic partnerships, the company has brought innovative new solutions .

to market, including eSIM provisioning, which delivers network flexibility for global deployments as connectivity ;

becomes increasingly embedded in devices. ;

Recognised consistently as a leading managed loT connectivity provider by industry analysts such as Gartner, :

Transforma insights, IDC, and Counterpoint Research, the company continues to innovate and expand its :

services, reinforcing its leadership in the market.

” At Wireless Logic, we’re dedicated to connecting the physical and digital worlds with seamless, secure, and .

scalable foT solutions taitored to diverse loT use cases. In collaboration with our valued business partners, we

focus on simplifying loT for our customers, enhancing their experience by enabling efficient, cost-effective, and ,

future-proof digital transformations. Together, our efforts are driving positive environmental and social change

. across the globe.” Oliver Tucker, Co-Founder and Chief Executive Officer.

Strategy , :
The company generates recurring revenue through its managed connectivity solutions and value-added .
services. Services are delivered via its proprietary infrastructure, including the Conexa gtobal loT network, and .
are tailored to regional needs while leveraging central platforms for operational efficiency.
The strategic pillars of the business are summarised below: :
1. Expanding global coverage and local services, offering flexibility and network independence.
2. Scaling infrastructure, investing in Conexa and core platforms.
3. Advancement of Remote SIM Provisioning (RSP) and Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite capabilities. .
4. Expansion across the loT value chain to reduce complexity and enhance interoperability.
5. Continuous enhancement of cybersecurity capabilities.
6. Strengthening go-to-market (GTM) strategy across brands and geographies.
7. Enabling scalable operations to deliver commercial leverage and customer value.
Objectives
The business objectives for the year included a focus on growing gross margin which increased by 9% and
growing the base of subscriptions which grew by 16% during the year. EBITDA before exceptional costs declined
at £18.3m for the year owing to planned increase in overheads as the company invests in growth activities.
