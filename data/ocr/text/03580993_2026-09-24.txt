Principal activities

GTT-EMEA Ltd. (the "Company"), is 100% owned by GTT Holdings Limited and the ultimate parent is
GTT Communications Inc. ("GTT"), a company registered in the United States of America. The group,
including GTT-EMEA Ltd., is a provider of telecommunications services. The company offers large and
multinational clients a broad portfolio of global communications services including the following:

— Secure Networking solutions including GTT Secure Connect, Managed software defined wide area
networking ("SD-WAN"), Managed Detection and Response, Distributed denial-of-service
("DDoS") Protection, Secure Remote Access, Secure Service Edge, and Managed Firewall; *

— Cloud Solutions including Edge Cloud Compute, Virtual Data Center (VDC), Application &
Database Management, and public cloud management; .

— Managed Networking including IP Transit, Dedicated Internet, Broadband Internet, Broadband
Wireless, Satellite and Cloud Connectivity, Multiprotocol label switching ("MPLS"), Virtual private
LAN service ("VPLS"), and Ethernet; ,

- Voice, including GTT Session Initiation Protocol ("SIP") Trunking, Cloud Phone Direct, and Virtual
Voice Network ("VVN");

— Professional Services including project management, program management, technical management,
customer incident management, cloud advisory services, and service management.

All services are supported by GTT EnvisionDX™, GTT's digital gateway providing visibility, insights,
orchestration, and control, enabling customers to achieve business missions and meet ongoing demand.
The group's global Tier 1 IP network delivers connectivity for clients around the world, servicing leading
multinational enterprises, carriers and government customers in over 100 countries. GTT-EMEA Ltd.
services customers in the United Kingdom, Europe and rest of the world.
: Review of the business

GTT-EMEA Ltd's strategic focus continues to be to deliver profitable growth through targeting Global and
Multi-national enterprise organisations with a portfolio of managed services solutions. As part of this :
strategy, GTT-EMEA Ltd. has shifted away from focussing on legacy business from UK Public Sector and
domestic business segments as well as infrastructure services.
The GTT Group's transfer pricing study characterizes each entity as a limited risk service provider or as an
entrepreneur based on each entity's functions and risks. GTT-EMEA Ltd. is characterized as an entrepreneur

. because it is the primary decision maker and is responsible for overall strategy, decision making and
management of all functional areas of the business and bears the risk. .
During most of 2025, churn continued to be higher than the new installs. In line with most recent GIT
business strategy, the Company has shifted its focus on enhanced networking products, which are bringing
stickier services to the customers that are less sensitive to annual churn and provide higher margin to GTT.

Principal risks and uncertainties

The Company's services are sold under agreements that generally have initial terms of between one and five

years. Following the initial term, these agreements generally automatically renew for successive month to

month, quarterly or annual periods, but can be terminated by the customers with relatively little notice during

the renewal period.

The Company is reliant on its suppliers to provide our customers with services. Any failure on the part of

suppliers to adequately supply the company, or to maintain the quality of facilities and services in the future,

could cause customers to experience delays in services and lower levels of customer care, which could cause

them to switch providers.

Energy efficiency action taken:

GTT is continuously working to improve the accuracy and reliability of the emissions data by refining

calculation methods and data collection processes. These improvements contributed to the 30% recorded

decrease in the total emissions from 2024 to 2025. The accuracy will also support more effective tracking of
the progress toward emissions reduction targets in future reporting cycles. In collaboration with industry

experts, GTT is conducting a detailed assessment of direct (Scope 1 and Scope 2) and indirect (Scope 3)

carbon emissions to better understand the overall environmental impact.

Methodology: GTT uses the GHG Protocol's Corporate Accounting and Reporting Standards, Scope 2

Guidance, and Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Reporting Standard for emissions calculations. :

Note: GTT is using a third party carbon consultancy firm to support our GHG accounting and they are using

their internal platform - Luumo - to support calculation.

GTT is reporting on both location and market-based emissions should we decide to move to renewable :

energy in the future. This will help GTT track the changes in emissions better.

GTT will build on its existing progress by formalising its greenhouse gas emissions reduction targets by

2027, with the ambition of aligning these with Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) requirements and

industry best practices. To support this process, GIT is currently working with a third-party carbon

consultancy firm to assess the feasibility of adopting Science Based Targets (SBTs).

Going Concern
The current financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.
Management identified certain indicators that might cast concerns on the relevance of the going concern
assumption, such as negative operating cash flows before receiving financing from group entities.
Management concluded that this entity is strongly dependent on the ultimate parent from a going concern
perspective.
The Company has received a formal letter of support from the ultimate parent, GTT Communications Inc.,
dated 2 September 2026. This confirms the group's intent and ability to provide financial support to the
Company and its affiliates for a period of at least 12 months from the date of approval of these financial
statements.
Management noted that the Company returned to profitability in 2025 and that the significant loss reported in
the prior year was primarily attributable to a non-recurring adjustment rather than a deterioration in the
underlying trading performance of the business.
Management has prepared detailed forecasts for the Company covering the period from 2026 to 2030. These
forecasts indicate continued revenue growth and increased profitability throughout the forecast period.
In performing the assessment, management considered business forecasts and a set of underlying
assumptions, including expected trading performance, transfer pricing arrangements, and the Company's
established funding and cash management structure. The directors consider these forecasts to be based on
reasonable and supportable assumptions and therefore appropriate for use in the going concern assessment.
Management regularly monitors financial performance and liquidity through forecasting, budgeting, and
cash flow review processes.
Based on the assessment performed, including the Company's projected revenue growth, return to
profitability, the availability of ongoing parental support and the cash management arrangements within GTT
Group, management has a reasonable expectation that the Company has adequate resources to continue in
. operational existence for at least twelve months from the date of approval of these financial statements. .

Accordingly, the financial statements have been prepared on a going concern basis.

Fees charged for ongoing services are generally fixed in price and billed on a recurring monthly

the related services, or usage above a fixed threshold, which are billed monthly in arrears. The

the usage based fees are excluded from the transaction price and are instead recognised as

terms, either for a specified renewal period (e.g., one year) or on a month-to-month basis.

receive and consume the benefit of the service as the Company performs.
Effective 2024, the management changed its accounting estimate for installation and setup fees,
recognised as Non-Recurring Revenue (NRR). Instead of recognizing NRR when invoiced, it is
now deferred and amortised over the average contract life. This change aligns with a deeper

services and provide no standalone benefit to the customer.

consideration it expects to be entitled to receive for such arrangements using the expected value

2.5.3 Transfer Pricing revenue
Up until 31 December 2023, the entity was determining and recording the transactions between
the enterprises in the GTT Group, on the basis of transfer pricing methodology applying the

Given the reliance of GIT-EMEA Ltd. and GTT US on each other to successfully execute on

5. Calculate the required transfer pricing adjustment to obtain the target EBIT;

3. Critical accounting estimates and judgements
The Company makes estimates and assumptions conceming the future. The resulting accounting
estimates will, by definition, seldom equal the related actual results. Estimates and judgements are
continually evaluated and are based on historical experience and other factors, including
expectations of future events that are believed to be reasonable under the circumstances.
The estimates and assumptions that have a significant risk of causing a material adjustment to the
carrying amounts of assets and liabilities within the next financial year are addressed below.
a) Useful economic lives of property, plant and equipment

. The annual depreciation charge for property, plant and equipment is sensitive to changes in the :

estimated useful economic lives and residual values of the assets. The useful economic lives and
residual values are reassessed annually. They are amended when necessary to reflect current
estimates, based on technological advancement, future investments, economic utilisation and the
physical condition of the assets. See Note 13 for the carrying amount of the tangible fixed assets, and
Note 2.9 for the useful economic life details.
b) Useful economic lives of intangible assets
The useful life of an intangible asset that arises from contractual or other legal rights shall not exceed
the period of the contractual or other legal rights but may be shorter depending on the period over
which the entity expects to use the asset.
Goodwill
The Company believes that technological and commercial changes in telecommunication and
technology sector are likely to happen within next 3 year. Hence, the most appropriate useful life for
amortisation of goodwill should be 3 years on straight-line basis. Goodwill was fully amortised in
the year end 31 December 2024.
The useful economic lives of the intangible assets are reassessed annually. They are amended when
necessary to reflect the current estimates based on the industry in which the asset operates, the
changes in the market demand for the products or services output from the asset, and technological

. advancements. See Note 12 for the carrying amount of the intangible assets, and Note 2.17 for the
useful economic life details.
c) Capitalised Commissions
The capitalisation of commissions involves judgement in determining whether the costs meet the
criteria for recognition as a prepayment (i.e. an asset) under FRS 102. These costs are recognised
where they are directly attributable to securing customer contracts and are expected to be recovered
through the future economic benefits of those contracts.
Estimation is required in assessing the expected duration of the customer relationship and the pattern
over which the related economic benefits are consumed. As of 31 December 2025, the carrying value
of capitalised internal commissions was £2.3 million (2024: £0.9 million). These amounts are
amortised on a systematic basis over an average period of 4 years, consistent with the period over
which the related revenue is recognised. .

j) Transfer pricing estimates
Transfer pricing determinations inherently involve significant judgement and estimation. In
accordance with FRS102, the Company applies its best estimates in determining the arm’s length

* Assessing the economic substance of transactions to determine the most relevant pricing method.
* Evaluating the impact of market conditions and changes in business strategies on transfer prices.
* Utilising provisional information or preliminary data when final information is not yet available,
with the understanding that adjustments will be made prospectively once the final data is
confirmed.

objectives to maximize group revenues and profits, as of 1 January 2024 the Residual Profit Split
Method (“RPSM”) was selected as the most appropriate method with all operating entities earning an
arm’s length NCP (“net cost plus”) of 7 % (exception is India where the % is 15) as first step,

EMEA Ltd. This is considered by management, as a change in the accounting estimate, and therefore
applied prospectively.
