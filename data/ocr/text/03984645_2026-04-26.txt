STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2025

Introduction

The directors present their Strategic Report for the year ended 31 December 2025.

Business review .

The principal activity of the Company throughout the year continued to be the provision of international
telecommunications traffic, encompassing both wholesale voice and retail services.

Throughout 2025, the Company's Voice business remained focused on high-volume traffic with robust margins,
further extending its geographical footprint to capture new margin opportunities. Turnover from Voice services
increased by 12.52% year-on-year, underpinning QXTEL's continued expansion and its increasingly diversified
traffic profile.

SMS services represented 7.41% of total turnover and 30.48% of total gross profit during the year. The
Company anticipates continued momentum in this segment going forward.

Principal risks and uncertainties

Competitive risks .

The Company operates within a highly competitive global market, with the primary risk stemming from sustained
pressure on operating margins driven by aggressive pricing competition and the growing prevalence of
alternative communication channels, including social messaging platforms. The Company addresses this risk by
delivering quality routing solutions and deploying a state-of-the-art Intelligent VoIP Switching Platform, which
enables dynamic routing based on quality, capacity, and the specific requirements of each customer.

Credit risks
As a provider of international telecommunication services, the Company relies on the successful termination of
traffic to other operators and the timely collection of receivables from its customer base. Credit exposure is
managed through real-time balance monitoring across all carriers. Additional mitigation is provided through credit
insurance arrangements and the reciprocal termination of traffic with key customers.

; Price risk
The Company is not exposed to price risk, as all applicable rates and charges are agreed in advance and
subject to regular review.

Exchange rate risk ;

The majority of the Company's Voice revenues are denominated in USD, whilst SMS revenues are primarily in
EUR. Certain day-to-day operational costs are incurred in GBP. The Company sources these currencies at
prevailing market rates and, based on current exposure levels, does not consider it necessary to adopt a formal
exchange rate risk management policy.

Future developments
The Company will continue to provide international long-distance voice termination and A2P (Application-to-
Person) SMS messaging services to telecom operators globally, with an ongoing commitment to driving growth
across both established and emerging geographical markets. The directors regard the diversification of traffic
and carrier profiles as central to positioning the Company as a key player in the sector, and intend to leverage
the Company's global footprint to integrate its voice and messaging capabilities under a Cloud-Based Platform
Services (CPaaS) model, made available to carrier and operator partners worldwide.

In addition, the Company has invested — and will continue to invest — substantial financial and human
resources in expanding its service portfolio for Mobile Network Operators. This includes loT services and
FinTech-enabled solutions, with the aim of diversifying revenue streams and reducing exposure to the volatility of
wholesale carrier voice traffic arising from global economic pressures and the growing demand for non-voice
products.

2.8 Pensions
Defined contribution pension plan
The.Company operates a defined contribution plan for its employees. A defined contribution plan is a
pension plan under which the Company pays fixed contributions into a separate entity. Once the
contributions have been paid the Company. has no further payment obligations.
The contributions are recognised as an expense in profit or loss when they fall due. Amounts not paid
are shown in accruals as a liability in the Balance Sheet. The assets of the plan are held separately
from the Company in independently administered funds.

2.9 Financial instruments
Financial assets and financial liabilities are recognised in the Balance Sheet when the Company
becomes a party to the contractual provisions of the instrument.
Trade and other debtors and creditors are classified as basic financial instruments and measured at
initial recognition at transaction price. Debtors and creditors are subsequently measured at amortised
cost using the effective interest rate method. A provision is established when there is objective
evidence that the Company will not be able to collect all amounts due.
Cash and cash equivalents are classified as basic financial instruments and comprise cash in hand
and at bank, short-term bank deposits with an original maturity of three months or less and bank
overdrafts which are an integral part of the Company's cash management.
Financial liabilities and equity instruments issued by the Company are classified in accordance with
the substance of the contractual arrangements entered into and the definitions of a financial liability
and an equity instrument. An equity instrument is any contract that evidences a residual interest in
the assets of the Company after deducting all of its liabilities. Equity instruments issued by the
Company are recorded at the proceeds received, net of direct issue costs.

2.10 Taxation
Tax is recognised in profit or loss except that a charge attributable to an item of income and expense
recognised as other comprehensive income or to an item recognised directly in equity is also
recognised in other comprehensive income or directly in equity respectively.
The current income tax charge is calculated on the basis of tax rates and laws that have been
enacted or substantively enacted by the balance sheet date in the countries where the Company
operates and generates income.

2.11 Dividends
Equity dividends are recognised when they become legally payable. ‘Interim equity dividends are
recognised when paid. Final equity dividends are recognised when approved by the shareholders at
an annual genera! meeting.
