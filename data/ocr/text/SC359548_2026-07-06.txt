Company for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Principal activities
The principal activity of the Company during the year was the supply of data broadcast Global Navigation Satellite
Systems (GNSS) augmentation services for precise positioning applications primarily to the offshore oil and gas
industry.
Results and a dividend
The Statement of Profit or Loss and Other Comprehensive Income for the year is set out on page 9. During the year,
no dividends were paid (2024: £nil).

statements. The financial statements are prepared under the going concern basis of accounting as detailed in note

Strategic Report for the year ended 31 December 2025
Business Review
In 2025, the Company’s revenue was £28.8 million (2024: £20.8 million). Revenues have increased compared to the
prior year, primarily due to steady market share growth within the oil and gas market and an increase in equipment
sales.
Net profit from operations for the year ended 31 December 2025 was £0.3 million (2024: £1.4 million) resulted in total
comprehensive loss attributable to the equity shareholders for the year of £9.3 million (2024: £4.9 million profit). The
decrease in net operating profit and total comprehensive income attributable to the equity shareholders between the
years was due to an impairment to intangible assets. The depreciation and amortisation charge of £3 million (2024:
£3 million) and employee benefits of £5.8 million (2024: £4.4 million) represent 10% and 20% respectively (2024:
15% and 21% respectively), of the total operating expense for the year of £29.6 million (2024: £20.7 million). Other
operating expenses as a percentage of total operating expense decreased 37% (2024: 40%). Other income for the
year was £1.2m (2024: £1.3m). Net financial loss for the year was £10 million (2024: £4.7 million gain). The taxation
income for the year ended 31 December 2025 was £0.4 million (2024: £1.2 million charge).
During the year, research and development activities continued in relation to achieving more accurate positioning,
faster convergence, and the next generation of GNSS constellations.
The Directors are satisfied with the performance and results of the Company for the year.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The management of the business and the execution of the Company's strategy are subject to a number of risks. Key
business risks and uncertainties affecting the Company are considered to relate to market pricing pressure and the
potential entrance of competitors in the market place.
The Company’s customer portfolio to whom it provides its services and products, is varied from large multinational
companies to smaller independent companies. The smaller independent customers tend to carry a larger credit risk,
, however, credit risk still affected all sizes of companies.
Financial risk management policies
The Company's operations potentially expose it to a variety of financial risks that include the effects of changes in
currency exchange rates, credit risk, liquidity risk and interest rate risk. The company has in place a risk management
programme that seeks to limit the adverse effects of these factors on the financial performance of the company. .
Details explaining the Company's financial risk management are described in note 2 to the financial statements.
Future Outlook
The demand for accurate, reliable positioning in many difficult environments continues to create demand for Veripos
equipment and services and is expected to increase in the coming years. Activity in offshore markets continues to
remain high, as countries focus on energy security, we anticipate this will continue for the foreseeable future.
