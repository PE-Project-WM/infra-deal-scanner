GROUP STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 DECEMBER 2024
The directors present their strategic report of the company and the group for the year ended 31 December 2024.
On Ist January 2024 Eurocoin Group Limited acquired by way of dividend received all of the issued shares of Eurocoin
GmbH., a company registered in germany and operating from Haar, Munich, together with its three subsidiaries,
Eurocoin Components s.l., a company incorporated in Spain and operating from La Coruna ( 60% owned); Elcoin s.a, a
company registered in Czechia ( wholly owned); and Eurocoin E-screen Limited, a company registered in England and
Wales and operating from Great Yarmouth.
The principal activity of the main operating subsidiary in the year, Eurocoin GmbH., is the value-added supply of
specialist components to European manufacturers of electronic gaming, entertainment and retail equipment.
Three other subsidiaries provide equipment developed and produced internally, service, support and replacement spares
to operators of sited equipment. The holding company provides services to the subsidiary companies.
REVIEW OF BUSINESS
The year saw a slight reduction in demand in the group's main markets, with tumover down from the expected level
achieved in 2023 of £17.8 million to £16.94 million; margins were well maintained, at 23.1% against the level of
22.17% in 2023, so that gross profit was slightly higher than achieved by the subsidiaries in 2023.
Expenditure increased following the decision to strengthen the group's management and operational activities, so that the
group profit after tax and the elimination of minority interests was £541,840, against the equivalent profits in 2023 of
£1,101,950.
The return on capital employed, which the directors regard as a key performance indicator, was
7.87% for 2024 against 17.3% for 2023
Overall the directors consider this a satisfactory performance by its operating subsidiaries and associates.

3. ACCOUNTING POLICIES - continued
Investments in associates
Investments in associate undertakings are recognised at cost.
Stocks
Stocks are valued at the lower of cost and net realisable value, after making due allowance for obsolete and slow
moving items. :
Taxation
Taxation for the year comprises current and deferred tax. Tax is recognised in the Consolidated Income
Statement, except to the extent that it relates to items recognised in other comprehensive income or directly in
equity.
Current or deferred taxation assets and liabilities are not discounted.
Current tax is recognised at the amount of tax payable using the tax rates and laws that have been enacted or
substantively enacted by the balance sheet date.
Deferred tax
Deferred tax is recognised in respect of all timing differences that have originated but not reversed at the balance

_ Sheet date.

Timing differences arise from the inclusion of income and expenses in tax assessments in periods different from
those in which they are recognised in financial statements. Deferred tax is measured using tax rates and laws that
have been enacted or substantively enacted by the year end and that are expected to apply to the reversal of the
timing difference.
Unrelieved tax losses and other deferred tax assets are recognised only to the extent that it is probable that they
will be recovered against the reversal of deferred tax liabilities or other future taxable profits.
Foreign currencies
Transactions in foreign currencies have been translated into sterling at average rates. Assets and liabilities of the
company and of the group at the year end denominated in foreign currencies have been translated at rates ruling
at the year end which have been taken as $1.254 =£1 for the US Dollar and €1.2047= £1 for the Euro. All
profits and losses on translation have been dealt with as part of the gross profit of the company and of the group.
There were no foreign currency transaction sin 2023.

are charged to profit or loss in the period to which they relate.
