FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 MARCH 2025
The directors present the strategic report for Seapeak Telecom Limited (the company) and its subsidiaries (the
group) for the year ended 31 March 2025
Business Review
FY24 saw a major reorganisation within the group in which the business was split into three subsidiaries of Seapeak
Telecom Ltd This change allowed greater emphasis to be placed on the different business streams and prepared
the Planettalk business for an intended disposal, which was completed in FY25 Following a decision to buyout the
minority shareholder of Wavecrest Networks Limited (hereafter, 'WNL') in FY25, a decision has been taken to
reverse the split of the PaaS business into a separate subsidiary, and the business of Wavecrest Innovations
Limited (hereafter, 'WIL') was transferred back to WNL on 1 April 2025 In light of this further reorganistion, the
goodwill and investment carrying values of the entities were again reviewed and impaired where necessary An
(mpairment charge of £207k (2024 £325k) was recognised In the period
The group has continued to invest in both the wholesale and PaaS businesses in the year to 31 March 2025,
building on prior year developments As the investment cycle closes, spend has reduced, thus impacting favourably
on EBITDA A fall in revenue - £41,050k (2024 £49,283k) - was as a result of continued decline in the size of the
market for international telecoms, and a decision to focus on higher margin revenue opportunities in the second half
of the year
In the PaaS business, investment continues to be made to move the core app infrastructure into a modem cloud
environment, with new app architecture launched for two key customers during the year, with another customer '
launch scheduted for F¥26 The wholesale telecoms business ts also completing a move into cloud infrastructure to
ensure service resilience as well as cost savings
After taking into account restructuring costs incurred, EBITDA (defined as earnings before interest, tax, depreciation
and amortisation) has subsequently increased from the previous year to £1,138k (2024 £657k) A focus on cost
efficiencies reduced base overheads significantly
The consolidated statement of financial position shows that the group's net assets have increased in the year by
47% to £2,784k (2024 £2,659k) The impact of profit achieved in the year was reduced by the payment of
dividends
The group operates ethical supply, antt-bnbery, and anti-slavery policies, and has a totally non-prejudicial approach
to employment, recruitment and remuneration
