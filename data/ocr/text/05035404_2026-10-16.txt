Strategic report
For the year ended 31 January 2026
Introduction -
. The directors are pleased to present their strategic report for Atlantic Projects Company (UK) Limited ("the
Company”) for the year ended 31 January 2026 ("FY2026").
Business review
The principal activity of the Company is new project construction and maintenance of existing facilities in the
power generation and industrial sectors. The Company provides a full range of engineering, procurement,
construction, commissioning, operations management, maintenance, project development, technical and other
consulting services to the power generation market, including the renewable energy sector.
In October 2021, the Company was contracted to construct a 2 x 330 MW natural gas-fired power plant in
Carrickfergus that is near Belfast, Northern Ireland, in an existing structure that was initially designed to enclose
coal-fired units (the “Kilroot Power Station” or the “Kilroot” project).
There have been a number of challenges related to the Kilroot project that have adversely impacted the Company’s
ability to execute as expected, including supply chain delays, material changes to the project, the COVID-19
omicron outbreak, the war in Ukraine and extreme weather. In addition, unresolved variations and claims have
disrupted the execution of the project and harmed the cash flow of this project. Unresolved project-related matters
negatively impacted the contract, costs and the project schedule. The Company provided 14 days’ notice to
terminate the Kilroot contract as a result of project owner breaches in April 2024. Those breaches were not
resolved during that 14-day period, as a result of which the contract was terminated on the 3 May 2024.
The Company has recognised the full cost of completing the Kilroot project up to the point of termination in
" FY2025. The Company has also recognised all the revenue expected from the Kilroot project in FY2025. Note 13
of the financial statements contains the contract balance that the Company expects to receive from the project
ownet. Management is confident that the full balance is recoverable. .
The Company, along with the support of its ultimate parent company, shall vigorously pursue all of its rights under
the contract including through legal means if necessary.
Key performance indicators for the Company ate revenue and gross margin.
‘The Company had continued success in FY2026 through working as a mechanical subcontractor’ on the North ‘
London Heat and Power Project and in its maintenance and outage business.
In FY2026 and subsequent periods, the Company plans to continue with its principal activities and based on strong
and identified business prospects, increase revenue and profitability.

company, or a public benefit entity concessionary loan.
