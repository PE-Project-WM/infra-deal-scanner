Group Strategic Report
for the year ended 31 December 2025 :
The directors present their strategic report of the company and the group for the year ended 31 December 2025.
Zavarco Plc is a public limited company incorporated in the United Kingdom under the Companies Act. The group's principal
activities are investment holding and real estate activities with own or leased property. There have been no significant
changes in the nature of the activities during the financial year.
The company is the ultimate parent undertaking of Zavarco Pic SDN. BHD, a private company incorporated in Malaysia.
REVIEW OF BUSINESS
Due to the ongoing legal dispute, the Group generated no revenue from its principal trading activities during the year. It
recognised consultancy income of RM678,740 (2024: RM120,000) within other operating income. The Group reported a loss
after tax of RM2.8 million (2024: loss of RM2.3 million).
The balance sheet shows the group's financial position at the year end and indicates net assets were RM 432m (2024 - RM
435m).
No Dividend have been paid or proposed.
KEY PERFORMANCE INDICATORS
The company's directors believe that key performance indicators specific to the group are not necessary or appropriate for
an understanding of the development, performance, or position of its business.
MATERIAL UNCERTAINTY ON GOING CONCERN
The recoverability of the unpaid subscriber share capital and amounts due from former related parties are part of legal
disputes, the outcome of which is still uncertain. These events or conditions indicate that a material uncertainty exists that
may cast significant doubt on the company's ability to continue as a going concern.
LEGAL PROCEEDINGS
The Company is involved in legal proceedings in the UK and Malaysia arising from disputes with former directors,
shareholders and other parties. In the UK, the Supreme Court ruled that an earlier declaration concerning unpaid shares did
not prevent the Company from bringing a separate payment claim. A subsequent judgment in that claim was made in favour
of the defendant; the Company has been granted permission to appeal.
In Malaysia, a claim brought by a bank against the Company and other defendants was struck out by the High Court. The
bank has appealed, and the Company continues to defend the claim.
PRINCIPAL RISKS AND UNCERTAINTIES
The management of the business and the execution of the Group's strategy are subject to certain risk as below.
Financial risk management .
In its ordinary operations, the Group is exposed to various risks such as capital risk, foreign currency risk, compliance risk,
credit risk, interest rate risk, credit risk and liquidity risks.
Capital risk management
The Group manages it capital to ensure that the company will be able to continue as a going concern while maximising the
return to the stakeholders through the optimisation of the debt and equity balances. The current capital structure of the
Group consists of equity comprising stated called up share capital and accumulated losses. The Group is in legal dispute
over recoverability of the unpaid share capital with its former shareholders.

Taxation

Current taxes are based on the results shown in the financial statements and are calculated according to local tax rules, using tax

rates enacted or substantially enacted by the statement of financial position date.

Deferred tax is recognised on temporary differences between the carrying amounts and tax bases of assets and liabilities, subject to

applicable exceptions. Deferred tax assets are recognised only where recovery against future taxable profits is probable. Deferred

tax is measured at the tax rates expected to apply when the differences reverse.

investments and other financial assets

Investments and other financial assets are initially measured at cost. Transaction costs are included as part of the initial

measurement, except for financial assets at fair value through profit or loss. Such assets are subsequently measured at either

amortised cost or fair value depending on their classification. Ciassification is determined based on both the business model within

which such assets are held and the contractual cash flow characteristics of the financial asset unless an accounting

mismatch is being avoided.

Financial assets are derecognised when the rights to receive cash flows have expired or have been transferred and the

consolidated entity has transferred substantially all the risks and rewards of ownership. When there is no reasonable

expectation of recovering part or all of a financial asset, it’s carrying value is written off.

Financial assets at fair value through profit or loss.

Financial assets not measured at amortised cost or at fair value through other comprehensive income are classified as financial
. assets at fair value through h profit or loss. Typically, such financial assets will be either: (i) held for trading, where they are acquired

for the purpose of selling inthe short-term with an intention of making a profit, or a derivative; or (ii) designated as such upon

initial recognition where permitted . Fair value movements are recognised in profit or loss.

Financial assets at fair value through other comprehensive Income

Financial assets at fair value through h other comprehensive income include equity investments which the consolidated entity

Impairment of financial assets

The consolidated entity recognises a loss allowance for expected credit losses on financial assets which are either

measured at amortised cost or fair value through other comprehensive income. The measurement of the loss allowance

depends upon the consolidated entity's assessment at the end of each reporting period as to whether the financial

instrument's cred it risk has increased significantly since initial recognition, based on reasonable and supportable information

that is available, without undue cost or effort to obtain.

Where there has not been a significant increase in exposure to credit risk since initial recognition, a 12 month expected

credit loss allowance is estimated. This represents a portion of the asset's lifetime expected credit losses that is attributable to

@ default event that is possible within the next 12 months. Where a financial asset has become credit impaired or where it is

determined that credit risk has increased significantly, the loss allowance is based on the asset's lifetime expected losses. The

amount of expected credi! loss recognised is measured on the basis of the probability weighted present value of anticipated cash

shortfalls aver the life of the instrument discounted at the original effective interest rate. The company has a credit risk exposure ta

a large judgement debtor relating to unpaid share capital.

No impairment was made as at 31 December 2025.

Business combinations
The Group accounts for acquisitions of businesses using the acquisition method. The consideration transferred and the identifiable
assets acquired and liabilities assumed are measured at their acquisition-date fair values, subject to the exceptions prescribed by the
relevant accounting standards. Acquisition-related costs are recognised as expenses when incurred, except for costs of issuing debt
or equity securities. Non-controlling interests that are present ownership interests are measured for each acquisition either at fair
value or at their proportionate share of the acquiree's identifiable net assets. Goodwill is the excess of consideration transferred, the
amount of any non-controlling interest and the fair value of any previously held interest over the net identifiable assets acquired. Any
bargain purchase gain is recognised in profit or loss after reassessing the acquisition accounting. Contingent consideration is
measured at fair value at the acquisition date and subsequently accounted for according to its classification. Provisional amounts are
adjusted retrospectively for new information about acquisition-date facts and circumstances during the measurement period, which
cannot exceed one year.
Consuitancy Income
Income from consultancy services is recognised as the services are provided, to the extent that the Group has satisfied its
performance obligations and is entitled to consideration. The amount recognised reflects the consideration expected in exchange for
those services. : :
Foreign curreny transactions

exchange differences generally recognised in profit or loss.
