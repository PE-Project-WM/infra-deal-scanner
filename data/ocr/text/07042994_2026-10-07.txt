The directors of Palantir Technologies UK, Ltd. (together with its branch, the “Company”) present their strategic report on the
affairs of the Company, together with the directors’ report, financial statements and auditor's report, for the year ended
31 December 2025.
The Company is a wholly owned subsidiary of Palantir International Inc., which is in tum a wholly owned subsidiary of Palantir
Technologies Inc. (the “Ultimate Parent Company”), the ultimate parent in Palantir’s global group of companies (collectively,
the “Group” or “Palantir”). The Company has a branch office in the Netherlands and subsidiaries in the United Kingdom (the
“UK”) and Qatar.
Principal Activity
The principal activity of the Company during the 2025 year was that of the provision of marketing and sales support services,
developmental support, technical implementation, and business support services, including certain contracting services, to the
Ultimate Parent Company as well as sales directly to customers based within and outside of the United Kingdom. The Ultimate
Parent Company builds software that empowers organisations to effectively integrate their data, decisions, and operations at
scale.
The compensation received by the Company for the services to the Ultimate Parent Company listed above is calculated as
attributable costs plus a mark-up percentage of service fee that is periodically reviewed and adjusted as deemed appropriate to
maintain an arm’s-length rate of compensation. For the year 2025, the service fee percentage amounted to 7% (2024: 7%).
Business Review
In 2025, the Company achieved turnover of £262,538,425 (2024: £247,298,448), an increase of £15,239,977 over the previous
year primarily due to increased activity. Operating expenses for the year amounted to £238,173,327 (2024: £223,061,169), an
increase of £15,112,158 over the previous year. The increase in 2025 operating expenses was primarily due to increases in
wages & staff related expenses and research & development expenses. Profit before taxation amounted to £25,133,656 (2024:
£25,314,204), a decrease of £180,548 primarily due to a decrease in interest receivable and similar income.
The Company’s statement of financial position as at 31 December 2025 is on page 19.
A significant portion of the Company's revenue comes from the Ultimate Parent Company, and accordingly the performance of
the Company is closely linked to that of the Ultimate Parent Company. The strategic objectives and future prospects of the
Company are, therefore, aligned with the Ultimate Parent Company. Further detail can be found in the Ultimate Parent
Company’s publicly available filings with the United States (“US”) Securities and Exchange Commission (“SEC”), including in
the Ultimate Parent Company’s most recent Annual Report on Form 10-K, Proxy Statement on Schedule 14A, and other
periodic reports of the Ultimate Parent Company, as may be amended or supplemented from time to time, none of which form
part of this report.
The Company's results are included in the consolidated financial statements of the Ultimate Parent Company, which are
available to the public and accessible on the SEC website (https://www.sec.gov).
Key Performance Indicators (“KPIs”)
The Group manages its business and measures the delivery of its strategic objectives through the application of KPIs at operating
business and group level, details of which can be found in the Ultimate Parent Company’s publicly available SEC filings. As the
Company continues to operate under a cost-plus arrangement with the Ultimate Parent Company, the Company’s directors are of
the opinion that analysis using KPIs is not necessary for an understanding of the development, performance or position of the
business.

Section 172 Statement ,
The Company’s directors have set out their section 172(1) statement in accordance with the Companies Act 2006 in relation to
stakeholder engagement for the year ended 31 December 2025. .
The Company’s directors are aware of their statutory duty to promote the success of the Company for the benefit of its members
as a whole, and in doing so the need to have regard to those factors set out in section 172(1)(a)-(f) of the Companies Act 2006.
The Company’s directors also consider the views of a wider set of stakeholders in the decisions they make, which includes
employees, customers, suppliers, as well as authorities, regulators and our surrounding communities.
The Company’s directors continue to be committed to ensuring that the Company builds software that empowers organisations
to effectively integrate their data, decisions and operations at scale, and to support that effort, they not only rely on the input and
contributions from internal stakeholders, but also gain valuable insights through our engagements and conversations with current
and prospective customers.
The Company's directors, alongside Palantir’s management team, continued in 2025 to promote engagement with its employees
by maintaining regular contact through meetings, communications, business updates and, where feasible, office visits. The
Company’s directors believe that this continued regular engagement with employees to assess satisfaction levels and receive
employee feedback facilitates an atmosphere of respect and trust that pushes boundaries for innovation. In doing so, we can
continue to meet the demands of our customers in developing, producing, and delivering superior products and solutions.
During 2025, management held seven employee Ask-Me-Anything (“AMA”) sessions, including UK-focused business updates
and an intern-specific session. These forums provided employees with opportunities to ask questions and provide feedback on
business, product and employment-related matters. Relevant themes arising from employee engagement were considered by
management and, where material, reported to the Board.
In addition, employee satisfaction, which is critical to the success of our business, is discussed as part of the Company’s
employee appraisal programme. This gives employees the opportunity to reflect on their growth, discuss how their managers and
teams can help support their ongoing development, and express their feedback and satisfaction in various areas.
The Company’s Board of Directors (the “Board”) recognises that having regard to these various stakeholder groups in their
decision-making contributes to the overall success of the Company and the broader Group.
While direct stakeholder engagement and corporate practices are owned and implemented at a group level by the management
team of the Ultimate Parent Company, communication with various stakeholders takes place at all levels within the organisation,
including with the Board via direct interactions with management, advisors, and key employees of the Company and the broader
Group.
Considering the Likely Consequences of Any Decision in the Long Term
The Company's directors have acted in a way that they considered, in good faith, to be most likely to promote the success of the
Company for the benefit of its members as a whole. As is normal for companies of its size, the Company’s directors delegate
authority for day-to-day management of the Company to certain managers, business leads, and other key employees. The Board
works with management, business leads and key employees to undertake a review of the Company’s strategy and priorities,
forming the basis for the strategic direction of the Company. The Board also considers the Company’s performance over the
period while reviewing its strategy. In addition, the Board considers input from specialists within the business regarding the
current phase of strategic development, as well as future areas of growth. The resulting assessment of future development helps
inform the Board's decision-making and the balance between short-term and long-term measures, actions and risks.

The Wellbeing of the Company’s Employees
Palantir and the Company's directors deeply care for the wellbeing of the Company’s employees and offer certain benefits to all
