Strategic report
Introduction
We are pleased to present the strategic report for Alchemy Telco Solutions UK Limited (‘the Company”) for
the financial year ended 31 March 2026.
This report provides an overview of the key activities, achievements, and future plans of the Company which
operates primarily in the smart device trading and collections business. :
Business review
During the financial year ended 31 March 2026, the principal activities of the Company were engaging in
smart device trading and collections business.
Our mission is to foster a more sustainable future by offering high-quality, refurbished technology products
that promote the principles of the circular economy. We firmly believe that technology should be accessible .
to all, and to achieve this, we provide reliable, affordable, and environmentally responsible products that
reduce electronic waste (e-waste).
The foundation of our business model lies in circularity. While certain customers and businesses may prefer
brand-new tech products, our circular market approach starts with these devices. The Company recovers,
refreshes, and remarkets devices, offering trade-in services that enable customers worldwide to recover
value from their used technology. Through a mature ecosystem of tools and services, we process, refurbish,
and repair technology, making it ready for reuse. Our dynamic remarketing model, driven by extensive

. pricing intelligence and a diverse range of sales channels, allows us to supply high-quality used and
refurbished technology to consumers, businesses, and partners across the globe. This approach not only
conserves the planet’s resources and contributes to the reduction of e-waste but also fosters the creation of
green jobs and promotes responsible consumption.
Business environment
The secondary device market has been experiencing significant growth, with secondary sales rising, while
new smartphone sales are starting to plateau. However, to take this market mainstream, education and
evangelism efforts are required.
Buyers are increasingly drawn to the greater affordability and sustainability offered by refurbished devices.
Addressing security and quality concerns will be vital to drive wider adoption. To achieve this, we must :
demonstrate that secondary devices are secure and that “refurbished” does not imply substandard quality.
Our current messaging primarily focuses on technical aspects rather than catering to the needs of our
diverse audience. By reframing our offerings to address the unique requirements of various stakeholders,
such as manufacturers, telcos, corporate IT buyers, and end-users, we can increase evangelism and gain

: greater media coverage. ; .

Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion (DEI) is a priority in our People Strategy. We are committed to achieving a
high evel of Diversity (the D); but adopt a fair and equitable treatment for all people (the E), and don’t

Environmental matters .
As mentioned earlier, the Company’s mission is to promote the circular economy. E-waste is one of the
fastest-growing waste streams in the world. Approximately 65 million metric tons were generated across
the world in 2025, and following increased demand for consumer electronics, global e-waste is on track to
reach 80 million metric tons a year by 2030. Alchemy is part of the solution to this problem. By collecting
and refurbishing used devices, we aid in global reduction of e-waste, protect the planet's finite resources, .
and add green jobs to the market. Alchemy has already given a new life to over 13 million devices and
avoided over 837m kg of CO2 vs. the manufacture of new products.
