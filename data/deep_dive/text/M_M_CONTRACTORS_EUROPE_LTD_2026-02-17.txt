STRATEGIC REPORT
FOR THE YEAR ENDED 31 OCTOBER 2024
Introduction
The directors present the strategic report for the year ended 31 October 2024.
Business review
During the reporting period, the company focused on utilities infrastructure and electric vehicle (EV) infrastructure development.
The company recognised the EV infrastructure sector as an emerging market in the UK and Ireland, positioning itself as a leader in delivering EV infrastructure projects. This recognition is underscored by several accolades, including the prestigious title of EV Contractor of the Year. This success has not only introduced a new revenue stream but has also significantly contributed to the overall revenue growth for the year.
Throughout the year, the company enhanced its internal management structure by promoting key senior staff to the Board of Directors and establishing a new Senior Leadership Team. Additionally, a comprehensive schedule of management and leadership training was implemented, preparing the organisation for future challenges and opportunities.
The Directors and Board are pleased with the company’s performance over the past year. They believe that the recent management changes will effectively support the company’s growth objectives. With a robust project pipeline for the upcoming year, the Board is optimistic about building on the successes achieved to date.
Principal risks and uncertainties
The company uses financial instruments throughout its business. The core finance and interest rate risks associated with the company's financial instruments (i.e. its interest-bearing loans, cash, short-dated liquid investments and finance leases, on the operational level trade receivables and payables) are currency risk, interest rate risk and credit risk. The board reviews and agrees policies for the prudent management of these risks as follows:
Currency risk
The company conducts business primarily in Sterling (£) and Euro (€) and has accounts in each currency where currency risk is monitored and managed.  
Finance and Interest rate risk
The company's objective in relation to interest rate management is to minimise the impact of interest rate volatility on interest costs in order to protect recorded profitability.  
Credit risk
The company has no significant concentrations of credit risk.  Customers who wish to trade on credit terms are subject to strict verification procedures in advance of credit being awarded and are continually being monitored.
Inflation risk
The company will continue to take steps to ensure the current inflation crisis in the UK and global economy does not materially impact on the business. Costs will be monitored and controlled closely to mitigate the impact of inflation on the business.
